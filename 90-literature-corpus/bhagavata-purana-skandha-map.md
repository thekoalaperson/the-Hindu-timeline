# The Bhāgavata Purāṇa — Skandha-by-Skandha Map

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/` — a per-canto map of the single most influential narrative source in this timeline.
> Up-link: [`./README.md`](README.md) · See also the parent survey [`./smriti-puranas.md`](smriti-puranas.md) and the avatāra catalog [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).

The **Bhāgavata Purāṇa** (*Śrīmad Bhāgavatam*, "the Beautiful [story] of the Lord") is the *Mahā-Purāṇa* most central to Vaiṣṇava *bhakti*. It is organized into **12 skandhas** ("cantos," literally "trunks/sections"), conventionally **335 adhyāyas** (chapters) and **~18,000 ślokas** (verses). It is one of the **sāttvic** six (see [`./smriti-puranas.md`](smriti-puranas.md)), attributed to **Vyāsa**, and exists in our timeline as the text Vyāsa composed *during this Kali Yuga* after Nārada chided him for cataloging dharma without celebrating the Lord (BhP 1.5).

- **The three-layer frame narration** — the Bhāgavata is told through nested dialogues, a structure worth fixing before the canto map:
  - **Outer frame:** the *sūta* (bard) **Ugraśravas / Sūta Gosvāmī** recites the whole to the sages of the **Naimiṣa forest** during Kali Yuga (BhP 1.1). See [`../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/`](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/).
  - **Middle frame:** **Śuka(deva) Gosvāmī**, son of Vyāsa, speaks the bulk to **King Parīkṣit** (Arjuna's grandson) on the bank of the Gaṅgā during the **seven days** before Parīkṣit's death by the snake Takṣaka — a death set in motion in the Mahābhārata aftermath. See [`../04-deep-dives/mahabharata/README.md`](../04-deep-dives/mahabharata/README.md).
  - **Inner frames:** embedded teacher–pupil dialogues — Maitreya–Vidura (skandhas 3–4), Nārada–Yudhiṣṭhira (7), Kapila–Devahūti (3), Yamarāja–Yamadūtas (6), Kṛṣṇa–Uddhava (11), and others.

- **Daśa-lakṣaṇa (ten marks)** — the Bhāgavata uniquely (BhP 2.10; 12.7) expands the classical *pañca-lakṣaṇa* to **ten topics**: *sarga, visarga, sthāna, poṣaṇa, ūti, manvantara, īśānukathā, nirodha, mukti, āśraya*. The whole 12-canto architecture is a deliberate enactment of these ten, climaxing in *āśraya* (the ultimate substratum, Kṛṣṇa). See [`./smriti-puranas.md`](smriti-puranas.md).

## The 12 Skandhas — contents map

### Skandha 1 — Introduction & the setting of the frame (19 chapters)
- **Naimiṣa-forest frame opens** — Sūta answers the sages' six questions; the *maṅgalācaraṇa* ("*janmādy asya yataḥ*," BhP 1.1.1) echoes the Brahma-sūtra opening, signaling Vedāntic ambition.
- **Nārada instructs Vyāsa** — Vyāsa, despondent after compiling the Vedas, Mahābhārata, and the *pañca-lakṣaṇa* Purāṇas, is told by Nārada to glorify Hari directly; the Bhāgavata is the result (BhP 1.5–7).
- **End of the Kṛṣṇa era & onset of Kali** — Kṛṣṇa's departure; **Kali Yuga formally begins** the moment Kṛṣṇa leaves the earth (traditionally **3102 BCE**). Parīkṣit confronts and restrains a personified **Kali** (BhP 1.16–17).
- **Parīkṣit cursed to die in seven days** — bitten-curse by the boy-brāhmaṇa Śṛṅgi; this curse is the dramatic engine for skandhas 2–12. (Source: BhP 1; see [`../04-deep-dives/mahabharata/16-mausala-parva.md`](../04-deep-dives/mahabharata/16-mausala-parva.md).)

### Skandha 2 — Cosmic form & the process of creation (10 chapters)
- **Virāṭ-rūpa (universal form)** — Śuka opens his teaching with the cosmic body of the Lord and the practice of dying in *yoga* meditation (apt, given Parīkṣit's deadline).
- **Catuḥ-ślokī Bhāgavata** — the "four-verse seed" (BhP 2.9.33–36) that Viṣṇu spoke to Brahmā, treated by the tradition as the Bhāgavata in miniature.
- **Daśa-lakṣaṇa stated** (BhP 2.10) — the ten marks; *sarga* and *visarga* (primary and secondary creation) introduced. Cross-link cosmogony: [`../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md`](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md).

### Skandha 3 — Creation continued, Varāha, and Kapila's Sāṃkhya (33 chapters)
- **Maitreya–Vidura dialogue** — frame for the cosmogonic core.
- **Brahmā's creation** from Garbhodaka-śāyī Viṣṇu; the *tattvas* unfold from *prakṛti*.
- **Varāha avatāra** — the Boar lifts the Earth from the cosmic waters and slays **Hiraṇyākṣa**; tied to the *Jaya–Vijaya* curse (Viṣṇu's gatekeepers reborn as demons across three lives). Cross-link: [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).
- **Kapila avatāra & Sāṃkhya** — Kapila teaches **Devahūti** (his mother) devotional *Sāṃkhya* and *bhakti-yoga*; a primary scriptural Sāṃkhya source. Cross-link: [`../05-concepts-and-dharma/atman-brahman-and-the-six-darshanas.md`](../05-concepts-and-dharma/atman-brahman-and-the-six-darshanas.md).

### Skandha 4 — Dakṣa, Dhruva, Pṛthu (31 chapters)
- **Dakṣa's sacrifice & Satī's self-immolation** — the rupture with Śiva. Cross-link: [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/sati-daksha-and-parvati.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/sati-daksha-and-parvati.md).
- **Dhruva** — the child-prince whose austerity wins Viṣṇu's vision and the immovable **pole-star** station (Dhruva-loka / Dhruva-maṇḍala).
- **King Pṛthu (Pṛthu avatāra)** — first consecrated king; "milks" the Earth (Pṛthvī) to restore abundance after the tyrant **Vena**. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md`](../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md).
- **The Pracetās & the *Puraṃjana* allegory** — soul-as-city parable taught via Nārada.

### Skandha 5 — Ṛṣabha, Bharata, and the bhuvana-koṣa (cosmography) (26 chapters)
- **Priyavrata** — Svāyambhuva Manu's son whose chariot-wheels carve the seven *dvīpas* (continents).
- **Ṛṣabha avatāra & Bharata** — Ṛṣabhadeva, the renunciant-king (claimed by Jain tradition as the first *Tīrthaṅkara*); his son **Bharata**, who through attachment to a deer is reborn as a deer, then as **Jaḍa Bharata**. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md`](../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md).
- **Bhuvana-koṣa (cosmic geography)** — the most detailed Purāṇic map: **Jambūdvīpa**, Mount **Meru**, the seven seas/continents, the **planetary systems** (Bhū-, Bhuvar-, Svar-loka up to Satya-loka), and the **28 narakas** (hells) under Yama. Cross-link: [`../06-sacred-geography/`](../06-sacred-geography/) and [`../00-time-and-cosmology/`](../00-time-and-cosmology/).

### Skandha 6 — Dharma, Ajāmila, Vṛtra (19 chapters)
- **Ajāmila** — the fallen brāhmaṇa saved at death by calling his son **"Nārāyaṇa"**; the locus classicus for the power of the *nāma* (name). Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/ajamila.md`](../04-deep-dives/ajamila.md).
- **Yamarāja instructs the Yamadūtas** — on *bhāgavata-dharma* vs. *vaidika-dharma*.
- **Vṛtrāsura** — the demon-devotee whose battle with Indra; the **Nārāyaṇa-kavaca** mantra-armor.
- **Citraketu** — king cursed by Pārvatī to be born the demon Vṛtra (linking the two episodes).

### Skandha 7 — Prahlāda & Narasiṃha (15 chapters)
- **Hiraṇyakaśipu's boon** — near-invincibility (not by man or beast, day or night, indoors or out, on earth or in sky, by no weapon) — the second life of the Jaya–Vijaya curse.
- **Prahlāda** — the demon's son, paradigmatic child-devotee; his teachings on *bhakti* to his classmates (the *nava-vidha bhakti*, nine limbs of devotion, BhP 7.5.23).
- **Narasiṃha avatāra** — the Man-Lion bursts from a pillar at twilight to disembowel Hiraṇyakaśipu, threading every clause of the boon. Cross-link: [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).
- **Varṇāśrama-dharma** — Nārada to Yudhiṣṭhira on the duties of the four *varṇas* and *āśramas*. Cross-link: [`../05-concepts-and-dharma/varna-ashrama-and-samskaras.md`](../05-concepts-and-dharma/varna-ashrama-and-samskaras.md).

### Skandha 8 — Gajendra, the Manus, Samudra-manthana, Vāmana, Matsya (24 chapters)
- **Gajendra-mokṣa** — the elephant-king seized by a crocodile, liberated when he surrenders to Viṣṇu; a touchstone of *śaraṇāgati* (surrender). Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/gajendra-moksha.md`](../04-deep-dives/gajendra-moksha.md).
- **The Manvantara catalog** — the 14 **Manus** (past, present, future) and their respective Indras, sages, and avatāras are listed here; we are in the **7th, Vaivasvata**. Cross-link: [`../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md`](../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md) and [`../01-manvantaras/`](../01-manvantaras/).
- **Samudra-manthana** — the churning of the milk-ocean: the **Kūrma avatāra** as pivot, the emergence of *amṛta*, **Dhanvantari**, Lakṣmī, and the **Mohinī avatāra** who tricks the asuras out of the nectar. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/samudra-manthana.md`](../04-deep-dives/samudra-manthana.md).
- **Vāmana avatāra** — the Dwarf reclaims the three worlds from the noble asura-king **Bali** in three strides (Trivikrama).
- **Matsya avatāra** — placed at the *end* of canto 8: the Fish saves **Satyavrata (Manu)**, the seeds of life, and the Vedas from the dissolution-flood — the Manvantara-transition par excellence.

### Skandha 9 — The dynasties (vaṃśa) (24 chapters)
- **Sūryavaṃśa (Solar) & Candravaṃśa (Lunar)** royal genealogies — the spine of the timeline's history, from Ikṣvāku and Ilā/Pururavas down to the Yādavas. Cross-link: [`../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md`](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md).
- **Rāma avatāra** — the Solar line's apex; condensed Rāmāyaṇa. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/ramayana/`](../04-deep-dives/ramayana/).
- **Paraśurāma avatāra** — the warrior-brāhmaṇa who clears the kṣatriyas twenty-one times.
- **Yayāti, Pururavas–Urvaśī** — Lunar-line morality tales on lust and renunciation. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/yayati.md`](../04-deep-dives/yayati.md).
- Genealogies converge on **Kṛṣṇa**, setting up canto 10.

### Skandha 10 — KṚṢṆA (the longest canto; ~90 chapters, ~4,000 verses)
- **The heart of the Bhāgavata** — Kṛṣṇa's full *carita*, traditionally divided **Pūrvārdha** (former half, Vṛndāvana) and **Uttarārdha** (latter half, Mathurā/Dvārakā). Set in the **Dvāpara Yuga**. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/krishna-lila.md`](../04-deep-dives/krishna-lila.md).
- **Birth & infancy** — born to Vasudeva and Devakī in Kaṃsa's prison; smuggled to Nanda and Yaśodā in Gokula/Vṛndāvana.
- **Bāla-līlā & demon-slayings** — **Pūtanā, Tṛṇāvarta, Śakaṭāsura, Aghāsura, Bakāsura, Dhenukāsura**; the **Kāliya-damana** (subduing the serpent); the *butter-thief*, the vision of the universe in his mouth.
- **Govardhana-līlā** — Kṛṣṇa lifts Mount Govardhana, displacing Indra-worship with devotion.
- **Rāsa-līlā** (BhP 10.29–33) — the circle-dance with the *gopīs*; theological core of *mādhurya-bhakti* and the *Gopī-gīta*.
- **Mathurā & Dvārakā** — slaying of **Kaṃsa**; conflicts with **Jarāsandha** and **Kālayavana**; building of **Dvārakā**; marriages to Rukmiṇī, Satyabhāmā, and the 16,100; defeat of **Narakāsura**.
- **Sudāmā / Kucela** — the poor friend; *bhakti* over wealth.

### Skandha 11 — The Uddhava Gītā & the end of the Yādavas (31 chapters)
- **The Yādava destruction** — a brāhmaṇa-curse causes the Yādava clan to annihilate itself at Prabhāsa; the *mausala* drinking-brawl. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/mahabharata/16-mausala-parva.md`](../04-deep-dives/mahabharata/16-mausala-parva.md).
- **Uddhava Gītā (Haṃsa Gītā)** — Kṛṣṇa's *final teaching*, to his friend **Uddhava**, on *jñāna*, *bhakti*, and renunciation — often called a sequel/companion to the **Bhagavad Gītā**. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/bhagavad-gita.md`](../04-deep-dives/bhagavad-gita.md).
- **The avadhūta's 24 gurus** — the wandering ascetic who learned from earth, wind, python, bee, etc. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/dattatreya.md`](../04-deep-dives/dattatreya.md).
- **The nine Yogendras** (Bhāgavata-dharma) before King Nimi.
- **Kṛṣṇa's disappearance** — struck in the heel by the hunter **Jarā**; with his departure the **Kali Yuga** begins in full (consistent with skandha 1).

### Skandha 12 — Kali Yuga prophecy, pralaya, and Kalki (13 chapters)
- **Future-dynasty prophecy** — Śuka foretells the post-Mahābhārata kings (Māgadha, Nanda, Maurya, Śuṅga, Andhra, etc.) down into Kali, in the past-tense-of-prophecy style. Cross-link dynasties: [`../03-lineages-vamsha/`](../03-lineages-vamsha/).
- **Kali-doṣa (the faults of the Age)** — the catalog of degeneration (BhP 12.2): truncated lifespans, dharma standing on one leg, religion measured by wealth, etc. Cross-link: [`../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/`](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/).
- **Kalki avatāra** — at Kali's end the Lord descends as **Kalki**, son of Viṣṇuyaśas in **Śambhala**, to destroy the wicked and inaugurate a new Satya Yuga. Cross-link: [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).
- **The four pralayas** — *nitya, naimittika, prākṛtika, ātyantika* (constant, occasional, elemental, final dissolution). Cross-link: [`../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md`](../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md).
- **Mārkaṇḍeya** — the *cira-jīvī* sage who survives the cosmic flood and sees the cosmos in the infant on the banyan leaf. Cross-link: [`../04-deep-dives/markandeya.md`](../04-deep-dives/markandeya.md), [`../04-deep-dives/the-chiranjivis.md`](../04-deep-dives/the-chiranjivis.md).
- **Colophon** — the verse-count is given (**18,000**), the Bhāgavata is praised as the essence of all Vedānta, and the *phala-śruti* (fruits of recitation) closes the text.

## The avatāra catalog within the Bhāgavata

- **Lists in skandhas 1 and 2** — the Bhāgavata gives its *avatāra* enumerations early:
  - **BhP 1.3** lists **22 (or 23) līlā-avatāras**, ending with the declaration *"ete cāṃśa-kalāḥ puṃsaḥ, kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam"* — "these are portions and part-portions of the Lord, **but Kṛṣṇa is the Lord himself**" (BhP 1.3.28), the signature Gauḍīya proof-text.
  - **BhP 2.7** gives a parallel, somewhat longer descent-list.
  - *Variant (count):* the avatāras are also called **innumerable** (*avatārā hy asaṅkhyeyāḥ*, BhP 1.3.26) — "like streams from an inexhaustible lake." The "ten" of the standard *Daśāvatāra* is a later crystallization not native to these lists. Cross-link: [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).
  - *Variant (Buddha):* the Bhāgavata's lists name **Buddha** as a *future*/coming avatāra (BhP 1.3.24), reflecting that the text predates fixing Buddha among the past ten in some reckonings [scholarly].

## Dating & textual variants

- **Traditional dating** — composed by **Vyāsa** at the cusp of Kali Yuga (so, c. 3102 BCE by scriptural reckoning), as the maturation of his earlier works [scripture]. ISKCON and many traditionalists hold a ~5,000-year antiquity.
- **Academic dating** — most scholars place the **final redaction in the 9th–10th century CE**, likely **South India** (Tamil country), by brāhmaṇa ascetics steeped in **Āḻvār** *bhakti*; some posit a Gupta-era (4th–6th c. CE) substratum [scholarly] [disputed].
  - *Evidence:* the text is cited by **al-Bīrūnī (c. 1030 CE)** and quoted by **Abhinavagupta**; it appears in 9th–12th-century South/East Indian inscriptions; the **oldest dated manuscript is c. 1124–25 CE** (Varanasi).
  - *Anomaly (van Buitenen):* the South-Indian Vaiṣṇava theologians **Yāmuna and Rāmānuja (10th–11th c.)** do **not** cite the Bhāgavata — a puzzle for the "mature South-Indian text by 900 CE" thesis [scholarly] [disputed].
- **Identity dispute — Bhāgavata vs. Devī-Bhāgavata** — Śākta tradition claims the **Devī-Bhāgavata** as the "true" Mahāpurāṇa; mainstream lists keep the Vaiṣṇava (Kṛṣṇa) Bhāgavata and demote the Devī-Bhāgavata to an Upapurāṇa. See [`./smriti-puranas.md`](smriti-puranas.md) and [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devi-mahatmya-and-the-great-goddess.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devi-mahatmya-and-the-great-goddess.md).
- **Verse/chapter-count variants** — the **18,000** total is canonical (and used in lists of Purāṇic verse-totals), but recensions differ slightly in chapter divisions; **335 chapters** is the common count. Canto 10 is sometimes split into two "halves" counted separately.
- **Commentarial recensions** — the text reads differently through its great commentaries: **Śrīdhara Svāmī** (*Bhāvārtha-dīpikā*, the standard), **Madhva** (*Bhāgavata-tātparya*, Dvaita), **Vijayadhvaja**, **Vīrarāghava** (Viśiṣṭādvaita), **Jīva Gosvāmī / Viśvanātha Cakravartī** (Gauḍīya, Acintya-bhedābheda). Each reads the *āśraya* (skandha 1/2) and the Rāsa-līlā (skandha 10) according to its school [scholarly].
