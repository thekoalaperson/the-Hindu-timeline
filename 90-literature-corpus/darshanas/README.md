# Darśana Root-Texts — Index

> Part of the **[Literature Corpus](../README.md)** · Master index: **[The Hindu Timeline](../../README.md)** · Parent survey: **[Upavedas, Vedāngas & the Six Darśanas](../upavedas-vedangas-darshanas.md)**.
> Landing page for the **mūla-sūtra** (root-text) profiles of the six orthodox (*āstika*) schools of Hindu philosophy — the **ṣaḍ-darśana** ("six viewpoints"), each accepting the **[Vedas](../shruti-vedas-and-upanishads.md)** as *pramāṇa* (authority). The schools are traditionally read in three complementary pairs: **Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika** (logic + atomism), **Sāṃkhya–Yoga** (enumeration + discipline), **Mīmāṃsā–Vedānta** (ritual exegesis + Brahman-inquiry, the *pūrva*- and *uttara-mīmāṃsā*).

---

## Two clocks — orientation

- **Scriptural / traditional clock.** Every root-text is ascribed to a primordial *r̥ṣi* (Kapila, Patañjali, Gautama, Kaṇāda, Jaimini, Bādarāyaṇa) and held to transmit timeless, Veda-rooted truth taught across the present [Vaivasvata Manvantara](../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). [traditional]
- **Academic clock.** The surviving sūtra/kārikā strata are layered compositions of roughly **c. 2nd c. BCE – 5th c. CE**, often post-dating the named founders and crystallizing oral schools; several "root" sūtras (e.g. the *Sāṃkhya-sūtra*) are demonstrably late. [scholarly]
- **Sūtra style.** Each text is a chain of *sūtras* (terse aphorisms) or *kārikās* (memory-verses) — dense, ambiguous by design, and intelligible only through a *bhāṣya* (commentary). The commentarial layer is where each school's real content lives. [scholarly]

## The six schools & their root-texts

- **Sāṃkhya** — dualist enumeration of *puruṣa* (consciousness) and *prakṛti* (matter), the 25 *tattvas*; ascribed to **Kapila**. Authoritative classical text: the **[Sāṃkhya-kārikā of Īśvarakṛṣṇa](./samkhya-karika.md)** (c. 350–500 CE) — used in place of the late, medieval *Sāṃkhya-sūtra*. (*Sāṃkhya-kārikā*; *Bhagavata Purāṇa* 3.24–33.)
- **Yoga** — the eight-limbed (*aṣṭāṅga*) discipline of stilling the mind (*citta-vṛtti-nirodha*); the practical twin of Sāṃkhya, adding **Īśvara**. Root-text: the **[Yoga-sūtra of Patañjali](../../04-deep-dives/yoga-sutras-of-patanjali.md)** (c. 2nd–4th c. CE), 4 *pādas*, ~195–196 sūtras — full profile (4 pādas, commentary chain, dating debates) lives in `04-deep-dives/`; survey entry in [Upavedas, Vedāngas & the Six Darśanas §3](../upavedas-vedangas-darshanas.md).
- **Nyāya** — logic, debate, and epistemology (*pramāṇa-śāstra*, *ānvīkṣikī*); 16 categories beginning with the four *pramāṇas*. Root-text: the **[Nyāya Sūtras of Akṣapāda Gautama](./nyaya-sutras.md)** (c. 2nd c. BCE – 2nd c. CE).
- **Vaiśeṣika** — atomistic ontology of substance, quality, and the categories (*padārtha*); Nyāya's sister realism. Root-text: the **[Vaiśeṣika Sūtras of Kaṇāda](./vaisheshika-sutras.md)** (c. 2nd c. BCE – 1st c. CE).
- **Pūrva-Mīmāṃsā** — exegesis of Vedic *dharma* and ritual injunction (*vidhi*), theory of *śabda* and *apauruṣeya* Veda. Root-text: the **[Mīmāṃsā Sūtras (Pūrva-Mīmāṃsā-sūtra) of Jaimini](./mimamsa-sutras.md)** (c. 3rd–1st c. BCE; 12 *adhyāyas*).
- **Vedānta (Uttara-Mīmāṃsā)** — inquiry into **Brahman** via the [Upaniṣads](../principal-upanishads-detail.md), the *prasthāna-traya* hinge. Root-text: the **[Brahma Sūtras (Vedānta-sūtra) of Bādarāyaṇa](./brahma-sutras.md)** (c. 1st c. BCE – 2nd c. CE; 4 *adhyāyas*, ~555 sūtras).
