# The 18 Upapurāṇas — Overview & Per-Text Notes

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/puranas/` — survey of the **eighteen Upa-Purāṇas** ("secondary Purāṇas"), the lesser canon paralleling the 18 Mahāpurāṇas.
> Up-link: [`../README.md`](../README.md) (puranas index) · [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) (literature corpus).
> Parent survey: [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) (verse-counts, guṇa scheme, list variants). Sibling files: the **Gāṇapatya pair** [`ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md`](ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md); the **Devī-Bhāgavata** dispute in [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) and [`markandeya-purana.md`](markandeya-purana.md) (Devī Māhātmya).

The **Upapurāṇas** ("subsidiary / secondary Purāṇas") are a second canon of **eighteen** texts that mirrors the 18 Mahāpurāṇas in form (sage-to-sage dialogue frame, *pañca-lakṣaṇa* pretensions, ascription to **Vyāsa**) but ranks below them in authority and is far more **sectarian, ritual-technical, and regional** in character. Unlike the Mahāpurāṇa lists — which are essentially stable — **the lists of 18 Upapurāṇas rarely agree**; the genre is open-ended, and counting every text ever styled "Upa-purāṇa" yields **nearly 100** (Hazra). They are the workhorses of **sect formation**: Śaiva ritual manuals (Śivadharma), Śākta tantra (Kālikā), Saura sun-worship (Sāmba, Saura), Gāṇapatya theology (Gaṇeśa, Mudgala — own file), and Vaiṣṇava avatāra-lore (Nārasiṃha). Most are **[late text]** relative to the Mahāpurāṇas and weak on the full *pañca-lakṣaṇa*. The foundational academic study is **R. C. Hazra, *Studies in the Upapurāṇas*** (2 vols., 1958–63) **[scholarly]**.

This file gives **per-text notes** on the eighteen and catalogs the **enumeration variants**. For the two **Gāṇapatya** upapurāṇas and the **Devī-Bhāgavata** (treated as Mahā- vs. Upa- in different lists) see the cross-links above.

## The enumeration is not fixed — list variants

The "18" is a target number, not a closed set. Different sources name different texts; the same name can mean different works; and several texts float between the Mahā- and Upa- canons.

- ***Variant — the standard Kūrma/Devī-Bhāgavata list:*** the most-cited enumeration (Devī-Bhāgavata Purāṇa 1.3; echoed in Kūrma & Garuḍa) runs: **Sanatkumāra, Nārasiṃha, Nāradīya (Bṛhannāradīya), Śiva(dharma), Durvāsasa, Kāpila, Mānava, Auśanasa, Varuṇa, Kālikā, Sāmba, Nandi (Nandā), Saura, Pārāśara, Āditya, Māheśvara, Bhāgavata (=Devī, or read **Bhārgava**), Vāsiṣṭha.** This is the spine of the present file.
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata vs. Bhārgava):* **Wilson** argued the list's 17th seat "Bhāgavata" is a corruption of **Bhārgava** (of Bhṛgu); other lists read it as the **Devī-Bhāgavata** itself. Catalog both. (Bhāgavata Mahā- vs. Devī-Bhāgavata Upa-: see [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).)
- ***Variant — the Wikipedia / "Major Upapurāṇas" list:*** a modern composite substitutes **Mudgala, Gaṇeśa, Vishṇudharmottara, Bhārgava** into seats otherwise held by Durvāsa/Bṛhannāradīya/Vāsiṣṭha — reflecting which texts actually **survive in print**.
- ***Variant — Reva-khaṇḍa (Skanda) list:*** opens Sanatkumāra, Nārasiṃha, **Nandā, Śivadharma, Durvāsasa, Bhaviṣya (Nāradīya)**, Kāpila… diverging from the Devī-Bhāgavata order.
- ***Variant — digest lists:*** medieval *nibandhas* — **Hemādri's *Caturvargacintāmaṇi*, Ballāla Sena's *Dānasāgara*, the *Auśanasa*** — each cite their own roster, frequently quoting Upapurāṇas now lost.
- ***Variant — floaters:*** the **Devī-Bhāgavata** (Mahā- in Vāyu/Matsya/Śākta lists, Upa- in Padma/Garuḍa/Kūrma), **Kalki**, **Vishṇudharmottara**, **Bṛhaddharma**, **Gaṇeśa/Mudgala**, and **Sūrya/Saura** vs. **Sāmba** overlap all migrate between rosters. **[disputed]**

## Per-text notes (the standard eighteen)

### Vaiṣṇava / avatāra-leaning
- **Sanatkumāra Purāṇa** - Narrated by the eternal boy-sage **Sanatkumāra** (eldest mānasa-putra of Brahmā); a devotional miscellany on *bhakti*, *tīrtha*, and *dāna*, leaning Vaiṣṇava. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — Sanatkumāra is a primordial, ageless ṛṣi.)* On Sanatkumāra among the four Kumāras: [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md).
- **Nārasiṃha (Nṛsiṃha) Purāṇa** - ~68 chapters on **Viṣṇu's Narasiṃha (man-lion) avatāra** slaying Hiraṇyakaśipu and saving Prahlāda; strongly Vaiṣṇava, with avatāra-stotras. *(Chronology: the Narasiṃha episode is set in a primordial cycle, narratively early — cross-link the avatāra spine [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).)*
  - *Variant:* not to be confused with the **Nṛsiṃha-tāpanīya Upaniṣad** (same deity, different genre).
- **Bṛhannāradīya / Nāradīya (Upa-)** - The *minor* Nārada text (distinct from the Mahāpurāṇa **Nārada/Nāradīya**, which has its own file [`narada-purana.md`](narada-purana.md)); Vaiṣṇava *bhakti*, *vrata*, and the glories of Hari's names. *(Chronology: framework/devotional.)*
  - *Variant:* some lists read seat #6 here as **Bhaviṣya** (cf. [`bhavishya-purana.md`](bhavishya-purana.md)) "related by Nārada."
- **Kāpila Purāṇa** - ~21 chapters ascribed to the sage **Kapila**, expounding **Sāṃkhya** philosophy in Purāṇic dress; later recension also a *sthala-māhātmya* of **Koṇārka/Odisha**. *(Chronology: framework — Kapila as avatāra of Viṣṇu and founder of Sāṃkhya; cross-link [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).)*
- **Mānava Purāṇa** - Ascribed to **Manu**; largely lost/unavailable, known mainly from citations on *dharma* and *ācāra*. *(Chronology: a Manu-narrated text — frame keyed to a manvantara; see [`../../01-manvantaras/README.md`](../../01-manvantaras/README.md).)* **[late text / largely lost]**
- **Bhārgava Purāṇa** - Ascribed to the **Bhṛgu** lineage (sometimes narrated by **Vasiṣṭha**); known chiefly as the alternate reading of the disputed 17th seat (see Bhāgavata-vs-Bhārgava variant above). *(Chronology: framework.)* **[disputed identity]**

### Śaiva
- **Śivadharma Purāṇa** - ~24 chapters; a **ritual manual for Śaiva laity** — *liṅga*-worship, vows, gifts, and the duties of the devotee, rather than narrative myth. An early, influential Śaiva *dharma*-text. *(Chronology: framework/ritual — timeless.)* Cross-link the liṅga origin: [`linga-purana.md`](linga-purana.md), [`shiva-purana.md`](shiva-purana.md).
- **Nandi / Nandā / Nandikeśvara Purāṇa** - Narrated by **Nandi(keśvara)**, Śiva's bull-faced gaṇa-chief and gatekeeper; Śaiva *māhātmya* and (in the Wilson/Skanda telling) **Kārttikeya recounts the story of Nandā** within it. *(Chronology: framework — Nandi as eternal attendant of Śiva.)* Cross-link Skanda/Kārttikeya: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md).
  - *Variant (Nandi vs. Nandikeśvara):* the names are used interchangeably across lists for what may be one text or a small cluster.
- **Māheśvara Purāṇa** - ~12 chapters; Śaiva, on **Śiva–Pārvatī's family and the assembly of deities**; largely unpublished. *(Chronology: framework.)* **[unpublished / obscure]**
- **Saura Purāṇa** - ~69 chapters; despite its **Saura (sun-worship)** name, the extant text is **strongly Śaiva** — Śiva–Pārvatī lore, *liṅga* theology, and polemic — a classic case of a sect re-labelling an older shell **[scholarly]**. *(Chronology: framework.)* Cross-link sun-worship and the Saura sect: [`brahma-purana.md`](brahma-purana.md) (Sūrya), [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/README.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/README.md).
- **Vāsiṣṭha / Vāsiṣṭha-Liṅga Purāṇa** - Ascribed to **Vasiṣṭha**; a Śaiva *liṅga*-māhātmya (the "Liṅga" tag distinguishes it from the Mahāpurāṇa **Liṅga**). *(Chronology: framework — Vasiṣṭha as Saptarṣi of the present manvantara; cross-link [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md).)*

### Śākta
- **Kālikā Purāṇa** - ~98 chapters; the major **Śākta tantra-Purāṇa**, centered on **Pārvatī/Kālī/Kāmākhyā** and the **Kāmarūpa (Assam)** cult. Notable **distinctive material**:
  - **The Rudhirādhyāya (ch. 67–78) — *bali* / blood-sacrifice manual** [disputed]: detailed prescriptions for **animal (and, exceptionally, human) sacrifice** to the Goddess, with **Vāmācāra** tantric rites — the genre's most explicit such text. *(Chronology: framework/ritual.)*
  - **The Naraka myth & Kāmarūpa kings:** the legend of the demon-king **Naraka(sura)** of Prāgjyotiṣa/Kāmarūpa, from whom the historical dynasties claimed descent — placing the text's composition in **Assam**. *(Chronology: narratively a primordial/Dvāpara-adjacent demon-king; the framing is regional-dynastic.)*
  - *Dating [scholarly]:* **Hazra** posits a lost older Bengal/Assam core; the extant text is **post-10th c. CE** (it references **Ratna Pāla of Kāmarūpa, c. 920–960**). **[late text]** Cross-link the Goddess: [`markandeya-purana.md`](markandeya-purana.md) (Devī Māhātmya), [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md).
- **Devī Purāṇa / Devī-Bhāgavata (Upa-)** - The **Śākta Goddess-Purāṇa**; counted an **Upapurāṇa** by Padma/Garuḍa/Kūrma but claimed a **Mahāpurāṇa** by Śākta tradition (the Bhāgavata-vs-Devī-Bhāgavata dispute). Glorifies the **Mahādevī** as supreme Brahman; contains the **Devī Gītā**. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology.)* Full treatment: [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md). **[disputed canon-rank]**

### Saura (sun-worship) & Naimittika
- **Sāmba Purāṇa** - ~84 chapters; the principal **Saura (sun-worship)** Purāṇa, framed by **Sāmba** (Kṛṣṇa's son), cured of leprosy by the **Sun**, founding the great sun-temple at **Mūlasthāna / Multan**; narrated by **Durvāsā**. Distinctive: it introduces the **Maga Brāhmaṇas** (Iranian/Scythian *Magi* sun-priests) into Hindu society. *(Chronology: narratively **Dvāpara Yuga** — Sāmba is Kṛṣṇa's son; cross-link [`../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/03-dvapara-yuga.md`](../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/03-dvapara-yuga.md).)*
  - *Dating [scholarly]:* **Hazra** dates the core **c. 650–850 CE**; the Maga material reflects **Scythian-patronized** sun-worship. **[late text]**
- **Āditya Purāṇa** - A **Saura** sun-worship text (Āditya = the Sun) on solar ritual, *vrata*, and *māhātmya*; survives mainly in citation. *(Chronology: framework/ritual.)* **[largely lost]**
- **Saura** — see under Śaiva above (the surviving "Saura" is paradoxically Śaiva; the genuinely solar texts are **Sāmba** and **Āditya**). *(Cross-listed.)*

### Miscellaneous narrators
- **Auśanasa / Auśanasa Purāṇa** - Ascribed to **Uśanas/Śukra** (Venus, *guru* of the asuras); a *dharma*/*nīti*-leaning text, also the name of a medieval digest. *(Chronology: framework — Śukra as asura-preceptor; cross-link [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md).)* **[largely lost]**
- **Varuṇa Purāṇa** - ~12 chapters narrated by **Varuṇa** (god of waters/cosmic order); now largely lost, cited for *tīrtha* and *dāna*. *(Chronology: framework.)* **[largely lost]**
- **Pārāśara Purāṇa** - ~18 chapters ascribed to **Parāśara** (father of Vyāsa); *dharma*-and-*ācāra* material, sometimes Vaiṣṇava. *(Chronology: framework — Parāśara as a Saptarṣi-line sage; cross-link [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md).)*
- **Durvāsasa Purāṇa** - Ascribed to the irascible sage **Durvāsā** (the same who narrates the Sāmba); appears in the standard list but is otherwise obscure/lost. *(Chronology: framework.)* **[largely lost]**

## Notable cross-canon points

- **Pañca-lakṣaṇa coverage [scholarly]:** as a class, the Upapurāṇas are **weak** on the five marks — they are **ritual manuals, sect-charters, and *māhātmyas*** far more than cosmogonies/genealogies; like the Gaṇeśa and Nāradīya, they are prime exhibits for the academic view that *pañca-lakṣaṇa* fits few real Purāṇas. (Parent: [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).)
- **Sectarian map:** **Śaiva** (Śivadharma, Saura, Māheśvara, Vāsiṣṭha-Liṅga, Nandi), **Śākta** (Kālikā, Devī), **Saura** (Sāmba, Āditya), **Vaiṣṇava** (Nārasiṃha, Bṛhannāradīya, Kāpila), **Gāṇapatya** (Gaṇeśa, Mudgala — [`ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md`](ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md)).
- **Dating (traditional vs academic):** *Traditional* — co-eternal revelation through Vyāsa, alongside the Mahāpurāṇas. *Academic* — overwhelmingly **medieval and regional**; **Hazra** dates most between the **7th and 12th c. CE**, with several (Kālikā, Bṛhaddharma, Devī-Bhāgavata) later still. Routinely **[late text]**; many show **[interpolation]**.
