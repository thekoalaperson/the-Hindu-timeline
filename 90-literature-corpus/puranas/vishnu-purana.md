# Viṣṇu Purāṇa

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/puranas/` — a per-Purāṇa source file in the literature corpus.
> Up-link: [`../README.md`](../README.md) (the Purāṇas index) · [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) (the literature corpus).
> See also the parent survey [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) and the companion [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).

The **Viṣṇu Purāṇa** is the **paradigmatic "model" Purāṇa** — the one text modern scholars (H. H. Wilson, who first translated it in 1840; Ludo Rocher; Wendy Doniger) repeatedly cite as the **purest exemplar of the *pañca-lakṣaṇa* ideal**, presenting cosmogony, re-creation, genealogy, the Manu-cycles, and dynastic history in cleaner, more cohesive order than almost any other Mahāpurāṇa. It is cast as a **dialogue between the sage Parāśara (vaktā)** — Vyāsa's father — **and his pupil Maitreya (śrotā)**, framed as Parāśara's answer to the question "what is the nature of this universe and all it contains?" It is a thoroughly **Vaiṣṇava** text of **Bhāgavata / Pāñcarātra** affinity, and in the *tri-guṇa* scheme (Pādma P. Uttara-khaṇḍa) it heads the **sāttvic** six (the Viṣṇu-presiding Purāṇas leading to *mokṣa*; see [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md)). It is celebrated as the **philosophical ground-text of Śrī-Vaiṣṇavism**: **Rāmānuja** drew on it to identify the Upaniṣadic Brahman with Viṣṇu.

- **Structure — six *aṃśas* (books/"portions"), 126 *adhyāyas*, traditionally ~23,000 ślokas (only ~7,000 survive):** the *aṃśa* (not *khaṇḍa* or *saṃhitā*) is its organizing unit, and the six neatly map onto the five *lakṣaṇas* plus a *mokṣa* coda. Chapter counts: **Aṃśa 1 (22), 2 (16), 3 (18), 4 (24), 5 (38 — the longest), 6 (8 — the shortest).**
  - **Aṃśa 1 — *sarga* (creation):** Viṣṇu/Vāsudeva as cause and cosmic center; Sāṅkhya *tattvas*; the four creations; Hiraṇyagarbha, Brahmā, the Prajāpatis; the **Prācetasas** and Dakṣa's re-creation; **Dhruva**, **Vena & Pṛthu**, **Prahlāda/Narasiṃha**.
  - **Aṃśa 2 — earth & cosmography:** Priyavrata's line, the **seven *dvīpas* and seven oceans**, **Bhārata-varṣa**, Mount Meru, the *bhuvana-koṣa*, the planetary spheres and the Sun's chariot; the **Bharata / Jaḍa-Bharata** cycle.
  - **Aṃśa 3 — *manvantara* & dharma:** the **14 Manus and their Manvantaras**, the **division of the Veda by the 28 Vyāsas**, *varṇāśrama-dharma*, *śrāddha* and the *saṃskāras*; closes with Viṣṇu's *māyā-moha* deluding the *asuras* (the proto-Buddha/heterodoxy episode).
  - **Aṃśa 4 — *vaṃśa / vaṃśānucarita* (genealogies):** the **Solar (Sūryavaṃśa)** and **Lunar (Candravaṃśa)** royal lines from Brahmā down, with **Parīkṣit named as the "present" king** (a key chronological anchor), ending in the **Kali-yuga king-list / prophecy**.
  - **Aṃśa 5 — *Kṛṣṇa-carita*:** the **complete life of Kṛṣṇa**, the fullest single *aṃśa*; a major pre-Bhāgavata source.
  - **Aṃśa 6 — *pratisarga* / liberation:** the **dissolution (pralaya)**, the **three kinds of cosmic time-cycles**, the **degeneration of the Kali-yuga**, and *yoga* / *brahma-jñāna* as the path to *mokṣa*.
- **Pañca-lakṣaṇa coverage — the gold standard:** uniquely among the Mahāpurāṇas it covers **all five marks fully and in balance** (*sarga, pratisarga, vaṃśa, manvantara, vaṃśānucarita*), with little of the *māhātmya*/pilgrimage padding that swells later texts — hence its textbook status. (Frame cross-links: [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/) and [`../../01-manvantaras/`](../../01-manvantaras/).)
- **Sectarian leaning:** firmly **Vaiṣṇava**, of **Bhāgavata/Pāñcarātra** cast, identifying Viṣṇu with the supreme Brahman, *paramātman*, and the material+spiritual cosmos; relatively **free of overt anti-Śaiva polemic** compared to other sectarian Purāṇas.
- **Dating:** *traditional:* composed by **Vyāsa**, eternal, narrated by Parāśara at the dawn of an age. *Academic [scholarly]:* among the **oldest extant Purāṇas in present form** — Wilson favored an early core; estimates range **V. Smith c. 400–300 BCE**, **Hazra c. 275–325 CE**, **Doniger c. 450 CE**; the **Kali king-list (Aṃśa 4) ending with the Andhra/Sātavāhana dynasty** gives a *terminus* around the **3rd–4th c. CE** [scholarly]. Rocher cautions the date "is as contested as that of any other Purāṇa." Critical edition: M. M. Pathak (1997–99); recent translation: McComas Taylor (2021).

## Notable / distinctive stories and sections

### Cosmogony and the framework (Aṃśa 1, 3, 6)
- **Creation by Viṣṇu/Vāsudeva (sarga)** — Viṣṇu as the material *and* efficient cause; the unfolding of *prakṛti* and the *tattvas*, the cosmic egg, Brahmā's emergence, and the four/secondary creations — the model Purāṇic cosmogony. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — start of a kalpa.)* Cross-link: [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) and [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md).
- **The 14 Manus and Manvantaras (manvantara)** — concise, well-ordered survey of the Manu-cycles, current being the **Vaivasvata (7th)**; a base text for the manvantara scheme. *(Chronology: spans the manvantaras.)* Cross-link: [`../../01-manvantaras/README.md`](../../01-manvantaras/README.md).
- **Division of the Veda by the 28 Vyāsas (Aṃśa 3)** — the recurring redaction of the Veda by a Vyāsa in each Dvāpara, situating Kṛṣṇa-Dvaipāyana as the 28th; a distinctive systematizing chapter. *(Chronology: one per mahāyuga across the Vaivasvata manvantara.)*
- **The pralaya and the three time-cycles (Aṃśa 6)** — *naimittika* (occasional), *prākṛtika* (elemental), and *ātyantika* (final/individual liberation) dissolutions, with *yoga* as the road to *mokṣa*; the *mokṣa*-coda that distinguishes its 6-book plan. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — kalpa- and Brahmā-life boundaries.)* Cross-link: [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/).

### Dhruva (Aṃśa 1) — *paradigmatic bhakti narrative*
- **Dhruva's tapas and the Pole Star** — the child prince **Dhruva**, son of **Uttānapāda** (of Svāyambhuva Manu's line), slighted by his stepmother, performs extreme austerity, receives Viṣṇu's *darśana*, and is exalted as the immovable **Dhruva-loka (Pole Star)**, around which the heavens turn. The Viṣṇu Purāṇa is a **primary, early source** for this beloved story. *(Chronology: very early — **Svāyambhuva manvantara**, framework antiquity; traditionally evoked as a Satya-yuga-type ideal.)* Cross-link: [`../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-01-svayambhuva.md`](../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-01-svayambhuva.md) and the Satya-yuga treatment in [`../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/01-satya-yuga.md`](../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/01-satya-yuga.md).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata P., skandha 4):* the Bhāgavata expands Dhruva with richer *bhakti* psychology and his later reign; the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's telling is leaner. Cross-link: [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).

### Vena and Pṛthu (Aṃśa 1)
- **The wicked king Vena and the milking of the Earth by Pṛthu** — the tyrant **Vena** is slain by the ṛṣis; from his body they churn forth the **first righteous king Pṛthu** (a Viṣṇu *aṃśāvatāra*), who "milks" the Earth (**Pṛthvī**, named for him) of grain and crops, becoming the model *cakravartin* and patron of agriculture. *(Chronology: legendary antiquity — early **Svāyambhuva**-era lineage.)* Cross-link the dedicated deep-dive: [`../../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md`](../../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md).

### Prahlāda and Narasiṃha (Aṃśa 1) — *unusual placement*
- **Hiraṇyakaśipu, Prahlāda, and Narasiṃha** — the *asura* king's persecution of his Viṣṇu-devoted son **Prahlāda**, and Viṣṇu's eruption as the **man-lion Narasiṃha** to slay the boon-protected tyrant. Distinctively, the Viṣṇu Purāṇa places the Prahlāda cycle in **Aṃśa 1** as a vehicle for *bhakti* doctrine (Prahlāda's discourses on devotion), and gives **less weight to the gory Narasiṃha climax** than the Bhāgavata. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — divine-vs-asura, traditionally a prior age; aligned with the avatāra sequence.)* Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) and [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata P., skandha 7):* the Bhāgavata foregrounds Narasiṃha's wrath and Prahlāda's salvific role for his lineage; the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's emphasis is on Prahlāda's *jñāna/bhakti* teaching.

### Bharata / Jaḍa-Bharata (Aṃśa 2)
- **Bharata the royal ascetic, the deer, and Jaḍa-Bharata** — King **Bharata** (of Priyavrata's line) renounces his kingdom but becomes attached to an orphaned fawn, is reborn a deer, then reborn as the seemingly idiot sage **Jaḍa-Bharata**, who finally instructs the king of Sauvīra in non-dualist wisdom (the "*bharata-gītā*"). A landmark teaching on **attachment and rebirth**. *(Chronology: legendary antiquity, in the line of Svāyambhuva/Priyavrata.)* Cross-link: [`../../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md`](../../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata P., skandha 5):* the Bhāgavata embeds Jaḍa-Bharata in the larger **Ṛṣabha → Bharata** (Bhārata-varṣa namesake) frame; the Viṣṇu Purāṇa carries the deer-rebirth core.
- **The Prācetasas and Dakṣa's re-creation (Aṃśa 1)** — the ten **Prācetasa** sons emerge from austerity in the waters and (with Dakṣa) repopulate the world; standard secondary-creation genealogy. *(Chronology: framework — secondary creation.)*

### Cosmography (Aṃśa 2)
- **The seven *dvīpas*, seven oceans, and Bhārata-varṣa** — the concentric-continents geography (Jambū at center, surrounded by salt, sugarcane-juice, wine, ghee, curd, milk, and freshwater oceans), Mount Meru, and the praise of **Bhārata** as the unique *karma-bhūmi* where liberation is won. *(Chronology: framework — cosmic geography of the present kalpa.)* Cross-link: [`../../06-sacred-geography/README.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/README.md) and [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/).

### Royal genealogies — the Sūrya- and Candra-vaṃśa (Aṃśa 4)
- **The Solar and Lunar dynasties from Brahmā to Parīkṣit** — the spine of *itihāsa-purāṇa* chronology: **Ikṣvāku and the Sūryavaṃśa** (incl. Mānd­hātṛ, Sagara and the descent of Gaṅgā, Raghu, **Rāma**, the line continuing to **Bṛhadbala**), and the **Candravaṃśa** (Yayāti's sons; the **Yādava** and **Paurava/Kuru** branches down to Kṛṣṇa, the Pāṇḍavas, and **Parīkṣit**, marked as the *current* king). The Viṣṇu Purāṇa is a **foundational source** for the canonical dynastic tree. *(Chronology: spans Satya→Dvāpara of the current mahāyuga; Parīkṣit at the Dvāpara–Kali junction.)* Cross-link: [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md) and [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md).
  - *Embedded legends:* Sagara's 60,000 sons and the **descent of Gaṅgā**; **Māndhātṛ**; **Yayāti** (cross-link [`../../04-deep-dives/yayati.md`](../../04-deep-dives/yayati.md)); Nimi and Janaka; Saubhari. Cross-link the Rāma summary: [`../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/README.md`](../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/README.md).

### The Kali-yuga king-list and prophecy (Aṃśa 4, ch. 24) — *historically pivotal*
- **The dynasties of the Kali age (the *bhaviṣya-rāja* prophecy)** — uniquely valuable to historians: cast as Parāśara's *prediction*, it lists the post-Parīkṣit dynasties of Magadha — **5 Pradyotas, 10 Śaiśunāgas, 9 Nandas, 10 Mauryas, 10 Śuṅgas, 4 Kāṇvas, and ~30 Āndhrabhṛtyas (Sātavāhanas)** — a near-**historical king-list** that anchors the text's late strata and is mined for ancient Indian chronology [scholarly]. It then foretells the rise of **mleccha and low-born kings**, fragmentation, and pervasive *adharma*. *(Chronology: the **Kali-yuga** of the current 28th mahāyuga — i.e., recorded history.)* Cross-link: [`../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md`](../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md) and [`../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/recorded-history.md`](../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/recorded-history.md).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata P., skandha 12; Matsya/Vāyu/Brahmāṇḍa):* parallel Kali king-lists appear across the Purāṇas with differing dynasty counts and regnal years; the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's list is among the most cited. The **Kalki** avatāra closes the age, destroying the *mleccha* kings and restoring the Kṛta-yuga. Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).
- **The vices of the Kali-yuga (Aṃśa 6)** — the famous lament: dharma reduced to a single quarter, wealth alone the mark of virtue, false ascetics, short lifespans, *mleccha* rule — yet Kali is praised as **the easiest age for liberation**, since mere remembrance of Viṣṇu yields what tapas yielded in earlier yugas. *(Chronology: the **Kali-yuga**.)* Cross-link: [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md).

### Kṛṣṇa (Aṃśa 5) — the longest book
- **The complete life of Kṛṣṇa** — birth at Mathurā to Devakī/Vasudeva, the move to Gokula, the *bāla-* and *gopa-līlā* (Pūtanā, the cart, Yamalārjuna, Kāliya, **Govardhana**, the *rāsa* with the gopīs), the slaying of **Kaṃsa**, the founding of **Dvārakā**, the marriages (Rukmiṇī, Satyabhāmā, the Syamantaka jewel), and the Mahābhārata-era role. As an **early, pre-Bhāgavata Kṛṣṇa biography**, Aṃśa 5 is a key text in the source-critical debate over Kṛṣṇa narrative (Harivaṃśa ↔ Viṣṇu P. ↔ Bhāgavata). *(Chronology: **Dvāpara-yuga**, 28th mahāyuga of the Vaivasvata manvantara.)* Cross-link the fuller treatments: [`../../04-deep-dives/krishna-lila.md`](../../04-deep-dives/krishna-lila.md), [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) (skandhas 10–11), and [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata vs Viṣṇu P. vs Harivaṃśa):* the **rāsa-līlā and bhakti rapture are far more elaborated in the Bhāgavata's Book 10**; the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's account is comparatively restrained and closer to the **Harivaṃśa** skeleton — scholars debate whether the Bhāgavata expanded the Viṣṇu P., whether the Viṣṇu P. abridged a common source, or whether both depend on the Harivaṃśa [scholarly].

### Dharma and ethics (Aṃśa 3)
- **Varṇāśrama, śrāddha, and the saṃskāras** — duties of the four *varṇas* and *āśramas*, ancestral rites, marriage and funerary rites, and universal ethical duties (*sāmānya-dharma*) transcending caste; the model Purāṇic dharma section. *(Chronology: framework — applicable across yugas.)* Cross-link: [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/varna-ashrama-and-samskaras.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/varna-ashrama-and-samskaras.md).
- **Viṣṇu's *māyā-moha* / the deluding of the asuras (Aṃśa 3)** — Viṣṇu emanates a deceiver (read by many as a **proto-Buddha figure**) to seduce the *asuras* from the Veda so the gods can defeat them; an early Purāṇic accommodation/critique of heterodoxy [disputed]. *(Chronology: framework — divine-vs-asura prehistory.)*
