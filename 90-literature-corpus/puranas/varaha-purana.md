# Varāha Purāṇa

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/puranas/` — a per-Purāṇa source file in the literature corpus.
> Up-link: [`../README.md`](../README.md) (the Purāṇas index) · [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) (the literature corpus).
> See also the parent survey [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) and the companion [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).

The **Varāha Purāṇa** is one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas, distinctive for its **revelatory frame**: it is largely narrated by **Viṣṇu in his Boar incarnation (Varāha) to the personified Earth-goddess Bhū-devī / Pṛthivī**, whom he has just rescued from the cosmic ocean — the *vrahā-uddhāra* (boar-lifting-the-earth) myth becomes the very setting for the dialogue. Far from the *pañca-lakṣaṇa* model of the [Viṣṇu Purāṇa](vishnu-purana.md), the extant text reads (so H. H. Wilson) as **"a religious manual, almost wholly occupied with forms of prayer and rules for devotional observances"** — a sprawling anthology of ***vrata*-māhātmyas, *tīrtha*-māhātmyas, *śrāddha* rules, and *dāna* (gift/merit) doctrine**, frequently questioned by scholars as to **whether it qualifies as a true Purāṇa at all** [scholarly]. Nominally **Vaiṣṇava** (it exalts Nārāyaṇa/Viṣṇu and the Mathurā realm) yet strikingly **eclectic**: it carries substantial **Śaiva and Śākta material** — origins of Gaṇeśa, Skanda, Sūrya, the **Mātṛkās**, and Durgā's slaying of demons — that sit only loosely with its Vaiṣṇava frame. It is also notable for what it *lacks*: unlike other Mathurā-centred texts it has **almost no Kṛṣṇa adoration** in its Mathurā section.

- **Structure — a composite of (per R. C. Hazra) four originally separate sections, joined into ~215–218 *adhyāyas*; tradition claims 24,000 ślokas but barely ~10,000 survive** [scholarly]. The *adhyāya*, not *khaṇḍa*/*aṃśa*/*saṃhitā*, is the working unit, though the closing block is styled a *saṃhitā*:
  - **Section 1 (ch. ~1–112):** **Sūta** narrates the **Varāha ↔ Pṛthivī (Bhū-devī)** dialogue — creation queries, *vrata*s, deity-origin myths keyed to *tithis*, *śrāddha*-kalpa, and tīrthas. The doctrinal core.
  - **Section 2 (ch. ~113–192):** **Sūta** relays what **Pṛthivī told Sanatkumāra** about her dialogue with Varāha — heavily **māhātmya** material (Mathurā, Gokarṇa-tīrtha, the merit of *tīrtha-yātrā*).
  - **Section 3 (ch. ~193–212) — the *Dharma-saṃhitā*:** framed as **Janamejaya ↔ Vaiśampāyana** — dharma, the **Naciketa / Yama-loka** narrative, and the **Gokarṇa the merchant** story of social service.
  - **Section 4 (ch. ~213+):** a **Brahmā ↔ Sanatkumāra** dialogue closing the text.
- **Pañca-lakṣaṇa coverage — minimal and lopsided:** *sarga* (creation) appears in the early Pṛthivī chapters, but *pratisarga*, *vaṃśa*, *manvantara*, and *vaṃśānucarita* are thin to absent — the text's weight is overwhelmingly on **ritual, *vrata*, and *tīrtha*** (the *upabṛṃhaṇa* / "religious-manual" stratum). This deficiency is exactly why its Purāṇic status is disputed [scholarly]. Cross-link the contrast: [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) and the model text [`./vishnu-purana.md`](vishnu-purana.md).
- **Sectarian leaning:** **Vaiṣṇava-Bhāgavata in label**, **eclectic-smārta in practice** — its prominent Śiva, Pārvatī, Gaṇeśa, Skanda, Sūrya, and **Devī/Mātṛkā** chapters make it a useful (if late) witness to **pan-sectarian temple religion** rather than a polemical Vaiṣṇava tract.
- **Dating:** *traditional:* revealed by Viṣṇu-Varāha to the Earth, recorded by Vyāsa, eternal. *Academic [scholarly]:* a **relatively late** Mahāpurāṇa — Wilson placed it around **"the age of Rāmānuja, the early 12th c. CE"**; most scholars see a **core perhaps by the 10th c. CE**, continuously **revised/interpolated** thereafter, with the *Dharma-saṃhitā* and several *māhātmya*s as **later accretions** [late text][interpolation]. The Mathurā/Nepal tīrtha-guides reflect **medieval** sacred geography.

## Notable / distinctive stories and sections

### The frame and cosmogony (Section 1)
- **Varāha lifts the Earth, then instructs her** — the Boar avatāra raises the submerged Earth-goddess **Bhū-devī / Pṛthivī** from the cosmic waters; her questions about creation, the yugas, and the incarnations set up the whole text. The *uddhāra* is the **frame device**, not a standalone episode, making this Purāṇa the **deity-to-Earth revelation par excellence**. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — a kalpa-opening / *varāha-kalpa* event.)* Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) and [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md).
  - *Variant (Viṣṇu P. / Bhāgavata skandha 3 / Liṅga P.):* most texts narrate the boar-lift as a single rescue (slaying **Hiraṇyākṣa**); the Varāha Purāṇa uniquely **extends it into a sustained teaching dialogue**. Cross-link: [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).
- **Pṛthivī's creation queries** — the unfolding of *ahaṃkāra* (ego) and the five elements, *mahat-tattva* and collective consciousness combining with the three *guṇas* to spin out the universe — a Sāṅkhya-flavoured *sarga* account. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — start of a kalpa.)*

### Mathurā-māhātmya — the supreme realm (Sections 1–2)
- **Mathurā as Viṣṇu's lotus-maṇḍala of tīrthas** — Mathurā is exalted as Viṣṇu's supreme abode, a **lotus-shaped *maṇḍala* ~20 *yojanas* across said to hold "60,000 *crore* tīrthas,"** with detailed guides to its sacred sites — yet **conspicuously without the Kṛṣṇa-*līlā* adoration** that fills other Mathurā texts, marking an **early/different stratum of Mathurā pilgrimage** [scholarly]. *(Chronology: framework — sacred geography of the present kalpa; some sites tied to the Dvāpara Kṛṣṇa-era.)* Cross-link: [`../../06-sacred-geography/README.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/README.md).
- **Govardhana-tīrtha** — the sacred hill glorified; the text recalls Kṛṣṇa-Varāha-era association with lifting Govardhana against Indra's rain. *(Chronology: Dvāpara-yuga, 28th mahāyuga.)* Cross-link the Kṛṣṇa episode: [`../../04-deep-dives/krishna-lila.md`](../../04-deep-dives/krishna-lila.md).

### Naciketa and the world of Yama (Section 3, *Dharma-saṃhitā*)
- **Naciketa's journey to Yama-loka** — narrated by **Vaiśampāyana**: the boy **Naciketa**, sent (cursed in anger) to the abode of death by his father **Uddālaka**, traverses **Yama's world**, sees the rewards of dharma and the punishments of sin, and **returns to describe it** — a vivid Purāṇic catalogue of heavens, hells, and post-mortem dharma that reworks the **Kaṭha-Upaniṣad's Naciketas** into an eschatological travelogue. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — the afterworld; narrative set in legendary antiquity.)* Cross-link: [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md) and the Upaniṣadic original [`../principal-upanishads-detail.md`](../principal-upanishads-detail.md).
  - *Variant (Kaṭha Upaniṣad):* in the Upaniṣad **Naciketas** wins three boons and the teaching on the Self (*ātman*) from Yama; the Varāha Purāṇa **drops the metaphysics and amplifies the heaven-and-hell tour** [late text]. *Variant (Mārkaṇḍeya / Garuḍa P.):* parallel hell-catalogues appear elsewhere — cross-link [`./markandeya-purana.md`](markandeya-purana.md).

### Gokarṇa the merchant — *the ethic of public works* (Section 3)
- **Gokarṇa and the merit of social service** — the long story of the merchant **Gokarṇa** holds up **planting trees, digging wells and tanks, laying out gardens, and renovating ruined temples/structures** as supreme acts of merit — an unusually **civic, infrastructural vision of *puṇya*** (welfare-as-piety). *(Chronology: framework — dharma teaching; narrative in legendary antiquity.)* Cross-link: [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md). (Distinct from the *Śiva-Purāṇa* Gokarṇa-tīrtha/Gokarṇeśvara material — cross-link [`./shiva-purana.md`](shiva-purana.md).)

### Origins of the Mātṛkās and the Goddess (Section 1) — *distinctive Śākta material*
- **The Mātṛkās born from Vaiṣṇavī's distracted mind** — in the Varāha Purāṇa's **unusual account**, the Mother-Goddesses arise not in battle but from the goddess **Vaiṣṇavī**, who loses concentration during austerity; the text uniquely counts **eight Mātṛkās**, naming **Yamunā (śakti of Yama) as the seventh** and **Yogeśvarī (a form of Pārvatī, sprung from flames of Śiva's mouth) as the eighth** — whereas almost all other Purāṇas and Āgamas list **seven (Sapta-Mātṛkā)**. A key, often-cited variant in Mātṛkā studies [scholarly]. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — divine origins, timeless.)* Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md).
  - *Variant (Devī-Māhātmya / Mārkaṇḍeya P.):* the standard telling has the **Sapta-Mātṛkā** emanate as *śaktis* of the gods to aid Durgā against **Śumbha-Niśumbha / Raktabīja**; the Varāha account's **"distracted mind" origin and eighth goddess Yogeśvarī** are its signature departures. Cross-link: [`./markandeya-purana.md`](markandeya-purana.md).
- **Durgā and the slaying of the buffalo-demon** — the Purāṇa also carries the Goddess's destruction of **Mahiṣāsura** and allied demons, evidence of the **antiquity and spread of the Durgā/Mahiṣa myth** beyond the *Devī-Māhātmya*. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — deva–asura conflict, timeless/prior-age.)* Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).

### Deity-origin myths keyed to *tithis* (Section 1)
- **Birth/origin chapters for Agni, the Aśvini-kumāras, Pārvatī, Gaṇeśa, the Serpents (Nāgas), Kārttikeya/Skanda, and Sūrya** — each origin myth is **attached to a specific lunar day (*tithi*)** and its observance, fusing **mythology with the ritual calendar** — a hallmark of the text's *vrata*-manual character. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — divine origins; observances recur annually.)* Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/README.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/README.md).

### Vratas, śrāddha, and tīrthas — the ritual core (Sections 1–2)
- **The *vrata*-māhātmyas** — extensive prescriptions for vows, fasts, *pūjā*s and *yajña*s for prosperity and purification, with their merit-fruits (e.g. *tithi*-vratas tied to the deity-origin chapters). The Varāha Purāṇa is a **primary source for medieval Vaiṣṇava-smārta observance** [late text]. *(Chronology: framework — observances of the present age.)* Cross-link: [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md).
- **The *śrāddha*-kalpa** — detailed rules for ancestor-worship: auspicious days, choice of brāhmaṇas, offerings, and *piṇḍa-dāna* procedure. *(Chronology: framework — ritual practice.)* Cross-link: [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/varna-ashrama-and-samskaras.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/varna-ashrama-and-samskaras.md).
- **The *tīrtha*-māhātmyas** — glorification of pilgrimage sites across **Mathurā and the Nepal region** and beyond, with their purifying and liberating power; among the **most pilgrimage-heavy of the Purāṇas**, reflecting **medieval sacred geography** [late text]. *(Chronology: framework — sacred geography of the present kalpa.)* Cross-link: [`../../06-sacred-geography/README.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/README.md).

### Edifying royal/sage narratives (Section 1)
- **Priyavrata enlightened by Nārada; Aśvaśira's liberation** — devotional vignettes in which a king (**Priyavrata**) gains past-life wisdom and heaven through *bhakti*, and a king (**Aśvaśira**) learns Nārāyaṇa's omnipresence and that **detached performance of duty (*niṣkāma-karma*) leads to liberation** — *bhakti*/*jñāna* teaching dressed as story. *(Chronology: legendary antiquity.)* Cross-link the cosmographic Priyavrata line in [`./vishnu-purana.md`](vishnu-purana.md).
