# Padma Purāṇa

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/puranas/` — one of the eighteen *Mahā-Purāṇas*.
> Up-link: [`../README.md`](../README.md) (puranas index) · [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) (timeline root).
> See also the parent survey [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) and the [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).

The **Padma Purāṇa** ("the Lotus Purāṇa," named for the *padma* on which Brahmā is born) is the second-largest *Mahā-Purāṇa*, a vast, stratified Vaiṣṇava encyclopedia claiming **~55,000 verses** (surviving manuscripts ~50,000). It is one of the six **sāttvic** Purāṇas (Viṣṇu-oriented, *mokṣa*-leading; see [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md)) and is, fittingly, the very text that promulgates the **guṇa-classification of the eighteen Purāṇas**. Traditionally narrated by **Pulastya** to **Bhīṣma**, and by **Sūta** to the Naimiṣa sages, with embedded **Śiva–Pārvatī** dialogues.

## Structure — recensions and khaṇḍas

- **Bengal recension** (older, manuscript-only, unpublished as a whole) — **five khaṇḍas**: **Sṛṣṭi** (creation), **Bhūmi** (earth), **Svarga** (heaven), **Pātāla** (netherworld), **Uttara** (final). This is the five-fold spine named in the project FOCUS.
- **Devanāgarī / Western (printed) recension** — **six khaṇḍas**, splitting/adding an **Ādi (Brahma) Khaṇḍa** before/around Svarga; this is the version most quoted in modern translations.
- ***Variant (recension divergence):*** the Bengal version lacks ~39 *Dharma-śāstra* chapters found in the Western version, and the two re-order material (e.g. the Puṣkara-tīrtha opening sits in Ādi/Svarga in the printed text). [scholarly]
- **Khaṇḍa contents:**
  - **Sṛṣṭi Khaṇḍa** — metaphysics and cosmogony in Bhīṣma–Pulastya dialogue; the **lotus-creation**; **Puṣkara** (Ajmer) as Brahmā's foremost *tīrtha*; many *vrata*/*dāna* prescriptions.
  - **Bhūmi Khaṇḍa** — geography of the earth plus king-cycles (**Pṛthu, Nahuṣa, Yayāti**) and sage-tales (Cyavana, Śivaśarman, Suvrata).
  - **Svarga Khaṇḍa** — the heavens: **Vaikuṇṭha**, and lokas of Sūrya, Indra, Agni, Yama, and the gandharvas/vidyādharas/apsarases, woven with myths; Indian river/place lore.
  - **Pātāla Khaṇḍa** — the **distinctive Rāma narration** (see below) plus a long Śiva–Pārvatī dialogue on Kṛṣṇa and heavy Śiva glorification.
  - **Uttara Khaṇḍa** — festival mythology, the **Gītā-Māhātmya** (18 chapters), **Bhāgavata-Māhātmya**, **Śiva-Gītā**, Advaita/Yoga/Upaniṣadic teaching, and (in Uttara 236) the **guṇa-classification of the Purāṇas**.

## Pañca-lakṣaṇa, leaning, dating

- **Pañca-lakṣaṇa coverage** — nominally complete (*sarga, pratisarga, vaṃśa, manvantara, vaṃśānucarita*) but heavily overgrown by *māhātmya* (tīrtha/vrata praise) and sectarian narrative; the dynastic king-lists are thinner than in Viṣṇu/Matsya/Bhāgavata.
- **Sectarian leaning** — predominantly **Vaiṣṇava** (avatāra-centric, Tulsī/Rādhā devotion), yet with **large Śaiva** strata (Śiva-Gītā, Jalandhara cycle) and **Śākta** touches — a hallmark of a multi-layered text. [interpolation]
- **Dating** — *traditional:* timeless Vyāsa-revelation. *Academic:* a stratified compilation spanning roughly the **4th–15th c. CE**, with core layers c. **750–1000 CE** and surviving manuscripts revised in the **14th–16th c.** (Vijayanagara-era temple references in late strata). The Uttara-khaṇḍa's *māhātmyas* and the guṇa-list are widely treated as **late additions**. [late text] [scholarly]

## Distinctive stories and episodes

- **Lotus cosmogony (padma-sṛṣṭi)** — Viṣṇu on the cosmic ocean; from his navel rises a **lotus** bearing **Brahmā**, who creates the worlds; the Purāṇa takes its name from this *padma*. (Cosmology/framework — see [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md).)
- **Guṇa-classification of the 18 Purāṇas** — the locus classicus (Uttara 236) sorting the Mahā-Purāṇas into **sāttvic / rājasic / tāmasic**, placing itself among the sāttvic. (Framework — covered in [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).) [late text]
- **Tulsī / Vṛndā & Jalandhara** — Vṛndā, chaste devotee-wife of the invincible asura **Jalandhara** (born of Śiva's wrath); to break his invincibility, **Viṣṇu deceives Vṛndā** in Jalandhara's guise; she curses Viṣṇu (the seed of Rāma–Sītā's separation) and self-immolates, reborn as the **tulsī plant**, eternally dear to Viṣṇu (origin of *Tulsī Vivāha*). (Likely Dvāpara/timeless deva–asura conflict.)
  - *Variant (Padma vs. Śiva/Skanda Purāṇa):* Padma centers Vṛndā's chastity and the tulsī-rebirth; the **Śiva Purāṇa** foregrounds Jalandhara's birth from Śiva's eye-fire and his death by Śiva's cakra; the **Skanda Purāṇa** adds further detail — cross-link [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md). [disputed]
  - *Variant (immolation):* in one telling Vṛndā dies on Jalandhara's pyre and Viṣṇu causes tulsī to grow from her ashes; in another she self-immolates after cursing Viṣṇu.
- **Pātāla-khaṇḍa Rāma narration** — a full **Rāma-carita differing from Vālmīki**, framing Rāma–Sītā explicitly as Viṣṇu–Lakṣmī; includes the **Aśvamedha** of Rāma, the **Lava–Kuśa** episode, and Sītā's earth-return, told devotionally. (Treta Yuga — cross-link the spine [`../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/02-treta-yuga.md`](../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/02-treta-yuga.md) and the deep dive [`../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/README.md`](../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/README.md).)
  - *Variant (Padma vs. Vālmīki):* the Padma version adds bhakti framing, divine self-awareness of the avatāra, and *māhātmya* interludes absent from Vālmīki. [late text]
- **Śakuntalā / Bharata, Yayāti, Nahuṣa, Pṛthu** — lunar/king-cycle retellings in Bhūmi Khaṇḍa; cross-link [`../../04-deep-dives/yayati.md`](../../04-deep-dives/yayati.md), [`../../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md`](../../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md), [`../../04-deep-dives/shakuntala-bharata.md`](../../04-deep-dives/shakuntala-bharata.md). (Treta/early Vaivasvata lineages.)
- **Gītā-Māhātmya** — 18 chapters praising each *adhyāya* of the **Bhagavad-Gītā** with framing parables of devotees liberated by its recitation. (Framework/devotional — cross-link [`../../04-deep-dives/bhagavad-gita.md`](../../04-deep-dives/bhagavad-gita.md).) [late text]
- **Bhāgavata-Māhātmya** — the famed allegory of **Bhakti** as a mother with sons **Jñāna** and **Vairāgya**, revived by recitation of the Bhāgavata; the **Gokarṇa–Dhundhukārī** ghost-liberation tale. (Kali Yuga / devotional frame.) [late text]
  - *Variant (Padma vs. Skanda):* a closely parallel Bhāgavata-Māhātmya circulates attributed to the **Skanda Purāṇa**; the two share the Bhakti-allegory with variant wording. [disputed]
- **Puṣkara-tīrtha-māhātmya** — Brahmā's sacrifice and the founding of **Puṣkara** (Ajmer) as the chief Brahmā shrine; with the Sāvitrī/Gāyatrī episode explaining Brahmā's near-absence of temples. (Framework/tīrtha lore — cross-link [`../../06-sacred-geography/`](../../06-sacred-geography/).)
- **Vrata and tīrtha tales (abundant)** — **Ekādaśī**, **Kārttika-māhātmya**, **Māgha-snāna**, Rādhā/Tulsī worship, *dāna* merits — the Purāṇa's bulk; many are post-canonical ritual charters. [late text]
- **Śiva-Gītā** — a Śaiva counterpart to the Bhagavad-Gītā in the Uttara Khaṇḍa: Śiva instructs Rāma in self-knowledge and grants the *pāśupata* weapon. (Treta frame / theological — cross-link [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md).) [interpolation]
