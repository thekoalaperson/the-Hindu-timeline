## The Division — 18 Mahā + 18 Upa

- **18 Mahāpurāṇas** ("great") + **18 Upapurāṇas** ("secondary"), conventionally **400,000 ślokas** total, all ascribed to **Vyāsa** and recited by the *sūta* **Ugraśravas / Romaharṣaṇa** at the Naimiṣa forest in this Kali Yuga (frame-story). Full enumeration, verse-counts, and the contested Upapurāṇa lists (which "rarely agree" — counting all texts ever called Upa- yields ~100): see [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).
- A Purāṇa is classically defined by the **pañca-lakṣaṇa** (five marks: *sarga, pratisarga, vaṃśa, manvantara, vaṃśānucarita*); the **Bhāgavata** expands this to **daśa-lakṣaṇa** (ten marks). See parent survey.

### The guṇa & sectarian groupings (traditional mnemonic)

Stated by **Śiva to Pārvatī, Pādma Purāṇa Uttara-khaṇḍa 236.18–21** — six Purāṇas per *guṇa*, each tied to a presiding deity and destination. **[Disputed / Vaiṣṇava-leaning polemic]**; boundary cases (Vāmana/Kūrma/Matsya/Skanda) shift between lists.

- **Sāttvic** (→ Viṣṇu; leads to *mokṣa*): **Viṣṇu, Bhāgavata, Nāradīya, Garuḍa, Padma, Varāha**.
- **Rājasic** (→ Brahmā; leads to *svarga*): **Brahma, Brahmāṇḍa, Brahmavaivarta, Mārkaṇḍeya, Bhaviṣya, Vāmana**.
- **Tāmasic** (→ Śiva/Agni; leads to lower realms): **Matsya, Kūrma, Liṅga, Śiva, Skanda, Agni**.
- Loosely, the same axis maps to **sectarian** affiliation: **Vaiṣṇava** (sāttvic core), **Brāhma/Saura/Śākta** (rājasic), **Śaiva** (tāmasic) — though most Mahāpurāṇas are encyclopedic and cross sectarian lines in practice.

### Canonical-list variant — the Śiva / Vāyu seat

- ***Variant — Vāyu vs. Śiva:*** Lists disagree on the 18th seat. The old Śaiva **Vāyu Purāṇa** is counted among the 18 by the **Matsya, Vāyu, Liṅga, Kūrma, Agni, and Bhāgavata** enumerations; lists that include the **Śiva Purāṇa** (e.g. Padma, Viṣṇu) drop Vāyu to hold the count at 18. The 11th-c. scholar **al-Bīrūnī** already recorded two rival lists (one with Śiva, one with Vāyu). If **both** are admitted, the genre has **19**, not 18. The two texts share heavy structural overlap (possibly one text before centuries of revision); many scholars take **Vāyu** as the older, genuine "great" Purāṇa and **Śiva** as a later substitute **[scholarly]**. (Full treatment in [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md), incl. the Bhāgavata vs. Devī-Bhāgavata dispute.)

## Per-Purāṇa story maps in this folder

Grouped by the guṇa/sectarian scheme above (mnemonic only). The **Bhāgavata** has its own dedicated map (skandha-by-skandha) rather than a file here. **Brahmāṇḍa** and **Vāyu** are covered in the parent survey and the spine; no standalone story-map file yet.

**Sāttvic / Vaiṣṇava**
- [`vishnu-purana.md`](vishnu-purana.md) — the "model" Purāṇa; tightest pañca-lakṣaṇa fit; full Sūrya/Candra genealogies + Book 4 Kali prophecy.
- [`bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) — *(in the parent folder)* the 12-skandha map; Kṛṣṇa carita + Kalki prophecy.
- [`narada-purana.md`](narada-purana.md) — Nāradīya; bhakti, vrata, tīrtha, and an index of the other 17 Purāṇas.
- [`garuda-purana.md`](garuda-purana.md) — narrated to Garuḍa; famed Preta-khaṇḍa (death, Yama, afterlife, rebirth).
- [`padma-purana.md`](padma-purana.md) — vast 5-khaṇḍa Vaiṣṇava encyclopedia; lotus-creation, avatāra lore, the guṇa-scheme itself.
- [`varaha-purana.md`](varaha-purana.md) — framed by the Varāha avatāra lifting the Earth; Mathurā tīrthas.

**Rājasic / Brāhma–Saura–Śākta**
- [`brahma-purana.md`](brahma-purana.md) — "Ādi Purāṇa"; Sūrya worship, Odisha/Jagannātha & Konark tīrthas.
- [`brahmavaivarta-purana.md`](brahmavaivarta-purana.md) — Kṛṣṇa–Rādhā theology; Gaṇeśa- and Prakṛti-khaṇḍas.
- [`markandeya-purana.md`](markandeya-purana.md) — contains the **Devī Māhātmya / Durgā Saptaśatī** (Mahiṣāsura, Śumbha–Niśumbha).
- [`bhavishya-purana.md`](bhavishya-purana.md) — "Purāṇa of the future"; prophecy + sun worship; notoriously interpolated **[late text / interpolation]**.
- [`vamana-purana.md`](vamana-purana.md) — framed by the Vāmana (dwarf) avatāra subduing Bali; Śaiva/Śākta material.

**Tāmasic / Śaiva–Āgneya**
- [`shiva-purana.md`](shiva-purana.md) — central Śaiva text; liṅga, Śiva–Pārvatī marriage, Gaṇeśa/Kārttikeya births, Dakṣa, Tripura, Andhaka.
- [`linga-purana.md`](linga-purana.md) — origin of the liṅga; the 28 Śiva yogeśvaras; Lakulīśa lore.
- [`skanda-purana.md`](skanda-purana.md) — the **largest** Purāṇa; khaṇḍas/sthala-māhātmyas; Skanda's birth, Tārakāsura's slaying.
- [`kurma-purana.md`](kurma-purana.md) — narrated by the Kūrma (tortoise) at the Samudra Manthana; contains the **Īśvara Gītā**.
- [`matsya-purana.md`](matsya-purana.md) — one of the oldest; framed by the Matsya avatāra saving Manu; classic pañca-lakṣaṇa & king-lists.
- [`agni-purana.md`](agni-purana.md) — encyclopedic "Veda of all sciences"; narrated by Agni.
