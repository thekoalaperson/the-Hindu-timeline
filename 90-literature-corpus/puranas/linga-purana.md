# Liṅga Purāṇa

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/puranas/` — a per-Purāṇa source file in the literature corpus.
> Up-link: [`../README.md`](../README.md) (the Purāṇas index) · [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) (the literature corpus).
> See also the parent survey [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md), the sibling [`shiva-purana.md`](shiva-purana.md), and the [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).

The **Liṅga Purāṇa** (*Liṅga Mahāpurāṇa*) is one of the eighteen *Mahāpurāṇas* and a core **Śaiva** text whose organizing symbol — announced in its very title — is the **liṅga** as the formless-yet-manifest sign (*liṅga* = "mark, sign") of Śiva, the supreme *para-brahman*. Where the Śiva Purāṇa is a sprawling devotional encyclopedia, the Liṅga Purāṇa is tighter and more **doctrinal/cosmological**: it fuses a **Sāṃkhya-flavored cosmogony** with Śaiva theism, derives the whole cosmos from an unmanifest *liṅga* (the *avyakta*/*pradhāna*), and frames Śiva-worship through the **Pāśupata-yoga** lineage. Its frame-narrative is the standard one — the sage **Sūta (Romaharṣaṇa/Lomaharṣaṇa)** reciting it to the ṛṣis assembled at **Naimiṣāraṇya**. It is distinctive for the doctrine of the **28 yoga-avatāras (Yogeśvaras)** of Śiva culminating in **Lakulīśa**, the **Liṅgodbhava** pillar myth, the ** aṣṭottara-śata (108)** and the **sahasranāma (1,008)** Śiva-names, and a strongly **tattva**-based metaphysics.

- **Structure (two *bhāgas*)** — the extant text is divided into a **Pūrva-bhāga** (~108 adhyāyas) and an **Uttara-bhāga** (~55 adhyāyas), totaling **~163 chapters** and a traditionally cited **~11,000 ślokas**:
  - **Pūrva-bhāga** — the doctrinal/cosmological heart: the unmanifest *liṅga* and the genesis of the universe (Sāṃkhya *pradhāna/avyakta* → *mahat* → cosmos), the **Liṅgodbhava** charter myth, *sṛṣṭi*/*pratisṛṣṭi*, the **28 Yogeśvaras** and Pāśupata-yoga, *kalpa/manvantara/yuga* time-reckoning, geography (the seven *dvīpas*, mountains, rivers), **astronomy** (sun, moon, planets, *nakṣatras* — chs. ~55–61), the great demon-slayings, the descriptions and worship-rules of the **liṅga**, and *tīrtha*-glorification (Vārāṇasī, Kedāra, Prayāga, Kurukṣetra).
  - **Uttara-bhāga** — more devotional/ritual: *vrata*, *dāna*, *snāna*, *pūjā* procedure, the **Śiva-sahasranāma**, the glory of the *praṇava* (Oṃ) and of *bhasma*/*rudrākṣa*, additional māhātmyas and *gītā*-type teaching.
  - *Variant (later insertion):* some manuscripts give the Uttara-bhāga only **~46 chapters**; scholars suspect the whole second part is a **later attachment** to an originally single-part cosmological text. [interpolation] [scholarly]
- **Pañca-lakṣaṇa coverage** — comparatively **strong** for a sectarian Purāṇa: *sarga* (primary creation from the liṅga/*pradhāna*) and *pratisarga* (dissolution) are central and systematic; *manvantara* (the 14 Manus) and *vaṃśa/vaṃśānucarita* (solar/lunar dynasties, ṛṣi-lineages) are present though subordinated to Śaiva doctrine. The genuine center of gravity is **liṅga-theology + Pāśupata-yoga + māhātmya/vidhi**. Cross-link the framework: [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/) and [`../../01-manvantaras/`](../../01-manvantaras/).
- **Sectarian leaning** — emphatically **Śaiva**, asserting Śiva-primacy with the liṅga as the prime symbol, yet **integrative**: Viṣṇu and Brahmā are honored, and the text gives prominence to the **Goddess** — its treatment of **Ardhanārīśvara** declares Devī the **mother of the universe**. Its philosophical idiom is **Sāṃkhya cosmology refitted to Śaiva theism**, with a clear **Pāśupata** stratum (the Lakulīśa/Yogeśvara lineage). [scholarly]
- **Dating** — *traditional:* eternal, taught by Śiva and transmitted via Sūta at Naimiṣa, abridged by **Vyāsa**. *Academic [scholarly]:* commonly placed **c. 5th–10th century CE**, with the core Śaiva-cosmological material older and the Uttara-bhāga/ritual layers later; the text "exists in many versions" and was **revised and expanded** over time (an accreted "library" rather than one composition). [late text]
- **Canon-list note** — the Liṅga Purāṇa's own enumeration of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas **names *Vāyu* (not Śiva)** as a member — one of the lists feeding the contested-18th-seat dispute. Cross-link [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) → "Canonical-list variants" and the sibling [`shiva-purana.md`](shiva-purana.md) §"The major canon variant."

## Notable / distinctive stories and sections

### Liṅgodbhava — the cosmic pillar of light (the charter myth)
- **Liṅgodbhava ("the rising of the liṅga")** — **Brahmā** and **Viṣṇu** quarrel over supremacy; Śiva manifests as an **infinite blazing pillar/column of fire (the jyotirliṅga)** transfixing all three worlds, and challenges the two to find its ends. **Viṣṇu** as the boar **Varāha** dives down, **Brahmā** on his **haṃsa** flies up; neither reaches an end. Viṣṇu admits failure honestly; **Brahmā lies** (citing a falling **ketakī flower** as false witness) and is **cursed to receive no worship**, while Viṣṇu's honesty is rewarded. The liṅga is thereby established as the supreme object of worship. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — a primordial, timeless theophany at the dawn of creation.)* Cross-link the shared treatment and variants: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md), [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md), and [`shiva-purana.md`](shiva-purana.md) §Liṅgodbhava.
  - *Variant (cross-text spread):* the Liṅgodbhava is shared by the **Liṅga, Kūrma, Vāmana, Skanda, Brahmāṇḍa, and Vāyu Purāṇas**; the Liṅga Purāṇa is one of the **principal loci** giving the liṅga its cosmological etiology (and the festival link to **Mahāśivarātri**). (*Śaiva Purāṇic tradition*)
  - *Variant (the unmanifest liṅga):* the Liṅga Purāṇa distinctively reads the pillar as the **manifest sign of the un-manifest** — Śiva as *avyakta-liṅga* (= Sāṃkhya *pradhāna/prakṛti*) from which the *vyakta* (manifest) cosmos unfolds; the etiology is thus **metaphysical**, not merely a quarrel-resolution. (*Liṅga Purāṇa, Pūrva-bhāga*) [scholarly]

### The 28 Yoga-avatāras (Yogeśvaras) and Lakulīśa — the Pāśupata charter
- **The twenty-eight Yogeśvaras** — to teach **Pāśupata-yoga**, Śiva (*Paśupati*) is said to incarnate **once in every Kali-Yuga**; across the **28 Kali-Yugas of the present (Vaivasvata) Manvantara** there are accordingly **28 yoga-avatāras (Yogeśvaras)**, **each with four disciples**, who transmit the yoga of liberation. *(Chronology: each appears in the **Kali-Yuga of its mahāyuga**, within the current **Vaivasvata Manvantara** of the **Śveta-Varāha Kalpa**.)* Cross-link the manvantara/mahāyuga frame: [`../../01-manvantaras/`](../../01-manvantaras/) and the Kali-Yuga file under [`../../01-manvantaras/`](../../01-manvantaras/).
  - The 28 (Liṅga Purāṇa list): **Śveta, Sutāra, Madana, Suhotra, Kāñcana, Lokākṣi, Jaigīṣavya, Dadhivāhana, Ṛṣabha, Muni, Ugra, Atri, Vāli, Gautama, Vedaśīrṣa, Gokarṇa, Guhāvāsī, Śikhaṇḍabhṛt, Jaṭāmālī, Aṭṭahāsa, Dāruka, Lāṅgalī, Mahākāya, Śūlī, Muṇḍīśvara, Sahiṣṇu, Somaśarman**, and the 28th. [disputed]
  - *Variant (the 28th = Lakulīśa):* a strong parallel tradition — shared by the **Vāyu Purāṇa (ch. 23), Kūrma Purāṇa (ch. 53), and Liṅga Purāṇa (ch. 24)** — names the **last/28th avatāra** the wandering ascetic **Lakulīśa / Lakulin / Nakulīśa** (Śiva descended at **Kāyāvarohaṇa/Kārvān**), founder of the historical **Pāśupata** sect, with **four disciples — Kuśika, Garga, Mitra, and Kauruṣya (Kauruṣa)** — who spread the cult. (*Vāyu/Kūrma/Liṅga Purāṇas; cf. the historical Lakulīśa, c. 1st–2nd c. CE*) [scholarly]
  - *Variant (28th name in the bare list):* some printed enumerations close the list with **"Jagadguru"** (or other names) rather than Lakulīśa, since the *Yogeśvara*-roster and the *Lakulin*-prophecy are two overlapping strata; treat the **Lakulīśa identification as the doctrinally weighty one**. [disputed]

### Śiva-tattva cosmology — the liṅga as the unmanifest source
- **Sāṃkhya-Śaiva cosmogony** — creation proceeds from the **unmanifest (*avyakta*/*pradhāna* = the supreme *liṅga*)** through **mahat** and the **tattvas** to the manifest world, with **Śiva-Śakti** as the ultimate ground; dissolution (*pratisarga*) folds the cosmos back into the liṅga. The text supplies *kalpa/manvantara/yuga* reckoning and the **astronomy of sun, moon, planets and *nakṣatras***. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — the metaphysics of the cosmic clock itself.)* Cross-link: [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/).
- **Ardhanārīśvara and the Goddess** — Śiva as half-male/half-female, with **Devī declared the mother of the universe** — a Śaiva framing that folds Śākta theology into the liṅga-cosmology. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — divine theophany, timeless.)* Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devi-mahatmya-and-the-great-goddess.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devi-mahatmya-and-the-great-goddess.md).

### The demon-slayings and divine deeds
- **Andhaka-vadha — the blind demon** — **Andhaka** ("the blind one"), here the (adoptive/natural) son of the asura **Hiraṇyākṣa**, swells into a monstrous form (in one description **a thousand heads/arms, two thousand eyes and legs**) and terrorizes the worlds; lusting after **Pārvatī** (in effect his mother), he is **impaled on Śiva's trident** until his pride is burnt away. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — divine deed, timeless.)* Cross-link the variants (each blood-drop spawning clones, the Saptamātṛkā, Andhaka's redemption as the gaṇa Bhṛṅgi): [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md) §3 and [`shiva-purana.md`](shiva-purana.md) §"Andhaka-vadha."
  - *Variant (redemption):* in the Liṅga/Kūrma/Śiva strand Andhaka is ultimately **converted into a devotee** and made a *gaṇa*-chief rather than annihilated. (*Liṅga Purāṇa; cf. Kūrma & Śiva Purāṇas*)
- **The Dakṣa-yajña and Vīrabhadra** — **Dakṣa's** great sacrifice, to which Śiva is slighted; the sacrifice is wrecked by **Vīrabhadra** (and Bhadrakālī), gods are mutilated — including the striking of **Bhaga's eye** and **Pūṣan's teeth** — and order is restored only when Śiva is honored. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — divine biography, timeless; pre-Pārvatī cycle.)* Cross-link the full event and variants: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/sati-daksha-and-parvati.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/sati-daksha-and-parvati.md).
  - *Variant (memorialized in name-lists):* the deed is fossilized in Śiva-epithets such as ***Bhagaghna*** ("destroyer of Bhaga's eye") and ***Dakṣayajñavināśana*** carried in the Liṅga Purāṇa's name-litanies. (*Liṅga Purāṇa*)
- **Tripura-dahana, Jālandhara, and the burning of Kāma** — the Liṅga Purāṇa also carries the standard Śaiva cosmic deeds: the burning of the three flying cities (**Tripura**), the slaying of **Jālandhara**, and **Kāma-dahana** (Kāma reduced to ash by the third eye). *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — Śaiva cosmic deeds, timeless.)* Cross-link: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md) §3.

### Jyotirliṅgas and sacred geography
- **Jyotirliṅgas and *tīrthas*** — the text describes self-manifest **liṅgas of light** and glorifies major Śaiva pilgrimage sites (**Vārāṇasī/Kāśī, Kedāra, Prayāga, Kurukṣetra**), tying the liṅga-cosmology to lived sacred geography. *(Chronology: framework — sacred geography active across yugas; founding legends set in mythic antiquity.)* Cross-link the dedicated catalog: [`../../06-sacred-geography/12-jyotirlingas.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/12-jyotirlingas.md) and [`../../06-sacred-geography/sapta-puri-and-sacred-cities.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/sapta-puri-and-sacred-cities.md).

### Name-litanies, ritual, and yoga
- **The aṣṭottara-śata (108) and sahasranāma (1,008) Śiva-names** — the Liṅga Purāṇa is a principal source for both the **108-name** *Śiva-aṣṭottara-śata-nāmāvali* and the **1,008-name** *Śiva-sahasranāma*, each a litany compressing Śiva's deeds and attributes (e.g. *Mahādeva, Maheśvara, Śūlī, Paśupati, Mṛtyuñjaya, Andhakāsura-sūdana, Bhagaghna*) into worship-formulae. *(Chronology: framework — living ritual, applicable across yugas.)* Cross-link the deities survey: [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md) and [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/).
  - *Variant (shared litanies):* the same **108** and **1,008** name-sets circulate (with reorderings) across the **Śiva, Liṅga, and Vāyu Purāṇas** and the Mahābhārata's Anuśāsana/Śānti-parvan; lists differ in several seats. (*cross-text*) [scholarly]
- **Liṅga-worship, *bhasma*, *rudrākṣa*, *praṇava*, and Pāśupata-yoga** — extensive *vidhi* (procedure) for installing and worshiping the liṅga, the merit of sacred **ash (vibhūti/bhasma)** and **rudrākṣa**, the **praṇava (Oṃ)** as Śiva's sound-body and the **Pañcākṣara** (*Oṃ Namaḥ Śivāya*), and the **eightfold Pāśupata-yoga** of the Yogeśvaras as the path to liberation. *(Chronology: framework — living ritual + soteriology.)* Cross-link: [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/) and [`../../06-sacred-geography/meru-kailash-and-cosmic-mountains.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/meru-kailash-and-cosmic-mountains.md).
