# Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/puranas/` — a per-Purāṇa source file in the literature corpus.
> Up-link: [`../README.md`](../README.md) (the Purāṇas index) · [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) (the literature corpus).
> See also the parent survey [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md) and the companion [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).

The **Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa** ("Purāṇa of the **Cosmic Egg**," *Brahma-aṇḍa*) is one of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas, traditionally counted **last** in the standard list and named for the **golden egg (hiraṇyagarbha / brahmāṇḍa)** from which the universe unfolds — the text claims to expound the cosmic egg as the measure and frame of all creation. It is closely **twinned with the [Vāyu Purāṇa](../../04-deep-dives/markandeya.md)** (the two share a large common stratum and were anciently a single *Vāyavīya/Brāhmāṇḍa* corpus split in transmission) [scholarly], which makes its cosmological-geographical core one of the **oldest Purāṇic layers**. Its enduring fame, however, rests on two great **embedded works** that travel under its name: the **Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa** (an Advaitin, bhakti-soaked retelling of Rāma's story, ~35% of the printed text) and the **Lalitā-Māhātmya / Lalitopākhyāna** (the supreme **Śrī-Vidyā / Śākta-Tantra** narrative of **Lalitā Tripura-Sundarī** and the slaying of **Bhaṇḍāsura**, ~20%, carrying the **Lalitā-Sahasranāma**). Roughly **30% is geographical *māhātmya*** (pilgrim's guides to Kāñcī, Kāśmīra, the Narmadā, Cuttack/Puruṣottama, etc.). It is also the traditional home of the **Adi-Sāṅkhya / Adi (primal) cosmology** material and **future-manvantara** accounts inherited from the Vāyu stratum.

- **Structure — three *bhāgas* (Parts), subdivided into *pādas*; *adhyāya*-based:**
  - **Pūrva-bhāga** (first part) — two *pādas*, the **Prakriyā-pāda** (~5 ch., the process/method of creation) and the **Anuṣaṅga-pāda** (~33 ch.); **38 chapters** total. This is the **Vāyu-shared cosmological core**: *sarga/pratisarga*, the *aṇḍa*, the *kalpa*, *yuga* and *manvantara* schemes, geography of the *dvīpa*s.
  - **Madhya-bhāga** (middle part) — one *pāda*, the **Upodghāta-pāda** (**74 chapters**): genealogies of gods, ṛṣis and kings, *vaṃśa*/dynastic chronicle, the **future manvantaras**, and *tīrtha*-lore.
  - **Uttara-bhāga** (last part) — one *pāda*, the **Upasaṃhāra-pāda** (**44 chapters**), which includes the concluding sections and to which the **Lalitopākhyāna (40 ch.)** is appended as a quasi-independent *māhātmya*.
  - **Total ~156 chapters.** Traditional verse-count **12,000 ślokas** (per the Matsya/Nāradīya lists); the printed **Veṅkaṭeśvara Press** vulgate runs to **~14,286 verses** [scholarly]. The critical/translated standard is the **Motilal Banarsidass *AITM* edition in 5 volumes (vols. 22–26)**, tr. **G. V. Tagare**.
  - **Embedded *Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa*** — **~4,500 verses, 65 *adhyāyas*, 7 *kāṇḍas*** (Bāla → Uttara), an Advaita-Vedānta + Rāma-bhakti recension; **not original to the old core** [late text][interpolation] (see dating).
- **Pañca-lakṣaṇa coverage — strong in the old core, swamped by appendices:** the Pūrva/Madhya *bhāgas* carry genuine **all five lakṣaṇas** (*sarga, pratisarga, vaṃśa, manvantara, vaṃśānucarita*) at high quality (its Vāyu-derived cosmology is among the most systematic), but the **bulk of the surviving printed text** is **devotional/Tantric (Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa, Lalitopākhyāna) and geographical *māhātmya***, not *pañca-lakṣaṇa*. Contrast the model in [`./vishnu-purana.md`](./vishnu-purana.md) and the survey [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).
- **Sectarian leaning — layered and ecumenical, but with a decisive Śākta crown.** The old core is **broadly smārta / Brahma-centred cosmology**; the **Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa** is **Vaiṣṇava-Advaitin (Rāma-bhakti)**; the **Lalitopākhyāna + Lalitā-Sahasranāma** make the work a **primary scripture of the Śrī-Vidyā / Śākta-Tantra** tradition. Net effect: one of the most **multi-sectarian** Mahāpurāṇas, but in living practice it is cited above all as a **Śākta** and **Rāma-bhakti** text.
- **Dating:** *traditional:* eternal, narrated by **Vāyu / Brahmā**, recorded by Vyāsa. *Academic [scholarly]:* **V. R. Ramachandra Dikshitar** dated the core as early as the **4th c. BCE**; most scholars place the **earliest core ~4th–6th c. CE** (sharing antiquity with the Vāyu), with the **present structure consolidating ~1000 CE** and continual later revision. The **Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa** is **much later — ~15th c. CE**, often associated with the **Rāmānanda** milieu [late text]; the **Lalitopākhyāna** likewise reflects the **medieval Śrī-Vidyā** efflorescence (commonly **~13th–16th c.**) [late text][interpolation].

## Notable / distinctive stories and sections

### The Lalitopākhyāna — Lalitā Tripura-Sundarī and the slaying of Bhaṇḍāsura (the Śākta crown)
- **Lalitā Tripura-Sundarī destroys Bhaṇḍāsura** — the signature narrative: in a dialogue of **Agastya and Hayagrīva** (Viṣṇu's horse-headed form of wisdom), the demon **Bhaṇḍāsura** is born when the **Gaṇa Citrakarma** shapes a man from the **ashes of Kāmadeva** (whom Śiva had burned with his third eye); blessed with strength, Bhaṇḍa and his brothers **Viśukra** and **Viśaṅga** conquer the worlds and drain them of love and vitality. The gods' fire-offering produces, from the **Cidagni-kuṇḍa**, the supreme Goddess **Lalitā Tripura-Sundarī** (Parāśakti / Rājarājeśvarī), who weds **Kāmeśvara (Śiva)**, raises her capital **Śrī-Nagara — whose plan IS the Śrī-Cakra** — and marches with an all-female host to **annihilate Bhaṇḍa and his city Śūnyaka with the Kāmeśvarāstra (Mahā-Kāmeśvara weapon)**. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — a timeless deva–asura / Śākta cosmogonic event; post-Kāma-dahana, prior-age.)* Cross-link [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devi-mahatmya-and-the-great-goddess.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devi-mahatmya-and-the-great-goddess.md), [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dasha-mahavidya.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dasha-mahavidya.md), and the Kāma-dahana in [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md).
  - *Distinctive Śrī-Vidyā detail:* Lalitā's **ministers** are **Mantriṇī (Śyāmalā / Rāja-Mātaṅgī, the lute-bearing minister)** and the **commander Daṇḍanāthā / Daṇḍinī (Vārāhī)**; the child-goddess **Bālā Tripura-Sundarī** (Lalitā's daughter) slays many of Bhaṇḍa's sons; the whole army, the **Śaktis**, and weapons are read as **emanations of the triangles and *āvaraṇa*s of the Śrī-Cakra** — the mythic charter of **Śrī-Vidyā Navāvaraṇa worship** [scholarly].
  - *Distinctive detail — Gaṇeśa's birth as obstacle-remover:* when **Viśukra** plants the **Jaya-vighna / Vighna-yantra** to paralyse the goddess's army with despondency, **Lalitā glances at Kāmeśvara and Mahā-Gaṇapati (Gaṇeśa) is born**, who shatters the obstruction — a **Śākta origin-myth of Gaṇeśa**. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — divine origins.)* Cross-link [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md).
- **The Lalitā-Sahasranāma** — the 1,000-name hymn to Lalitā, **embedded in the Lalitopākhyāna** and recited by the **Vāg-Devatās** to Hayagrīva, is the single most influential **Śākta stotra**; it encodes the **Śrī-Cakra, Kuṇḍalinī, and Pañcadaśī/Ṣoḍaśī mantra** doctrine. *(Chronology: framework — liturgical/timeless.)* Cross-link [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dasha-mahavidya.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dasha-mahavidya.md) and the Tantra survey [`../agamas-tantras-dharmashastras.md`](../agamas-tantras-dharmashastras.md).
  - *Variant (Lalitā-Tripura-Sundarī across Śākta texts):* the same myth and goddess recur in the **Tripurā-Rahasya** and **Vāmakeśvara/Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava Tantra**; the Brahmāṇḍa's **Lalitopākhyāna is the principal Purāṇic witness** that fixed the narrative for the Śrī-Vidyā lineage [scholarly].
  - *Variant (Devī-Māhātmya parallel):* Lalitā's fire-birth-from-collective-divine-tejas and her host of mother-Śaktis mirror **Durgā's manifestation** in the [Devī-Māhātmya](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devi-mahatmya-and-the-great-goddess.md); both are Mahāpurāṇic charters of the **Great Goddess**, but Lalitā is keyed to **Śrī-Cakra esotericism** rather than battlefield narrative.

### The Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa — Rāma as Brahman (the great Vaiṣṇava-Advaita embedding)
- **The "spiritual Rāmāyaṇa"** — ~4,500 verses across **7 kāṇḍas (Bāla → Uttara)**, retelling Rāma's life as a **vehicle of Advaita Vedānta and Rāma-bhakti**: Rāma is consistently the **supreme Brahman/Parabrahman**, Sītā the **Yoga-Māyā / Prakṛti**, and the action is interlaced with philosophical hymns (notably the **Rāma-Hṛdaya** and **Rāma-Gītā** to Lakṣmaṇa). It is the **doctrinal seedbed of Tulsīdās's Rāmcaritmānas**. *(Chronology: the Rāma-story belongs to **Tretā-yuga of the present (28th) mahāyuga, Vaivasvata manvantara**; the text itself is a late composition.)* Cross-link the spine [`../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/02-treta-yuga.md`](../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/02-treta-yuga.md) and the deep-dive Rāmāyaṇa set [`../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/README.md`](../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/README.md).
  - *Distinctive detail — Māyā-Sītā:* the Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa carries the **"shadow Sītā" (Māyā-Sītā)** doctrine — the **real Sītā is hidden in fire and an illusory double is abducted** by Rāvaṇa, recovered at the *agni-parīkṣā* — a theology absolving Sītā of contact with the demon. *(Chronology: Tretā-yuga.)* Cross-link [`../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/6-yuddha-kanda.md`](../../04-deep-dives/ramayana/6-yuddha-kanda.md).
    - *Variant (Kūrma P. / Brahmavaivarta P.):* the Māyā-Sītā / *chāyā*-Sītā motif also appears in those Purāṇas; the Vālmīki *Rāmāyaṇa* has **no shadow-Sītā** [scholarly]. Cross-link [`./kurma-purana.md`](./kurma-purana.md), [`./brahmavaivarta-purana.md`](./brahmavaivarta-purana.md).
  - *Variant (attribution & date):* traditionally ascribed to **Vyāsa within the Brahmāṇḍa**; academically a **~15th-c.** independent work, often linked to **Rāmānanda's** north-Indian Rāma-bhakti, later folded into the Purāṇa [late text].

### Paraśurāma cycle — the axe-bearing avatāra
- **Paraśurāma, the brāhmaṇa-warrior** — the Brahmāṇḍa carries the **Bhārgava/Paraśurāma** material: **Jamadagni and Reṇukā**, the theft and recovery of the cow **Kāmadhenu/Surabhi**, the slaying of the thousand-armed **Kārtavīrya Arjuna (Sahasrārjuna)**, and Paraśurāma's vow to **clear the earth of kṣatriyas twenty-one times**. *(Chronology: spans the **Tretā/Dvāpara junction** of the present mahāyuga; the **sixth avatāra**, the *cirañjīvī* who survives into later ages.)* Cross-link [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md), the immortals roll [`../../04-deep-dives/the-chiranjivis.md`](../../04-deep-dives/the-chiranjivis.md), and the lunar-line context [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md).
  - *Variant (Mahābhārata / Bhāgavata):* the Sahasrārjuna feud and the **21-fold kṣatriya massacre** are also in the *Mahābhārata* (Vana/Śānti parvas) and **Bhāgavata skandha 9**; cross-link [`../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md`](../bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md).

### The Adi (primal) cosmology and Adi-Sāṅkhya — the cosmic egg
- **The brahmāṇḍa and the unfolding of the elements** — the Pūrva-bhāga's **Prakriyā/Anuṣaṅga *pādas*** give the text's namesake teaching: from the **unmanifest (avyakta / Pradhāna)** arise *mahat*, *ahaṃkāra*, the *tanmātra*s and the gross elements, condensing into the **golden cosmic egg (brahmāṇḍa)** within which Brahmā fashions the worlds — a **Sāṅkhya-flavoured ("Adi-Sāṅkhya") cosmogony** shared with the Vāyu Purāṇa. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology — the very start of the kalpa, before Brahmā's day.)* Cross-link [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md), [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md), and [`../../06-sacred-geography/meru-kailash-and-cosmic-mountains.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/meru-kailash-and-cosmic-mountains.md).
- **The four yugas, the kalpa, and *pralaya*** — the old core lays out the **mahāyuga / manvantara / kalpa** arithmetic and the dissolution sequence, one of the base witnesses for the timeline's chronology. *(Chronology: framework/cosmology.)* Cross-link [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md), [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md), and [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md).

### Future manvantaras and the manu-genealogies
- **The coming Manus (Sāvarṇi and after)** — sharing the **Vāyu** stratum, the Madhya-bhāga details the **fourteen Manus**, including the **future manvantaras** (the Sāvarṇi group: Sāvarṇi, Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi, Brahma-Sāvarṇi, Dharma-Sāvarṇi, Rudra-Sāvarṇi, Deva-Sāvarṇi, Indra-Sāvarṇi) with their Indras, ṛṣis and devas. *(Chronology: the **second half of the present (Śveta-Vārāha) kalpa** — manvantaras 8–14, still future.)* Cross-link the future-Manu spine [`../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md`](../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md) through [`../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md`](../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md), and the index [`../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/README.md`](../../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/README.md).
- **Solar and lunar dynasties** — *vaṃśa* chronicle of the **Sūrya- and Candra-vaṃśa** kings, the *vaṃśānucarita* lakṣaṇa. *(Chronology: across the manvantaras of the present kalpa.)* Cross-link [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md).

### Pṛthu and the milking of the earth (shared old-core myth)
- **Pṛthu levels the earth and milks Pṛthivī** — the archetypal first consecrated king **Pṛthu** flattens the ground and, threatening the earth-as-cow, **milks her of all crops and treasures**, giving the earth her name **Pṛthivī**; a core *vaṃśa*-cosmogony episode present in the Vāyu-shared layer. *(Chronology: **Svāyambhuva / very early** in the manvantara sequence of the present kalpa.)* Cross-link the deep-dive [`../../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md`](../../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md).

### Geographical *māhātmyas* — the pilgrim's third of the book
- **Tīrtha and *kṣetra* guides** — roughly a third of the printed text is **sthala/tīrtha-māhātmya**: notably a **Kāñcī-māhātmya** (the goddess **Kāmākṣī** at Kāñcīpuram, a major **Śrī-Vidyā** seat tied to Lalitā), a **Narmadā-māhātmya**, and guides to **Kāśmīra, Puruṣottama-kṣetra (Puri/Cuttack)** and other sites. *(Chronology: framework — sacred geography of the present age; individual sites keyed to legendary events.)* Cross-link [`../../06-sacred-geography/README.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/README.md), the goddess-seats [`../../06-sacred-geography/51-shakti-peethas.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/51-shakti-peethas.md), and [`../../06-sacred-geography/sapta-puri-and-sacred-cities.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/sapta-puri-and-sacred-cities.md).
  - *Distinctive note:* the **Kāñcī-Kāmākṣī** link makes the Brahmāṇḍa a bridge between its **Lalitopākhyāna theology** and a **living South-Indian Śrī-Vidyā pilgrimage cult** [scholarly].
