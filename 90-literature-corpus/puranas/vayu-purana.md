# Vāyu Purāṇa — Story-Map

> Hierarchy location: `90-literature-corpus/puranas/` — a per-Purāṇa source file in the literature corpus.
> Up-link: [`./README.md`](README.md) (the Purāṇas index) · [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) (the literature corpus). Parent survey: [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md); twin text: [`./brahmanda-purana.md`](brahmanda-purana.md).

The **Vāyu Purāṇa** (*Vāyavīya Purāṇa*, "the Purāṇa narrated by **Vāyu**," the wind-god) is among the **oldest of the eighteen Mahāpurāṇas** — by many scholars the single oldest surviving Purāṇic stratum **[scholarly]**. It is **anciently twinned with the [Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa](brahmanda-purana.md)**: the two share a vast common cosmological–geographical–genealogical core and were very likely **one *Vāyavīya/Brāhmāṇḍa* corpus** before centuries of separate transmission split them. Narrated by the wind **Vāyu** (hence its self-designation as a **Śaiva** text, since Vāyu/Prāṇa is read as a form of Śiva), it is the **base witness** for the timeline's chronology of *kalpa*, *manvantara* and *yuga*, and the home of one of the most influential **Gayā-māhātmya** (ancestral-pilgrimage) sections — see [`../../06-sacred-geography/gaya-pitru-tirtha.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/gaya-pitru-tirtha.md). Its **canonical status is disputed**: some standard lists (Padma, Viṣṇu) seat the **Śiva Purāṇa** in its slot instead, dropping Vāyu to hold the count at 18 — see the Śiva/Vāyu variant in [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).

- **Structure — four *pādas* (feet/quarters), *adhyāya*-based:** the text is organised in the classic **Prakriyā → Anuṣaṅga → Upodghāta → Upasaṃhāra** sequence, the same fourfold frame it shares with the Brahmāṇḍa.
  - **Prakriyā-pāda** — the *process/method* of creation (a few opening chapters): the unmanifest, the cosmic egg, the start of the *sarga*. *(Period: cross-yuga — framework cosmogony.)*
  - **Anuṣaṅga-pāda** — the *connected/main* section (the bulk): cosmology, geography of the *dvīpa*s, *manvantaras*, ṛṣis, gods, *yuga-dharma*. *(Period: cross-yuga.)*
  - **Upodghāta-pāda** — the *introduction-to-genealogy* section: royal *vaṃśa* chronicle of Sūrya- and Candra-vaṃśa, *tīrtha*-lore including the Gayā material. *(Period: cross-yuga.)*
  - **Upasaṃhāra-pāda** — the *conclusion*: dissolution (*pralaya*), recapitulation, the fruits of recitation. *(Period: cross-yuga.)*
  - **Verse-count and editions:** traditionally **24,000 ślokas**; surviving printed text (Veṅkaṭeśvara / Ānandāśrama, **112 chapters**) runs ~**11,000–12,000 verses** **[scholarly]**. Standard translation: **Motilal Banarsidass *AITM* (vols. 37–38), tr. G. V. Tagare**; an older partial English version exists in the **Bibliotheca Indica** series.
- **Pañca-lakṣaṇa coverage — exceptionally strong (a model "old" Purāṇa):** the Vāyu carries **all five lakṣaṇas** (*sarga, pratisarga, vaṃśa, manvantara, vaṃśānucarita*) at high quality, with relatively little later devotional overlay compared with medieval Purāṇas — one reason scholars treat it as a benchmark for reconstructing the *Purāṇic* archetype. Contrast the survey [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).
- **Sectarian leaning — Śaiva, but archaic and broadly smārta.** Narrated by Vāyu and praising **Śiva/Maheśvara** (including **Pāśupata**-flavoured passages and the **Lakulīśa/Māheśvara yogeśvaras**), yet its cosmology and genealogy are **pan-Hindu and ecumenical**; it predates the sharp sectarian Purāṇas. *(Period: cross-yuga.)*
- **Dating:** *traditional:* eternal, recited by **Vāyu**, redacted by **Vyāsa**. *Academic [scholarly]:* an early core perhaps **~3rd–5th c. CE** (some, e.g. Hazra, push portions earlier), with the **Padma/eclipse and dynastic notices** giving internal date-markers; continual revision through the **first millennium**. The Gupta-era king-lists make it a key **historical** source (two-clocks: the *scriptural* frame is timeless, but its **vaṃśānucarita** preserves datable **Mauryan, Śuṅga, Andhra/Sātavāhana, and Gupta** dynastic memory).

## Notable / distinctive sections and narratives

### Cosmogony — the cosmic egg and the Adi-Sāṅkhya unfolding
- **The brahmāṇḍa and the elements** — from the **unmanifest (avyakta / Pradhāna)** arise *mahat*, *ahaṃkāra*, the *tanmātra*s and the gross elements, condensing into the **golden cosmic egg** within which Brahmā shapes the worlds; a **Sāṅkhya-flavoured ("Adi-Sāṅkhya") cosmogony** identical to the Brahmāṇḍa's. *(Period: cross-yuga — start of the kalpa.)* Cross-link [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) and [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md).
- **Kalpa, manvantara, yuga arithmetic** — the Vāyu lays out the **fourteen Manus**, the **mahāyuga / manvantara / kalpa** numbers and the **four yugas** with their *dharma* and decline; one of the base witnesses for the timeline's chronology. *(Period: cross-yuga.)* Cross-link [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md), [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md), [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md).

### Yuga-dharma and the decline of the ages
- **The yuga-dharma teaching** — a detailed exposition of how *dharma* contracts from Kṛta/Satya to Kali, the lengths and characteristics of each age, and the **degeneration of varṇa, lifespan and virtue** — a passage shared closely with the Brahmāṇḍa and underlying many later Kali-prophecies. *(Period: cross-yuga.)* Cross-link [`../../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md`](../../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md).

### The Gayā-māhātmya — the supreme ancestral pilgrimage
- **Gayāsura and the Gayā tīrtha** — the Vāyu carries one of the principal Purāṇic **Gayā-māhātmyas**: the pious *asura* **Gayāsura**, so holy that all who saw him were liberated, is **pinned to the earth** (by the gods / Viṣṇu's footprint, with a sacrifice on his body) so that his sanctified ground becomes the supreme **pitṛ-tīrtha**, where a single *piṇḍa*-offering liberates one's forebears. *(Period: cross-yuga — etiology of a sacred site.)* Cross-link the dedicated node [`../../06-sacred-geography/gaya-pitru-tirtha.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/gaya-pitru-tirtha.md).
  - *Variant (Agni / Garuḍa / Brahma Purāṇas):* the Gayāsura legend and Gayā-māhātmya recur across several Purāṇas; the **Vāyu** version is among the **oldest and most cited** for the *śrāddha* liturgy **[scholarly]**.

### Geography — the cosmos and the earth
- **The seven dvīpas and Mount Meru** — systematic **Purāṇic geography**: Jambūdvīpa, the concentric continents and oceans, **Meru** as world-axis, the *varṣa*s of Bhārata, the courses of the sacred rivers and the **motion of the heavenly bodies** (jyotiḥ-cakra). *(Period: cross-yuga.)* Cross-link [`../../06-sacred-geography/meru-kailash-and-cosmic-mountains.md`](../../06-sacred-geography/meru-kailash-and-cosmic-mountains.md).

### Royal genealogies and dynastic chronicle (the historical clock)
- **Solar and lunar dynasties** — the **Sūrya- and Candra-vaṃśa** king-lists (the *vaṃśa* / *vaṃśānucarita* lakṣaṇas), running from the Manus through the heroes of the present age. *(Period: cross-yuga.)* Cross-link [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md).
- **The Kali-yuga dynasties** — uniquely valuable **future-tense king-lists** of **Bārhadratha, Pradyota, Śiśunāga, Nanda, Maurya, Śuṅga, Kāṇva, Andhra (Sātavāhana)** and the **Guptas** — preserving genuine **historical memory** of post-Bhārata-war India. *(Period: kali-historical — recast as prophecy from the frame's standpoint.)* Cross-link [`../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md`](../../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md).
  - *Variant (Matsya / Viṣṇu / Bhāgavata):* these "Kali-dynasty" prophecies are a shared Purāṇic stratum; the **Vāyu, Brahmāṇḍa and Matsya** preserve the **fullest and most archaic** Andhra/Gupta notices, central to the academic dating of the genre **[scholarly]**. Cross-link [`./brahmanda-purana.md`](brahmanda-purana.md) and the Vāyu/Śiva-list variant in [`../smriti-puranas.md`](../smriti-puranas.md).

### Future manvantaras and the Sāvarṇi Manus
- **The coming Manus** — sharing its core with the Brahmāṇḍa, the Vāyu details the **fourteen Manus** including the **future manvantaras** (the Sāvarṇi group) with their Indras, ṛṣis and devas. *(Period: future — manvantaras 8–14 of the present kalpa.)* Cross-link the future-Manu spine [`../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md`](../../01-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md) and the index [`../../01-manvantaras/README.md`](../../01-manvantaras/README.md).

### Music, ṛṣis and Pāśupata-Śaiva lore
- **The Gītā-alaṃkāra / music section** — the Vāyu is notable for an **early treatise on music** (svaras, *grāma*s, *mūrcchanā*s, *tāla*) embedded in its discussion of the arts and of Śiva as **Naṭarāja**-like lord of sound — one of the oldest Purāṇic witnesses to Indian musicology **[scholarly]**. *(Period: cross-yuga.)*
- **The Lakulīśa / Māheśvara yogeśvaras** — passages naming the **incarnations of Śiva as yoga-teachers** (the line culminating in **Lakulīśa**, founder-figure of the **Pāśupata** school), shared with the [Liṅga Purāṇa](linga-purana.md). *(Period: cross-yuga — divine descents across ages.)*

## Sources

- Vāyu Purāṇa (primary text; *Vāyavīya Purāṇa*) — the four *pādas* (Prakriyā, Anuṣaṅga, Upodghāta, Upasaṃhāra); cosmogony, *dvīpa*-geography, manvantara/yuga schemes, Sūrya/Candra and Kali-dynasty genealogies, the Gayā-māhātmya, the music section, and the Lakulīśa/Pāśupata material.
- G. V. Tagare (tr.), *The Vāyu Purāṇa* (Ancient Indian Tradition and Mythology, Motilal Banarsidass) — structure, chapter count, and translated content.
- Purāṇic concordances and surveys **[scholarly]** — for the Vāyu–Brahmāṇḍa twinning, the disputed Vāyu/Śiva canonical seat, the verse-count traditions (24,000 vs. printed ~11,000–12,000), and the academic dating of the Kali-dynasty (Maurya–Gupta) king-lists.
