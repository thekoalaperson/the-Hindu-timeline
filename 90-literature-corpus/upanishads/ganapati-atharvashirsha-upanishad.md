# Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa (Gaṇapati Upaniṣad)

> Part of [`90-literature-corpus/upanishads/`](./README.md) — a per-text deep-dive into the **Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa**, also called the **Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa** or simply the **Gaṇapati Upaniṣad** ("Atharvan head-text of Gaṇapati"): the principal **Gāṇapatya minor Upaniṣad** and the single most-recited Gāṇapatya scripture, in which **Gaṇeśa (Gaṇapati / Vināyaka)** is declared in the second person to be the absolute — *"tvam eva pratyakṣaṃ tat tvam asi, tvaṃ sākṣād ātmāsi nityam,"* and *"tvaṃ brahmā tvaṃ viṣṇus tvaṃ rudraḥ … tvaṃ sākṣād brahmāsi."* The Śaiva/Gāṇapatya counterpart of the Śākta [`./devi-upanishad.md`](./devi-upanishad.md) (Devī Atharvaśīrṣa) and the Śaiva [`./kaivalya-upanishad.md`](./kaivalya-upanishad.md); for the sectarian-Upaniṣad framing see [`./vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md`](./vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md). For the deity himself see [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md); for its parent Veda [`../vedas/atharvaveda.md`](../vedas/atharvaveda.md); for the Gāṇapatya Purāṇas [`../puranas/ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md`](../puranas/ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md); for its Tantric/Āgama ritual world [`../agamas-tantras-dharmashastras.md`](../agamas-tantras-dharmashastras.md). Master index: [`../../README.md`](../../README.md).

## 1. Identity, Placement, Dating

- **Parent Veda & class** — the Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa is affiliated to the **Atharvaveda** and is one of the small group of **"Atharvaśīrṣa / śīras" Upaniṣads** (head-texts) that attach a single deity's self-revelation to the Atharvan corpus — alongside the **Devī, Sūrya, Nārāyaṇa and Rudra (Atharvaśiras) Atharvaśīrṣas**. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa; Atharvaveda affiliation)* — see [`../vedas/atharvaveda.md`](../vedas/atharvaveda.md).
- **The names** — circulated as the **Gaṇapati Upaniṣad**, the **Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa**, or in liturgy simply as the **Atharvaśīrṣa**; *atharvaśīrṣa* marks it as a **recited "crown/head text" (stotra)** of the Atharvan school rather than a discursive treatise. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa)*
- **Sectarian grouping** — counted among the **Śaiva Upaniṣads** of the **Muktikā** list of 108 (the Adyar editors place it in the *Śaiva-Upaniṣads* volume), but it is in substance the **flagship Śruti-class text of the Gāṇapatya sampradāya** — the now-minor sect (one of the *ṣaṇmata* "six paths" systematised under Śaṅkara's name) that worships **Gaṇapati as the supreme Brahman**. *(Source: Muktikā Up.; Adyar Śaiva-Upaniṣads)* — Śaiva by canonical filing, Gāṇapatya by content. [grouping varies by frame]
- **Length** — a **short text of roughly 10 sections / ~14–20 mantras**, largely hymnic and mantric; among Gāṇapatya texts it is by far the **best-known and most-recited**, functioning as the sect's daily proof-text and liturgical core. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa)*
- **Dating (two clocks)** —
  - *Academic:* a **late, sectarian (Gāṇapatya) minor Upaniṣad**, composed well after the *mukhya* corpus — generally placed in the **post-classical / medieval centuries CE** (often the latter half of the 1st millennium into the 2nd), broadly contemporaneous with the rise of organised Gāṇapatya worship and the **Gaṇeśa / Mudgala Purāṇas**; among the latest accretions to the 108-canon. [scholarly] [late text]
  - *Variant (orthodox / traditional):* eternal *apauruṣeya* Śruti "seen" by the ṛṣi (the colophon names **Gaṇaka ṛṣi**) and fixed at the Dvāpara–Kali junction (~3102 BCE; see the Kali-Yuga onset constant), not "authored" in a CE stratum. Both clocks are recorded here; neither is corrected. [scripture vs. academic dating]
- **Why it matters in one line** — it is the **Gāṇapatya "Thou art Brahman" text**: where the Devī Atharvaśīrṣa puts the mahāvākya in the Goddess's mouth and the Kaivalya teaches *"śivo'ham,"* this text addresses **Gaṇeśa directly** as the absolute, supplying the sect's Brahman-identification, *mūla-mantra* and Gāyatrī in one short recitation. [pivotal Gāṇapatya charter]

## 2. The Identification — Gaṇeśa as Brahman ("tvam eva")

- **The opening salutation** — the text opens *"oṃ namaste gaṇapataye"* — **"Oṃ, salutation to thee, Gaṇapati"** — addressing the deity in the **second person** throughout, the distinctive *tvam* ("thou") frame that runs through the whole Upaniṣad. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 1)*
- **The direct identity (the core verse)** — *"tvam eva pratyakṣaṃ tat tvam asi, tvam eva kevalaṃ kartāsi, tvam eva kevalaṃ dhartāsi, tvam eva kevalaṃ hartāsi, tvam eva sarvaṃ khalv idaṃ brahmāsi, tvaṃ sākṣād ātmāsi nityam"* — **"Thou alone art the visible *tat tvam asi*; thou alone art the maker, sustainer and destroyer; thou indeed art all this Brahman; thou art verily the eternal Self."** *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 1)* — the **doctrinal heart**: it folds the Chāndogya mahāvākya **"tat tvam asi"** ("that thou art") into a **direct address to Gaṇeśa**, making him the *pratyakṣa* (perceptible) Brahman.
- **The Trimūrti and all the gods collapsed into Gaṇeśa** — *"tvaṃ brahmā tvaṃ viṣṇus tvaṃ rudras tvam indras tvam agnis tvaṃ vāyus tvaṃ sūryas tvaṃ candramāḥ, tvaṃ brahma bhūr bhuvaḥ svar oṃ"* — **"Thou art Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, Indra, Agni, Vāyu, Sūrya, the Moon; thou art Brahman, the three worlds, and Oṃ."** *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 2)* — the sectarian operation: **the entire pantheon and the praṇava are predicated of Gaṇeśa**, who is thereby the one reality of which all gods are forms. [the unifying Gāṇapatya thesis]
- **Identity with ṛta, satya and the cosmic order** — Gaṇapati is hymned as **ṛtaṃ (cosmic order), satyaṃ (truth)**, the *avyaya* (imperishable), source of the worlds — the metaphysical absolute of the *mukhya* Upaniṣads named as Gaṇeśa. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 2)*

## 3. The Mūla-Mantra — the gaṇa-bīja "gaṃ"

- **The seed-syllable theology** — the Upaniṣad **constructs and enshrines the Gaṇapati *mūla-mantra* (root-mantra)** by phonetic exegesis: take the letter **ga**, add the nasal **ṇa-kāra** (the *anusvāra/bindu*), and *"tāreṇa ṛddham"* — **crowned with the praṇava (Oṃ)** — yielding the *bīja* **"gaṃ" (gaṃkāra)**, declared the deity's sound-body. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 3)* — the famous *"gakāraḥ pūrva-rūpam, akāro madhyama-rūpam, anusvāraś cāntya-rūpam, bindur uttara-rūpam"* dissection of the bīja into its phonemes.
- **"gaṃ" as the whole mantra** — the resulting **eka-akṣara (single-syllable) Gaṇapati mantra "oṃ gaṃ"** (and the expanded *"oṃ gaṃ gaṇapataye namaḥ"*) is the **root invocation of all Gāṇapatya worship**, used in *japa*, *nyāsa* and the opening of every rite as the obstacle-remover's seed. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 3)* — its Śruti-class enshrinement here is the warrant for treating the Tantric *bīja* as Vedic-grade; cf. the parallel *navārṇa* enshrinement in [`./devi-upanishad.md`](./devi-upanishad.md).
- **The ekadanta / Gaṇapati mantra block** — the text continues with the longer **mantra "eka-dantāya vidmahe …"** and an extended salutational litany naming the deity's forms; the **single-tusked (ekadanta)** epithet recurs as a meditative focus. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 4)*

## 4. The Gaṇeśa Gāyatrī

- **The source of the Gaṇeśa Gāyatrī** — this Upaniṣad supplies the standard **Gaṇapati / Gaṇeśa Gāyatrī**: *"eka-dantāya vidmahe, vakra-tuṇḍāya dhīmahi, tan no dantī (/ buddhiḥ) pracodayāt"* — **"May we know the Single-Tusked One, may we meditate on the Curved-Trunk One; may that Tusked One impel us."** *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 4)* — the Gāṇapatya counterpart to the solar Gāyatrī, embedding Gaṇeśa-worship in the standard **vidmahe–dhīmahi–pracodayāt** frame.
  - *Variant (third pāda):* recensions and traditions read the closing as **"tan no dantī pracodayāt,"** **"tan no buddhiḥ pracodayāt,"** or **"tan no danti pracodayāt"** — naming the Tusked One, or *buddhi* (intellect), as the impelling power. [regional liturgical variants]
- **The dhyāna of the form** — the text gives a **dhyāna-śloka describing the icon**: the **single-tusked (ekadanta), four-armed** deity bearing **noose (pāśa) and goad (aṅkuśa)**, the broken tusk and the **rad­ish/lotus**, red-hued, with the mouse (*ākhu*) vehicle — fixing the meditative image worshipped in pūjā. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa 5)* — see the icon material in [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md).

## 5. Liturgical Role — Daily Worship, Abhiṣeka, Aṅgārakī Caturthī

- **The opening invocation of every rite** — because Gaṇeśa is the **vighnahartā (obstacle-remover)** invoked first in all Hindu ceremony, the Atharvaśīrṣa is the **standard Gaṇapati-pūjā recitation** — chanted at the start of worship, *vrata*s and saṃskāras to clear obstacles. [practice-centred]
- **The Gaṇapati abhiṣeka liturgy** — the text is the **core stotra of the Gaṇapati abhiṣeka** (ceremonial bathing of the image): it is recited (often in **multiple āvartana / repetitions — 21, 108, etc.**) while pouring water, milk and other substances over the mūrti, the most characteristic Gāṇapatya temple-and-household rite. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa; Gāṇapatya liturgy)*
- **Aṅgārakī (Aṅgāraka) Caturthī** — recitation is especially prescribed on **Saṅkaṣṭī Caturthī** (the fourth lunar day of the dark fortnight) and, with greatest merit, on **Aṅgārakī Caturthī** — a Saṅkaṣṭī Caturthī falling on a **Tuesday (Aṅgāraka = Mars)** — when mass Atharvaśīrṣa recitation and abhiṣeka are performed for relief from obstacles and Maṅgala-doṣa. [regional / Maharashtrian practice especially]
- **Most-recited Gāṇapatya text** — overall the Atharvaśīrṣa is the **single most common Upaniṣadic text in Gaṇeśa worship**, parallel in function to how the Devī Atharvaśīrṣa serves Goddess-pūjā and the Rudram serves Śiva-pūjā. [practice keystone]

## 6. The Phala-śruti — Fruit of Recitation

- **Merit and protection (the *phala-śruti*)** — the closing section promises that **one who studies / recites this Atharvaśīrṣa attains Brahman, is freed from all obstacles, becomes happy and is untouched by every great sin**; the reciter **"becomes successful in all things"** and gains the deity's grace. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa, closing)* — a standard recitation-merit formula tying the meditative hymn to living pūjā.
- **Times, qualifications, and ritual fruit** — recitation **in the evening destroys the day's sin, in the morning the night's; at both junctions one becomes sinless**; recited **before fire, before the image, or with darbha grass / fasting** it confers eloquence (*mahā-prajñā*), and accompanied by **modaka (sweet) offerings** it grants the desired fruit. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa, phala-śruti)* — the **modaka-offering** clause is the textual root of Gaṇeśa's modaka association in worship.
- **The teaching-restriction** — like other esoteric Atharvaśīrṣas, the colophon warns it **should not be given to the unworthy / undisciplined** (*aśiṣya*), reserving the *vidyā* for the initiated. *(Source: Gaṇapaty-atharvaśīrṣa, colophon)* [initiatory caveat]

## 7. Relation to the Gaṇeśa Material and the Gāṇapatya Purāṇas

- **Śruti charter beneath the Gāṇapatya Purāṇas** — the Atharvaśīrṣa supplies the **Vedānta backbone (Gaṇeśa = Brahman)** that the **Gaṇeśa Purāṇa** and **Mudgala Purāṇa** then expand into full mythology and theology — the *upapurāṇa*s narrating Gaṇeśa's avatāras and supremacy, with the Atharvaśīrṣa as their compressed mantric core. See [`../puranas/ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md`](../puranas/ganesha-and-mudgala-upapuranas.md). [Śruti-to-Purāṇa scaffold]
- **The bīja and the deity-lore** — the *"gaṃ"* mūla-mantra and ekadanta/Gaṇapati Gāyatrī of this text are the **mantric layer** of the broader Gaṇeśa deity-cycle (birth, the elephant head, the broken tusk, the mouse, *prathama-pūjya* status) treated in [`../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md`](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md). [text ↔ deity link]
- **Tantric / Āgama affiliation** — the *bīja*-construction, *nyāsa*, *dhyāna* and abhiṣeka method place the Atharvaśīrṣa squarely on the **Śruti–Tantra bridge**: it is the Vedic-class face of the Gāṇapatya *mantra-śāstra* and **Gaṇapati Āgama** practice catalogued in [`../agamas-tantras-dharmashastras.md`](../agamas-tantras-dharmashastras.md). [scriptural-to-Tantra bridge]
- **Doctrinal place in the sectarian triad** — structurally the same move as the **Devī Atharvaśīrṣa (Śākta)**, the **Śvetāśvatara–Kaivalya (Śaiva)** and the **tāpanīya/Nārāyaṇa group (Vaiṣṇava)** — each claims the non-dual summit for one deity; the Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa claims it for **Gaṇeśa**, completing the picture for the minor Gāṇapatya path. See [`./vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md`](./vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md). [do not merge traditions]

## 8. Why the Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa Matters

- **The Gāṇapatya charter-text in Śruti** — it gives the **Gaṇeśa-as-supreme-Brahman** doctrine a footing in the *Muktikā* canon, the warrant by which Gāṇapatyas read the Upaniṣadic absolute as **Gaṇapati** and treat their *bīja* (gaṃ) and Gāyatrī as **Vedic-grade**.
- **A self-contained liturgical kit** — uniquely it packs **identity-mantra, *mūla-bīja*, Gāyatrī, dhyāna and phala-śruti** into one short recitation, which is why it became the **default text of all Gaṇeśa worship** — from the first-invoked obstacle-remover of any rite to the festival abhiṣeka of Aṅgārakī Caturthī.
- **Two clocks held open** — its **late-CE academic dating** (post-classical, alongside the Gaṇeśa/Mudgala Purāṇas) and the **traditional eternal-Śruti** frame (Gaṇaka ṛṣi) are both recorded here without reconciliation; section-counts (~10) and the Gāyatrī's third pāda vary by recension. [disputed] [scripture vs. academic dating]
