# Garbha Upaniṣad

> Part of [`90-literature-corpus/upanishads/`](./README.md) — a per-text deep-dive into the **Garbha Upaniṣad** (*"the Upaniṣad of the Womb / the Embryo"*), the short prose **sāmānya Upaniṣad of the Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda** that is Hinduism's classic **scriptural treatise on embryology**: the elemental make-up of the body, the month-by-month development of the fetus, the soul's **recollection in the womb** and its forgetting at birth, and the count of bones, joints and humours. Companion overview in [`../principal-upanishads-detail.md`](../principal-upanishads-detail.md); parent Veda in [`../vedas/yajurveda.md`](../vedas/yajurveda.md); for the medical tradition that quotes it see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/ayurveda-and-the-science-of-life.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/ayurveda-and-the-science-of-life.md); for the vital airs it enumerates see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/prana-and-the-vital-airs.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/prana-and-the-vital-airs.md); for the body-sheaths frame see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/pancha-koshas.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/pancha-koshas.md); for the rebirth logic see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/purusharthas-and-karma-samsara-moksha.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/purusharthas-and-karma-samsara-moksha.md). For the cosmic-clock framing of Śruti see [`../shruti-vedas-and-upanishads.md`](../shruti-vedas-and-upanishads.md). Master index: [`../../README.md`](../../README.md).

## 1. Identity, Placement, Dating

- **Parent Veda & class** — the Garbha is a **minor (sāmānya-Vedānta) Upaniṣad attached to the Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda** (Black Yajurveda); it stands outside the ten/twelve *mukhya* set but is exceptionally prominent because of its unique anatomical-embryological content. *(Source: Garbha Up.; Muktikā tradition)* — see [`../vedas/yajurveda.md`](../vedas/yajurveda.md).
- **Muktikā placement** — counted in the **Muktikā list of 108 Upaniṣads** and grouped among the **sāmānya (general Vedānta) Upaniṣads**. *(Source: Muktikā Upaniṣad; sāmānya group)*
- **The name** — *garbha* = "**womb / embryo / fetus**"; the text is literally **"the secret teaching about the embryo,"** an exposition of how the body is composed and how the conceptus grows month by month inside the mother. *(Source: Garbha Up.)*
- **Scale & form** — a very short **prose tract** (commonly counted as a few short sections / verses), structured as a **systematic enumeration** — element-counts, then a calendar of gestation, then the soul's experience in the womb and its loss of memory at birth. *(Source: Garbha Up.)*
- **Traditional ascription** — the tradition attributes the teaching to the sage **Pippalāda** (the same ṛṣi named in the Praśna Upaniṣad), to whom the text is often credited. *(Source: Garbha Up.; tradition)* [disputed authorship]
- **Dating (two clocks)** —
  - *Academic:* a **post-classical minor Upaniṣad**, later than the *mukhya* corpus; its embryology runs closely parallel to the *garbha-avakrānti* (descent-into-the-womb) chapters of the **Caraka- and Suśruta-Saṃhitā**, so it is usually placed in the **classical-to-early-medieval** Āyurvedic milieu. Precise dates are uncertain. [scholarly] [late text]
  - *Variant (orthodox / traditional):* eternal *apauruṣeya* Śruti of the Yajurveda, "seen" by the ṛṣis and not "authored" in a CE stratum — fixed for tradition at the Dvāpara–Kali junction (~3102 BCE; Kali onset). Both clocks are recorded; neither is corrected. [scripture vs. academic dating]
- **Why it matters in one line** — it is the **single most-cited Śruti text on conception, fetal development and the embodiment of the soul**, a touchstone in **medical history (Āyurveda)** and in arguments about Hindu views of the body, rebirth and personhood. [reception-centred]

## 2. The Body as a Composite — Elements, Humours, Counts

- **The five elements (pañca-bhūta)** — the body is built of **earth, water, fire, air and space (ākāśa)**, each contributing its quality — what is hard is earth, what is fluid is water, what is hot is fire, what moves is air, what is hollow/cavity is space. *(Source: Garbha Up. 1)* [elemental anatomy]
- **The fivefold of each element** — in each of the five elements a fivefold function is distinguished; e.g. **in earth: name, form, ... ; in water: tongue/taste, ...** — the text drills the **five-times-five** scheme of how the elements manifest in the senses and faculties. *(Source: Garbha Up. 1)* [enumerative]
- **The three humours / doṣas** — the body is governed by the constitutional types — **the three doṣic balances** (the kapha/pitta/vāta scheme of the medical literature); the Upaniṣad gives the count of **constituents and their proportions**. *(Source: Garbha Up.; cf. Āyurveda)* — see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/ayurveda-and-the-science-of-life.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/ayurveda-and-the-science-of-life.md).
- **The six "tastes/supports" and the seven dhātus** — the text lists the **six rasas (tastes)** that nourish the body and the **bodily constituents (dhātus)**, integrating dietary-physiological doctrine into the embryological frame. *(Source: Garbha Up.)* [shared with Āyurvedic physiology]
- **The anatomical census** — the Upaniṣad gives explicit numbers for the parts of the body: **360 bones**, a count of **joints, sinews/ligaments, hairs, and the measures of blood, marrow, fat and other fluids** (drops/handfuls), plus the **vital airs (prāṇas)**. *(Source: Garbha Up.)* — the bone-count and fluid-measures are its most-quoted "data." [enumerative anatomy] — for the vital airs see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/prana-and-the-vital-airs.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/prana-and-the-vital-airs.md).
  - *Note (manuscript variation):* the **exact counts (bones, joints, fluid-measures) vary between recensions and editions**; the figures are read symbolically as well as literally in later commentary. [disputed][recension detail]

## 3. Conception and the Calendar of Gestation

- **Conception (ṛtu-kāla)** — a child is conceived from the **union of blood (mother's śoṇita) and seed (father's śukra/retas)** at the fertile season; the **dominance of seed or blood, or their equality, determines sex** — predominance of the male contribution yields a son, of the female a daughter, equality yields a **napuṃsaka** (intersex), and disturbance/division yields **twins**. *(Source: Garbha Up. 2–3)* [classical embryology of sex-determination]
- **First night to the kalala** — on the **first night** the conceptus is a **mixed drop (kalala)**; **within seven nights** it becomes a **bubble (budbuda)**; **in a fortnight a lump (piṇḍa)**; **within a month it hardens**. *(Source: Garbha Up. 3)* [stage-sequence]
- **Second month — the head and limbs** — in the **second month** the **head and the regions of the limbs** begin to form (the differentiation of the parts). *(Source: Garbha Up. 3)*
- **Third month — the feet, joints, and divisions** — in the **third month** the **feet/lower limbs, the joints/articulations** and the major divisions of the body emerge. *(Source: Garbha Up. 3)*
- **Fourth month — belly, hips and the cavities** — in the **fourth month** the **abdomen, hips and the bodily cavities** are formed and the body fills out. *(Source: Garbha Up. 3)*
- **Fifth month — the spine/back** — in the **fifth month** the **vertebral column / back** develops. *(Source: Garbha Up. 3)*
- **Sixth month — face, nose, eyes, ears** — in the **sixth month** the **face, nose, eyes and ears** (the apertures and sense-organs of the head) appear. *(Source: Garbha Up. 3)*
- **Seventh month — the soul enters / animation** — in the **seventh month** the fetus is **endowed with the jīva (the individual soul)** and becomes animate; some readings place full ensoulment here. *(Source: Garbha Up. 3)* [ensoulment month]
- **Eighth month — completion** — in the **eighth month** the fetus is **complete in all its parts (sarvāṅga-saṃpūrṇa)**, fully formed and nourished. *(Source: Garbha Up. 4)*
  - *Note (variant readings):* editions differ slightly on which organ is assigned to which month (e.g. whether sense-organs come in the sixth or seventh month, and exactly when the jīva enters); the **broad eight-month sequence is stable**, the per-month details vary. [disputed][recension detail]

## 4. The Soul in the Womb — Recollection and Forgetting

- **The fetus remembers its past lives** — in the womb the embodied soul, fully developed, **recollects its previous births** — its good and evil deeds (*karma*) and the chain of births it has passed through — and grasps that it is bound to **saṃsāra**. *(Source: Garbha Up. 4)* [in-utero recollection] — see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/purusharthas-and-karma-samsara-moksha.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/purusharthas-and-karma-samsara-moksha.md).
- **The vow of liberation** — in anguish at the prospect of yet another round of birth and death, the soul **resolves: "if I am delivered from this womb, I will resort to Sāṃkhya-Yoga / to Nārāyaṇa (Maheśvara), the destroyer of the faults that bring such fruit, and so attain liberation."** *(Source: Garbha Up. 4)* [the womb-vow]
- **The agony of birth** — the fetus is described as **suffering in the womb**, squeezed and afflicted, surrounded by impurity and fire-like heat; it is born only after great distress. *(Source: Garbha Up. 4)* [vivid suffering-in-the-womb topos]
- **The forgetting at birth (vaiṣṇava-vāyu / māyā)** — but at the moment of birth, struck by the **"Vaiṣṇava wind" (vaiṣṇava-vāyu)** — the cosmic illusory force — the newborn **forgets its past lives, its births and its resolve**, and so re-enters worldly life in ignorance; the soul's vow is wiped clean by māyā. *(Source: Garbha Up. 4)* [forgetting-at-birth doctrine] — links to [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/maya-and-avidya.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/maya-and-avidya.md).

## 5. The Knower of the Body — Closing Teaching

- **Why the body matters spiritually** — having anatomized the body, the text turns to **who dwells in it**: the body is the field, and the **knower within (the kṣetrajña / Self)** is to be discerned — the embryology is finally a frame for **self-knowledge**, not mere physiology. *(Source: Garbha Up. 5)* — compare the sheath-analysis in [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/pancha-koshas.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/pancha-koshas.md).
- **The fires of the body** — a closing passage identifies the **internal "fires" (agnis)** that cook food and sustain life, and (in some recensions) closes with the praise of one who knows this teaching. *(Source: Garbha Up. 5)* [digestive-fire / antaragni motif]
- **Phala / colophon** — the text ends as a short Upaniṣadic colophon attributing the teaching to the sage **Pippalāda** and praising the knowledge of the womb-and-body as conducive to release. *(Source: Garbha Up.; colophon)* [varies by recension]

## 6. Reception & Why the Garbha Matters

- **The Śruti charter of Hindu embryology** — it is the **principal Upaniṣadic source for the doctrine that a person grows month-by-month from a blood-and-seed drop into an ensouled, karma-laden being** — endlessly cited in **histories of Indian medicine and embryology** alongside the Caraka/Suśruta *garbha-avakrānti* chapters. [medical-history-defining]
- **Parallel to Āyurveda, not derived in tradition** — the month-by-month scheme, the sex-determination rule, and the dhātu/doṣa counts **mirror the classical medical Saṃhitās**; academically this places the text in the same milieu, while tradition treats the shared content as **eternal revelation later systematized by physicians**. *(Source: cf. Caraka-Saṃhitā, Śārīrasthāna; Suśruta-Saṃhitā)* [shared with Āyurvedic corpus][scholarly] — see [`../../05-concepts-and-dharma/ayurveda-and-the-science-of-life.md`](../../05-concepts-and-dharma/ayurveda-and-the-science-of-life.md).
- **A rebirth-doctrine text** — the **in-utero recollection and the forgetting-at-birth** make it a key witness to the Hindu account of **how karma carries across lives yet is hidden from the newborn** — frequently quoted in discussions of saṃsāra and personhood. [doctrine-defining]
- **Two clocks held open** — academically a **post-classical minor Śruti** woven from the Āyurvedic embryology of the early-medieval period; traditionally **timeless Yajurvedic revelation** taught by Pippalāda, its anatomy and gestation-calendar eternal truth rather than period medicine. Both are kept side by side. [scripture vs. academic dating]

## Sources

- **Garbha Upaniṣad** (primary), Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda — the full short text cited inline: the five-element / fivefold composition of the body and the anatomical census (bones, joints, fluids, prāṇas) (sec. 1); conception from blood and seed and the sex/twin/napuṃsaka rule (sec. 2–3); the month-by-month gestation calendar from *kalala* to the eight-month complete fetus (sec. 3–4); the soul's recollection of past lives, the womb-vow of liberation, the agony of birth, and the forgetting at birth by the *vaiṣṇava-vāyu* (sec. 4); the knower-of-the-body and internal-fires close and the Pippalāda colophon (sec. 5).
