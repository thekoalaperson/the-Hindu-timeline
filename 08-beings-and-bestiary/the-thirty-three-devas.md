# The Thirty-Three Devas — Vasus, Ādityas, Rudras

> Part of [08 · Beings & Bestiary](README.md) → the **canonical roster of the gods themselves** — the Vedic "33" (*trayastriṃśat devāḥ*) that sit at the very top of the [vertical hierarchy](README.md) of beings.
> Cosmic address: dwellers of **Svarga** and the upper [lokas](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md); the working pantheon of the [Vedas](../90-literature-corpus/shruti-vedas-and-upanishads.md) and [vedic-deities-and-suktas.md](../90-literature-corpus/vedic-deities-and-suktas.md). For the Purāṇic re-framing of these gods see [02 · Deities & Cosmic Hierarchy](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/).

The **Thirty-Three Devas** (*trayastriṃśat*, "thirty-three") are the oldest enumerated god-list in the tradition — the standing pantheon already named as a closed set of **33** in the **Ṛgveda** itself (RV 1.34.11; 1.139.11; 8.30.2; 9.92.4) and itemized in the **Brāhmaṇas** and **Upaniṣads**. The classical breakdown, given by **Yājñavalkya** in the **Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad** (3.9.1–9) and in the **Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa**, is **8 Vasus + 11 Rudras + 12 Ādityas + 2** (the last pair being **Indra & Prajāpati**, or in the Mahābhārata/Purāṇic reckoning the **two Aśvins**). This node catalogs all four classes member-by-member, every major **name-list variant** across the Ṛgveda, Brāhmaṇas, Upaniṣads, Mahābhārata and the major Purāṇas, the incarnation-links (notably **Bhīṣma = the Vasu Prabhāsa/Dyaus**), and unpacks the famous **"33 koṭi" → "330 million"** mistranslation. The set is **finite and closed at 33** by definition, but the *names filling each slot* are **open and text-dependent** — flagged throughout.

---

## The frame: "33," "33 koṭi," and the "330 million" gloss

- **Trayastriṃśat devāḥ** ("the thirty-three gods") - The Ṛgveda repeatedly invokes a **fixed body of 33** divinities (e.g. RV 1.34.11 "may the three-and-thirty gods come"; 8.30.2). The number is canonical and pan-Vedic, recurring in Avestan kinship (the *thrayastr̥mśat* of Indo-Iranian shared inheritance). *(Source: Ṛgveda; comparative Indo-Iranian.)*
- **The standard partition** - **8 Vasus + 11 Rudras + 12 Ādityas = 31**, completed by **Indra & Prajāpati** (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 3.9.2) **or** by the **two Aśvins** (Mahābhārata; common Purāṇic count) **= 33**. *(Source: Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. 3.9.1–9; Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa; MBh.)*
  - *Variant (the "+2", BĀU):* Yājñavalkya names the final two as **Indra** (the *thunder*, "all this") and **Prajāpati** (the *sacrifice / the animals*). *(Source: Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. 3.9.2.)*
  - *Variant (the "+2", epic/Purāṇic):* the **Aśvins** (Nāsatya & Dasra), twin horse-headed physicians, fill the last two slots — the count used when the list is recited as the working pantheon. [disputed] *(Source: Mahābhārata; popular Purāṇic gloss.)*
  - *Variant (deeper count):* the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad's dialogue famously **expands and contracts** the gods — Yājñavalkya answers "**3306**," then "**33**," then "**6**," then "**3**," then "**2**" (matter & breath), then "**1½**," then finally "**One**" (Brahman). The 33 is thus an *intermediate* tier, not the floor. *(Source: Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. 3.9.1.)*
- **"33 koṭi" → the "330 million" mistranslation** - Later texts (Atharvaveda-adjacent strata, Purāṇic and devotional usage) speak of **trayastriṃśati koṭi**. **Koṭi** (कोटि) is a homonym: it means both **"crore / ten million"** *and* **"type, class, category, peak, point."** The phrase originally meant **"the 33 categories/eminences of gods"**; read with the numeric sense it produces the popular but secondary **"33 crore = 330 million gods."** [disputed] *(Source: Brihadāraṇyaka Up.; Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa; lexical — Monier-Williams s.v. koṭi.)*
  - *Note:* the "330 million" figure is **not Ṛgvedic** — the Saṃhitā knows only **33**. The inflation belongs to the later popular register; both readings are attested in living tradition. *(Source: comparative/lexical scholarship.)* [late text]
- **Theological reading** - The 33 are not 33 separate persons so much as **33 cosmic functions / powers** (devatā = "shining ones," from √div): the elemental supports (Vasus), the vital/destructive powers (Rudras), and the sovereign light-principles of time (Ādityas), crowned by sovereignty (Indra) and the sacrifice/progenitor (Prajāpati). *(Source: Vedic exegesis; Sāyaṇa; Yāska's Nirukta tri-loka scheme — earth/Vasus, mid-air/Rudras, heaven/Ādityas.)*
  - *Variant (Nirukta tripartition):* **Yāska** sorts all Vedic gods into **three** by location — **terrestrial (pṛthivī-sthāna)**, **atmospheric (antarikṣa-sthāna)**, **celestial (dyu-sthāna)** — mapping loosely onto **Vasus → Rudras → Ādityas**. *(Source: Yāska, Nirukta 7.5.)*

## I. The Eight Vasus (Aṣṭa-Vasu) — gods of the elements & lit world

The **Vasus** ("the good/bright ones," "dwellers"; *vasu* = wealth, the abodes of all that exists) are the **eight elemental deities** — the material substrate of the cosmos: earth, water, fire, wind, the lit sky, sun, moon, and the fixed star. Parentage varies: **sons of Dharma (or Manu) by Vasu, a daughter of Dakṣa** (Mahābhārata); **children of Kaśyapa & Aditi** (Rāmāyaṇa). They are attendants of **Indra** and later of **Viṣṇu**. *(Source: MBh Ādi Parva; Rāmāyaṇa; Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*

The canonical Mahābhārata/Purāṇic ogdoad:

- **Dhara / Dharā** (धर) - **Earth**; the supporting/bearing principle. The steadiest of the Vasus. *(Source: MBh; Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*
- **Anala** (अनल), also **Agni / Pāvaka** - **Fire**; identified with [Agni](../90-literature-corpus/vedic-deities-and-suktas.md); father (in one cycle) of Kārttikeya's nurse-fires. *(Source: MBh; Bhāgavata.)*
- **Anila** (अनिल), i.e. **Vāyu** - **Wind / air**; the breath of the cosmos, the moving principle. *(Source: MBh; Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*
- **Āpa / Āpas / Aha** (आप / अह) - **Water** (Āpa) in most lists; the **Mahābhārata's oldest reading gives "Aha" / "Ahar"** ("day") here, with **Āpa (water)** substituted in later texts. [disputed] *(Source: MBh — "Aha"; Viṣṇu/Bhāgavata Purāṇa — "Āpa.")*
- **Dhruva** (ध्रुव) - **The Pole Star / the fixed, motionless one**; immovability, the cosmic axis. *(Source: MBh; Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*
- **Soma** (सोम) - **The Moon** (and the soma-draught); waxing/waning, the cup of the gods. Distinct from the planet-graha [Candra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md). *(Source: MBh; Bhāgavata.)*
- **Prabhāsa** (प्रभास) - **Light / the dawn-sky / splendour**; **the Vasu who incarnated as [Bhīṣma](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md)** (see below). Some lists equate him with the 27 lunar mansions. *(Source: MBh Ādi Parva; Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*
- **Pratyūṣa / Pratyūṣa** (प्रत्यूष) - **The Sun / break of dawn / first light**; father of the sage Devala in some accounts. *(Source: MBh; Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*

### Vasu name-list variants by text

- *Variant (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 3.9.3):* a more abstract ogdoad — **Agni (fire), Pṛthivī (earth), Vāyu (air), Antarikṣa (mid-space), Āditya (sun), Dyaus (sky), Candramas (moon), Nakṣatrāṇi (the stars)** — "for in them all this *vasu* (wealth/world) is placed, therefore they are Vasus." *(Source: Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. 3.9.3.)*
- *Variant (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva — Gaṅgā episode):* **Dhara, Dhruva, Soma, Aha (Ahar), Anila, Anala (Pāvaka/Agni), Pratyūṣa, Prabhāsa** — the list whose theft of Vasiṣṭha's cow drives the Bhīṣma story. *(Source: MBh 1.99.)*
- *Variant (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15):* **Āpa, Dhruva, Soma, Dharā (Dhava), Anila, Anala, Pratyūṣa, Prabhāsa**, with their offspring (Viśvakarman is born of Prabhāsa). *(Source: Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15.)*
- *Variant (alternate/folk enumeration):* **Bhūmi, Samudra, Agni, Vāyu, Aṃśuman, Ākāśa, Varcas, Prabhāsa** — a less common naturalistic set. [folk] *(Source: cited in secondary compilations.)*

### The Vasus' curse & Bhīṣma — the great incarnation-link

- **The theft of Nandinī / Kāmadhenu** - The eight Vasus, led by **Prabhāsa** at the urging of his wife, **stole the wish-cow Nandinī (daughter of Kāmadhenu)** from the sage **Vasiṣṭha**. Cursed to be **born among mortals**, seven for a short term, but **Prabhāsa (the actual thief) for a full human lifespan**. *(Source: MBh Ādi Parva, Sambhava Parva 1.99.)*
- **Gaṅgā as their mother** - The river-goddess **[Gaṅgā](../06-sacred-geography/)** agreed to bear them by King **Śāntanu** and **drowned the first seven new-borns at birth** — releasing them from the curse. *(Source: MBh Ādi Parva.)*
- **The eighth = Bhīṣma** - Śāntanu stayed her hand at the **eighth**; **Prabhāsa lived on as Devavrata, renowned as [Bhīṣma](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md)** ("the terrible-vowed"), grand-uncle of the Kauravas/Pāṇḍavas and central figure of the [Mahābhārata](../90-literature-corpus/smriti-itihasa-epics.md). His celibacy-vow and boon of *icchā-mṛtyu* (death at will) trace to this Vasu-origin. *(Source: MBh Ādi Parva.)*
  - *Variant (which Vasu?):* most texts name the incarnate Vasu **Prabhāsa**; some retellings name **Dyaus** (see BĀU's "Dyaus" Vasu) as the sky-Vasu reborn as Bhīṣma, the two being identified. [disputed] *(Source: MBh vs. popular retelling.)*

## II. The Twelve Ādityas (Dvādaśa-Āditya) — the solar sovereigns & the twelve months

The **Ādityas** ("sons of **[Aditi](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/)**," the boundless mother) are the **sovereign light-gods** — guardians of *ṛta* (cosmic order) and moral law, headed in the Ṛgveda by **Varuṇa** and **Mitra**. Their number **grew historically**: the **Ṛgveda counts 6–8** (RV 2.27.1 names 7: Mitra, Aryaman, Bhaga, Varuṇa, Dakṣa, Aṃśa + Sūrya/Mārtāṇḍa as the rejected 8th); the **Brāhmaṇas fix the count at 12**, mapping them onto the **12 solar months / 12 forms of the sun**. *(Source: Ṛgveda 2.27; Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa; Taittirīya Br.)*

The common Purāṇic dodecad (representative):

- **Mitra** (मित्र) - God of **friendship, contracts, the daylit sun**; paired inseparably with Varuṇa as upholders of oaths. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas.)*
- **Varuṇa** (वरुण) - Lord of **cosmic order (ṛta), waters, the night-sky, oaths and the noose**; chief Āditya of the Ṛgveda, later demoted to ocean-/water-god. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas.)* — see [Vedic deities](../90-literature-corpus/vedic-deities-and-suktas.md).
- **Aryaman** (अर्यमन्) - God of **patronage, hospitality, marriage-customs and the **Milky Way**; guardian of Ārya social bonds. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas.)*
- **Bhaga** (भग) - God of **wealth, fortune, the share/portion** doled out; gives his name to *bhāgya* (luck). Blinded by Vīrabhadra at Dakṣa's sacrifice. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas; [Dakṣa-yajña](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/sati-daksha-and-parvati.md).)*
- **Vivasvān / Vivasvat** (विवस्वत्), i.e. **[Sūrya](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md)** - The **Sun proper**; father of **Manu Vaivasvata** (forefather of mankind), **Yama, Yamī, the Aśvins, Śani**. The chief and "father" Āditya. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas.)* — see [Sūryavaṃśa](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md).
- **Pūṣan** (पूषन्) - God of **roads, herds, nourishment, safe journeys and the meeting of the dead**; toothless (teeth knocked out at Dakṣa's sacrifice), eats only gruel. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas.)*
- **Savitṛ** (सवितृ) - The **impeller / vivifying sun** of the **[Gāyatrī mantra](../90-literature-corpus/vedic-deities-and-suktas.md)** (RV 3.62.10); the sun's stimulating power, distinguished from visible Sūrya. *(Source: RV 3.62; Purāṇas.)*
- **Tvaṣṭṛ** (त्वष्टृ) - The **divine artificer / shaper**; forges Indra's *vajra*, fashions forms in the womb; father of Viśvarūpa and Vṛtra. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas.)*
- **Aṃśa / Aṃśu / Aṃśuman** (अंश) - God of the **allotted share / the apportioned portion**; closely doubled with Bhaga. *(Source: RV; Purāṇas.)*
- **Dhātṛ / Dhātā** (धातृ) - The **establisher / creator-disposer**; ordainer of health, marriage and fate; sometimes equated with Prajāpati/Brahmā. *(Source: Brāhmaṇas; Purāṇas.)*
- **Indra / Śakra** (इन्द्र) - As an Āditya, the **sovereign of the gods, wielder of the vajra, slayer of Vṛtra**; appears in most Purāṇic dodecads (the lists differ on whether Indra or a duplicate solar name fills this slot). *(Source: Viṣṇu/Liṅga Purāṇa.)* — see [02 · Deities](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).
- **Viṣṇu** (विष्णु) - The **youngest and greatest Āditya** (RV "Viṣṇu the wide-strider"); listed as the climactic 12th, later identified with **[Vāmana](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md)**, the dwarf-avatāra, "head of all the Ādityas." *(Source: RV; Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Bhāgavata.)*

### Āditya name-list variants by text

- *Variant (Ṛgveda 2.27.1 — the original 7/8):* **Mitra, Aryaman, Bhaga, Varuṇa, Dakṣa, Aṃśa** (six), plus **Sūrya/Mārtāṇḍa** as the **rejected/cast-off eighth** (the "dead egg" Aditi flings away and recovers). *(Source: RV 2.27; RV 10.72.8–9.)*
- *Variant (Mahābhārata):* **Dhātṛ, Mitra, Aryaman, Śakra (Indra), Varuṇa, Aṃśa, Bhaga, Vivasvat, Pūṣan, Savitṛ, Tvaṣṭṛ, Viṣṇu** — Viṣṇu last and greatest. *(Source: MBh Ādi Parva.)*
- *Variant (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.6 / 8.13):* **Dhātṛ, Aryaman, Mitra, Varuṇa, Indra, Vivasvat, Tvaṣṭṛ, Viṣṇu, Aṃśuman (Aṃśa), Bhaga, Pūṣan, Parjanya** — note **Parjanya** (rain-god) and **Aṃśuman** appear, replacing Savitṛ/Dhūti. *(Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa.)*
- *Variant (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15):* **Viṣṇu (head), Aryaman, Śakra (Indra), Tvaṣṭṛ, Varuṇa, Dhūti, Bhaga, Savitṛ, Vivasvat, Aṃśa, Mitra, Pūṣan.** *(Source: Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15.)*
- *Variant (Liṅga Purāṇa):* **Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Indra (head), Tvaṣṭṛ, Varuṇa, Dhātā, Bhaga, Savitṛ, Vivasvat, Aṃśuman, Mitra, Pūṣan.** *(Source: Liṅga Purāṇa.)*
- *Note (open slot):* across texts the unstable members are **Dakṣa / Dhātṛ / Dhūti / Brahmā / Parjanya / Savitṛ / Aṃśa**; the **stable core** is **Mitra, Varuṇa, Aryaman, Bhaga, Vivasvat (Sūrya), Pūṣan**. The roster of *names* is **open**; the **count is fixed at 12**. [disputed]

## III. The Eleven Rudras (Ekādaśa-Rudra) — the howling/storm & vital powers

The **Rudras** ("the howlers / ruddy ones," from √rud "to cry/roar," or *rudra* "fierce") are the **eleven fierce deities**, the storm-band of the Ṛgveda (the **[Maruts](../90-literature-corpus/vedic-deities-and-suktas.md)** are "sons of Rudra"), later wholly absorbed into the persona of **[Śiva-Rudra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md)** as his **eleven forms (Ekādaśa-Rudra)**. A common physiological reading equates them with the **ten prāṇas (vital airs) + the ātman/manas** that depart at death. *(Source: Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. 3.9.4; Śiva/Purāṇic tradition.)*

- *Reading (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. 3.9.4):* "The **ten vital breaths (prāṇas)** and the **ātman as the eleventh** — when they depart from this mortal body they make the kinsfolk weep (*rud*); therefore they are Rudras." *(Source: BĀU 3.9.4.)*

Because **no two scriptures agree** on the names, the lists are given variant-by-variant rather than as a single roster:

- *Variant (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva):* **Mṛgavyādha, Sarpa (Śarva), Nirṛti, Ajaikapāda, Ahirbudhnya, Pinākin, Dahana, Īśvara, Kapālin, Sthāṇu, Bharga** (often given via the alt-list Mṛgavyādha/Sarpa/Niṛti/Ajaikapāt/Abhivardhana/Pinākī/Dahana/Īśvara/Kapālī/Sthāṇu/Bharga). *(Source: MBh Ādi Parva.)*
- *Variant (Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa):* **Aja, Ekapāda (Ekapāt), Ahirbudhnya, Hara, Śambhu, Tryambaka, Aparājita, Īśāna, Tribhuvana, Tvaṣṭṛ (Twaṣṭā), Rudra.** *(Source: Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa.)*
- *Variant (Viṣṇu Purāṇa):* **Manyu, Manu, Mahmasa (Mahinasa), Mahān, Śiva, Ṛtudhvaja, Ugraretas, Bhava, Kāma, Vāmadeva, Dhṛtavrata.** *(Source: Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*
- *Variant (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12):* **Manyu, Manu, Mahinasa, Mahān, Śiva, Ṛtadhvaja, Ugraretas, Bhava, Kāla, Vāmadeva, Dhṛtavrata** — born from Brahmā's brow when he wept, hence "Rudra." *(Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12.)*
- *Variant (Padma Purāṇa):* **Ṛtadhvaja, Manu, Manyu, Ugraretas, Mahān, Śiva, Bhava, Kāla, Mahinasa, Vāmadeva, Dhṛtavrata.** *(Source: Padma Purāṇa.)*
- *Variant (Śaiva Āgamic / "11 forms of Śiva"):* the **Ekādaśa-Rudra** worshipped in *Ekādaśa-Rudrābhiṣeka* — commonly **Kapālī, Piṅgala, Bhīma, Virūpākṣa, Vilohita, Śāstā (Śāstṛ), Ajapāda (Ajaikapāda), Ahirbudhnya, Śambhu, Caṇḍa, Bhava** (lists differ by tradition/maṭha). [late text] *(Source: Śaiva Āgama; Ekādaśa-Rudra liturgy.)*
- *Note (open slot):* the **count is fixed at 11**; the **names are wholly text-specific**, no canonical master-list exists. Recurring members include **Aja/Ajaikapāda, Ahirbudhnya, Bhava, Śiva, Īśāna, Sthāṇu, Hara, Tryambaka**. [disputed]
- *Cross-class note:* **Nirṛti** and **Ahirbudhnya** ("serpent-of-the-deep") appear among Rudras while elsewhere being a goddess of decay and a [nāga](nagas-and-serpents.md)-serpent respectively — the Rudra-band absorbs liminal/chthonic powers. *(Source: comparative Vedic.)*

## IV. The completing pair (slots 32–33)

- **Indra** (इन्द्र) - **King of the devas**, wielder of the *vajra*, Vṛtra-slayer, lord of Svarga and rain; named by Yājñavalkya as the 32nd. (Also counted among the Ādityas — the lists overlap.) *(Source: BĀU 3.9.2; RV.)* — see [02 · Deities](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).
- **Prajāpati** (प्रजापति) - "Lord of creatures," the **progenitor / sacrificial creator** (later **[Brahmā](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md)**); the 33rd in Yājñavalkya's count. *(Source: BĀU 3.9.2; Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa.)*
- *Variant (the Aśvins):* in the Mahābhārata/Purāṇic recitation the last two are the **Aśvins** —
  - **Nāsatya** & **Dasra** (the **Aśvinī-kumāras**) - **Twin horse-headed physicians of the gods**, sons of Vivasvat/Sūrya and Saṃjñā (in mare-form); harbingers of dawn, healers, rescuers; fathers of **Nakula & Sahadeva**. *(Source: RV; MBh.)*
  - Their inclusion (over Indra/Prajāpati) is the **popular count** that yields the familiar "8+11+12+2 Aśvins = 33." [disputed] *(Source: MBh; Purāṇic gloss.)*

## Synopsis table — the 33 at a glance

- **8 Vasus** — elemental supports (earth, water, fire, wind, sky-light, sun, moon, pole-star). *Terrestrial/material plane.*
- **11 Rudras** — fierce/vital powers, the 10 prāṇas + ātman; the eleven forms of [Śiva-Rudra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md). *Atmospheric/vital plane.*
- **12 Ādityas** — sovereign light-gods / 12 solar months, guardians of *ṛta*. *Celestial plane.*
- **+2** — **Indra & Prajāpati** (Upaniṣadic) *or* the **two Aśvins** (epic/Purāṇic). *Sovereignty + sacrifice / healing twins.*
- **= 33** *trayastriṃśat devāḥ* — the closed Vedic pantheon; later glossed (via *koṭi*) as the "330 million." [disputed]

> **Open-endedness flag:** The **number 33 is fixed and canonical.** The **names filling the Vasu, Āditya, and (especially) Rudra slots are open and text-dependent** — every major Purāṇa supplies its own list. This node catalogs the principal attested rosters; it does not claim a single "correct" set, per repo variant policy.
