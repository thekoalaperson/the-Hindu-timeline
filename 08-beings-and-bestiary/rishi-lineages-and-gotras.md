# Ṛṣi Lineages & the Gotra System

> Part of [08 · Beings & Bestiary](README.md) → the **human-divine seers** who sit just above the human dynasties: the great ṛṣi families and the **gotra/pravara** clan-system that channels their descent. Where [the-thirty-three-devas.md](the-thirty-three-devas.md) rosters the gods and [../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md) the kṣatriya kings, this node rosters the **brahmaṛṣi pole** — the patriarchs from whom every brahmin gotra is reckoned. Up-link: [README.md](README.md).
> See also the seership-office node [../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md) (the rotating *manvantara* committee of seven), and the Saptarṣi sky-asterism (Ursa Major) treated there.

The **ṛṣis** ("seers," from √dṛś / √ṛṣ — "those who see/utter") are the human termini of the *śruti* chain: what Brahmā emits and the gods sing, the ṛṣis "see" and hand on. A finite set of **patriarch-seers** founds the entire brahmin descent system through **gotra** (the exogamous clan, named for one apical ṛṣi) and **pravara** (the recited 1/3/5-ṛṣi pedigree that fixes the sub-line). The root-set is canonically **7 or 8** Brahmaṛṣis, fanning into **18 ṛṣi-gaṇas** (clan-groups) and from there into "many tens" of gotras. This node catalogs the **rishi-classes**, the **root gotras and their variant counts**, and the great families — **Bhārgavas, Āṅgirasas, Atris, Kaśyapas, Vāsiṣṭhas, Viśvāmitra/Kauśikas, and Agastya** — member-by-member with source-flagged variants. The gotra roster as a whole is **open-ended** (regional and sampradāya lists differ) — flagged throughout.

---

## 1. The Classes of Ṛṣi — the rank-ladder of seers

The tradition grades seers into ranks by attainment and origin (not a single canonical list; the common five-rank scheme below is the most cited):

- **Brahmaṛṣi** (ब्रह्मर्षि) - The **highest** grade: a seer who has fully realized **Brahman**, mastered the Vedas, and won mastery of the creative/Prajāpati function. The **Saptarṣi** are the archetypal Brahmaṛṣis; attaining "Brahmaṛṣi" is precisely the threshold the **Viśvāmitra** arc dramatizes (§7). *(Source: Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa; Mahābhārata.)*
- **Maharṣi** (महर्षि) - "Great seer" — a sage of vast tapas and authorship below full Brahmaṛṣi realization; e.g. **Vyāsa, Bhṛgu, Atri, Kaśyapa, Vālmīki, Bharadvāja**. The rank most ṛṣi-authors of the Saṃhitās occupy. *(Source: Purāṇic/epic usage; Indica Today, *Gotras and Pravaras*.)*
- **Rājarṣi** (राजर्षि) - "Royal seer" — a **kṣatriya king** who attained ṛṣi-hood through tapas without renouncing rule (or before it): e.g. **Janaka of Mithilā, Viśvāmitra in his pre-Brahmaṛṣi phase, Ṛtuparṇa, Pṛthu, Kakṣīvān**. *(Source: epic/Purāṇic; Rāmāyaṇa.)*
- **Devarṣi** (देवर्षि) - "Divine seer" — a sage of **deva-station**, freely moving the three worlds: pre-eminently **Nārada**, also **Atri, Kaśyapa, Bhṛgu** in some lists, and the divine messengers. *(Source: Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata Purāṇa.)*
- **(lowest) Ṛṣi / Śrutarṣi / Kāṇḍarṣi** - The base grade "Ṛṣi"; some texts insert **Śrutarṣi** (master of *śruti*, e.g. **Suśruta**) and **Kāṇḍarṣi** (master of a Veda-*kāṇḍa*, ritual-section seers) as intermediate ranks. The full ladder is **not fixed** — lists give five, six, or seven grades. [disputed] *(Source: Indica Today, *Gotras and Pravaras*; Amarakośa-tradition lexica.)*
- *Variant (the seven-grade scheme):* an expanded order runs **Brahmaṛṣi > Maharṣi > Paramaṛṣi > Devarṣi > Śrutarṣi > Rājarṣi > Kāṇḍarṣi**, adding **Paramaṛṣi** ("supreme seer") above Devarṣi. [scholarly] *(Source: traditional lexicographic enumerations.)*

## 2. The Saptarṣi — the apical seven

The **Saptarṣi** ("seven seers") are the brahmaṛṣi-pole re-constituted every *manvantara* (full per-era rosters and the variant lists — Vedāntic, Purāṇic, Jaiminīya, etc. — are at [../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md)). For the **gotra system** what matters is which seven (or eight) seed the clans — and this list differs from the astronomical Saptarṣi:

- **Purāṇic / astronomical seven (Ursa Major)** - **Marīci, Atri, Aṅgiras, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu, Vasiṣṭha** — the mind-born Prajāpatis mapped onto the Dipper-stars. *(Source: Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7; Bṛhat Saṃhitā.)*
- **Bṛhadāraṇyaka / gotra-founder seven** - **Atri, Bharadvāja, Gautama, Jamadagni, Kaśyapa, Vasiṣṭha, Viśvāmitra** — historical Vedic clan-founders; this is essentially the **current (Vaivasvata) manvantara** roster and the basis of brahmin gotras. *(Source: Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. 2.2.4.)*
- *Variant (+ eighth, Agastya):* an **eighth gotra** is added for **Agastya**, the seer tied to the southward spread of Vedic culture — yielding the **"eight" root gotras**. *(Source: Britannica, *gotra*; Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. tradition.)*

## 3. The Gotra System — definitions

- **Gotra** (गोत्र, lit. "cow-pen / enclosure" → "clan") - The **exogamous patrilineal clan**, each named for one **apical (gotra-pravartaka) ṛṣi** from whom all members claim unbroken male descent. Pāṇini's classic rule **"apatyaṃ pautra-prabhṛti gotram"** (Aṣṭādhyāyī 4.1.162) defines gotra as descent **from a grandson onward** of the founder. *(Source: Pāṇini, Aṣṭādhyāyī; Bṛhadāraṇyaka Up. — "the offspring (apatya) of these seven are gotras.")*
- **Pravara** (प्रवर, "the invocation / the chosen ones") - The **recited ancestral formula** of the most-exalted seers of one's direct line, chanted in ritual (saṃkalpa, marriage, śrāddha) to invoke the forebears. It distinguishes the **sub-line within a gotra**. *(Source: Indica Today; Āpastamba Śrauta Sūtra.)*
  - *Variant (pravara-count names):* a pravara names **one, two, three, five, or seven** ṛṣis — **Ekārṣeya** (1), **Dvyārṣeya** (2), **Tryārṣeya** (3, the common case), **Pañcārṣeya** (5), **Saptārṣeya** (7). *(Source: Indica Today, *Gotras and Pravaras*.)*
- **Exogamy rule** - **Sa-gotra** (same-gotra) marriage is barred — members count as siblings; **sa-pravara** (sharing any pravara-ṛṣi, even across different gotras) is **especially** barred; **sapiṇḍa** kinship within 7 generations is likewise prohibited. *(Source: Wikipedia, *Gotra*; Dharmaśāstra.)*
- **Note (non-brahmin & adopted gotras)** - Those without a ṛṣi-line (and many non-brahmins, temple-servants, and converts) are conventionally assigned the **Kāśyapa gotra** ("all are children of Kaśyapa"), since Kaśyapa is the universal progenitor. [folk] *(Source: Wikipedia, *Gotra*; popular custom.)*

## 4. The 8 Root Gotras → 18 Gaṇas

The gotra system telescopes: **8 root Brahmaṛṣis → 18 ṛṣi-gaṇas (clan-groups) → "many tens" of gotras.** The eight roots and their gaṇa-counts (Baudhāyana/Āpastamba pravara-tradition):

- **Bhṛgu** (Bhārgavas) — heads **5 gaṇas**: *Jamadagni, Vītahavya, Gṛtsamada, Vadhryaśva, Vainya.* *(Source: Indica Today; Indiafacts.)*
- **Aṅgiras** (Āṅgirasas) — heads **8 gaṇas**: *Gautama, Bharadvāja, Kapi, Kaṇva, Mudgala, Virūpa, Hārita, Viṣṇuvṛddha.* *(Source: Indica Today; Indiafacts.)*
- **Kaśyapa** — heads **1 gaṇa** (Kāśyapa). *(Source: Indica Today.)*
- **Atri** — heads **1 gaṇa** (Ātreya). *(Source: Indica Today.)*
- **Vasiṣṭha** — heads **1 gaṇa** (Vāsiṣṭha). *(Source: Indica Today.)*
- **Agastya** — heads **1 gaṇa** (Āgastya). *(Source: Indica Today.)*
- **Viśvāmitra** — heads **1 gaṇa** (Kauśika). *(Source: Indica Today.)*
- **The 18 gaṇas (full list):** *Jamadagni, Vītahavya, Gṛtsamada, Vadhryaśva, Vainya | Gautama, Bharadvāja, Kapi, Kaṇva, Mudgala, Virūpa, Hārita, Viṣṇuvṛddha | Kaśyapa, Atri, Vasiṣṭha, Agastya, Viśvāmitra.* *(Source: Indica Today; Indiafacts.)*
- *Variant (the "seven" vs "eight" root count):* the chief-Brahmaṛṣi root-list is most often given as **seven — Bhṛgu, Aṅgiras, Kaśyapa, Atri, Vasiṣṭha, Agastya, Viśvāmitra** (note: this replaces the astronomical Marīci/Pulastya/Pulaha/Kratu with the historical gotra-founders), with **Agastya** as the late eighth in the Bṛhadāraṇyaka-derived count. The two "sevens" (astronomical vs gotra) do **not** coincide. [disputed] *(Source: Indica Today; Britannica.)*
- *Variant (Āpastamba/Baudhāyana totals):* the pravara-sūtras enumerate **49 gotra-groups** under these roots in some recensions; regional Smārta and Śrīvaiṣṇava lists expand the total open-endedly. The roster is **open-ended**. *(Source: Baudhāyana Śrauta Sūtra pravarādhyāya; Śrīvaiṣṇava pravara compilations.)*

## 5. The Bhārgavas — line of Bhṛgu

The **Bhārgavas** are the descendants of **Bhṛgu**, the fire-seer; mythologically the **purohitas (priests) of the Daityas/Dānavas**, masters of the **Sāmaveda** and of revival-magic — a brahmin line shading into kṣatriya martial fury (Paraśurāma). *(Source: Wikipedia, *Bhargava*; Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva.)*

- **Bhṛgu** (भृगु) - Apical patriarch; a **mind-born son of Brahmā** and a Prajāpati. Father (by Khyāti) of **Lakṣmī's foster-line and Dhātṛ/Vidhātṛ**; author of the **Bhṛgu Saṃhitā** (astrology). Famous for **kicking Viṣṇu's chest** (the *Bhṛgu-pada* test of the Trimūrti) and cursing/blessing accordingly. *(Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa 10.89; Padma Purāṇa; Wikipedia, *Bhrigu*.)*
- **Śukra / Uśanas Kāvya** (शुक्र) - Son of Bhṛgu; **Daitya-guru**, preceptor of the asuras, and the **planet Venus** ([Śukra of the Navagraha](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md)). Holder of the **Mṛtasañjīvanī vidyā** (won from Śiva) to revive the slain. *(Source: Mahābhārata; Wikipedia, *Bhargava*.)* — see the asura-side framing in [asura-wars-and-roster.md](asura-wars-and-roster.md).
- **Cyavana** (च्यवन) - Bhṛgu's son by Pulomā; rejuvenated by the **Aśvins** (the origin-myth of **Cyavanaprāśa**) after marriage to the princess **Sukanyā**; secured the Aśvins a soma-share. *(Source: Mahābhārata, Vana Parva; Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 4.1.5.)*
- **Aurva** (और्व) - Grandson-line of Cyavana; the seer of the **submarine fire (Vāḍava/Aurva-agni)** born of his world-burning wrath against the kṣatriyas, restrained into the ocean-mare's-mouth fire. *(Source: Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva.)*
- **Ṛcīka** (ऋचीक) - Son of Aurva; married **Satyavatī** (daughter of king Gādhi, sister of Viśvāmitra) — the **caru-swap myth** in which the brahmin and kṣatriya rice-cakes are exchanged, producing a martial brahmin line and a priestly king. *(Source: Mahābhārata; Viṣṇu Purāṇa.)*
- **Jamadagni** (जमदग्नि) - Son of Ṛcīka & Satyavatī; a **Saptarṣi** of the current manvantara, holder of the wish-cow **Kāmadhenu (Surabhi/Suśīlā)**; husband of **Reṇukā**; slain by **Kārtavīrya Arjuna's** sons over the cow — the trigger of Paraśurāma's vengeance. *(Source: Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 9.15–16.)*
- **Paraśurāma / Bhārgava Rāma** (परशुराम) - Youngest son of Jamadagni & Reṇukā; the **6th [avatāra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) of Viṣṇu**, the **axe-bearing** ciraṃjīvī who cleared the earth of corrupt kṣatriyas **21 times**. Reclaimed Konkan/Kerala from the sea; guru of **Bhīṣma, Droṇa, Karṇa**. *(Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa 9.15–16; Mahābhārata.)*
- **Dadhīci / Dadhyañc** (दधीचि) - Bhārgava (sometimes Āṅgirasa [disputed]) ascetic who **gave his bones** to forge Indra's **Vajra**; teacher of the *Madhu-vidyā*. *(Source: Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.9–10.)*
- *Variant (Vītahavya/Gṛtsamada absorption):* the gotra-tradition folds **Vītahavya, Gṛtsamada, Vadhryaśva, Vainya** into the Bhārgava gaṇa-head set (see §4); Gṛtsamada is the seer of **Ṛgveda Maṇḍala II** — originally an Āṅgirasa adopted into the Bhṛgu line. [disputed] *(Source: Bṛhaddevatā; pravara-tradition.)*

## 6. The Āṅgirasas — line of Aṅgiras

The **Āṅgirasas** (descendants of **Aṅgiras**, the fire-priest seer of the **Ṛgveda** and **Atharvaveda**) are the **largest** root-cluster — 8 gaṇas — and the priestly line of the **gods** (counterpart to the Bhārgava asura-priests). *(Source: Ṛgveda; Indica Today.)*

- **Aṅgiras** (अङ्गिरस्) - Apical seer; mind-born son of Brahmā, a Prajāpati and (in the astronomical set) a Saptarṣi. Co-revealer of the **Atharvaveda** (the *Atharvāṅgirasa*). Father of Bṛhaspati, Utathya, Saṃvarta. *(Source: Ṛgveda; Atharvaveda; Mahābhārata.)*
- **Bṛhaspati** (बृहस्पति) - Son of Aṅgiras; **Deva-guru**, preceptor of the gods, lord of prayer (**Brahmaṇaspati**), and the **planet Jupiter** ([Bṛhaspati of the Navagraha](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md)). The counterweight to Śukra. *(Source: Ṛgveda; Mahābhārata.)*
- **Bharadvāja** (भरद्वाज) - Son (in epic genealogy, of Bṛhaspati/Utathya line); a **Saptarṣi** of the current manvantara, seer of **Ṛgveda Maṇḍala VI**, founder of the great **Bhāradvāja gotra**; father of **Droṇa**. *(Source: Ṛgveda; Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva.)*
  - *Bhāradvāja pravara (3):* **Āṅgirasa, Bārhaspatya, Bhāradvāja.** *(Source: Śrīvaiṣṇava pravara lists.)*
- **Gautama / Gotama** (गौतम) - Āṅgirasa seer of **Ṛgveda Maṇḍala I (part)**; husband of **Ahalyā** (the Indra-curse / stone-and-restoration myth tied to Rāma); founder of the **Gautama gotra**; ancestor-name of **Śatānanda** (Janaka's purohita). The Nyāya-sūtra **Gautama (Akṣapāda)** also bears this gotra. *(Source: Ṛgveda; Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa.)*
- **Garga / Gārgya** (गर्ग) - Āṅgirasa seer-astronomer (the **Garga Saṃhitā**); Yādava family-priest who named **Kṛṣṇa & Balarāma**. *(Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa 10.8; Garga Saṃhitā.)*
  - *Gārgya pravara (5):* **Āṅgirasa, Bārhaspatya, Bhāradvāja, Śainya, Gārgya.** *(Source: Śrīvaiṣṇava pravara lists.)*
- **Kaṇva** (कण्व) - Āṅgirasa seer of many **Maṇḍala VIII** hymns; foster-father of **Śakuntalā** at his āśrama (the Kālidāsa frame). *(Source: Ṛgveda; Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva.)*
- **Mudgala, Kapi, Virūpa, Hārita, Viṣṇuvṛddha** - The remaining Āṅgirasa gaṇa-heads, each founding its gotra-cluster; **Mudgala** is eponym of the *Mudgala Purāṇa* (Gaṇeśa). *(Source: Indica Today; pravara-tradition.)*
- *Variant (Dīrghatamas / Kakṣīvān):* the blind seer **Dīrghatamas** and his son **Kakṣīvān** (a Rājarṣi) are Āṅgirasas in the Utathya sub-line, ancestors of the **Aṅga–Vaṅga–Kaliṅga** kṣatriya lines. *(Source: Ṛgveda 1.18; Mahābhārata.)*

## 7. The remaining root families

### Atris (line of Atri)
- **Atri** (अत्रि) - Mind-born son of Brahmā, a Prajāpati, **Saptarṣi**; seer of **Ṛgveda Maṇḍala V**; husband of **Anasūyā** (paragon of *pātivratya*). By the Trimūrti's boon, father of **Soma (Candra)**, **Dattātreya** (Viṣṇu-aspect), and **Durvāsas** (Śiva-aspect) — three deities born as his sons. Founder of the **Ātreya gotra**. *(Source: Ṛgveda; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1; Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa.)*
  - *Ātreya pravara (3):* **Ātreya, Ārcanānasa, Śyāvāśva.** *(Source: pravara-tradition.)*
- **Durvāsas** (दुर्वासस्) - Atri's son, the **short-tempered sage** whose curses/boons drive countless myths (Kuntī's mantra; Śakuntalā's curse; Ambarīṣa's Sudarśana). *(Source: Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 9.4.)*

### Kaśyapas (line of Kaśyapa)
- **Kaśyapa** (कश्यप) - The **universal progenitor**: by Dakṣa's daughters (Aditi, Diti, Danu, Kadrū, Vinatā, …) he fathers the **Ādityas/devas, Daityas, Dānavas, Nāgas, Garuḍa/Aruṇa**, and most beings — making the whole [bestiary](README.md) one half-sibling feud. A **Saptarṣi**; founder of the **Kāśyapa gotra** (and the default gotra for the gotra-less). Eponym of **Kashmir** (*Kaśyapa-mīra*). *(Source: Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.6.)*
  - *Kāśyapa pravara (3):* **Kāśyapa, Āvatsāra, Naidhruva.** *(Source: pravara-tradition.)*

### Vāsiṣṭhas (line of Vasiṣṭha)
- **Vasiṣṭha** (वसिष्ठ) - Mind-born son of Brahmā (or of Mitra-Varuṇa & Urvaśī); **Saptarṣi**; seer of **Ṛgveda Maṇḍala VII**; **purohita of the Ikṣvāku/[Sūryavaṃśa](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md)** kings (Rāma's guru-line). Holder of the wish-cow **Nandinī/Kāmadhenu**; arch-rival of **Viśvāmitra** (the cow-war and the death of his 100 sons). His wife **Arundhatī** = the star Alcor (Mizar-Alcor pair; see [saptarishi.md §3](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md)). Founder of the **Vāsiṣṭha gotra**. *(Source: Ṛgveda; Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa; Mahābhārata.)*
  - **Śakti & Parāśara** - Vasiṣṭha's son **Śakti** (killed by Viśvāmitra's agency) and grandson **Parāśara** (author of the Viṣṇu Purāṇa, father of **Vyāsa**) — so the Vāsiṣṭha line is the literary ancestry of the **Mahābhārata** itself. *(Source: Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.1; Mahābhārata.)*

### Viśvāmitra / Kauśikas (line of Viśvāmitra)
- **Viśvāmitra** (विश्वामित्र) - **The anomaly**: born **Kṣatriya** (king **Kauśika/Viśvaratha**, of the Kuśika–Gādhi line, [Chandravaṃśa](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md)-adjacent Amāvasu branch), he forced his way to **Brahmaṛṣi** rank by colossal tapas — the only Saptarṣi who was *not* a born brahmin. Seer of **Ṛgveda Maṇḍala III** including the **Gāyatrī mantra (RV 3.62.10)**; tester/guru of **Rāma & Lakṣmaṇa** (Bāla Kāṇḍa); creator of **Triśaṅku's heaven**; broken by **Menakā** (→ Śakuntalā) and **Rambhā**. Founder of the **Kauśika / Viśvāmitra gotra**. *(Source: Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa 51–65; Ṛgveda III; Mahābhārata.)*
  - *Variant (Kauśika vs Viśvāmitra gotra):* "**Kauśika**" is used both as a synonym for the Viśvāmitra gotra **and** as one of its pravara-sub-gotras (Viśvāmitra's many adopted sons spawned numerous sub-lines — Madhucchandas, Devarāta/Śunaḥśepa, Aṣṭaka, etc., classed under several pravara-formulae). [disputed] *(Source: Tamil Brahmins forum; Baudhāyana pravara lists.)*
  - *Viśvāmitra pravara (3):* **Vaiśvāmitra, Aghamarṣaṇa, Kauśika** (one common formula). *(Source: pravara-tradition.)*

### Agastya (the eighth root)
- **Agastya** (अगस्त्य) - "Pot-born" (**Kumbhasambhava / Maitrāvaruṇi**), son of Mitra-Varuṇa & Urvaśī (twin-origin with Vasiṣṭha); seer of **Ṛgveda I** hymns; the sage who **drank the ocean** dry (to expose the Kālakeya asuras) and **pressed down the Vindhya mountains**, opening the **southward** spread of Vedic culture — hence his gotra is the late **eighth** added to the seven. Husband of **Lopāmudrā**; reputed first **Tamil** grammarian (*Agattiyam*) and **Siddha** patriarch. Founder of the **Āgastya gotra**. *(Source: Ṛgveda I; Mahābhārata, Vana Parva; Britannica, *gotra*; Tamil Siddha tradition [folk].)*

## 8. Open-endedness & cross-links

- The **gotra roster is finite-in-principle but open-in-practice**: the **8 roots → 18 gaṇas** is stable, but downstream gotra-counts (49, "many tens," and regional Smārta/Śrīvaiṣṇava/Nambūdiri expansions) **do not converge**. Pravara-formulae also vary by sūtra-school (Āpastamba, Baudhāyana, Āśvalāyana). The roster is therefore **flagged open-ended**. *(Source: Baudhāyana/Āpastamba pravara-sūtras; Śrīvaiṣṇava compilations.)*
- For the **rotating Saptarṣi office** per manvantara, the sky-asterism (Ursa Major), and all variant seven-lists: [../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md).
