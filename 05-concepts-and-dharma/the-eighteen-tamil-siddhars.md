# The Eighteen Tamil Siddhars (Pathinen Siddhar) & the Siddha Tradition

> Summary node within [05-concepts-and-dharma](./README.md) of *The Hindu Timeline*. Back to the [master index](../README.md).
> **Canonical home:** the full treatment — rosters and variants, the Siddha medical-alchemical system, the scripture and song-canon — lives at [**The Tamil Siddhars (Patiṉeṇ Cittar)**](../07-acharyas-and-sampradayas/tamil-siddhars.md) in the ācāryas-and-sampradāyas section, per the section map.

The **Siddhars** (Tamil *cittar*, from Skt. *siddha*, "perfected one") are the South-Indian Tamil Śaiva lineage of mystic-physician-alchemists who pursued **deathlessness of the body** (*kāya-siddhi*) through yoga, alchemy (*irasavātam*/*rasavāda*, the secret salt-elixir **muppu**) and the indigenous medical system they founded, **Siddha-vaidya**. Their canonical college is the **Patiṉeṇ Cittar** — "the Eighteen Siddhars," headed by **Agastya (Agattiyar)**, with Tirumūlar (author of the *Tirumantiram*), Bhogar (the Palani *navapāṣāṇa* alchemist), Koṅkaṇar, Karuvūrār, Pāmbāṭṭi-cittar and Civavākkiyar among the most-cited; rosters vary by tradition and are cataloged, never merged. The two clocks apply sharply: tradition frames the founders as **deathless adepts alive across yugas** [traditional], while the datable poetic corpus runs **c. 6th–18th c. CE** [scholarly].
