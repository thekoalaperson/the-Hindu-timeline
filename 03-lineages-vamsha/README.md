# Lineages (Vaṃśa)

> Where this sits: the **genealogical threads** that run through the chronological spine. Most named human
> kings and heroes of the timeline descend from two great dynasties founded by the children of
> [Vaivasvata Manu](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/README.md).

A cross-cutting reference: the dynasties span multiple yugas, so they are gathered here and linked from
the individual [yuga event files](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/README.md).

  plus the Saptarishi lineages, with Purāṇic variants.
