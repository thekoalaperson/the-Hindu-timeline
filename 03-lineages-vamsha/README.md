# Lineages (Vaṃśa)

> Where this sits: the **genealogical threads** that run through the chronological spine. Most named human
> kings and heroes of the timeline descend from two great dynasties founded by the children of
> [Vaivasvata Manu](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/README.md).

A cross-cutting reference: the dynasties span multiple yugas, so they are gathered here and linked from
the individual [yuga event files](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/README.md).

### Solar line (Sūryavaṃśa / Ikṣvāku, seat Ayodhyā)
- [Anaraṇya — the Ikṣvāku king whose curse dooms Rāvaṇa](anaranya-ravana-curse.md) — the dying curse, planted during Rāvaṇa's digvijaya, that pre-scripts the rākṣasa's death at a descendant's (Rāma's) hand.
- [The Raghuvaṃśa Kings — Dilīpa, Raghu, Aja](raghuvamsha-kalidasa-kings.md) — the three connected Kālidāsa episodes (cow-service, world-conquest and gold-rain, the Indumatī elegy) in the generations just above Daśaratha and Rāma.

### Lunar line (Candravaṃśa — Pauravā/Kuru and allied branches)
- [Gādhi and the caru-swap](gadhi-and-the-caru-swap.md) — the Kānyakubja hinge from which the Bhārgava (→ Jamadagni → Paraśurāma) and Kuśika (→ Viśvāmitra) lines fork by a ritual mix-up.
- [Jahnu — sage-king and eponym of Jāhnavī](jahnu.md) — drinks and releases the descending Gaṅgā, making her his "daughter" and fixing the river's epithet.
- [Uparichara Vasu — the sky-faring Cedi king](uparichara-vasu.md) — the upstream ancestor whose fallen seed begets Satyavatī, quietly re-legitimising the whole Kuru genealogy.
- [Saṃvaraṇa & Tapatī](samvarana-tapati.md) — the marriage to Sūrya's daughter that grafts Solar blood onto the Kuru line; charter-tale of the purohita's office.
- [Kuru — the eponym](kuru-the-eponym.md) — the king who ploughs Kurukṣetra into a dharma-kṣetra and names both warring houses of the Mahābhārata.
- [Devāpi](devapi.md) — the renounced elder prince, Vedic rain-priest, and yogic immortal who waits out Kali Yuga to restore the dynasty.
- [Śāntanu — king of Hastināpura](shantanu.md) — husband of Gaṅgā and Satyavatī; the hinge-king whose second marriage forces Bhīṣma's vow.
- [Parīkṣit — first king of Kali](parikshit.md) — the Śṛṅgī curse, the seven-day vigil, and the recitation-frame of the *Bhāgavata Purāṇa*.
- [Janamejaya and the Sarpa-satra](janamejaya-sarpa-satra.md) — the snake sacrifice that is the *Mahābhārata*'s own outer telling-frame.
