# Manvantara 10 — Brahma-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Hierarchy: 1st Day of Brahma's 51st year (Shveta-Varaha Kalpa) → **10th of 14 Manvantaras**. A *future* age, three Manvantaras after the current [7th, Vaivasvata](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). The third of the four consecutive "Sāvarṇi" Manus (Sūrya/Sāvarṇa-descended line: [8 Sāvarṇi](manvantara-08-savarni.md) → [9 Dakṣa-sāvarṇi](manvantara-09-daksha-savarni.md) → **10 Brahma-sāvarṇi** → [11 Dharma-sāvarṇi](manvantara-11-dharma-savarni.md)). Up-link: [the 14 Manvantaras README](README.md).

**Duration:** ~306,720,000 human years (71 Mahayugas + 1 sandhyā), like every Manvantara. See [framework constants](../../00-time-and-cosmology/).

The Brahma-sāvarṇi Manvantara is an **unmanifest future age**: it has not yet occurred in the present [Shveta-Varaha Kalpa](../). The Puranas catalogue it only as a roster — the Manu, his sons, the Indra, the seven sages (saptarṣis), the classes of gods, and the avatar of Viṣṇu who upholds dharma in that age — without narrative episodes. Accordingly there are **no stories** attached to it, and the record is genuinely sparse; the entries below are the standard list-form of the Purana cosmographies, with variants catalogued where the texts disagree.

## The Manu

- **Brahma-Sāvarṇi Manu** — presiding Manu of the age; tenth in the sequence of 14. Named in the Bhagavata as the **son of Upaśloka** (Upaśloka being reckoned in the Sāvarṇi / Sun-god line). His sobriquet "Brahma-sāvarṇi" places him with the *sāvarṇa* ("of like complexion / class") group of future Manus. (Future age) (Bhagavata Purana VIII.13.21-23).
  - *Variant (Vishnu Purana III.2):* the name **Brahma-sāvarṇi** is given for the 10th Manu, but no parentage or sons are detailed; the Purana simply lists him in sequence after Dakṣa-sāvarṇi and before Dharma-sāvarṇi.

- **Sons of the Manu** — described as **ten sons**, of whom **Bhūriṣeṇa** is named as foremost (Bhagavata Purana VIII.13). The remaining nine are not consistently enumerated in the cited sources; the Purana names the chief and abbreviates the rest as "and others." [Record sparse — full ten-son list not preserved in the indexed texts.]

## Indra and the classes of gods

- **Indra of this Manvantara** — **Śambhu** (Bhagavata Purana VIII.13). (Śambhu here is the title of the office-holder; the same word is also an epithet of Śiva, but in this context it names the king of the gods for the age, said to be a **friend of the avatar Viśvaksena**.)
  - *Variant (Vishnu Purana III.2):* **Śānti** is named as the Indra of the Brahma-sāvarṇi Manvantara.

- **Classes of devas** — the divine orders ruled over by Śambhu. The two principal traditions name them differently:
  - *Bhagavata Purana VIII.13:* the gods of this age are the **Suvāsanas** (also read **Sudhāmas**) and **Viruddhas** — the standard pairing carried in this entry's FOCUS. (Future age)
  - *Vishnu Purana III.2:* names the deva-classes as **Sudhāma** and **Viśuddha** (a Viśvadeva-type grouping), each with a set membership.
  - *Note [disputed]:* the deva-class lists are the single most variable element across the Brahmāṇḍa / Vāyu / Mārkaṇḍeya recensions for this age; the spellings Suvāsana / Sudhāma / Sudharman and Viruddha / Viśuddha shift between manuscripts. Treat the names as catalogued aliases rather than fixed.

## The Saptarṣis (Seven Sages)

- **Saptarṣis** — the seven sages who guard knowledge in the age: **Haviṣmān (Haviṣmat), Sukṛti, Satya, Apāṃmūrti, Nābhāga, Apratimaujas, Satyaketu** (Vishnu Purana III.2). (Future age)
  - *Variant (Bhagavata Purana VIII.13):* names **Haviṣmān** as foremost of the seven sages ("Haviṣmān and others"), consistent with the Vishnu Purana's lead sage but without re-listing all seven.

## Avatar of Viṣṇu

- **Viśvaksena** — Viṣṇu manifests as **Viśvaksena** to assist Manu Brahma-sāvarṇi and uphold dharma. He is described as a **friend of Indra Śambhu**, **born from the womb of Viśūcī** (Viśucī) **in the house of a brahmana named Viśvasṛṣṭa (Viśvasraṣṭā / Viśvasrashta)** (Bhagavata Purana VIII.13). (Future age)
  - *Variant (definitional/Purana Index):* **Śrīmūrti** is given as the name of "the manifestation of Hari" for this age, alongside or as an alternate title of Viśvaksena. [Variant title]
  - *Cross-reference:* "Viśvaksena" is also the standing name of Viṣṇu's chief attendant / commander-in-chief (senāpati) in [Vaishnava deity tradition](../../05-deities/); here it is reused as the avatar-name for this Manvantara, a common pattern of office-names recurring across the future ages.

## Why the record is sparse

As an unmanifest future age, the Brahma-sāvarṇi Manvantara has **no event-narrative** in the Puranic corpus — only the standardized roster (Manu · sons · Indra · sages · deva-classes · avatar) that every future Manvantara receives. The chief disagreements between sources are:
1. the **Indra's name** — Śambhu (Bhagavata) vs Śānti (Vishnu Purana); and
2. the **deva-class names** — Suvāsanas/Viruddhas (Bhagavata) vs Sudhāma/Viśuddha (Vishnu Purana).
Both texts agree on the lead sage **Haviṣmān**, the Manu's name **Brahma-sāvarṇi**, and the avatar **Viśvaksena**.
