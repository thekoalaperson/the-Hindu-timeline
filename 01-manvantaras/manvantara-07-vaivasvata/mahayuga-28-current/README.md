# The 28th Mahāyuga (Current)

> Hierarchy address: Brahmā's 51st year → Shveta-Varāha Kalpa (current Day of Brahmā) → 7th Manvantara ([Vaivasvata Manu](../README.md)) → **28th of its 71 Mahāyugas** → currently in its **Kali Yuga**.
> This is the "you are here" node one level above the four individual yuga files. It frames the whole Satya→Kali arc we are living through and points down into each quarter.

---

## What a Mahāyuga is (this one in particular)

- **Definition.** A Mahāyuga (Chaturyuga / Divya-yuga) = one full revolution of the four yugas in the descending ratio 4:3:2:1 = **4,320,000 human years** (Satya 1,728,000 + Treta 1,296,000 + Dvapara 864,000 + Kali 432,000). (Vishnu Purana 1.3; Bhagavata Purana 3.11; Surya Siddhanta 1.15–21; Manusmriti 1.68–71)
- **Position in the Manvantara.** A Manvantara contains **71 Mahāyugas** (+ a connecting Satya-length sandhi). This is the **28th**: 27 have fully elapsed, and within the 28th, **Satya, Treta and Dvapara are complete** — we stand inside its **Kali Yuga**. (Vishnu Purana 1.3; Bhagavata Purana 12.2)
- **Elapsed within the 28th Mahāyuga:** Satya (1,728,000) + Treta (1,296,000) + Dvapara (864,000) + Kali-so-far (≈5,127 yrs as of 2026 CE) ≈ **3,893,127 years** of this Mahāyuga gone; ≈ **426,873 years** of Kali remain before the cycle resets to a new Satya.
- **Why this Mahāyuga matters above all others:** it is the one in which **Vishnu descended as Krishna** at the Dvapara–Kali junction, the **Mahābhārata war** was fought, and the **Vedas were redacted into four** by the current Vyāsa. It is the most densely "recorded" Mahāyuga in the entire tradition. (Bhagavata Purana 1; Mahabharata; Vishnu Purana 4–5)

## The Satya → Kali arc of THIS Mahāyuga (overview of the four child files)

Each yuga below has its own file. The dharma-bull (vṛṣa) loses one leg per yuga: 4 → 3 → 2 → 1.

- **Satya / Krita Yuga** (1,728,000 yrs; dharma on 4 legs) — the golden age opening this Mahāyuga: humanity virtuous, long-lived, meditation-centered; no formal scripture or ritual needed. Key descents/figures associated with the Satya-Yuga slot include the early Matsya, Kurma, Varaha and Narasimha avatāra contexts in cyclic listings. (Vishnu Purana 1; Bhagavata Purana) → see [01-satya-yuga.md](01-satya-yuga.md)
- **Treta Yuga** (1,296,000 yrs; dharma on 3 legs) — sacrifice (yajña) and kingship arise; dharma declines by a quarter. The **Rāmāyaṇa** epoch is conventionally placed here: Vishnu as **Rāma**, also **Vāmana** and **Parashurāma** in cyclic schemes. (Valmiki Ramayana; Vishnu Purana) → see [02-treta-yuga.md](02-treta-yuga.md)
- **Dvapara Yuga** (864,000 yrs; dharma on 2 legs) — virtue and vice in balance; disease and discord begin. **Vyāsa compiles the Vedas** here (see below); **Krishna's life** and the lead-up to the **Mahābhārata** fall at this yuga's close. → see [03-dvapara-yuga.md](03-dvapara-yuga.md)
- **Kali Yuga** (432,000 yrs; dharma on 1 leg) — the present age of strife, begun **3102 BCE**; ~5,127 yrs elapsed (2026 CE). Promised closing descent: **Kalki**, who ends Kali and restores Satya. → see [04-kali-yuga/README.md](04-kali-yuga/README.md)

## Recorded events / defining features of the 28th Mahāyuga

- **The current Vyāsa: Krishna-Dvaipāyana Veda-Vyāsa.** "Vyāsa" is an office, not a name — one Vyāsa per Dvapara Yuga divides the single Veda into branches. In this Manvantara there have been **28 Vyāsas**; the **28th and current** is **Krishna-Dvaipāyana** (dark-complexioned, born on an island, dvīpa). He split the Veda into **Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva** and taught them to four pupils (Paila, Vaiśampāyana, Jaimini, Sumantu), and authored the **Mahābhārata** and (by tradition) the Puranas and Brahma Sūtras. (Vishnu Purana 3.3–3.4; Bhagavata Purana 1.4; Mahabharata Adi Parva)
  - *Variant (Vishnu Purana 3.3, list of Vyāsas):* the 28 Vyāsas are enumerated in order; notable named bearers include **Vasiṣṭha** (1st), **Sage Śakti** (Parāśara's father, 25th), **Parāśara** himself, **Jātūkarṇya**, and Krishna-Dvaipāyana (28th). The lists in different Puranas differ in spelling/order — catalog as a known textual variance.
  - *Variant (Vishnu Purana / Bhagavata):* the **29th Vyāsa**, in the *next* Dvapara, will be **Aśvatthāmā** (son of Drona), one of the chiranjivis. [forward-looking]
- **Vishnu's descents this Mahāyuga.** In standard avatāra-to-yuga mapping for this very cycle: **Rāma** (Treta), **Krishna** (end of Dvapara), and **Kalki** (end of Kali, still future). Many lists also slot the earlier avatāras (Matsya … Narasimha, Vāmana, Parashurāma) into the Satya/Treta of this same Mahāyuga. (Bhagavata Purana 1.3; Gita Govinda; cyclic Purana lists)
- **The Mahābhārata war & Krishna's departure.** Fought near the Dvapara–Kali junction; **Krishna's departure (3102 BCE)** is the traditional marker for **Kali Yuga's onset**. The Bhagavata states that on the day Krishna left this world, Kali "fully entered." (Bhagavata Purana 1.15; Mahabharata; Surya Siddhanta epoch)
- **Lord Caitanya / closing-age teachers.** In Gaudiya tradition, the next descent after Krishna in this Mahāyuga is **Chaitanya Mahāprabhu** within the present Kali Yuga, propagating the nāma-saṅkīrtana yuga-dharma. (Bhagavata Purana 11.5.32 [as interpreted]; Chaitanya Charitamrita) [sampradāya-specific]

## Key VARIANT: the reversed Treta–Dvapara order in this Mahāyuga

- **The anomaly.** Several traditions hold that **this particular (28th) Mahāyuga is unique**: the normal sequence Satya → Treta → Dvapara → Kali is **disrupted, with Treta and Dvapara reversed**, precisely because Krishna descends in it. (Bhagavata Purana commentary tradition; Gaudiya Vaishnava sources)
  - *Variant (mainstream Puranic):* No reversal — the order is the standard Satya → Treta → Dvapara → Kali, and Krishna simply appears at the end of Dvapara as in every avatāra chronology. This is the dominant reading. (Vishnu Purana; Bhagavata Purana mainstream)
  - *Variant (a "mysterious verse" tradition):* a Sanskrit verse listing **Satya, Dvāpara, Treta, Kali** has long puzzled commentators; some read it as evidence the 28th Mahāyuga's middle yugas swap, others as scribal/poetic ordering. [disputed — treated as an exegetical crux, not settled doctrine]
- *Note:* this variant only concerns the **internal ordering** of the 28th Mahāyuga; the totals (4,320,000 yrs) and our present location (Kali) are unaffected.
