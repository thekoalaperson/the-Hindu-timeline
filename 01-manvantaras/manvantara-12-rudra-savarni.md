# Manvantara 12 — Rudra-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Hierarchy: [the-14-manvantaras](README.md) → **12th Manvantara (Rudra-Sāvarṇi)**. A *future* manvantara within the current Śveta-Varāha Kalpa; the fifth of the future set, two manvantaras still lie ahead after it. Each spans ~306,720,000 human years (71 mahāyugas + 1 sandhyā). Up-link: [the-14-manvantaras README](../README.md).

## Orientation
- We are presently in the **7th ([Vaivasvata](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/))** manvantara. Rudra-Sāvarṇi is the **12th**, so it lies wholly in the future. Manvantaras 8–14 form the *future* set whose officers are catalogued chiefly in the **Bhāgavata Purāṇa (8.13)** and the **Viṣṇu Purāṇa (3.2)**; they are rosters, not narratives.
- It is the fifth of the so-called **Sāvarṇi-line** future Manus. In the **Bhāgavata Purāṇa (8.13)** grouping, **six Manus (the 8th–13th)** carry the epithet *Sāvarṇi* ("of like colour/varṇa"), traditionally understood as kindred forms of the Sāvarṇi prototype: [Sāvarṇi (8th)](manvantara-08-savarni.md), [Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi (9th)](manvantara-09-daksha-savarni.md), [Brahma-Sāvarṇi (10th)](manvantara-10-brahma-savarni.md), [Dharma-Sāvarṇi (11th)](manvantara-11-dharma-savarni.md), **Rudra-Sāvarṇi (12th)** — the fifth of the six — and [Deva-Sāvarṇi (13th)](manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md). The final [Indra-Sāvarṇi (14th)](manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md) is likewise styled *Sāvarṇi* in some lists, which would make seven; the standard Bhāgavata count of those expressly grouped is six (matching the sibling files [08](manvantara-08-savarni.md) and [13](manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md)).
- As with all future ages, **no story-events are recorded** in the canon — only the standard five-slot officer roster (Manu, his sons, the Indra, the Saptarṣis, the deva-classes, and the upholding Viṣṇu-avatāra). What follows enumerates that roster fully and catalogues the variant readings; honesty demands noting that the narrative record here is genuinely sparse.

## Officers of the 12th Manvantara

### The Manu — Rudra-Sāvarṇi
- **Rudra-Sāvarṇi** — twelfth Manu, presiding sovereign of mankind for this age (*bhavitā rudra-sāvarṇi rājan dvādaśamo manuḥ*, Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.27).
  - *Variant (folk / Mānava etymology):* the name is read as "the Sāvarṇi (sun-born line) associated with Rudra," and in some later/folk tellings Rudra-Sāvarṇi is glossed as a son or aṃśa of **Rudra (Śiva)**, paralleling how the 8th Manu Sāvarṇi is a son of Sūrya/Vivasvat and the 10th (Brahma-Sāvarṇi) and 11th (Dharma-Sāvarṇi) carry their patron-deity's name. The Purāṇic verses themselves give only the name, not a parentage. [folk]
- **Sons of the Manu:** headed by **Devavān (Devavat)** in the Viṣṇu Purāṇa; the Bhāgavata pairs **Devavān and Upadeva** with **Devaśreṣṭha** and others as the chief sons (*devavān upadevaś ca devaśreṣṭhādayaḥ sutāḥ*, BhP 8.13.27).
  - *Variant (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2):* names **Devavān** alone as head of the Manu's sons; **Upadeva** and **Devaśreṣṭha** are the Bhāgavata's additions.

### The Indra — Ṛtadhāma
- **Ṛtadhāma (Ṛtadhāman)** — the king of the devas who holds the office of [Indra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md) during this manvantara (*ṛtadhāmā ca tatrendraḥ*, BhP 8.13.28; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2). The Indra-ship is an office that rotates each manvantara; Ṛtadhāma occupies it here.
  - *Variant (spelling):* rendered **Ṛtudhāma / Ṛtadhāman / Ṛtadhāmā** across recensions and translations; all denote the same figure.

### The Saptarṣis (Seven Sages)
- The seven sages of Rudra-Sāvarṇi's age, by the **Viṣṇu Purāṇa (3.2)** list — a *tapas*-themed septet, every name built on *tapas* ("austerity"):
  - **Tapasvī** (Tapaswī)
  - **Sutapas**
  - **Tapomūrti** ("austerity-embodied")
  - **Taporati**
  - **Tapodhṛti**
  - **Tapodyuti**
  - **Tapodhana**
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.28):* gives the sages summarily as **Tapomūrti, Tapasvī, Agnīdhraka, and others** (*ṛṣayaś ca tapomūrtis tapasvy agnīdhrakādayaḥ*) — introducing **Agnīdhraka**, who does not appear in the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's seven *tapas*-names. The two lists are reconciled as different recensional rosters of the same septet. [disputed]

### Classes of Devas (deva-gaṇas)
- **The Haritas and others** (*devāś ca hāritādayaḥ*, BhP 8.13.28) — in the Bhāgavata the Haritas are the named class of gods for this manvantara, "and the rest" (*ādayaḥ*) left unenumerated.
  - *Variant (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2):* the Viṣṇu Purāṇa does **not** compress the roster — it names **four** deva-classes for this very manvantara: **Haritas, Lohitas, Sumanasas, and Sukarmas, each comprising fifteen** (Wilson translation, Book 3 ch. 2). So the Lohitas, Sumanasas and Sukarmas are the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's own canonical content for Rudra-Sāvarṇi, not the Bhāgavata's; the two texts simply differ in fullness — the Bhāgavata abbreviates ("Haritas and others") where the Viṣṇu Purāṇa enumerates.
  - *Note on multi-class schemes:* the Bhāgavata gives only "the Haritas and others," whereas the Viṣṇu Purāṇa tabulates the four fifteen-membered troops above. Where popular summaries add still further or variantly-spelled classes (e.g. *Rohita*, *Surāpa*) beyond these two canonical lists, those additions are extrapolations from the neighbouring Sāvarṇi manvantaras rather than verses specific to Rudra-Sāvarṇi. [scholarly]

### The Viṣṇu Avatāra — Svadhāman (Sudhāman)
- **Svadhāman (Sudhāman / Svadhāma)** — the partial incarnation (*aṃśa*) of [Viṣṇu](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md) who upholds dharma and the Manu's rule during this age (*svadhāmākhyo harer aṃśaḥ sādhayiṣyati tan-manoḥ*, BhP 8.13.29).
  - **Parentage:** born of the womb of **Sunṛtā**, wife of **Satyasaha** (Satyasahas) — *antaraṃ satyasahasaḥ sunṛtāyāḥ suto vibhuḥ* (BhP 8.13.29). He is thus the son of Satyasaha and Sunṛtā, descending to govern the affairs of Rudra-Sāvarṇi's manvantara.
  - *Variant (name):* **Svadhāman / Svadhāma** vs **Sudhāman** — both renderings of the avatāra named at **Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.29**; "Sva-dhāman" ("self-abiding / of own abode") and "Su-dhāman" ("of good abode") are alternative readings/transliterations of the same Bhāgavata avatāra, not a Viṣṇu-Purāṇa-vs-Bhāgavata split. The **Viṣṇu Purāṇa (3.2) names no manvantara-avatāra** for this (or any future) age — it lists only Manu, Indra, deva-classes, Rishis and the Manu's sons — so the avatar belongs to the Bhāgavata alone (cf. the 9th–11th manvantara files, which note the same). [disputed]

## Placement in cosmic time
- **Within the kalpa:** the 12th of 14 manvantaras of the present [Śveta-Varāha Kalpa](../) — i.e. day 1 of the 51st year of [Brahmā](../00-time-and-cosmology/) (see the [cosmic-time-hierarchy](../00-time-and-cosmology/)). It begins only after the 8th–11th future manvantaras have run; counting from now (mid-Vaivasvata), it lies roughly 4½ full manvantaras — on the order of **1.4 billion human years** — in the future, plus the remainder of the present age.
- **Internal structure:** like every manvantara it comprises **71 mahāyugas** (each = 4,320,000 years) plus a connecting *sandhyā* of one Kṛta-yuga's length, totalling ~306,720,000 years, ruled throughout by Manu Rudra-Sāvarṇi with Indra Ṛtadhāma and the avatāra Svadhāman sustaining dharma.

## Notes & cautions
- Spellings vary by recension/translation: Indra **Ṛtadhāma vs Ṛtudhāma**; avatāra **Sudhāman vs Svadhāman**; sage **Tapomūrti** stable but the seventh-sage roster differs (Viṣṇu Purāṇa's *tapas*-heptad vs Bhāgavata's "Tapomūrti, Tapasvī, Agnīdhraka, and others"). Both traditions are catalogued above.
- As a *future* age, **no narrative events, wars, descents, or sage-stories are attested** in the primary texts — only the officer roster. Any "event" attributed to this manvantara in popular sources is, on present evidence, extrapolation rather than canon. [scholarly]
