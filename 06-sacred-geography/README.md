# Sacred Geography — Overview & Index

> Cosmic address: [The Hindu Timeline](../README.md) → **Sacred Geography**. Where the divine is mapped onto the earth — the pilgrimage layer of the tradition, complementing the time hierarchy in [00-time-and-cosmology](../00-time-and-cosmology/README.md).

This area indexes the **tirtha-kshetra** network: the physical sites where, in Hindu thought, the eternal touches the earthly. It is one of the deepest sections of the corpus — **85 child files** detail individual tīrthas, kṣetras, rivers, mountains, and whole pilgrimage circuits, each with its sthala-purāṇa legends and sources.

- **Tirtha (crossing-place)** - A *tīrtha* is a "ford" or junction between worlds that touch and do not touch; literally a river-crossing, extended to any holy site enabling spiritual passage (Rigveda; Mahabharata *Tirtha-yatra parva*; Skanda Purana *Tirtha-mahatmya*).
- **Kshetra (sacred field)** - A bounded holy precinct/zone (e.g. Kurukshetra, Purushottama-kshetra/Puri, Kashi); each carries a *sthala-purana* / *mahatmya* praising its power (Padma & Skanda Puranas).
- **Bharata as punya-bhumi** - The Indian subcontinent is held to be the "land of merit" (*karma-bhumi*/*punya-bhumi*) where dharma and pilgrimage are uniquely possible (Vishnu Purana 2; Bhagavata Purana 5).
- **Mount Meru — cosmic axis** - The mythic golden world-axis (*axis mundi*) at the center of Jambudvipa, around which the lokas are arranged; a cosmographic, not terrestrial, mountain (see [00 cosmography](../00-time-and-cosmology/README.md); Bhagavata & Vishnu Puranas).
  - *Variant (Puranic):* Meru is sometimes identified with / projected onto the Himalaya; pilgrimage texts treat earthly peaks as its reflections.
- **Mount Kailash — Shiva's abode** - The earthly Himalayan/Tibetan peak revered as Shiva and Parvati's dwelling, often equated with Meru in popular tradition though scripturally distinct (Shiva & Linga Puranas; see [shiva-myth-cycle](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md)).
- **Sthala-mahatmya source layer** - Site-glorification literature concentrated in the **Skanda Purana** (largest Purana, encyclopedic Shaiva pilgrimage handbook) and **Padma Purana**, with material across Vayu, Kurma, Brahma, Bhavishya, and Narada Puranas (see [smriti-puranas](../90-literature-corpus/smriti-puranas.md)).
