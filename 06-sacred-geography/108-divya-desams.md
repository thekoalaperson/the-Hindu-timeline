# The 108 Divya Desams — Vishnu Abodes of the Alvars

> The 108 *Divya Desams* ("divine/sacred places") are the holy abodes of **Vishnu** (and his consort **Lakshmi**/Thayar) glorified in the *mangalasasanam* (auspicious benediction) of the twelve **[Alvar](../07-acharyas-and-sampradayas/alvars-and-nayanars.md)** poet-saints, collected in the **Naalayira Divya Prabandham** (4,000 Tamil verses). They form the master pilgrimage-grid of the **Sri Vaishnava / Tamil Vaishnava** tradition. A temple is a Divya Desam *only* if at least one Alvar sang it; later temples not in the Prabandham are demoted to the secondary tier of *Abhimana Sthalams*.

The number **108** (the auspicious *ashtottara-shatam*) is canonical, though the surviving Prabandham verses name a slightly fluctuating set; tradition fixes the roster at 108 by counting two **celestial** abodes alongside the 106 earthly ones. Of the 106 earthly: **84 in Tamil Nadu, 11 in Kerala, 4 in Uttar Pradesh, 3 in Uttarakhand, 2 in Andhra Pradesh, 1 in Gujarat, and 1 in Nepal**. The traditional classification, however, is by **Naadu** (ancient Tamil country-divisions), not modern states.

## The Canonical Naadu Groupings (count)

The standard Sri Vaishnava enumeration (followed by *divyadesam.com*, *prapatti.com*, and Wikipedia) groups the 108 as:

- **Chola Nadu — 40** (the Kaveri delta heartland; the densest cluster)
- **Pandya Nadu — 18** (the far south: Madurai, Tirunelveli, Ramanathapuram)
- **Thondai Nadu / Tondaimandalam — 22** (the Pallava north: Kanchipuram, Chennai, Mahabalipuram)
- **Malai Nadu / Chera Nadu — 13** (the hill/west country: Kerala + 2 Tamil borderlands)
- **Nadu Nadu — 2** (the in-between country: the Cuddalore/Tindivanam belt)
- **Vada Nadu / North — 11** (north India + Tirumala/Ahobilam + Dwaraka + Nepal)
- **Vinnulaga / Celestial — 2** (Tiruparkadal and Vaikuntha — beyond earth)

*Total = 40 + 18 + 22 + 13 + 2 + 11 + 2 = 108.* [Counting variants below.]

## The Two Celestial (Vinnulaga) Divya Desams

These two are sung by the Alvars but lie *outside the earthly plane*; they anchor the entire grid in Vaishnava cosmology. See [Vaikuntha & cosmic abodes](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md).

- **Tiruparkadal (Ksheera Sagara / Vyuha)** — the **Ocean of Milk**, on which Vishnu reclines as **Kshirabdhinatha** on **Adishesha**, attended by **Kadalmagal Nachiyar / Lakshmi**. This is the *Vyuha* (emanation) state of the Lord and the cosmic source of the Dashavatara descents (cf. [Samudra Manthana](../04-deep-dives/samudra-manthana.md)). Sung by all/several Alvars as the heavenly counterpart of earthly reclining shrines.
- **Tirupparamapadam (Vaikuntha / Paramapadam / Para)** — the supreme, eternal, deathless abode (*nitya-vibhuti*); Vishnu as **Paramapada Nathan** with **Periya Piratti (Sri/Lakshmi)**, seated on Adishesha under the Vaikuntha vimana. The *Para* (transcendent) state and the soul's final goal (*moksha*) in Sri Vaishnava theology. The whole pilgrimage is read as a rehearsal of the journey to this place.

## Chola Nadu — 40 (the Kaveri Delta Heartland)

Densest cluster; centred on Srirangam. Format: **place** — *deity (posture)* · Thayar · vimana / tirtha · (Alvar/note).

- **Srirangam (Tiruvarangam)** — **Ranganatha / Nam Perumal** (reclining on Adishesha) · **Ranganayaki** · *Pranavakara vimana* / Chandra-pushkarini. The **first and foremost** Divya Desam, called **Bhuloka Vaikuntham** ("Vaikuntha on earth"); the only shrine sung by **all twelve Alvars** (also the *charama* resting place of the Sri Vaishnava acharyas). Largest functioning Hindu temple complex; see [temple networks](./temple-networks-and-circuits.md).
- **Tirukkozhi (Uraiyur)** — Azhagiya Manavalan (standing) · Komalavalli (born here as a Chola princess) · Kalyana vimana.
- **Tirukkarambanur (Uttamar Kovil)** — Purushottaman (standing) · Poornavalli · Udyoga vimana.
- **Tiruvellarai** — Pundarikakshan (standing) · Shenbagavalli / Pankajavalli · Vimalakriti vimana.
- **Tiru Anbil** — Vadivazhagiya Nambi (reclining) · Azhagiyavalli · Taraka vimana.
- **Tirupper Nagar (Koviladi)** — Appakkudathan (reclining) · Indra Devi · Indra vimana.
- **Tiru Thanjaimamani Koil (Thanjavur)** — Neelamegha Perumal (seated) · Senkamalavalli · Soundarya vimana.
- **Tirukkandiyur** — Hara-Sapa-Vimochana Perumal (standing; "freed Shiva of Brahma-slaying curse") · Kamalavalli.
- **Tirukkoodaloor** — Vaiyam-Katha (Aaduthurai) Perumal (standing) · Padmasani.
- **Tirukkavithalam (Kabisthalam)** — Gajendra Varadan (reclining) · Ramamanivalli — the **Gajendra-moksha** sthala (cf. elephant-king saved from the crocodile).
- **Tirupullam Bhoothankudi** — Valvil Raman (reclining) · Hemambujavalli — Jatayu tirtha; **Ramayana** site of Jatayu's cremation.
- **Tiru Aadhanoor** — Aandalakkum Ayan (reclining) · Ranganayaki · Pranava vimana.
- **Tirukkudanthai (Kumbakonam, Sarangapani)** — **Aaravamudhan / Sarangapani** (reclining, *Uthana-sayanam*) · Komalavalli · Vaideeka vimana — one of the Pancharanga kshetras.
- **Tiru Vinnagar (Oppiliappan, Thirunageswaram)** — Oppiliappan (standing; "served food without salt") · Bhumi Devi · Vishnu vimana.
- **Tirunaraiyur (Nachiyar Koil)** — Tirunaraiyur Nambi (kalyana kolam) · **Vanjulavalli** · Srinivasa-Hema vimana — see **Tirunaraiyur counting variant** below.
- **Tirucherai (Saranathan)** — Saranathan (standing) · Saranayaki · Sara vimana.
- **Tirukkannamangai** — Bhaktavatsala Perumal (standing) · Abhishekavalli.
- **Tirunandhipura Vinnagaram (Nathan Koil)** — Jagannathan (seated) · Shenbagavalli.
- **Tiruvelliyankudi** — Kola-valvil Raman (reclining) · Maragathavalli.
- **Tiru Indaloor (Mayiladuthurai)** — Parimala Ranganathar (reclining) · Parimala Ranganayaki.
- **Tiruvazhundoor (Therazhundur)** — Devadirajan / Aamaruviappan (standing) · Senkamalavalli · Garuda vimana — birthplace of poet Kamban.
- **Tiru Sirupuliyur** — Arulmakadal / Krupasamudra Perumal (reclining) · Tirumamagal Nachiyar.
- **Tirukkannapuram** — Sowrirajan / Neelamegha Perumal (standing) · Kannapura Nayaki — an *ashta-naga* / *pancha-Krishna* sthala.
- **Tirunagai (Nagapattinam)** — Soundararajan / Neelamegha Perumal (standing) · Soundaryavalli.
- **Tirukkannankudi** — Loganathan / Shyamala Meni Perumal (standing) · Aravindavalli (Loganayaki).
- **Tiru Thalaichanga Nanmadiyam** — Nanmadiya Perumal (standing) · Talaichanga Nachiyar · Chandra vimana.
- **Kazhicheerama Vinnagaram (Sirkazhi)** — **Trivikraman / Thadalan** (Vamana measuring the worlds) · Lokanayaki — birthplace of Tirumangai Alvar's region.
- **Tiruvellakkulam (Annan Kovil)** — Srinivasan (standing) · Alarmel Mangai.
- **Tiru Devanar Thogai** — Deivanayakan (standing) · Kadalmagal Nachiyar.
- **Tiruvali–Tirunagari** — Lakshmi Narasimhar / Vayalali Manavalan (seated) · Amritavalli — the **home soil of Tirumangai Alvar**, who sang the most Divya Desams.

- **Tiru Sempon Sei Kovil** — Perarulalan (standing) · Alli-Maamalar Nachiyar · Kanaka vimana.
- **Tiru Vann Purushottamam** — Purushottaman (standing) · Purushottama Nayaki — Tirupparkadal tirtha.
- **Tiru Vaikuntha Vinnagaram (Oppiliappan-area, Sirkazhi belt)** — Vaikuntanathan (the famed *three-tier* shrine: seated/reclining/standing) · Vaikunthavalli.

*The 40 span the Tiruchirappalli, Thanjavur–Kumbakonam, Mayiladuthurai, and Sirkazhi sub-clusters; some tour-lists shuffle 1-2 between Chola Nadu and Nadu Nadu (see variants).*

## Pandya Nadu — 18 (Madurai, Tirunelveli, Ramanathapuram)

Includes the famed **Nava Tirupathi** (nine temples along the Tamraparni river, sung as a unit by **Nammalvar**, who was born here at Azhwarthirunagari).

**The Nava Tirupathi (9):**
- **Tirukkurugur (Azhwarthirunagari)** — Aadhinathar (standing) · Adhinatha Nachiyar — **birthplace of Nammalvar** (Maran/Satakopan), foremost of the Alvars.
- **Tirukkoloor** — Vaithamanidhi Perumal (standing) · Kumudavalli — birthplace of Madhurakavi Alvar.
- **Tirupperai (Thenthiruperai)** — Magaranedunkuzhaikathar (standing) · Kuzhaikkadu Valli.
- **Tiruvaikuntham (Srivaikuntam)** — Vaikuntanathan / Kallapiran (standing) · Vaikunthavalli.
- **Tiruvaragunamangai (Natham/Nanguneri belt)** — Vijayaasana Perumal (seated) · Varagunavalli.
- **Tirukkulandai (Perungulam)** — Mayakkoothan (reclining) · Alamelmangai.
- **Tiruppulingudi** — Kaichinavendan (reclining) · Malarmagal Nachiyar.
- **Tirutholaivillimangalam (Irattai Tirupathi)** — Devapiran & Aravinda Lochanar (two shrines) · Karunthadankanni.
- **Tiruvaramangai (Nanguneri)** — Vanamamalai / Deivanayaga Perumal (reclining) · Sirumamagal / Varamangai Nachiyar.

**The other 9 (Madurai–Srivilliputhur–Ramanathapuram):**
- **Tirukkurungudi** — Nindra/Irundha/Kidandha Nambi (the "Vaishnava Nambi," standing) · Kurungudivalli Nachiyar.
- **Tiruvanparisaram (Thiruppadi, Kanyakumari belt)** — Tirukkuralappan (standing) · Kamalavalli — a Vamana sthala.
- **Tirukkoodal (Koodal Azhagar, Madurai)** — Koodal Azhagar (seated/standing/reclining tri-form) · Madhuravalli.
- **Tirumaliruncholai (Azhagar Kovil)** — **Kallazhagar / Sundararaja** (standing) · Sundaravalli — the great Madurai hill-shrine; sung by **Periyalvar and Andal**.
- **Tiru Mohur** — Kalamegha Perumal (standing) · Mohanavalli.
- **Tirukkoshtiyur** — Uragamellanayan / Sowmya Narayana (reclining) · Tirumamagal — where Ramanuja learned the *charama sloka* secret.
- **Tirumeyyam** — Satyagiri Natha / Satyamoorthy (reclining) · Uyyavanda Nachiyar.
- **Tirupullani (Adi Jagannatha)** — Kalyana Jagannathar (reclining) · Padmasani — Ramayana **Sethu** site where Rama did *darbha-sayanam*.
- **Tiruthangal (near Sivakasi) & Srivilliputhur** — **Srivilliputhur (Vatapatrasayi)**: Vatapatrasayi (reclining on banyan leaf) · **Andal/Goda** & Soodikkodutha Sudarkodi — **birthplace of Andal and Periyalvar**; origin of the *Tiruppavai*. (Thiruthangal: Nindra Narayana Perumal · Senganmal Nachiyar.)

## Thondai Nadu / Tondaimandalam — 22 (Kanchipuram, Chennai, Mahabalipuram)

The Pallava north; centred on **Kanchipuram**, which alone holds ~14-15 Divya Desams.

**Kanchipuram cluster (~15):**
- **Tirukkachi (Attigiri, Varadaraja Perumal)** — **Varadaraja / Devarajan** (standing on Hastagiri) · Perundevi Thayar · Punyakoti vimana — the supreme Kanchi shrine; sung by many Alvars.
- **Ashtabhujakaram** — Aadikesava (eight-armed, standing) · Alarmel Mangai.
- **Tiru Vekka (Yathothkari)** — Yathothkari / Sonna-vannam-seitha Perumal (reclining, head-to-south) · Komalavalli — Vegasetu legend.
- **Tiru Velukkai** — Azhagiya Singar (Narasimha, standing) · Amritavalli.
- **Tiruthanka (Deepaprakasar)** — Deepaprakasar (standing) · Maragathavalli — linked to Vedanta Desika.
- **Tirukkalvanur** — Aadi Varaha Perumal (standing) · Anjilai Valli (within Kamakshi temple).
- **Tiru Ooragam** — Ulagalantha Perumal (Trivikrama, foot raised) · Amudavalli.
- **Tiru Neeragam** — Jagadeeswarar · Nilamangai Valli.
- **Tiru Kaaragam** — Karunakara Perumal (standing) · Padmamani.
- **Tirukkaar Vanam** — Kalvar / Tirukkarvanar (standing) · Kamalavalli.
- **Tiruparameswara Vinnagaram (Vaikunta Perumal)** — Vaikunta Nathan (three-tier: seated/reclining/standing) · Vaikunthavalli — Pallava-era structural temple.
- **Tiru Pavala Vannam** — Pavalavannar (standing) & Pachaivannar · Pavalavalli.
- **Tiru Nilathingal Thundam** — Nilathingal Thundathan (standing) · same-named Thayar — inside the **Ekambareswarar (Shiva)** temple; a rare Hari-Hara co-sthala.
- **Tiru Paadagam** — Pandava Dootha Perumal (huge seated form, ~25 ft; Krishna as Pandavas' envoy) · Rukmini & Satyabhama.
- **Tiruputkuzhi** — Vijayaraghava Perumal (reclining) · Maragathavalli — Jatayu tirtha (Ramayana).

**Chennai–Mahabalipuram–Arakkonam cluster (~7):**
- **Tiruvallikkeni (Triplicane, Chennai)** — **Parthasarathy** (standing, battle-scarred face from Mahabharata) · Vedavalli · with shrines for the five Pancharatra forms.
- **Tiruneermalai** — Neervanna Perumal (standing) & Ranganatha (reclining, hill) · Animamalar Mangai.
- **Tiruvidanthai (Tiruvedanthai)** — Nithya Kalyana Perumal (standing; "ever-marrying") · Komalavalli.
- **Tirukkadalmalai (Mahabalipuram)** — Sthala Sayana Perumal (reclining on ground, *without Adishesha*) · Nilamangai — the Pallava shore-temple complex.
- **Tirunindravur (Thirunindravur)** — Bhaktavatsala Perumal (standing) · Ennai-Petra-Thayar (Sudavalli).
- **Tiruvallur (Tiruevvuloor)** — Veeraraghava Perumal (reclining) · Kanakavalli — major healing/Salihotra sthala.
- **Tirukkadigai (Sholingur)** — Yoga Narasimha (hilltop, seated) · Amritavalli — the Periya/Chinna malai twin-hill shrine.

## Malai Nadu / Chera Nadu — 13 (Kerala + 2 borderlands)

The "hill country" of the west coast. **11 in Kerala** (the Chera land) plus **2 Tamil borderlands** sometimes counted here. Several sung by **Kulasekhara Alvar** (himself a Chera king). The Pamba-river **Chengannur cluster** holds the *Pancha-Pandava* set.

- **Tiruvananthapuram (Anantha Padmanabhaswamy)** — **Padmanabha** (vast reclining form on Anantha, viewed through three doors) · Sri Devi & Bhu Devi — sung by Nammalvar; the royal Travancore shrine, fabled for its vaults.
- **Tiruvanvandoor** — Paambanaiyappa / Kamalanathan (standing) · Kamalavalli — a Pandava-cluster shrine.
- **Tiruvaranvilai (Aranmula, Parthasarathy)** — Parthasarathy (standing) · Padmasani — Aranmula snake-boat festival.
- **Tirucchenkundroor (Chengannur)** — Imayavarappa Perumal (standing) · Rakthapankajavalli.
- **Tiruppuliyoor (Chengannur, Kuttanad)** — Mayapiran (standing) · Porkodi Nachiyar.
- **Tiruvalvazh (Thiruvalla)** — Kolapiran / Sri Vallabha (standing) · Selva Tirukozhundu / Vatsalyavalli.
- **Tirukkadithanam (Changanassery)** — Adbhuta Narayana (standing) · Karpagavalli.
- **Tirukkatkarai (near Ernakulam)** — Katkarai Appan / Vamana (standing) · Vatsalyavalli — a Vamana sthala.
- **Tirumoozhikkalam (near Cochin airport)** — Moozhikkalathan / Sri Krishna (standing) · Madhuraveni.
- **Tiru Naavaay (Bharatappuzha, Malappuram)** — Naavaay Mukundan (standing) · Malarmangai — the Mamankam-festival river site.
- **Tiruvithuvakkodu (Palakkad/Thrissur belt)** — Uyyavantha Perumal / Abhayapradan (standing) · Vithuvakkottuvalli — sung by Kulasekhara Alvar (the "Vithuvakkodu" decade).
- **Thirukkurungudi-area / Tiruvanparisaram borderlands** — in some Malai Nadu reckonings the two southern Tamil temples (Tiruvanparisaram, Tirukkurungudi) are counted toward the 13 rather than Pandya Nadu — see variants. (Most lists keep Kerala at 11 + 2 Tamil to reach 13.)

## Nadu Nadu — 2 (the In-Between Country)

The small "middle country" belt (Cuddalore/Villupuram); some lists fold these into Chola Nadu or Thondai Nadu, but the classical reckoning isolates them.

- **Tiruchitrakootam (Chidambaram, Govindaraja)** — Govindaraja Perumal (reclining) · Pundarikavalli — Vishnu shrine within the great Nataraja (Shiva) temple; sung by Kulasekhara and Tirumangai.
- **Tirukkovilur** — Ayan / Ulagalanda Perumal (Trivikrama, standing) · Poongkoval Nachiyar — where the first three (*Mudhal*) Alvars — **Poigai, Bhutha, Pey** — met in a storm-narrowed corridor and the *Iyarpa* Prabandham began. (The third Nadu Nadu node in some lists is **Tiruvaheendrapuram/Cuddalore** — Devanathan · Hemambujavalli — usually placed in Nadu Nadu or Chola Nadu depending on source.)

*Note: the strict "Nadu Nadu = 2" count varies by source between Chidambaram, Tirukkovilur, and Tiruvaheendrapuram; see variants.*

## Vada Nadu / North — 11 (North India + AP + Gujarat + Nepal)

The "northern country," reaching from Andhra to the Himalayas and Nepal. These are the Alvars' *imagined/sung* northern abodes (most Alvars never travelled there physically).

- **Tirumala–Tirupati (Venkatachalapathy/Srinivasa)** — **Venkateswara / Srinivasa** (standing on the seven hills) · **Alamelu Manga / Padmavathi** (at Tiruchanur) — the most-visited temple on earth; sung by all the early Alvars; reckoned as the threshold between Vada and Thondai Nadu.
- **Tiru Singavel Kundram (Ahobilam, AP)** — **Nava Narasimha** (nine Narasimha shrines on the hill) · Amritavalli / Chenchu Lakshmi — the prime **Narasimha** kshetra (cf. [Narasimha avatar](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md)); sung by Tirumangai Alvar.
- **Tiru Dwaraka (Gujarat)** — **Kalyana Narayana / Dwarakadhish** (standing) · Rukmini/Kalyana Nachiyar — Krishna's ocean-city capital (cf. [Krishna's life](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md)).
- **Tiru Ayodhya (UP)** — **Rama** (standing/seated) · Sita — birthplace and capital of Rama (cf. [Ramayana](../04-deep-dives/ramayana/README.md)).
- **Tiru Naimisaranyam (Naimisha, UP)** — Devaraja Perumal / Sri Hari (standing) · Harilakshmi / Pundarikavalli — the forest where the **Puranas were recited** to the sages by Suta (cf. [Puranas corpus](../90-literature-corpus/smriti-puranas.md)).
- **Tiruvaaipadi (Gokul/Ayarpadi, UP)** — Navamohana Krishna / Navaneetha Krishna (the butter-thief child) · Rukmini — Krishna's cowherd childhood ground.
- **Tiru Vadamathura (Mathura, UP)** — Govardhanesa / Sri Krishna (standing) · Satyabhama — **Krishna's birthplace**; the Mathura/Govardhan/Gokul nodes together fill UP's four.
- **Tirukkandam (Devaprayag, Uttarakhand)** — Neelamegha / Purushottama Perumal (standing) · Pundarikavalli — at the Alaknanda–Bhagirathi confluence.
- **Tiruppiridhi (Joshimath / Nandaprayag belt, Uttarakhand)** — Paramapurusha Perumal (seated) · Parimala Valli.
- **Tiruvadari Ashramam (Badrinath, Uttarakhand)** — **Badri Narayana** (seated, meditating; the Nara-Narayana penance) · Aravindavalli — the northernmost earthly Divya Desam; overlaps the [Char Dham](./char-dham-and-chota-char-dham.md).
- **Tiru Salagramam (Muktinath, Mustang, Nepal)** — **Sri Moorthy / Srimoorthy Perumal** (standing) · Sri Devi (Sridevi Nachiyar) — the only Divya Desam **outside India**, at ~3,800 m by the Gandaki/Kali-Gandaki river, source of the **shaligram** (ammonite Vishnu-stones) and sacred *saligrama-shila* worship.

*That is exactly 11 for Vada Nadu (Tirumala, Ahobilam, Dwaraka, Ayodhya, Naimisaranyam, Gokul, Mathura, Devaprayag, Joshimath, Badrinath, Muktinath).*

## Counting Variants & Disputed Nodes

The "108" is fixed, but *which* temple sits where — and how borderline shrines are counted — shifts between authorities. [disputed]

- **Tirunaraiyur (Nachiyar Koil) "eleven" variant** [disputed] — A traditional reckoning holds that the single sthala of **Tirunaraiyur** (Nachiyar Koil) was sung in a way that counts as **eleven Divya Desams in one place**, or alternatively that **Tirunaavaay** / a cluster compresses several names. Most modern lists resolve this by counting Tirunaraiyur as **one**, which is why the surviving Prabandham place-names number slightly differently from a naive 108 — the tradition pads/merges to reach the auspicious 108. (Flagged as an open-ended roster question; *prapatti.com* and *divyadesam.com* both note discrepancies in the raw verse-count vs. the canonical 108.)
- **Nava Tirupathi as 9** — the nine Tamraparni temples are individually Divya Desams but pilgrimaged as one *navagraha*-linked unit; no double-counting.
- **Irattai Tirupathi (Tholaivillimangalam)** — two shrines (Devapiran, Aravinda Lochanar) at one place, counted as **one** Divya Desam.
- **Nadu Nadu drift** [disputed] — Chidambaram (Govindaraja), Tirukkovilur, and Tiruvaheendrapuram are variously assigned to Nadu Nadu vs. Chola/Thondai Nadu across sources; the regional sub-totals stay 40/18/22/13/2/11/2 only under the standard *divyadesam.com* scheme.
- **Tirumala's region** [disputed] — Tirupati is counted under **Vada Nadu** in the 11-list above, but some schemes place it at the head of **Thondai Nadu** (its old frontier); totals are adjusted accordingly.
- **Malai Nadu 11-vs-13** — Kerala proper has 11; the figure 13 includes the two southernmost Tamil borderland temples (Tiruvanparisaram, Tirukkurungudi) that other schemes return to Pandya Nadu.

## Mangalasasanam: Which Alvars Sang (roster)

Each Divya Desam qualifies by Alvar benediction; the spread is uneven. [Alvars overview](../07-acharyas-and-sampradayas/alvars-and-nayanars.md).

- **Nammalvar (Satakopan)** — sang the most, **~37 Divya Desams** (and the entire grid in essence via the *Tiruvaymozhi*).
- **Tirumangai Alvar** — sang the largest *number of temples* overall (the great temple-pilgrim Alvar; reached ~86 sthalas in tradition), including most of the northern and Chola nodes.
- **Periyalvar** — ~18 Divya Desams; **Andal (Goda)** — ~11 (Srivilliputhur, Srirangam, Tirumala, Mathura/Gokul, Tirumaliruncholai, etc.).
- **Kulasekhara Alvar** — ~9 (notably the Kerala and northern Rama/Krishna sthalas, being a Chera king).
- **The first three (Mudhal) Alvars** — Poigai, Bhutha, Pey — concentrated on Tirukkovilur, Tirumala, Kanchi, Srirangam.
- **Srirangam alone** is sung by **all twelve**.

## Abhimana Sthalams (secondary tier)

Vishnu temples held in great esteem and traditionally associated with the Alvars/acharyas but **not** formally sung as Divya Desams in the surviving Prabandham; they are a separate honorific class (not part of the 108). [folk]/[late text]

- **Examples** — Tiruvattaru (Adikesava, Kanyakumari), Tirukkurungudi-area sub-shrines, Thiruvellarai annexes, Nanjil-Nadu temples, Pandharpur (Vitthala), and several Kanchi/Chennai shrines. Rosters of Abhimana Sthalams are **not fixed** and vary widely by sampradaya and modern compilers — flagged as an open-ended list.
- **Function** — they extend the pilgrimage network without altering the canonical 108; some later Sri Vaishnava lineages promote favoured local temples into this tier.
