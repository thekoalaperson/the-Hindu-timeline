# Śrīmuṣṇam — The Bhū-Varāha Kṣetra

> *The foremost southern abode of the **Boar** avatāra, and one of the eight **self-manifest** (svayam-vyakta) Viṣṇu shrines — the Bhū-Varāha "twin" of Tirumala. Parent: [Sacred Geography](README.md) · Up to: [Master Index](../README.md) · Related: [Aṣṭa Svayam-vyakta Viṣṇu Kṣetras](ashta-svayamvyakta-vishnu-kshetras.md) · [Avatāra Kṣetras — Śrīkūrmam & Śrīmuṣṇam](avatara-kshetras-srikurmam-srimushnam.md) · [Tirumala–Tirupati](tirumala-tirupati-venkateshwara.md) · [108 Divya Deśams](108-divya-desams.md) · [Daśāvatāra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md)*

**Śrīmuṣṇam** (Cuddalore district, Tamil Nadu) is the chief southern **Varāha-kṣetra** and the principal pilgrimage seat of the **Bhū-Varāha** ("Earth-Boar") avatāra of [Viṣṇu](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) — the Boar who lifted the sunken Earth-goddess on his tusk. With **[Tirumala](tirumala-tirupati-venkateshwara.md)** (where Varāha holds prior right to the hill) it forms the principal **Varāha-avatāra pilgrimage pair** of the South. It is counted among the **[Aṣṭa svayam-vyakta kṣetras](ashta-svayamvyakta-vishnu-kshetras.md)** — the eight shrines whose mūrtis are held to have *arisen on their own* rather than been carved. This file details the Śrīmuṣṇam *sthala-purāṇa* (the white-boar tank / Śveta-Varāha and the **Nityakalyāṇa** legend) and its place in the self-manifest class; for the paired Kūrma abode see the **[Avatāra Kṣetras](avatara-kshetras-srikurmam-srimushnam.md)** file.

---

## I. The deity and the kṣetra

- **Śrī Bhū-Varāha-svāmī** — the presiding deity: Viṣṇu as the **Boar (Varāha)**, third of the [Daśāvatāra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md), shown **standing with Bhū-devī (the Earth-goddess) seated on his left thigh/elbow** — the cosmic instant of the Earth raised from the *pralaya*-waters. (Wikipedia, *Bhuvarahaswamy Temple, Srimushnam*; Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya)
- **The Hiraṇyākṣa rescue** — the governing avatāra-myth: the demon **Hiraṇyākṣa** rolls the Earth into the cosmic ocean; Viṣṇu becomes the Boar, slays him, and **lifts Bhū-devī on his tusk** back to her place — the deed the *kṣetra* localizes and makes permanent here. (Varāha Purāṇa; *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 3.13–19)
- **"Bhū-Varāha," not Yajña-Varāha** — Śrīmuṣṇam venerates specifically the **Bhū-Varāha** (Earth-uplifting Boar with the goddess), distinct from the **Yajña-Varāha** (Boar as embodiment of sacrifice) emphasised in some other Varāha shrines. (temple tradition) [regional]
- **A constant member of the eight** — among the variably-listed **[Aṣṭa svayam-vyakta](ashta-svayamvyakta-vishnu-kshetras.md)** (Tirumala, Śrīraṅgam, Śrīmuṣṇam, Naimiṣāraṇya, Puṣkara, Badarī, Śālagrāma, Toṭādri/Vānamāmalai), Śrīmuṣṇam appears in **nearly every recension** of the list — it is one of the most stable entries. (Vaiṣṇava tradition) [traditional]
  - *Variant (membership flux):* lists vary — some swap Puṣkara, Naimiṣāraṇya or Toṭādri for other shrines — but Tirumala, Śrīraṅgam and Śrīmuṣṇam are the fixed core. [disputed]

## II. The sthala-purāṇa (Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya)

The site has its own **Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya**, traditionally transmitted within the **[Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa](../90-literature-corpus/smriti-puranas.md)** and drawing on the **Varāha Purāṇa**'s Varāha-kṣetra glorification.

- **The Boar stays after the rescue** — the *māhātmya*'s central claim: after lifting the Earth, Varāha **did not vanish** but remained *permanently visible* at Śrīmuṣṇam for the welfare of beings — making the *kṣetra* the standing residence of the avatāra. (Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa, Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya)
  - *Variant (Bhū-devī's petition):* in some tellings the Earth-goddess herself **asks Varāha to stay manifest** at this spot, grounding the perpetual-presence claim. [regional]
- **Etymology — "the snatched place"** — *Śrī-muṣṇam* is glossed from the root *muṣ-* ("to snatch / carry off"): the place where Varāha **snatched the Earth** from the demon — or, devotionally, the place that "snatches away" the pilgrim's sins. (Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya) [folk etymology]
- **The Śveta-Varāha kalpa anchor** — the deed is sited in the present **[Śveta-Varāha kalpa](../00-time-and-cosmology/README.md)** ("White-Boar aeon"), the *kalpa* named for this very avatāra — fixing Śrīmuṣṇam on the cosmogonic clock. (Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya) [traditional]

### The white-boar tank — Śveta-Varāha / the tank legend

- **The Nitya-puṣkariṇī tank** — the temple's sacred tank, **Nitya-puṣkariṇī** ("ever-flowing pool"), is held to **never run dry** and to be fed by all holy *tīrthas*; bathing here is the prescribed first act of the pilgrim and the centre of the *māhātmya*'s merit-claims. (Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya; temple tradition) [regional]
- **The white-boar / Śveta-Varāha manifestation** — the *svayam-vyakta* localization-myth: the self-revealed Boar-icon is associated with the appearance of a **white boar (Śveta-Varāha)** at the tank, by which Viṣṇu **made himself manifest** at the spot — the white boar being the emblem of the current *kalpa*. (Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya; temple tradition) [folk]
  - *Variant (sage's tapas):* as at sister self-manifest shrines, a sage's or king's *tapas* is sometimes given as the occasion that **drew the icon to reveal itself**; the standard *svayam-vyakta* framing. [folk]
  - *Variant (lost-and-refound):* the usual self-manifest motif — the icon **buried / hidden and later re-discovered**, worship re-established by a later devotee — also attaches to Śrīmuṣṇam. [folk]

### Nityakalyāṇa — the "eternal wedding"

- **Nityakalyāṇa-perumāḷ** — Śrīmuṣṇam's distinctive cult-name: the Lord here is invoked as **Nityakalyāṇa**, "(of the) **eternal / perpetual auspiciousness (or wedding)**" — the deity in whom *kalyāṇa* (marital weal, auspicious fortune) is permanently present. (temple tradition) [regional]
- **The vow / boon of perpetual auspiciousness** — devotees petition the Lord here especially for **marriage and auspicious household fortune**, the *sthala* being held to confer *nitya-kalyāṇa* — wedded weal that never wanes — on its worshippers. (temple tradition; sthala usage) [folk]
  - *Variant (Bhū-devī as eternal bride):* the name is also read through **Bhū-devī's perpetual presence on Varāha's body** — the avatāra eternally "wedded" to the rescued Earth — making the rescue itself the *nitya-kalyāṇa*. [regional]

## III. The two clocks — myth-time vs. monument-time

- **The traditional (yuga / kalpa) clock** — the Boar-icon is **timeless / svayam-vyakta**, manifest in the **Śveta-Varāha kalpa** at the moment of the cosmic Earth-rescue, and continuously present since. [traditional]
- **The historical (inscriptional) clock** — the **standing monumental temple** is a **Vijayanagara–Nāyaka foundation** (largely 16th c. CE), built and endowed under Vijayanagara authority and the Tanjore / Senji (Gingee) Nāyakas; **Acyutarāya** and **Kṛṣṇappa Nāyaka** figure in its patronage tradition, and it is noted for an ornate carved **stone *ratha*-maṇḍapa (chariot hall)**. (Wikipedia, *Bhuvarahaswamy Temple, Srimushnam*; *South Indian Inscriptions*; ASI) [scholarly]
- **The māhātmya as a late layer** — academically the **Śrīmuṣṇa-māhātmya** is a **[late text]** glorification-layer over older Varāha-devotion; continuous attested worship runs from the **Vijayanagara–Nāyaka era into the present [Kali-yuga](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md) historical period**, even as the *kṣetra* claims kalpa-antiquity. [scholarly]

## IV. Place in the network

- **The Bhū-Varāha pole of the Varāha pilgrimage** — Śrīmuṣṇam is the **southern Bhū-Varāha seat**; with **[Tirumala](tirumala-tirupati-venkateshwara.md)** (where the pilgrim takes Varāha-svāmī's *darśana* first, by the Boar's prior claim to the hill) it anchors the Varāha-avatāra circuit, alongside the Varāha–Narasiṃha fusion at **[Siṃhācalam](simhachalam-varaha-narasimha.md)**. [scholarly]
- **Self-manifest, not Āḻvār-sung** — Śrīmuṣṇam belongs to the **[svayam-vyakta](ashta-svayamvyakta-vishnu-kshetras.md)** tier, **not** the canonical **[108 Divya Deśams](108-divya-desams.md)** (no Āḻvār *maṅgalāśāsanam* sings it); it ranks as an **Abhimāna-sthalam** — a much-revered shrine just outside the 108. [scholarly]
- **The avatāra-abode roster** — paired with **Śrīkūrmam** (the lone major Kūrma temple), Śrīmuṣṇam supplies one of the two thinnest avatāra-abodes; see the **[Avatāra Kṣetras](avatara-kshetras-srikurmam-srimushnam.md)** file for the joint treatment. [scholarly]
