# Ācāryas & Sampradāyas — Index

> Section root: `07-acharyas-and-sampradayas/` — the great **teachers (ācāryas)** and the **sectarian lineages / monastic orders (sampradāyas, panthas, mathas)** that carry Sanātana Dharma through the **historical** stretch of the Kali-Yuga. Up-link: repo [`../README.md`](../README.md).
> Where [`90-literature-corpus/`](../90-literature-corpus/) asks *"which text?"* and [`01-…/recorded-history.md`](../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/recorded-history.md) asks *"what is datable?"*, this section asks **"who transmitted it, and through which living institution?"**

This is the human-institutional layer. Nearly everything here sits in the **first ~1.2% of Kali Yuga** (post-3102 BCE traditional / mostly 1st millennium CE onward academically) — the bhakti, Vedānta, and tantric reform movements that reorganized Hindu practice into named lineages with founders, succession-seats (*pīṭhas* / *mathas*), commentarial canons, and initiation chains (*paramparā*). Dating is given **[traditional]** (matha-succession / hagiographic) vs **[scholarly]** (epigraphic / text-critical) where they diverge — see the [two-clocks method](../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/recorded-history.md). The philosophical scaffolding (the six [Darśanas](../90-literature-corpus/upavedas-vedangas-darshanas.md) and the Vedānta sub-schools) is cataloged in the literature corpus; this section maps those darśanas onto the **orders that embody them**.

## The streams at a glance

Hinduism's sectarian field is conventionally sorted by chosen supreme deity (*iṣṭa-devatā*), cross-cut by Vedānta school. Per repo variant policy, all major attributions are cataloged.

- **Vaiṣṇava** — Viṣṇu / Kṛṣṇa / Rāma as supreme. Bhakti-dominant; organized into **four canonical sampradāyas** (the *cāturvaidya*), each with a founding ācārya and a Vedānta position. (Source: Wikipedia, *Vaishnavism*)
  - *The four (Source: Wikipedia, "Vaishnavism"; Poojn.in, "Four Vaishnava Sampradayas"):*
    - **Śrī Sampradāya** — founder **Rāmānuja** (trad. 1017–1137 CE); school = **Viśiṣṭādvaita** (qualified non-dualism). Rooted in the Tamil [Āḻvārs](../90-literature-corpus/tamil-canon-sangam-and-bhakti.md). [scholarly]
    - **Brahma Sampradāya** — founder **Madhva / Ānandatīrtha** (1238–1317 CE); school = **Dvaita** (dualism); seat-cluster of the Udupi aṣṭa-mathas. [scholarly]
    - **Rudra Sampradāya** — traced to **Viṣṇusvāmī** (14th c.), best known via **Vallabha** (1479–1531 CE); school = **Śuddhādvaita** (pure non-dualism), the Puṣṭimārga. [scholarly]
    - **Kumāra (Nimbārka) Sampradāya** — founder **Nimbārka** (13th c. [disputed], some traditions much earlier); school = **Dvaitādvaita** (dualistic non-dualism). [traditional]
  - *Later Kṛṣṇa-bhakti orders [scholarly]:* **Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism** — **Caitanya Mahāprabhu** (1486–1534 CE), Acintya-bheda-abheda, ritually affiliated to the Madhva line; modern global arm = ISKCON. **Vārkarī** of Maharashtra — Jñāneśvar, Nāmdev (1270–1350), Eknāth, Tukārām (1608–50), Viṭṭhala-centred. **Ekasaraṇa Dharma** — **Śaṅkaradeva** (1449/1483–1568) in Assam. (Source: Wikipedia, *Chaitanya Mahaprabhu*, *Varkari*, *Ekasarana Dharma*)
- **Śaiva** — Śiva as supreme; the most internally diverse stream. (Source: Wikipedia, *Shaivism*)
  - *Major lineages [scholarly]:* **Śaiva Siddhānta** — earliest tantric Śaiva sampradāya, c. 5th c., dualist, Tamil [Tirumuṟai](../90-literature-corpus/tamil-canon-sangam-and-bhakti.md) canon. **Nāyaṉārs** — 63 Tamil poet-saints, 6th–8th c. (Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar; Periya Purāṇam). **Kashmir Śaivism (Trika/Krama)** — non-dual, post-850 CE (Vasugupta, Abhinavagupta). **Vīraśaiva / Liṅgāyat** — **Basava**, 12th c.; Iṣṭaliṅga worship. **Nāth Sampradāya** — Matsyendranāth (9th–10th c.), **Gorakhnāth** (12th–13th c.), haṭha-yoga. (Source: Wikipedia, *Shaivism*, *Kashmir Shaivism*, *Nath*)
- **Śākta** — the Goddess (Devī / Śakti) as supreme; tantra-centred, Śrī Vidyā and Kālī/Tripurasundarī lineages, deeply interwoven with non-dual Śaivism (in Kashmir Śaivism Śakti is internal to Śiva). (Source: Wikipedia, *Shaivism* — Śākta integration)
- **Smārta** — non-sectarian Vedāntic householder tradition; **pañcāyatana / ṣaṇmata** worship (five-or-six deities as equal faces of one *Brahman*). Organized around **Ādi Śaṅkara** and the **Daśanāmī** monastic order. (Source: Grokipedia, *Smarta tradition*; Wikipedia, *Adi Shankara*)
  - *Founder:* **Ādi Śaṅkara** — **Advaita Vedānta**; trad. 788–820 CE, recent scholarship c. 700–750 CE [disputed]; organizer of hundreds of monasteries into the ten-name (*daśanāmī*) system and the four cardinal **āmnāya pīṭhas** (Śṛṅgeri, Purī, Dvārakā, Jyotirmaṭha). (Source: Wikipedia, *Adi Shankara*)

The **Vedānta schools** these orders carry — Advaita, Viśiṣṭādvaita, Dvaita, Dvaitādvaita, Śuddhādvaita, Acintya-bheda-abheda — are the doctrinal differentiators; their place inside the six orthodox darśanas is set out in [Upavedas, Vedāṅgas & the Six Darśanas](../90-literature-corpus/upavedas-vedangas-darshanas.md).
