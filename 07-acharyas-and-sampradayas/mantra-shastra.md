# Mantra-Śāstra — the Tantric Science of Mantra

> Detail node under [`07-acharyas-and-sampradayas/`](README.md) — the **operative technical science** of Tantric mantra: how a mantra is extracted, anatomized, installed on the body, purified, awakened, and deployed. Distinct from the *devotional sound-theology* (why mantras work, the praṇava, the Gāyatrī, *japa* and *nāma*) catalogued in [`../05-concepts-and-dharma/mantra-japa-and-murti-puja.md`](../05-concepts-and-dharma/mantra-japa-and-murti-puja.md); this is the *engineering manual* that underlies it. Up-link: section index [`README.md`](README.md).
> Sits in the historical Kali-Yuga as a textual corpus: the great mantra-handbooks (*Śāradā-tilaka*, *Mantra-mahodadhi*, *Prapañcasāra*) are **c. 11th–16th c. CE** [scholarly], though they claim Āgamic/ṛṣi revelation. Two-clocks method: [`recorded-history.md`](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/recorded-history.md). This is the named science underlying [Śrīvidyā & the Śākta-Tantra lineages](shakta-and-tantra-lineages.md), the [Nāth & Siddha sādhanā](nath-sampradaya-and-the-siddhas.md), and all Āgamic ritual — none of which can be activated without **dīkṣā** (initiation from a guru).

---

## Orientation: what "mantra-śāstra" names

- **Mantra-śāstra** — the technical discipline (*śāstra*) treating mantra as an operable instrument rather than a hymn: its extraction, structure, ritual installation, purification, "awakening," preparatory regimen, and applied uses. The *practical* core of the Āgama/Tantra corpus, as against its *philosophical* core (*śabda-brahman*, *spanda*, *mātṛkā*-cosmogony). It presupposes the sound-theology summarized in the [devotional mantra file](../05-concepts-and-dharma/mantra-japa-and-murti-puja.md). (Source: Śāradā-tilaka Tantra; Goudriaan & Gupta, *Hindu Tantric and Śākta Literature*)
- **The two clocks** — *scripturally*, the science is eternal Śiva-revelation; *historically*, it crystallizes in datable manuals — **Lakṣmaṇadeśika's Śāradā-tilaka** (c. 11th c.), **Mahīdhara's Mantra-mahodadhi** (1588/1589 CE), the **Prapañcasāra** (ascribed to Śaṅkara [disputed]), the **Tantrarāja**, **Meru-tantra**, and the **Lakṣmī-tantra** (a Pāñcarātra Āgama). (Source: Goudriaan & Gupta; Wikipedia, *Mantra-mahodadhi*)

## 1. Mantroddhāra — extracting the mantra from the code

- **Mantroddhāra ("raising-up / extraction of the mantra")** — the deliberately *encrypted* procedure by which a mantra's actual syllables are recovered from a coded description, so that the unintiated cannot simply read a mantra off the page. The manuals never print a powerful mantra plainly; they give a recipe. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Mantra-mahodadhi)
- **Prastāra — the syllable-grids** — tabular arrangements of the *mātṛkā* (the 50/51 Sanskrit phonemes, *a* to *kṣa*) laid out as a grid, with the *bīja* of a mantra specified by **row-and-column coordinates** (e.g. "the letter at the junction of the fire-row and the wind-column, crowned with the *bindu*"). The reader must hold the grid to decode. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi; Bühnemann, *The Worship of Mahāgaṇapati*)
- **Bīja-keys (coded epithets)** — each phoneme and diacritic carries a stock of secret synonyms: *vahni / agni* = **r**, *indu / soma / candra* = the **anusvāra/bindu (ṃ)**, *vāmanetra* / *vāmākṣi* = **ī**, *kāmarāja* = **klīṃ**, *māyā* = **hrīṃ**, *lakṣmī / ramā* = **śrīṃ**, *praṇava* = **oṃ**. A verse stringing these epithets *is* the mantra, in disguise. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Varivasyā-rahasya of Bhāskararāya on the Śrīvidyā)
- **Worked example — the Śrīvidyā pañcadaśākṣarī** — Bhāskararāya's **Varivasyā-rahasya** is essentially a commentary *on the uddhāra* of the fifteen-syllable Lalitā-Tripurasundarī mantra (the three *kūṭas*: *vāgbhava*, *kāmarāja*, *śakti*), decoding each letter from its coded designation. (Source: Bhāskararāya, *Varivasyā-rahasya*) — see [Śrīvidyā lineage](shakta-and-tantra-lineages.md).
  - *Variant (Kādi vs Hādi uddhāra):* the same fifteen syllables are *extracted differently* in the Kādimata (begins *ka*) and Hādimata (begins *ha*) schools, giving distinct decodings of "the" Śrīvidyā. (Source: Bhāskararāya; Hindupedia, *Sri Vidya*) [traditional]

## 2. Anatomy of a mantra — the six "limbs" of the viniyoga

- **The mantra is a body, not a string of sounds** — before recitation the manuals prefix a ***viniyoga*** ("application") declaring six attributes; reciting these is held to be a prerequisite for efficacy, like knowing a tool's specification before use. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Agni Purāṇa, mantra chapters)
  - **Ṛṣi** — the seer to whom the mantra was first revealed; honoured by touching the **head** (the seer "sits" at the crown).
  - **Chandas** — the meter; touched at the **mouth**.
  - **Devatā** — the presiding deity, the *living tenant* of the mantra; touched at the **heart**.
  - **Bīja** — the seed-syllable, the mantra's potency-condensate; touched at the **anus/root** or private region (the generative base).
  - **Śakti** — the energizing power that makes the seed "fruit"; touched at the **feet**.
  - **Kīlaka** — the "pin/wedge/bolt" that *locks* the mantra shut until the proper key turns it; touched at the **navel**. A mantra recited without resolving its *kīlaka* is held to be "nailed shut" and inert. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Mantra-mahodadhi)
- **Bīja-mantra mechanics** — a *bīja* is read as a layered compound: a *base consonant* (the deity's letter) + a *vowel* (*svara*, giving emotional/elemental colour) + the *bindu* (*ṃ*, the *nāda-bindu*, the unmanifest point where sound dissolves into silence). *Hrīṃ* = h (Śiva/ether) + r (fire) + ī (Māyā) + ṃ (dispeller of sorrow) — every component is exegeted. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi; Woodroffe, *The Garland of Letters*)

## 3. Nyāsa — installing sound on the body

- **Nyāsa ("placing, depositing")** — the ritual *installation* of mantra-syllables onto points of the practitioner's own body, transmuting the body into the deity's body before worship: "one must become a god to worship a god" (*nādevo devam arcayet*). Performed by touch + utterance + visualization together. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Lakṣmī-tantra)
  - **Ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa** — installation on the **six limbs**: heart (*hṛdaya*), head (*śiras*), tuft (*śikhā*), armour (*kavaca*), three-eyes (*netra*), and weapon/hand-clap (*astra*), each sealed with the relevant *aṅga-mantra* (...*namaḥ, svāhā, vaṣaṭ, huṃ, vauṣaṭ, phaṭ*). (Source: Śāradā-tilaka)
  - **Kara-nyāsa** — installation on the **hands**: thumbs, index, middle, ring, little fingers, palms-and-backs — fixing the six *aṅga*-mantras onto the fingers. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - **Aṅga-nyāsa** — the parallel installation onto the **body-limbs** (chest, head, etc.), usually paired with the kara-nyāsa. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka)
  - **Vyāpaka-nyāsa ("pervading placement")** — *suffusing the whole body* with the mantra by stroking from head to foot, so no part is left un-divinized. (Source: Lakṣmī-tantra; Bühnemann)
  - **Mātṛkā-nyāsa** — installation of **all fifty mātṛkā letters** (*a* to *kṣa*, the "little mothers," the phonemic matrix of the cosmos) across the body, each letter with its *bindu*, divinizing the body as the sonic universe. The most elaborate and characteristically Śākta form; pre-supposes the *mātṛkā* cosmology and the [cakra/kuṇḍalinī](../05-concepts-and-dharma/chakras-and-kundalini.md) body. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Woodroffe, *Garland of Letters*)

## 4. Mantra-saṃskāras and mantra-caitanya

- **The sixteen mantra-saṃskāras** — a powerful mantra received at *dīkṣā* is held to be raw and possibly "defective," and must be *refined* by **sixteen purifications** (*ṣoḍaśa-saṃskāra*) modelled on the human life-cycle *saṃskāras*. Standard list (per **Śāradā-tilaka** / **Prapañcasāra**): *janana* (birth), *jīvana* (vivification), *tāḍana* (beating), *bodhana* (awakening), *abhiṣeka* (anointing), *vimalīkaraṇa* (cleansing), *āpyāyana* (nourishing), *tarpaṇa* (satiation), *dīpana* (kindling), *gupti* (concealment), *āpyāyana/recanā*, etc. — performed by writing the mantra in a *prastāra* and operating on its letters with specific bīja-keys. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Prapañcasāra Tantra; Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - *Variant (list length & names):* the count is canonically sixteen but the named operations differ between *Śāradā-tilaka*, *Prapañcasāra* and the *Mantra-mahodadhi*; some give ten *doṣas* (faults) to be removed instead. (Source: Goudriaan & Gupta) [disputed]
- **Mantra-caitanya ("awakening the mantra's consciousness")** — the central premise: a mantra can be *suptа* ("asleep") or even *śapta* ("cursed") and therefore inert; it must be **awakened** so its resident deity (*devatā-caitanya*) is conscious in it. Without *caitanya* the recitation is mere noise. Achieved through the saṃskāras, through *prāṇa* (breath) infusion, and through the guru's transmitted *vīrya* ("potency"). (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Woodroffe, *Garland of Letters*)
  - **Mantra-doṣas & "asleep/cursed" mantras** — the manuals catalogue scores of faults — a mantra may be *sleeping, cursed, broken, blind, paralyzed, infant, aged, intoxicated, hostile,* etc. — each with a corrective rite; the *kīla* (locking) and *utkīlana* (un-pinning) operations resolve the "bolted" condition. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi; Śāradā-tilaka) [late text]

## 5. Puraścaraṇa — the preparatory regimen

- **Puraścaraṇa ("preliminary procedure")** — the disciplined *front-loading* a sādhaka must complete before a mantra becomes *siddha* ("perfected, mastered") and usable for results. It is the qualifying course; only after it does *prayoga* (application) work. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Mantra-mahodadhi; Meru-tantra)
- **The five (or more) components** — classically:
  - **Japa** — a fixed *count* of repetitions, conventionally **100,000 per syllable** of the mantra (so a six-syllable mantra ⇒ 600,000 *japa*), over a vow of days. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - **Homa** — fire-oblation of **one-tenth** the japa-count, offering *āhutis* into the *kuṇḍa*.
  - **Tarpaṇa** — water-libation (satiation of the deity) of **one-tenth** the homa-count.
  - **Abhiṣeka / Mārjana** — sprinkling-anointing, **one-tenth** the tarpaṇa-count.
  - **Brāhmaṇa-bhojana** — feeding of Brahmins / the realized, **one-tenth** again — closing and "sealing" the regimen. (Source: Śāradā-tilaka; Meru-tantra)
  - *Variant (decadal rule):* the manuals encode the whole as a *daśāṃśa* ("one-tenth at each stage") cascade — japa : homa : tarpaṇa : abhiṣeka : bhojana = 10 : 1 : 0.1 : 0.01 : 0.001 of the base; counts and conditions vary by mantra-class and intended *prayoga*. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi) [traditional]

## 6. Ṣaṭkarma — the six applied rites (prayogas)

- **Ṣaṭkarma ("six acts") / ṣaṭ-prayoga** — the applied, results-oriented deployments of a *siddha* mantra, the operative end of the science. Each act is assigned its own **deity, season, direction, seat (āsana), bīja-vowel, finger, and mood** — a complete correspondence-table the manuals tabulate. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi; Śāradā-tilaka; Agni Purāṇa). Listed conventionally light-to-fierce:
  - **Śānti** — *pacification*, quelling disease/ill-fortune; deity **Rati / a benign white form**, season *spring/varṣā*, direction **east/north**, white colour, seat facing east. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - **Vaśīkaraṇa** — *subjugation/attraction*, bending another's will; deity **Vāṇī / Kāmadeva-Rati**, season *spring*, direction **north**, **red**, the *kāma-bīja* (klīṃ). (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - **Stambhana** — *immobilization/paralysis*, arresting an enemy, an army, water, poison; deity often **a yellow Lakṣmī/Gaṇeśa form**, season *autumn (śarad)*, direction **east**, **yellow**. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - **Vidveṣaṇa** — *creating enmity/estrangement*, sowing discord between allies; deity **Jyeṣṭhā/a fierce form**, season *winter (śiśira)*, direction **south-west**, **smoke-colour**. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - **Uccāṭana** — *eradication/driving-out*, expelling someone from home or post; deity **Nirṛti/Bhairava**, season *summer (grīṣma)*, direction **south-west/south**, **black**, *vāyu*-charged. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi; Śāradā-tilaka)
  - **Māraṇa** — *killing/destruction*, the fiercest; deity **Kālī/Mṛtyu/Rudra**, season *the rains/cremation-time*, direction **south** (Yama's quarter), **black**, performed in the *śmaśāna*. (Source: Mantra-mahodadhi)
  - *Variant (order, count & assignments):* the *six* are sometimes counted differently (some lists add *ākarṣaṇa*, "summoning," or *poṣṭi/poṣaṇa*, "nourishing," giving seven or eight *karmas*); the deity/season/direction tables differ between the *Mantra-mahodadhi*, *Śāradā-tilaka*, *Tantrarāja* and *Agni Purāṇa* recensions. (Source: Goudriaan & Gupta; Agni Purāṇa) [disputed]
  - *Caution [scholarly]* — five of the six are *abhicāra* ("hostile sorcery"); the tradition restricts them to high *adhikāra*, hedges them with *dīkṣā* and secrecy, and many teachers permit only **śānti** for ordinary practice — paralleling the [vāmācāra/dakṣiṇācāra](shakta-and-tantra-lineages.md#vāmācāra-vs-dakṣiṇācāra--the-two-paths) caution.
