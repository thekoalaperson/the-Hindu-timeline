# The Four Yugas & the Mahāyuga Cycle

> Where this sits: **00-time-and-cosmology** — the core "clock face" of the timeline. The four yugas (Satya → Treta → Dvapara → Kali) form one **Mahāyuga** (Chaturyuga); **71 Mahāyugas + 1 sandhya = 1 Manvantara**; **1000 Mahāyugas = 1 Kalpa** (a Day of Brahmā). We currently sit in the Kali Yuga of the 28th Mahāyuga of the 7th (Vaivasvata) Manvantara — see [our address](../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/).

---

## The Mahāyuga at a glance

- **The cycle (Chaturyuga / Mahāyuga):** four yugas of declining length in a **4 : 3 : 2 : 1** ratio; their sum = **12,000 deva-varṣa = 4,320,000 human years** (Vishnu Purana 1.3; Bhagavata Purana 3.11; Surya Siddhanta 1.15–21).
- **Conversion:** **1 divine year (deva-varṣa) = 360 human years.** Each yuga = a main period + a *sandhyā* (dawn/transition-in) + a *sandhyāṃśa* (dusk/transition-out); each twilight = **1/10 of the yuga's main period** (Vishnu Purana 1.3.8–11).
- **Direction of time:** the yugas run **longest → shortest**, mirroring a steady *decline* of dharma. The cycle then resets: Kali is followed by a new Satya, and the wheel turns again (Mahabharata, Shanti Parva 231).

| Yuga | Main (deva-yr) | Sandhyā | Sandhyāṃśa | Total (deva-yr) | ×360 = Human years | Dharma legs |
|------|------|------|------|------|------|------|
| **Satya (Kṛta)** | 4000 | 400 | 400 | 4800 | **1,728,000** | 4 (100%) |
| **Treta** | 3000 | 300 | 300 | 3600 | **1,296,000** | 3 (75%) |
| **Dvapara** | 2000 | 200 | 200 | 2400 | **864,000** | 2 (50%) |
| **Kali** | 1000 | 100 | 100 | 1200 | **432,000** | 1 (25%) |
| **Mahāyuga** | — | — | — | **12,000** | **4,320,000** | — |

---

## The sandhyā / sandhyāṃśa (twilights)

- **What they are:** transitional "dawn" and "dusk" periods buffering each yuga, during which the qualities of the adjacent yugas blend; dharma does not flip instantly but fades and rises gradually (Vishnu Purana 1.3.8–11; Manusmriti 1.69–71).
  - *Sandhyā* = the dawn period *before* the yuga's main span; *sandhyāṃśa* = the dusk period *after* it.
  - Each = **1/10 of the yuga's main period** → Satya 400+400, Treta 300+300, Dvapara 200+200, Kali 100+100 (deva-years).
- **Why it matters for events:** epochal transitions (e.g. the Mahabharata war, Krishna's departure marking the Dvapara→Kali turn) are read as occurring *within* a twilight, which is why traditions place "Kali had not yet fully begun" at the war (Bhagavata Purana 1.18; 12.2).
- **Variant (Manusmriti 1.68–71):** gives the same 4:3:2:1 totals but frames the divine-year math through the gods' day/night rather than the Purana's explicit sandhyā arithmetic — the human-year totals agree.

---

## The decline of dharma — the Bull of Dharma (Vṛṣa)

- **The image:** Dharma is a bull (sometimes a cow) standing on its legs; one leg is lost each yuga, so virtue diminishes 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 quarters, restored to 4 at the next Satya (Bhagavata Purana 1.16–17; Mahabharata Vana Parva 149).
- **The four legs (pillars of dharma):** **tapas** (austerity), **śauca** (purity/cleanliness), **dayā** (compassion), **satya** (truthfulness) (Bhagavata Purana 1.17.24).
  - *Satya:* all four legs intact.
  - *Treta:* tapas lost → 3 legs.
  - *Dvapara:* tapas + śauca lost → 2 legs (dayā + satya remain).
  - *Kali:* only **satya** (truth) remains → 1 leg, and even that is assailed (Bhagavata Purana 1.17).
- **Variant (Mahabharata, Vana Parva 149.13–39 / Markandeya):** lists declining dharma in quarters (pāda) without always naming the same four virtues; the "lost leg each yuga" arithmetic is constant across sources.

## Satya Yuga (Kṛta Yuga) — the Age of Truth

> 1,728,000 human years · Dharma on 4 legs · associated **varṇa: Brāhmaṇa**.

- **Defining trait:** dharma is whole; no falsehood, no want, no disease, no caste-by-conflict; people are virtuous by nature and need no scripture or ritual to compel them (Vishnu Purana 1.3; Mahabharata Shanti Parva 231).
- **Lifespan & stature:** humans live **~100,000 years** and are of great stature; meditation (*dhyāna*) is the prescribed means of liberation (Bhagavata Purana 12.3; Manusmriti 1.85–86).
- **Means of yoga (yuga-dharma):** **meditation / contemplation** is the recommended path (Manusmriti 1.85: "different dharmas for different ages").
- **Avatāras placed here:** **Matsya, Kūrma, Varāha, Narasimha** — the early descents of Vishnu (Bhagavata Purana; Garuda Purana). See [avatars by yuga](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/).
- **Society:** no division of labor by strife; a single class living in spontaneous righteousness (Vishnu Purana 1.6) — sometimes glossed as the "Brāhmaṇa age."

---

## Treta Yuga — the Age of Three-Quarter Dharma

> 1,296,000 human years · Dharma on 3 legs · associated **varṇa: Kṣatriya**.

- **Defining trait:** dharma declines by one quarter; **sacrifice and ritual (yajña)** arise as the means to maintain order; agriculture, kingship and the first wars appear (Vishnu Purana 1.3; Bhagavata Purana 12.3).
- **Lifespan:** humans live **~10,000 years** (Bhagavata Purana 12.3; Mahabharata).
- **Means of yuga-dharma:** **yajña / sacrificial ritual** is the prescribed path to liberation (Manusmriti 1.86).
- **Avatāras placed here:** **Vāmana, Paraśurāma, and Rāma** — most prominently **Rāma**, whose story (the Rāmāyaṇa) is the signature event of Treta (Valmiki Ramayana; Bhagavata Purana 9). See [Treta events](../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/02-treta-yuga.md).
  - *Variant:* some Vaishnava lists place **Paraśurāma** spanning the Treta–Dvapara junction (he is a *chiranjivi*, deathless, and meets both Rāma and later figures).
- **Society:** kingship and the warrior order (Kṣatriya) rise to defend a now-imperfect dharma — the "Kṣatriya age."

---

## Dvapara Yuga — the Age of Half Dharma

> 864,000 human years · Dharma on 2 legs (dayā + satya) · associated **varṇa: Vaiśya**.

- **Defining trait:** virtue and vice are balanced 50/50; disease, discord and desire appear; the Vedas are divided (by Vyāsa) because human memory and intellect have weakened (Vishnu Purana 1.3; Bhagavata Purana 1.4, 12.3).
- **Lifespan:** humans live **~1,000 years** (Bhagavata Purana 12.3).
- **Means of yuga-dharma:** **temple worship / deity-archana (pūjā)** is the prescribed path (Bhagavata Purana 12.3.52; Manusmriti 1.86 frames it as *jñāna*-based for this age in some readings).
- **Signature events:** the **Mahābhārata** and the life of **Kṛṣṇa** (Kṛṣṇa avatāra) belong to the end of Dvapara; the great war and Kṛṣṇa's departure mark the **Dvapara → Kali transition** (Mahabharata; Bhagavata Purana 1.18, 11.31). See [Dvapara events](../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/03-dvapara-yuga.md).
- **The two legs:** Dayā (compassion) and Satya (truth) remain standing (popular Bhagavata reading).
- **Society:** trade, agriculture and merchant order (Vaiśya) characterize the age — the "Vaiśya age."

---

## Kali Yuga — the Age of Strife (current)

> 432,000 human years · Dharma on 1 leg (satya only) · associated **varṇa: Śūdra**.

- **Defining trait:** dharma stands on a single leg; quarrel, hypocrisy, greed and decay dominate; spiritual merit becomes hard to earn yet **easy to lose**, and even small effort yields great fruit (Bhagavata Purana 12.2–3; Vishnu Purana 6.1).
- **Lifespan:** humans live **~100 years (declining)**; by the *end* of Kali, lifespans shrink toward 20–30 years and human stature diminishes (Bhagavata Purana 12.2; Mahabharata Vana Parva 188–190 — the "Markandeya prophecy").
- **Means of yuga-dharma:** **nāma-saṅkīrtana / chanting the divine name** — uniquely easy and effective in Kali; "what was attained by meditation, sacrifice, and worship in earlier ages is attained in Kali simply by chanting Hari's names" (Bhagavata Purana 12.3.51–52).
- **Start epoch:** traditionally **3102 BCE** (17/18 Feb, midnight), aligned with Kṛṣṇa's departure (Surya Siddhanta; Aryabhatiya). **Elapsed as of 2026 CE ≈ 5,127–5,128 years**; **remaining ≈ 426,873 years**.
- **End / reset:** Kali closes with the **Kalki avatāra**, who destroys adharma and ushers in a new Satya Yuga, restarting the Mahāyuga (Bhagavata Purana 12.2; Kalki Purana; Vishnu Purana 4.24). See [Kali Yuga node](../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md).
- **Society:** characterized by the lowest order in the classical scheme (Śūdra age) and by collapse of the varṇa-āśrama system itself (Vishnu Purana 6.1).
  - *Variant (Mahabharata Vana Parva 188, Markandeya's prophecy):* graphic end-of-Kali decline — rulers as plunderers, dharma reduced to mere assertion, then Kalki's restoration.

## How the four yugas repeat — Mahāyuga → Manvantara → Kalpa

- **1 Mahāyuga (Chaturyuga)** = Satya+Treta+Dvapara+Kali = **4,320,000 years** (Surya Siddhanta 1.15–21; Vishnu Purana 1.3).
- **1 Manvantara = 71 Mahāyugas + 1 sandhyā** (a connecting twilight = one Satya Yuga, 1,728,000 yr) ≈ **306,720,000 years**, ruled by one Manu (Vishnu Purana 1.3; Bhagavata Purana 3.11).
  - So the four yugas cycle **71 times** within each Manu's reign before the manvantara-sandhyā closes it.
- **1 Kalpa (Day of Brahmā) = 1000 Mahāyugas = 14 Manvantaras + 15 sandhyās = 4,320,000,000 years** (4.32 billion); an equal Night of Brahmā follows (Bhagavata Purana 3.11; Vishnu Purana 1.3).
- **We are here:** Kali Yuga → of the **28th Mahāyuga** → of the **7th (Vaivasvata) Manvantara** → of the Śveta-Varāha Kalpa → 1st day of Brahmā's 51st year. See [the cosmic clock](./README.md) and [the 14 Manvantaras](../01-brahma-current-life/the-14-manvantaras/).

## Variant: Sri Yukteswar's electric/precessional yuga cycle [non-canonical, 1894]

> A modern reinterpretation rejecting the million-year scale. Catalogued here per the variant policy; **not** the Puranic framework used elsewhere in this repo.

- **Core claim:** the Purāṇic numbers are a misinterpretation. The true Mahāyuga = **24,000 years**, tied to the **precession of the equinoxes** (Sri Yukteswar, *The Holy Science*, 1894, Introduction).
- **Mechanism:** the Sun and its solar system orbit a "dual" star / *grand center* (Viṣṇunābhi) once in ~24,000 years; proximity to this center raises human consciousness, distance lowers it — hence the rise and fall of ages.
- **Two arcs:** **12,000 ascending** (virtue rising) + **12,000 descending** (virtue falling). Per arc: **Kali 1,200 (+200 transition), Dvapara 2,400 (+300), Treta 3,600 (+400), Satya 4,800 (+400)** = 12,000.
- **Current age — the big divergence:** Yukteswar holds **Kali ended ~AD 1700** and we are now in the **ascending Dvapara Yuga** (since ~AD 1699), explaining the rise of science and electricity. This directly contradicts the orthodox view that we are deep in Kali Yuga (Sri Yukteswar; Yogananda's lineage; [dwapara-yuga.org](https://www.dwapara-yuga.org/)).
- *Variant within the variant (Aryabhata):* keeps the 3102 BCE Kali epoch but treats yuga sub-divisions as **equal quarters** (each 1,080,000 yr) rather than the 4:3:2:1 graded model — still million-year scale, unlike Yukteswar.
