# The Manvantara System & the 14 Manus

> Cosmology spine node. A *manvantara* is the second-largest division of a Kalpa (Day of Brahma): 14 of them, each ruled by a Manu, fill one Kalpa. We are in the **7th — Vaivasvata**. See [units-of-time.md](units-of-time.md) and [kalpa-and-pralaya.md](kalpa-and-pralaya.md) for the surrounding clock; for our exact position see [../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/).

## 1. What a Manvantara Is

- **Definition** — A *manvantara* (manu + antara, "the interval/age of a Manu") is the reign-period of one Manu, the progenitor and lawgiver of humankind for that age. Fourteen successive manvantaras constitute one **Kalpa** (Day of Brahma). (Vishnu Purana 3.1-2; Bhagavata Purana 8.1; Manu Smriti 1.61-63)
- **Duration** — Each manvantara = **71 Mahayugas** (≈ 306,720,000 human years), plus inter-manvantara junctures (*sandhi*, each = 1 Satya Yuga = 1,728,000 yrs). 14 manvantaras + 15 sandhis = 1 Kalpa = 4.32 billion yrs. (See FRAMEWORK CONSTANTS §3.)
  - *Variant (computation):* Texts differ on whether the 15 sandhis are counted "inside" the 14 manvantaras or as separate junctures; the standard reconciliation adds them separately so the Kalpa totals exactly 1000 Mahayugas. (Surya Siddhanta; Vishnu Purana 1.3)
- **What gets recreated each manvantara** — At every manvantara turnover, an entire cosmic administration is renewed: a new **Manu**, a new **Indra** (king of the devas), a new set of **Devas** (god-classes), the **Saptarishis** (seven sages who re-promulgate the Vedas), the **sons of Manu** (founding kings/lineages), and a **manvantara-avatara of Vishnu** who supports dharma and the Manu through the age. (Vishnu Purana 3.1-2; Bhagavata Purana 8.1.3-5)
- **The Manu's role** — The Manu is the ruler of the earth and the source of human dharma for his age: he fathers the royal lineages, upholds law, and is "saved" across the dissolution to seed the next humanity. The lawbook *Manu Smriti* is framed as the teaching of the **first** Manu, Svayambhuva. (Manu Smriti 1; Vishnu Purana 3.1)
- **Indra's role** — "Indra" is an **office, not a person**: each manvantara has its own Indra (our current one is **Purandara**). The title rotates; even mortals of sufficient merit can attain Indrahood for a manvantara. (Bhagavata Purana 8.1, 8.13; Vishnu Purana 3.1-2)
- **Saptarishis' role** — The seven sages anchor each manvantara, preserving and re-teaching the Vedas after each yuga-decline and at each new manvantara; they also vary by age. (Vishnu Purana 3.1-2)

## 2. The 14 Manus (current Shveta-Varaha Kalpa)

Standard order (Vishnu Purana 3.1-2 / Bhagavata Purana 8.1, 8.13). **The first six are nearly universal; disagreement grows from the 8th onward** (the "Savarni" cluster). (Wikipedia: Manu (Hinduism))

- *Variant (13th/14th naming):* The pair Savarnis 13-14 appear as **Raucya** and **Bhautya** in some Puranic lists rather than "Deva-savarni / Indra-savarni." (Markandeya Purana; cross-Puranic note)
- *Variant (4th Manu):* spelled/named **Tamasa** in Bhagavata & Vishnu Purana but **Tapasa** in some readings. (Wikipedia: Manvantara)

## 3. Per-Manvantara Administration — and the BIG variant (Vishnu vs Bhagavata)

Each manvantara has its own Indra, god-classes, Saptarishis, and Vishnu-avatar. **The two principal source lists disagree substantially**, especially on Indras and avatars. Both are cataloged below.

### 3a. Vishnu avatar of each manvantara — TWO TRADITIONS

| # | Manu | Avatar — **Bhagavata Purana 8.1** | Avatar — **Vishnu Purana 3.1-2** |
|---|------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Svayambhuva | Yajna | Yajna |
| 2 | Svarochisha | Vibhu | **Ajita** |
| 3 | Uttama | Satyasena | **Satya** |
| 4 | Tamasa | Hari | Hari |
| 5 | Raivata | Vaikuntha | **Manasa** |
| 6 | Chakshusha | Ajita | **Vaikuntha** |
| 7 | **Vaivasvata** | **Vamana** | **Vamana** |
| 8 | Savarni | Sarvabhauma | (future) |
| 9 | Daksha-savarni | Rshabha | (future) |
| 10 | Brahma-savarni | Vishvaksena | (future) |
| 11 | Dharma-savarni | Dharmasetu | (future) |
| 12 | Rudra-savarni | Sudhama | (future) |
| 13 | Deva-savarni | Yogeshvara | (future) |
| 14 | Indra-savarni | Brihadbhanu | (future) |

- **Note the conflict:** for the 2nd, 3rd, 5th, and 6th manvantaras the Bhagavata and Vishnu Puranas assign the SAME avatar-names to DIFFERENT slots (e.g. *Ajita* = 6th in Bhagavata but 2nd in Vishnu Purana; *Vaikuntha* = 5th in Bhagavata but 6th in Vishnu Purana). Both traditions agree only that **Vamana is the avatar of our own (7th) Vaivasvata manvantara**, where he subdues the three worlds and returns them to Indra (the Bali story). (Bhagavata Purana 8.1, 8.15-23; Vishnu Purana 3.1)

- Both lists agree that the **current Indra is Purandara** (an epithet of the present Indra/Shakra). (Bhagavata Purana 8.13; Vishnu Purana 3.2)
- *Variant (8th-manvantara Indra):* The future Indra is named **Bali** — the demon-king pushed down by Vamana in the present age is foretold to *become* Indra in the Savarni manvantara, the classic "demotion-to-promotion" arc. (Bhagavata Purana 8.13, 8.22-23)

### 3c. God-classes (Devas) of each manvantara — Vishnu Purana

- **2 Svarochisha:** Paravatas & Tushitas — **3 Uttami:** Sudhamas, Satyas, Shivas, Pradarshanas, Vasavartis — **4 Tamasa:** Surupas, Haris, Satyas, Sudhis (27 each) — **5 Raivata:** Amitabhas, Abhutarajasas, Vaikunthas, Sumedhasas (14 each) — **6 Chakshusha:** Adyas, Prastutas, Bhavyas, Prithugas, Lekhas (8 each). (Vishnu Purana 3.1)
- **7 Vaivasvata (current):** **Adityas, Vasus, Rudras** (VP); the Bhagavata expands the present devas to Adityas, Vasus, Rudras, Vishvedevas, Maruts, Ashvini-Kumaras, and Ribhus. (Vishnu Purana 3.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13)

## 4. The Saptarishis — past, present, future

The seven sages rotate by manvantara. (Vishnu Purana 3.1-2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13)

- **2 Svarochisha (VP):** Urja, Stambha, Prana, Dattoli, Rishabha, Nischara, Arvarivat
- **3 Uttami (VP):** the seven sons of Vasishtha
- **4 Tamasa (VP):** Jyotirdhama, Prithu, Kavya, Chaitra, Agni, Vanaka, Pivara
- **5 Raivata (VP):** Hiranyaroman, Vedashri, Urdhvabahu, Vedabahu, Sudhaman, Parjanya, Mahamuni
- **6 Chakshusha (VP):** Sumedhas, Virajas, Havishmat, Uttama, Madhu, Abhinaman, Sahishnu
- **7 Vaivasvata — CURRENT Saptarishis:** **Kashyapa, Atri, Vasishtha, Vishvamitra, Gautama, Jamadagni, Bharadvaja.** (Bhagavata Purana 8.13; Vishnu Purana 3.2)
  - *Variant (ordering/membership):* the Vishnu Purana lists them as **Vasishtha, Kashyapa, Atri, Jamadagni, Gautama, Vishvamitra, Bharadvaja** — same seven, different sequence. Some lists substitute **Marichi** or other Prajapatis; the "current Saptarishi" set is not perfectly fixed across texts. (Vishnu Purana 3.2)
- **8 Savarni (future):** named seers include **Galava** and **Parashurama** (one of the chiranjivi "immortals" said to survive into the next manvantara). (Bhagavata Purana 8.13)
- **14 Indra-savarni (future):** seers include **Agni-bahu / Bahu** etc. (Bhagavata Purana 8.13)

## 5. Vaivasvata — Our Own Manu (key events)

- **Origin & name** — Vaivasvata = "son of Vivasvat" (Vivasvat = Surya, the sun); also called **Shraddhadeva** ("lord of the funeral/faith rite"). Born of Surya and Saranyu/Chhaya. He is the *7th* Manu and the present progenitor of humankind. (Vishnu Purana 3.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13)
- **The Matsya flood** — Vishnu as **Matsya** (the fish) warns Vaivasvata of a coming deluge; the Manu builds a boat, ties it to Matsya's horn, and carries the **seven sages, the seed of all creatures, and the Vedas** safely across the pralaya — the founding-of-the-current-humanity story. (Matsya Purana; Bhagavata Purana 8.24; Shatapatha Brahmana 1.8.1; Mahabharata, Vana Parva)
  - *Variant (earliest form):* the **Shatapatha Brahmana** version names the Manu simply as **Manu** (no Vaivasvata epithet) and frames it as a personal salvation tale, predating the cosmic 14-Manu schema. (Shatapatha Brahmana 1.8.1)
  - *Variant (pre-flood role):* Vaivasvata is described as **king of Dravida** before the flood in some retellings. (Matsya Purana; cf. Wikipedia: Manu (Hinduism))
- **The royal lineages** — Vaivasvata's sons found the great dynasties: **Ikshvaku** (the Suryavamsha / Solar line), plus Nabhaga, Dhrishta, Sharyati, Narishyanta, Nabhanedishtha, Karusha, Prishadhra, and Vasuman; his offspring **Ila/Sudyumna** seeds the Chandravamsha (Lunar line) through Pururavas. (Vishnu Purana 3.2, 4.1; Bhagavata Purana 9.1) — see [../03-lineages-vamsha/](../03-lineages-vamsha/).
  - *Variant (count of sons):* texts give "nine sons" (VP) but enumerations vary slightly; the gender-shift of Ila/Sudyumna is itself a major variant (born female, became male, alternated). (Vishnu Purana 4.1; Bhagavata Purana 9.1)
- **Vamana avatar** — The manvantara-avatara for our age is **Vamana**, the dwarf-brahmin who reclaims the three worlds from Bali in three steps and restores them to Indra Purandara. (Bhagavata Purana 8.15-23; Vishnu Purana 3.1)

## 6. Where We Are Now

- **6 manvantaras elapsed** this Kalpa (Svayambhuva → Chakshusha); we are in the **7th, Vaivasvata**.
- Within it: **27 Mahayugas complete**, and within the **28th** Mahayuga the Satya, Treta, and Dvapara Yugas are done — we are in **Kali Yuga** (begun 3102 BCE; ≈ 5,127-5,128 years elapsed as of 2026 CE). (FRAMEWORK CONSTANTS §4)
- **8 manvantaras still to come** (Savarni → Indra-savarni), after which this Kalpa ends and Brahma's night (pralaya) falls. See [kalpa-and-pralaya.md](kalpa-and-pralaya.md).
