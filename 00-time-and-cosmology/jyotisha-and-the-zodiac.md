# Jyotiṣa — The Zodiac & Cosmic Influences

> Hierarchy location: `00-time-and-cosmology/` — the science that *reads* the sky-clock this section builds. Where [units of time](units-of-time.md) builds duration from the truti up to the kalpa, and [the 27 Nakṣatras](nakshatras.md) maps the Moon's lunar mansions, this file catalogues the **rāśis (zodiac signs), grahas (planets), and bhāvas (houses)** of **Jyotiṣa**, "the eye of the Veda." The nine planetary deities themselves are catalogued in full at [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md); the Vedāṅga that contains this science lives at [`../90-literature-corpus/upavedas-vedangas-darshanas.md`](../90-literature-corpus/upavedas-vedangas-darshanas.md). See the section [README](README.md) for how this node connects to the rest of cosmology.

**Jyotiṣa** (ज्योतिष, "[science] of the heavenly lights") is the sixth and reckoned the most important of the six **Vedāṅgas** ("limbs of the Veda"), called **vedacakṣus**, "the eye of the Veda" — because it fixes the *correct time* for every Vedic rite, the original ritual purpose from which both Hindu astronomy (*gola/gaṇita*) and astrology (*phalita/horā*) descend. Its roster is **finite and closed**: **12 rāśis × 9 grahas × 12 bhāvas** (overlaid on the 27/28 nakṣatras), plus a fixed set of derived sub-divisions. The classical canon of attributes (lordship, element, modality, deity) is stable across Parāśara, Varāhamihira and later authors; where authors *disagree* (e.g. the two extra lords Rāhu/Ketu, the sidereal-vs-tropical zodiac) the variants are catalogued below. (Sources: [Dharmawiki *Vedanga Jyotisha*](https://dharmawiki.org/index.php/Vedanga_Jyotisha_(%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%99%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D)); [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)

## 1. The three skandhas (branches) of Jyotiṣa

Classical jyotiṣa is divided into three **skandhas** ("trunks"); together they are sometimes called the *jyotiṣa-traya* and a master of all three is a *saṃhitā-pāraga*.

- **Siddhānta (Gaṇita / Gola)** — the *mathematical-astronomical* trunk: spherics, planetary mean motions, eclipse computation, the calendar (pañcāṅga). This is the **astronomy** proper, codified in the *Siddhānta* texts (see §6). (Source: [Dharmawiki](https://dharmawiki.org/index.php/Vedanga_Jyotisha_(%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%99%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D)).)
- **Horā (Phalita / Jātaka)** — the *predictive* trunk: natal astrology (jātaka), horary (praśna), electional (muhūrta). The word **horā** (whence *horoscope*) is traditionally derived from *aho-rātra* ("day-night") by dropping first and last syllable; the foundational text is the **Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra**. (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)
- **Saṃhitā** — *mundane / collective* astrology: omens, comets, mundane events, meteorology, architecture (vāstu), and natural phenomena affecting kingdoms and crops; the locus classicus is Varāhamihira's **Bṛhat Saṃhitā**. (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)
- *Variant (later schools):* some authors split **Horā** further into **Jātaka** (natal), **Praśna/Tājika** (horary, the latter Perso-Arabic-influenced [late text]), and **Muhūrta** (electional), giving a "six-limbed" enumeration rather than three. [scholarly]

## 2. The 12 Rāśis (zodiac signs)

A **rāśi** ("heap, sign") is a **30° slice of the 360° ecliptic** (12 × 30° = 360°); each contains **2¼ nakṣatras** (9 nakṣatra-pādas, since 27 × 4 ÷ 12 = 9). The list begins at **Meṣa** (0° of sidereal Aries) and runs counter-clockwise. Each sign carries a fixed bundle: **adhipati (lord/ruling graha)**, **tattva (element)**, **svabhāva (modality)**, **gender**, **direction**, and body-part in the cosmic *Kālapuruṣa*. (Sources: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology); [Prayag Pandits](https://prayagpandits.com/vedic-astrology-all-you-need-to-know/).)

- **Meṣa** (मेष, Aries) — Ram. Lord **Maṅgala/Mars**; **fire** (agni); **movable** (chara); masculine; East. Head of the Kālapuruṣa. Exaltation of the Sun. (Source: [Wisdom of Planets](https://www.wisdomofplanets.com/12-rashis-in-vedic-astrology/).)
- **Vṛṣabha** (वृषभ, Taurus) — Bull. Lord **Śukra/Venus**; **earth** (pṛthvī); **fixed** (sthira); feminine. Exaltation of the Moon. Face/neck.
- **Mithuna** (मिथुन, Gemini) — Twins (often a couple). Lord **Budha/Mercury**; **air** (vāyu); **dual** (dvisvabhāva); masculine. Shoulders/arms.
- **Karka / Karkaṭa** (कर्क, Cancer) — Crab. Lord **Candra/Moon**; **water** (jala); **movable**; feminine; North. Exaltation of Jupiter. Chest.
- **Siṃha** (सिंह, Leo) — Lion. Lord **Sūrya/Sun**; **fire**; **fixed**; masculine. The only sign owned by the Sun. Heart/stomach.
- **Kanyā** (कन्या, Virgo) — Maiden (with sheaf/lamp). Lord **Budha/Mercury**; **earth**; **dual**; feminine. Exaltation of Mercury itself. Hips/belly. — *Variant (some schools):* assigned a secondary/mūlatrikoṇa link to **Ketu** [disputed]. (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)
- **Tulā** (तुला, Libra) — Scales. Lord **Śukra/Venus**; **air**; **movable**; masculine; West. Exaltation of Saturn; the only "inanimate" sign. Lower abdomen.
- **Vṛścika** (वृश्चिक, Scorpio) — Scorpion. Lord **Maṅgala/Mars**; **water**; **fixed**; feminine. Debilitation of the Moon. Genitals.
- **Dhanus / Dhanu** (धनुष, Sagittarius) — Bow / Archer-centaur. Lord **Bṛhaspati/Jupiter**; **fire**; **dual**; masculine. Thighs.
- **Makara** (मकर, Capricorn) — Sea-goat / crocodile-deer (the makara). Lord **Śani/Saturn**; **earth**; **movable**; feminine; South. Exaltation of Mars. Knees.
- **Kumbha** (कुम्भ, Aquarius) — Water-pot / pot-bearer. Lord **Śani/Saturn**; **air**; **fixed**; masculine. Ankles/calves. — *Variant:* secondary co-lord **Rāhu** in some schools (paralleling Vṛścika/Mars–Ketu) [disputed]. (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)
- **Mīna** (मीन, Pisces) — Two fish. Lord **Bṛhaspati/Jupiter**; **water**; **dual**; feminine. Exaltation of Venus. Feet — completing the Kālapuruṣa. (Source: [Wisdom of Planets](https://www.wisdomofplanets.com/12-rashis-in-vedic-astrology/).)

**The threefold modality (svabhāva)** — *Chara* (movable: Meṣa, Karka, Tulā, Makara — the cardinal/equinoctial-solstitial signs), *Sthira* (fixed: Vṛṣabha, Siṃha, Vṛścika, Kumbha), *Dvisvabhāva* (dual/mutable: Mithuna, Kanyā, Dhanus, Mīna). (Source: [Astrolksharma](https://astrolksharma.com/blogs/news/12-rashis-zodiac-signs-in-vedic-astrology-complete-guide).)

**Other standard rāśi-classifications** (each rāśi belongs to all of these simultaneously):
- **Tattva (element)** groups of 4: agni (1/5/9), pṛthvī (2/6/10), vāyu (3/7/11), jala (4/8/12).
- **Liṅga (gender):** odd signs masculine/cruel, even signs feminine/gentle.
- **Day/night (divā/rātri)** and **directional** (diś) assignments used in muhūrta.
- **Body-type:** quadruped (catuṣpada), human (nara), watery (jalachara), reptile/insect (kīṭa) — affecting praśna outcomes.

## 3. The 9 Grahas (planets) — astrological roles

The **navagraha** ("nine seizers") are catalogued as deities, vāhanas, gems, days and directions in full at [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md). Here only their **jyotiṣa signification (kāraka-tva), benefic/malefic nature, and dignity** are listed. The grahas *act* upon the chart; the rāśis and bhāvas are the *fields* they act within. (Source: [Prayag Pandits](https://prayagpandits.com/vedic-astrology-all-you-need-to-know/).)

- **Sūrya (Sun)** — kāraka of *ātman* (soul), father, king, vitality, authority. Mild malefic. Lord of Siṃha; exalted in Meṣa (10°), debilitated in Tulā.
- **Candra (Moon)** — kāraka of *manas* (mind), mother, emotion, public. Benefic when waxing, malefic when waning. Lord of Karka; exalted in Vṛṣabha (3°), debilitated in Vṛścika.
- **Maṅgala (Mars)** — kāraka of energy, courage, siblings, land, war. Natural malefic. Lord of Meṣa & Vṛścika; exalted in Makara (28°), debilitated in Karka.
- **Budha (Mercury)** — kāraka of intellect, speech, commerce, learning. Benefic but "takes the colour" of its associations. Lord of Mithuna & Kanyā; exalted in Kanyā (15°), debilitated in Mīna.
- **Bṛhaspati / Guru (Jupiter)** — kāraka of wisdom, dharma, children, wealth, the guru. **Greatest natural benefic.** Lord of Dhanus & Mīna; exalted in Karka (5°), debilitated in Makara.
- **Śukra (Venus)** — kāraka of love, marriage, art, comfort, vehicles. Natural benefic. Lord of Vṛṣabha & Tulā; exalted in Mīna (27°), debilitated in Kanyā.
- **Śani (Saturn)** — kāraka of karma, longevity, sorrow, discipline, the lowly, the slow. **Greatest natural malefic.** Lord of Makara & Kumbha; exalted in Tulā (20°), debilitated in Meṣa.
- **Rāhu** (north lunar node) — **chāyā-graha** (shadow planet), no physical body; signifies obsession, foreigners, illusion (māyā), worldly desire. Malefic. Owns no sign in the orthodox scheme. — *Variant:* exalted in Vṛṣabha/Mithuna and co-lord of Kumbha per some schools [disputed].
- **Ketu** (south lunar node) — **chāyā-graha**, the headless trunk; signifies detachment, mokṣa, the occult, past-life karma. Malefic-but-spiritual. — *Variant:* co-lord of Vṛścika and exalted in Vṛścika/Dhanus per some schools [disputed]. (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)

**Count & roster variants:**
- *Variant (7 vs 9):* the oldest layer (Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa, and the seven-day week) counts only the **7 visible grahas** (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) — the *sapta-graha* / *vāra* set. **Rāhu and Ketu** (the lunar nodes, computed not seen) are added later to make **9**. (Source: [Dharmawiki](https://dharmawiki.org/index.php/Vedanga_Jyotisha_(%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%99%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D).)
- *Variant ("upa-grahas"):* some texts add a set of **shadowy sub-planets / sensitive points** — e.g. **Gulika/Māndi, Dhūma, Vyatipāta, Parivesha, Indracāpa, Upaketu** — extending the roster well past nine; these are **open-ended** and school-specific. [late text]
- *Note:* the **outer planets** (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) are **absent** from classical jyotiṣa, which knows only bodies visible to the naked eye; their inclusion is a **modern Western-influenced** add-on, not canonical. [scholarly]

## 4. The 12 Bhāvas (houses)

A **bhāva** ("state of being, field of life") is one of twelve sectors of the chart (*bhāva-cakra*), counted from the **lagna** (ascendant — the degree of the ecliptic rising on the eastern horizon at the moment in question), running counter-clockwise. The signs are the *fixed* zodiac; the houses are the *moment-specific* overlay anchored to the lagna. Each bhāva has a primary **significance** and a *bhāva-kāraka* (natural significator graha). (Sources: [Wikipedia *Bhāva*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bh%C4%81va); [Astrokarak](https://www.astrokarak.com/blog/12-houses-in-vedic-astrology).)

- **1st — Tanu / Lagna** — body, self, personality, head, vitality, early life. Kāraka: Sun. (Kendra + Trikoṇa.)
- **2nd — Dhana** — wealth, speech, face, family, accumulated assets, food. Kāraka: Jupiter.
- **3rd — Sahaja / Parākrama** — siblings, courage, communication, short journeys, hands. Kāraka: Mars.
- **4th — Sukha / Bandhu** — mother, home, land, vehicles, heart, happiness, education. Kāraka: Moon (& Mercury). (Kendra.)
- **5th — Putra / Vidyā** — children, intellect, *pūrva-puṇya* (past merit), creativity, mantra. Kāraka: Jupiter. (Trikoṇa.)
- **6th — Ari / Roga** — enemies, disease, debts, service, obstacles, litigation. Kāraka: Mars & Saturn. (Dusthāna / Upachaya.)
- **7th — Yuvati / Kalatra** — spouse, marriage, partnership, trade, foreign travel, "death-inflicting" (māraka). Kāraka: Venus. (Kendra.)
- **8th — Randhra / Āyus** — longevity & death, transformation, the occult, inheritance, sudden events. Kāraka: Saturn. (Dusthāna.)
- **9th — Dharma / Bhāgya** — fortune, father, guru, religion, higher learning, pilgrimage. Kāraka: Jupiter & Sun. (Trikoṇa — the most auspicious house.)
- **10th — Karma / Rājya** — career, action, status, authority, public standing. Kāraka: Mercury, Jupiter, Sun, Saturn. (Kendra.)
- **11th — Lābha / Āya** — gains, income, elder siblings, friends, fulfilment of desire. Kāraka: Jupiter. (Upachaya.)
- **12th — Vyaya / Mokṣa** — loss, expenditure, isolation, foreign lands, the bed, liberation. Kāraka: Saturn (& Ketu for mokṣa). (Dusthāna.) (Sources: [Astrokarak](https://www.astrokarak.com/blog/12-houses-in-vedic-astrology); [Path of Light](https://thepathoflight.yoga/vedic-astrology-jyotisha/bhavas-houses-in-vedic-astrology-and-what-they-represent/).)

**House-group classifications:**
- **Kendra (aṅgular / quadrant):** 1, 4, 7, 10 — "Viṣṇu-sthānas," strong and stabilising. (Source: [Jagannath Hora](https://jagannathhora.com/houses/).)
- **Trikoṇa (trinal):** 1, 5, 9 — "Lakṣmī-sthānas," most auspicious; the dharma-trine.
- **Dusthāna (difficult):** 6, 8, 12 — "trika," houses of suffering, loss and transformation.
- **Upachaya (growing):** 3, 6, 10, 11 — improve with time; good for malefics.
- **Mārakasthāna (death-dealing):** 2 and 7 — "killer" houses in longevity analysis.
- *Variant (house systems):* the **Bhāva (whole-sign / equal)** system vs the **Śrīpati / Porphyry-style unequal-bhāva** system disagree on cusp computation; North-Indian charts fix houses and rotate signs, South-Indian charts fix signs (squares) and mark the lagna — a *display* variant, not a doctrinal one. [scholarly]

## 5. Derived structures — the Ṣaḍvarga & dṛṣṭi

Beyond the three primary rosters, jyotiṣa enumerates fixed **sub-divisional charts (vargas)** and **aspect (dṛṣṭi)** rules:

- **Ṣaḍvarga** (six standard divisions): **Rāśi (D-1, whole sign), Horā (D-2, ½-sign, wealth), Drekkāṇa/Decanate (D-3, ⅓, siblings), Navāṃśa (D-9, 1/9, marriage & dharma — the most important), Dvādaśāṃśa (D-12, parents), Triṃśāṃśa (D-30, misfortunes).** — *Variant:* the **Ṣoḍaśavarga** extends this to **16 divisional charts** (adding D-4, D-7, D-10, D-16, D-20, D-24, D-27, D-40, D-45, D-60 *ṣaṣṭyāṃśa*), per Parāśara. (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)
- **Dṛṣṭi (planetary aspect):** every graha aspects the **7th house** from itself; **special aspects** — Mars also 4th & 8th, Jupiter also 5th & 9th, Saturn also 3rd & 10th. Rāhu/Ketu's aspects are disputed [disputed].
- **Daśā systems (time-of-fruition):** the dominant is **Viṃśottarī daśā** (120-year cycle keyed to the natal nakṣatra — linking jyotiṣa directly back to [the nakṣatras](nakshatras.md)); alternatives include **Aṣṭottarī, Yoginī, Kālacakra, Cara (Jaimini)** daśās. *Open-ended:* dozens of conditional daśās exist. (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)

## 6. Jyotiṣa, the Vedāṅga & the Siddhānta texts

- **Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa** (वेदाङ्गज्योतिष) — the oldest extant astronomical text (c. 1400–1200 BCE in core, redacted later), ascribed to **Lagadha**, surviving in two recensions: the **Ṛgveda recension (Ārca Jyotiṣa, 36 verses)** and the longer **Yajurveda recension (Yājuṣa Jyotiṣa, 43 verses)**. Its purpose is explicitly **calendrical/ritual** — computing tithis, the 5-year *yuga* luni-solar cycle, intercalary months — **not predictive astrology**. It tracks the **Sun and Moon against the 27 nakṣatras** and does **not yet use the 12 rāśis**, which enter later. (Source: [Dharmawiki *Vedanga Jyotisha*](https://dharmawiki.org/index.php/Vedanga_Jyotisha_(%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%99%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D).)
- **The Siddhāntas** — the mathematically mature class of texts emerging around the start of the Common Era; Varāhamihira's **Pañcasiddhāntikā** (6th c. CE) summarises the five then-current: **Sūrya (Saura), Paitāmaha (Brahma), Vāsiṣṭha, Romaka, and Pauliśa** Siddhāntas. (Source: [Dharmawiki](https://dharmawiki.org/index.php/Vedanga_Jyotisha_(%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%99%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D).)
- **Sūrya Siddhānta** — the surviving cornerstone of Hindu mathematical astronomy (extant recension ~ mid 1st millennium CE), framed as revealed by the **Sun-god (Sūrya)** to the asura **Maya**. It gives planetary mean motions, the diameter/circumference of the Earth (treated as a sphere), eclipse and parallax computation, and the **Mahāyuga of 4,320,000 years** as the master cycle that synchronises all planetary revolutions — tying jyotiṣa's astronomy directly to the [yuga system](yugas-and-mahayuga.md) and the [units of time](units-of-time.md). (Sources: [Vic DiCara](https://vicdicara.blog/2023/01/04/sidereal-zodiac-in-surya-siddhanta/); [Snowcrest *Yuga Cycles*](http://www.snowcrest.net/sunrise/aayugacycles.htm).)
- **Foundational horā texts:** **Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra** (attrib. sage Parāśara — the root natal text), **Bṛhat Jātaka** & **Bṛhat Saṃhitā** (Varāhamihira), **Sārāvalī** (Kalyāṇavarmā), **Phaladīpikā** (Mantreśvara), **Jaimini Sūtra** (the Jaimini system). (Source: [Wikipedia *Hindu astrology*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_astrology).)

## 7. Precession, ayanāṃśa & yuga-timekeeping

This is the technical heart that links the zodiac to deep cosmic time:

- **Precession of the equinoxes** — the slow westward drift of the equinoctial point against the fixed stars, ~**50.3″ per year** (one full circle ≈ 25,772 years). The Sūrya Siddhānta and Hindu tradition register this as the stars/signs "lagging behind." (Source: [Indian Astrology Software blog](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/ayanamsa-and-the-sidereal-zodiac/).)
- **Ayanāṃśa** (अयनांश, "precessional component") — the angular gap between the **tropical zodiac** (sāyana, anchored to the equinox) and the **sidereal zodiac** (nirayana, anchored to the fixed stars). It grows ~50″/year. The two zodiacs **coincided once** — the Sūrya Siddhānta's reference epoch is conventionally taken near **499 CE (zero ayanāṃśa at Meṣa-saṅkrānti)**; today the gap is roughly **24°**. (Source: [Indian Astrology Software blog](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/ayanamsa-and-the-sidereal-zodiac/).)
- **Hindu jyotiṣa is sidereal (nirayana)** — it measures signs from a fixed star-point, **unlike modern Western tropical astrology**, which is the single largest practical divergence between the two systems. (Source: [Vic DiCara](https://vicdicara.blog/2023/01/04/sidereal-zodiac-in-surya-siddhanta/).)
- *Ayanāṃśa value variants:* the choice of zero-point is **not unique** and is an open scholarly dispute — **Lahiri / Citrapakṣa** (the Indian government's official ayanāṃśa, anchored to Citrā/Spica), **Raman**, **Kṛṣṇamūrti (KP)**, **Fagan-Bradley**, and **Sūrya-Siddhānta** ayanāṃśas differ by up to ~2°. (Source: [Indian Astrology Software blog](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/ayanamsa-and-the-sidereal-zodiac/).)
- *Precession-model variants:* the **Sūrya Siddhānta** itself appears to describe a **trepidation / libration (dolana)** model — the ayanāṃśa oscillating between roughly **+27° and −27°** rather than circulating endlessly — whereas modern astronomy holds precession to be **continuous/circular**. This is a genuine doctrinal-vs-empirical divergence. [scholarly] [disputed] (Source: [Indian Astrology Software blog](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/ayanamsa-and-the-sidereal-zodiac/).)
- **Yuga-synchronisation:** the Sūrya Siddhānta sets all planets in conjunction at the start of the Kali-yuga (traditional **18 Feb 3102 BCE**) and uses the 4,320,000-year Mahāyuga as the least common period of their revolutions — so the planetary clock and the cosmological clock are, by construction, the same clock. (Source: [Snowcrest *Yuga Cycles*](http://www.snowcrest.net/sunrise/aayugacycles.htm).)

## 8. Traditional jyotiṣa vs modern astronomy [scholarly]

- **Shared root, divergent aim:** both descend from Siddhāntic *gola/gaṇita*; modern astronomy kept the empirical-predictive geometry and discarded the *phalita* (omen/destiny) interpretation, while jyotiṣa retained both as one science.
- **Geocentric vs heliocentric:** Siddhāntic models are **geocentric** with epicyclic corrections (manda/śīghra) that *numerically* approximate Kepler-accurate positions, but the **frame** is Earth-centred; modern astronomy is heliocentric.
- **Nine "grahas" ≠ nine planets:** in jyotiṣa **Sun and Moon are grahas**, **Rāhu/Ketu are computed nodes**, and the three telescopic outer planets are absent — so the jyotiṣa roster is **not** the modern planetary list. [scholarly]
- **Sidereal vs tropical:** the ~24° ayanāṃśa means a person "born under Aries" tropically is typically jyotiṣically **Pisces** — the systems disagree on sign by nearly a full sign. (Source: [Vic DiCara](https://vicdicara.blog/2023/01/04/sidereal-zodiac-in-surya-siddhanta/).)
- **Status:** modern science classifies *phalita* (predictive) jyotiṣa as a pseudoscience, while recognising Siddhāntic *gaṇita* as a genuine pre-telescopic astronomical tradition; this file catalogues the *traditional* system as a knowledge-object, not an endorsement of its predictive claims. [scholarly]
