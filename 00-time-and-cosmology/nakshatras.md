# The 27 Nakṣatras

> Hierarchy location: `00-time-and-cosmology/` — the celestial "address system" that sits alongside the [units of time](./units-of-time.md). Where that file builds time from the truti upward, this file maps the *space* the Moon travels through: the 27 (or 28 with Abhijit) lunar mansions of the ecliptic. The presiding planets here are catalogued in full at [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md); the curse-frame deity Daksha lives in [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/sati-daksha-and-parvati.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/sati-daksha-and-parvati.md). See the section [README](./README.md) for how this node connects to the rest of cosmology.

A **nakṣatra** ("that which does not decay / a star-cluster") is a **lunar mansion** — a 13°20′ (i.e. 800′) slice of the 360° ecliptic, so that 27 × 13°20′ = 360°. The Moon, traversing the zodiac in roughly 27.3 days (its **sidereal month**), spends about one day in each mansion; the nakṣatra it occupies on a given day names that day. The list is **finite and closed at 27** for natal/sidereal purposes, with a famous **28th, Abhijit**, inserted for ritual timing. Each nakṣatra carries a fixed bundle: a **presiding deity (devatā)**, a **symbol**, a **ruling planet (nakṣatra-svāmī / lord)**, plus jyotiṣa attributes (gaṇa, yoni, nāḍī, varṇa, 4 pādas of 3°20′ each). The deity list is one of the oldest in the corpus — recited in **Taittirīya Saṃhitā 4.4.10** and **Atharvaveda 19.7** — and is what ties the sky-roster to Vedic theology. (Sources: Wikipedia *List of Nakshatras*; Taittirīya Saṃhitā 4.4.10.)

## 1. The myth: Candra's 27 wives & the Rohiṇī-favouritism curse

This is the foundational story that **personifies the nakṣatras as the 27 daughters of Dakṣa**, married collectively to **Candra / Soma** (the Moon-god, see [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md)). It is the etiological myth for the Moon's **phases**.

- **The 27 brides** — Dakṣa Prajāpati gave **all twenty-seven** of his nakṣatra-daughters to Candra in marriage on the condition that he treat them equally. They *are* the lunar mansions, so the Moon's monthly journey is literally a visit to each wife in turn. (Source: Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.6.)
- **Rohiṇī favoured** — Candra became enamoured of **Rohiṇī** (the 4th nakṣatra, the "red one," Aldebaran) and lingered with her, neglecting the other twenty-six. (Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa; *Sanskrit Srujan* retelling.)
- **The complaint & curse** — the slighted wives appealed to their father. **Dakṣa cursed Candra** with **kṣaya-roga** (wasting disease / "consumption," often read as tuberculosis), so that his brilliance would **dwindle away**. (Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.6; *Dharma Station*.)
- **The compromise — waxing & waning** — Candra wasted to nothing; the gods (and in some tellings Candra's own penance to Śiva at **Somnath / Prabhāsa**) interceded. Dakṣa, unable to revoke his word, **modulated** it: the Moon would **wane (kṣaya) for half the month and wax (vṛddhi) back for the other half**. This is the origin of **Kṛṣṇa Pakṣa** (waning fortnight) and **Śukla Pakṣa** (waxing fortnight). (Source: Bhāgavata Purāṇa; *Mythical Musings*.)
- **Śiva connection** — because Candra worshipped Śiva to be cured and Śiva wears the crescent (Candraśekhara), the waxing crescent is read as the "saved" portion of the Moon. (Source: Śiva Purāṇa; see [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md).)

- *Variant — count of wives (Source):* the **27** wives equal the 27 nakṣatras; a few late/folk retellings say **28** to include **Abhijit**, but the canonical Purāṇic myth fixes 27 (one per lunar mansion). [late text]
- *Variant — disease named (Source):* Bhāgavata calls it **rāja-yakṣma / kṣaya** (consumption); folk versions say simply "loss of light." [folk]
- *Variant — who interceded (Source):* Viṣṇu Purāṇa has the **wives + Dakṣa** relent; Śiva Purāṇa centres **Śiva's grace at Prabhāsa-tīrtha**. [disputed]

## 2. The 27 Nakṣatras — full roster

Each entry: **Name** (meaning / Western star) — *presiding deity*; *symbol*; *lord*; ecliptic span. Lords cycle in the fixed **Vimśottarī** order (Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rāhu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury) three times through the 27 — see §4. (Sources: Wikipedia *List of Nakshatras*; AnytimeAstro; Taittirīya Saṃhitā 4.4.10.)

1. **Aśvinī** ("the horse-twins"; β & γ Arietis) — *Aśvinī Kumāras* (twin physician-gods); *horse's head*; lord **Ketu**; 0°00′–13°20′ Meṣa. The "physicians of the devas," nakṣatra of healing & swift starts.
2. **Bharaṇī** ("the bearer"; 35/39/41 Arietis) — *Yama* (lord of death/dharma); *yoni* (female organ); lord **Venus**; 13°20′–26°40′ Meṣa. Bearing, restraint, transformation through death.
3. **Kṛttikā** ("the cutters / the Pleiades"; η Tauri) — *Agni* (fire); *knife / razor / flame*; lord **Sun**; 26°40′ Meṣa–10°00′ Vṛṣabha. The six Pleiad-nurses who suckled **Kārttikeya/Skanda** (see [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ganesha-and-kartikeya.md)).
4. **Rohiṇī** ("the red one"; Aldebaran, α Tauri) — *Brahmā* (in some lists *Prajāpati*); *cart / chariot / temple / banyan*; lord **Moon**; 10°00′–23°20′ Vṛṣabha. The Moon's **favourite wife** of §1; growth, fertility, beauty.
5. **Mṛgaśīrṣa / Mṛgaśiras** ("deer's head"; λ Orionis) — *Soma / Candra*; *deer's head*; lord **Mars**; 23°20′ Vṛṣabha–6°40′ Mithuna. Searching, the antelope's head; linked to the Prajāpati-as-deer chase myth.
6. **Ārdrā** ("the moist / storm"; Betelgeuse, α Orionis) — *Rudra* (the howler, storm-Śiva); *teardrop / diamond / human head*; lord **Rāhu**; 6°40′–20°00′ Mithuna. Destruction-before-renewal, fresh after rain.
7. **Punarvasu** ("return of the good / the two restorers"; Castor & Pollux) — *Aditi* (mother of the Ādityas); *bow & quiver*; lord **Jupiter**; 20°00′ Mithuna–3°20′ Karka. Renewal, return, boundless nurture.
8. **Puṣya** ("the nourisher / the blossom"; γ/δ/θ Cancri) — *Bṛhaspati* (guru of the gods, planet Jupiter); *cow's udder / lotus / arrow*; lord **Saturn**; 3°20′–16°40′ Karka. The most **auspicious** nakṣatra; nourishment, spiritual energy.
9. **Āśleṣā** ("the embrace / entwiner"; ε/δ/etc. Hydrae) — *Sarpas / Nāgas* (serpent-deities; see [`../08-beings-and-bestiary`](../08-beings-and-bestiary)); *coiled serpent*; lord **Mercury**; 16°40′–30°00′ Karka. The serpent's embrace; cunning, kuṇḍalinī, poison-and-medicine.
10. **Maghā** ("the mighty / the bountiful"; Regulus, α Leonis) — *Pitṛs* (the ancestral fathers/manes); *royal throne / palanquin*; lord **Ketu**; 0°00′–13°20′ Siṃha. Lineage, ancestry, royal power, inheritance.
11. **Pūrva Phalgunī** ("the former red one / fig"; δ & θ Leonis) — *Bhaga* (Āditya of fortune/enjoyment); *front legs of a bed / hammock / fig tree*; lord **Venus**; 13°20′–26°40′ Siṃha. Pleasure, marriage, relaxation, creativity.
12. **Uttara Phalgunī** ("the latter red one"; Denebola, β Leonis) — *Aryaman* (Āditya of patronage/contracts); *back legs of a bed / hammock*; lord **Sun**; 26°40′ Siṃha–10°00′ Kanyā. Patronage, friendship-contracts, beneficence.
13. **Hasta** ("the hand"; α–ε Corvi) — *Savitṛ / Sūrya* (the impeller Sun); *hand / closed fist / palm*; lord **Moon**; 10°00′–23°20′ Kanyā. Skill, craft, what is "in hand," dexterity.
14. **Citrā** ("the bright / brilliant"; Spica, α Virginis) — *Tvaṣṭṛ / Viśvakarmā* (divine architect; see [`../09-artifacts-symbols-and-arts`](../09-artifacts-symbols-and-arts)); *bright jewel / pearl*; lord **Mars**; 23°20′ Kanyā–6°40′ Tulā. Brilliance, design, ornament, illusion (māyā).
15. **Svātī** ("the self-going / independent / the sword"; Arcturus, α Boötis) — *Vāyu* (wind-god); *young plant-shoot blown by wind / coral / sword*; lord **Rāhu**; 6°40′–20°00′ Tulā. Independence, movement, trade, scattered like wind.
16. **Viśākhā** ("the forked / two-branched"; α & β Librae) — *Indra-Agni* (the dual deity Indrāgnī); *triumphal arch / forked branch / potter's wheel*; lord **Jupiter**; 20°00′ Tulā–3°20′ Vṛścika. Goal-focus, fork in the road, determined ambition.
17. **Anurādhā** ("the subsequent Rādhā / following success"; β/δ/π Scorpii) — *Mitra* (Āditya of friendship/contracts); *triumphal archway / lotus / staff*; lord **Saturn**; 3°20′–16°40′ Vṛścika. Friendship, devotion, balance, cooperation.
18. **Jyeṣṭhā** ("the eldest / senior"; Antares, α Scorpii) — *Indra* (king of the devas); *circular amulet / earring / umbrella*; lord **Mercury**; 16°40′–30°00′ Vṛścika. Seniority, protection, the "elder" who must be courageous.
19. **Mūla / Mūlā** ("the root"; λ & υ Scorpii, the Scorpion's tail) — *Nirṛti* (goddess of dissolution/calamity, alt. *Alakṣmī*); *bundle of roots / tied roots / elephant goad*; lord **Ketu**; 0°00′–13°20′ Dhanus. Getting to the root, destruction-of-the-old, the galactic centre region.
20. **Pūrva Āṣāḍhā** ("the former invincible"; δ & ε Sagittarii) — *Āpas / Āpaḥ* (the waters; alt. *Varuṇa*); *elephant tusk / hand-fan / winnowing basket*; lord **Venus**; 13°20′–26°40′ Dhanus. Invincibility, declarations, purifying water.
21. **Uttara Āṣāḍhā** ("the latter invincible"; ζ & σ Sagittarii) — *Viśvedevas* (the "All-Gods," collective); *elephant tusk / planks of a bed*; lord **Sun**; 26°40′ Dhanus–10°00′ Makara. Final victory, lasting accomplishment, righteousness.
22. **Śravaṇa** ("the ear / the hearing"; Altair, α/β/γ Aquilae) — *Viṣṇu* (alt. *Sarasvatī* in some lists); *ear / three footprints (of Viṣṇu's Trivikrama strides)*; lord **Moon**; 10°00′–23°20′ Makara. Listening, learning, fame through hearing (śruti).
23. **Dhaniṣṭhā / Śraviṣṭhā** ("the wealthiest / most famous"; α–δ Delphini) — *Eight Vasus* (the elemental gods; see [`../03-lineages-and-genealogies`](../03-lineages-and-genealogies)); *drum / damaru / flute*; lord **Mars**; 23°20′ Makara–6°40′ Kumbha. Music, rhythm, wealth, fame.
24. **Śatabhiṣā / Śatatārakā** ("the hundred healers / hundred stars"; γ Aquarii cluster) — *Varuṇa* (cosmic-order/waters god); *empty circle / hundred stars / hundred flowers*; lord **Rāhu**; 6°40′–20°00′ Kumbha. Healing, secrecy, the veiled hundred physicians.
25. **Pūrva Bhādrapadā** ("the former blessed feet / lucky one"; α & β Pegasi) — *Aja Ekapāda* ("the one-footed goat/unborn," a form of Rudra/Agni); *swords / two front legs of a funeral cot / man with two faces*; lord **Jupiter**; 20°00′ Kumbha–3°20′ Mīna. Fiery transformation, intensity, the "first" blessed feet.
26. **Uttara Bhādrapadā** ("the latter blessed feet"; γ Pegasi & α Andromedae) — *Ahirbudhnya* ("the serpent of the deep," a Rudra-form); *back legs of a funeral cot / twins / water-serpent*; lord **Saturn**; 3°20′–16°40′ Mīna. Depth, stability, the cosmic serpent's calm.
27. **Revatī** ("the wealthy / abundant"; ζ Piscium) — *Pūṣan* (Āditya, herdsman & protector of travellers/flocks); *pair of fish / drum*; lord **Mercury**; 16°40′–30°00′ Mīna. Nourishment, safe journeys, the shepherding "last" nakṣatra that ends the zodiac.

### Deity-attribution variants
- *Variant — Rohiṇī's deity (Source):* most jyotiṣa tables give **Brahmā / Prajāpati**; the Vedic *Taittirīya Saṃhitā* gives **Prajāpati** explicitly. [disputed]
- *Variant — Phalgunī pair (Source):* AnytimeAstro assigns **Pūrva → Aryaman, Uttara → Bhaga**; the older/common jyotiṣa convention reverses it (**Pūrva → Bhaga, Uttara → Aryaman/Sūrya**). Both are attested; the Vedic deities of the pair are simply "the two Bhaga/Aryaman lights." [disputed]
- *Variant — Śravaṇa's deity (Source):* **Viṣṇu** (standard) vs **Sarasvatī** (some Tamil/late tables) — the Vedic deity is **Viṣṇu** (the three-strides footprints). [late text]
- *Variant — Mūla's deity (Source):* **Nirṛti** (Vedic) vs **Alakṣmī / Pitṛs** in some popular lists. [folk]
- *Variant — Mṛgaśiras' deity (Source):* **Soma/Candra** (standard) vs **Marīci/Prajāpati** in deer-chase retellings. [disputed]
- *Variant — Citrā's deity (Source):* **Tvaṣṭṛ** (Vedic) = **Viśvakarmā** (Purāṇic) — same divine-architect figure under two names.

## 3. Abhijit — the 28th nakṣatra

- **Abhijit** ("the victorious / never-defeated"; **Vega, α Lyrae**) — *presiding deity* **Brahmā** (alt. *Hari/Viṣṇu* in some lists); *symbol* a **triangle / three-cornered nut**; *lord* often given as **Sun** or simply unassigned in Vimśottarī. It is inserted as a **short slice (~4°13′)** straddling the **end of Uttara Āṣāḍhā and the start of Śravaṇa**, conventionally **6°40′–10°53′20″ Makara**. (Sources: Wikipedia *Abhijit (nakshatra)*; VedicMarga.)
- **Why excluded from the 27** — Vega lies **well north of the ecliptic**, so the Moon never literally transits it; classical astronomers therefore kept the natal/sidereal scheme at **27** and treated Abhijit as supernumerary. (Source: Wikipedia.)
- **Mythic charter** — in the **Mahābhārata (Vana Parva / Āraṇyaka Parva)**, Abhijit is said to have **"slipped down" / descended** from the sky, contesting with Rohiṇī for seniority — narrated to **Skanda/Indra** as why an extra star was needed; it is the nakṣatra of "victory," patronised for warfare and undefeatable undertakings. (Source: Mahābhārata III; *Medium / GKM Astrology*.)
- **Ritual use — Abhijit Muhūrta** — a daily ~**48-minute** window centred on **local solar noon** (the 8th of the day's 15 muhūrtas), treated as **universally auspicious** for beginnings — independent of the *star* Abhijit. (Source: VedicMarga; AstroSight.)

- *Variant — count (Source):* "**28 nakṣatras**" is the **Atharvaveda / Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa** older count (Kṛttikā-led, including Abhijit); the "**27**" scheme (Aśvinī-led) became standard in classical siddhāntic astronomy. [disputed]
- *Variant — list head (Source):* the **oldest Vedic lists begin with Kṛttikā** (Pleiades, marking the vernal equinox c. 2000 BCE); precession later shifted the count to **begin with Aśvinī**. [scholarly]
- *Variant — Abhijit's lord (Source):* given as **Sun**, **Brahmā-as-graha**, or **none** depending on system. [disputed]

## 4. Role in jyotiṣa & muhūrta

- **Janma-nakṣatra (birth star)** — the nakṣatra the Moon occupies at birth defines a person's "star," used for naming syllables (each nakṣatra's 4 **pādas** assign birth-name syllables), gaṇa/yoni compatibility, and **Nakṣatra-doṣa** checks. (Source: Wikipedia.)
- **Vimśottarī Daśā** — the 120-year planetary-period system is **seeded by the janma-nakṣatra's lord**: the 27 nakṣatras map onto the **9 grahas** (each graha rules 3 nakṣatras), and the daśā sequence + balance at birth flows from there. Cross-link the grahas at [`../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/navagraha.md). (Source: jyotiṣa standard.)
- **Lord cycle (3 repetitions of 9)** — Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rāhu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury → (Aśvinī…Āśleṣā) → repeat (Maghā…Jyeṣṭhā) → repeat (Mūla…Revatī).
- **Muhūrta (electional timing)** — nakṣatras are graded for activities (e.g. **Puṣya** = best for almost everything except marriage; **Mūla, Jyeṣṭhā, Āśleṣā, Maghā** = "**Gaṇḍānta**"/sandhi junctions, treated cautiously). Daily **Abhijit Muhūrta** (§3) overrides many flaws. (Source: VedicMarga; AstroSight.)
- **Gaṇa / yoni / nāḍī classes** — each nakṣatra is tagged **Deva / Manuṣya / Rākṣasa gaṇa**, an animal **yoni** (for marriage matching), and one of three **nāḍīs** (used in the 36-point **Aṣṭakūṭa / Guṇa-Milan** compatibility score). [the full per-nakṣatra gaṇa/yoni/nāḍī grid is an open-ended jyotiṣa roster — flagged here, not exhaustively tabulated.] (Source: jyotiṣa standard.)
- **Festival anchoring** — many festivals are nakṣatra-fixed: e.g. **Onam** (Śravaṇa/Thiruvonam), **Kṛṣṇa Janmāṣṭamī** (Rohiṇī), **Rāma Navamī** (Punarvasu). [folk/regional]
