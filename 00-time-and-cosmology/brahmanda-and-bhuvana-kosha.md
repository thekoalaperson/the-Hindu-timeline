# The Brahmāṇḍa & Bhuvana-kośa — the Cosmic Egg and its Sheaths

> The single container that holds everything else in this section. The **Brahmāṇḍa** ("Brahmā's egg") is the *whole universe as one ovoid vessel*: a shell of **seven concentric elemental sheaths**, each ten times the last, enclosing the lotus-disc earth-plane below and the stacked heavens above, half-flooded by the **Garbhodaka** ocean on which **Viṣṇu** reclines — and itself only one bubble among **infinite** other eggs adrift in the **Causal Ocean**. The Purāṇic chapters that map this vessel are a named genre: the **bhuvana-kośa** ("world-sheath / world-treasury").

## 1. The image — egg, not sphere

The Purāṇas consistently picture the cosmos as an **egg (aṇḍa)**, not an abstract sphere: an ovoid with a hard, layered shell and a fluid interior, "Brahmā's egg" because the demiurge **Brahmā** is born inside it (from the lotus-navel of Viṣṇu) and his lifetime is co-extensive with it.

- **Brahmāṇḍa = Brahmā + aṇḍa** — "the egg of Brahmā." The term names *both* the physical universe-vessel and, by extension, an entire creation-cycle; the Purāṇa titled **Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa** takes its name from this cosmography. (*Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa*; *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 3.26, 5)
- **The golden womb-egg (Hiraṇyagarbha)** — in the older creation hymn the egg is the *Hiraṇyagarbha*, "golden womb," floating on the primordial waters for a cosmic year before splitting into heaven and earth; the Brahmāṇḍa cosmography is the developed Purāṇic descendant of this Vedic image. (*Ṛgveda* 10.121; *Manusmṛti* 1.5–13) — see [Cosmogony / Creation Accounts](./cosmogony-creation-accounts.md).
- **Co-extensive with Brahmā's life** — the egg exists for one **Brahmā lifetime** (100 Brahmā-years = a *Mahā-kalpa*) and dissolves at the **Prākṛta (elemental) pralaya**, when the seven sheaths reabsorb into prakṛti. See [Brahmā's Lifetime](./brahma-lifetime.md) and [Kalpa & Pralaya](./kalpa-and-pralaya.md).

## 2. The seven elemental sheaths (sapta-āvaraṇa) — the shell

The defining structural claim: the egg is wrapped in **seven concentric coverings (āvaraṇa)**, each the next *tattva* outward in the Sāṅkhya order of evolution, and **each ten times the thickness of the one it encloses**. This is the egg's "skeleton" and the feature these chapters add to the dvīpa/loka maps. (*Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 3.26.51–52; 5.20.38; **6.16.37**; cf. *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* 2.7)

- **1. Earth (bhūmi / pṛthivī)** — the innermost shell, immediately around the manifest world of dvīpas and lokas. The interior (dvīpas + lokas) is sometimes counted as bounded by this first covering at ~500 million yojanas across (see §3).
- **2. Water (ap / jala)** — ten times the earth-shell.
- **3. Fire (tejas / agni)** — ten times the water-shell.
- **4. Air (vāyu)** — ten times the fire-shell.
- **5. Ether / space (ākāśa / kha)** — ten times the air-shell.
- **6. Ego (ahaṅkāra / bhūtādi)** — the individuating principle; ten times the ether-shell. (The first of the two *subtle* sheaths, where the order leaves the gross elements and re-enters Sāṅkhya psychology.)
- **7. The great principle (mahat / mahā-tattva)** — cosmic intelligence/*buddhi*, the outermost and subtlest shell, ten times the ego-shell; beyond it is only unmanifest **prakṛti (pradhāna)**. (*Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 6.16.37; 3.26)

- **The order is Sāṅkhya evolution run in reverse-outward** — creation evolves *inward/downward* mahat → ahaṅkāra → ether → air → fire → water → earth → manifest world; the shells therefore read *outward* as earth → … → mahat, each sheath enclosing its own product. This is why the two "extra" sheaths (ahaṅkāra, mahat) are *psychic* tattvas wrapping the five *physical* ones. (*Bhāgavata* 3.26; cf. [Cosmogony / Creation Accounts](./cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) §Sāṅkhya tattvas)
- **Aggregate shell thickness** — because each layer is 10× the last, the seven sheaths together are astronomically thicker than the interior. Richard L. Thompson's reading of the *Bhāgavata* gives the **aggregate covering as on the order of ten million times the diameter** of the inner egg — i.e. the manifest world of dvīpas/lokas is a vanishing speck at the center of an immense layered shell. [scholarly]
  - *Variant (covering count):* some retellings collapse the gross five into a single "elements" shell and count fewer layers; the dominant *Bhāgavata* number is **seven**, matching the seven tattvas above.

## 3. Diameter, the interior, and the yojana problem

- **Bhū-maṇḍala ≈ 500 million yojanas across** — the horizontal lotus-disc of seven dvīpas/oceans spans the egg's interior at roughly **50 crore (500,000,000) yojanas**, "from one side of the Brahmāṇḍa to the other," per the *Bhāgavata*. This is the *interior* width; the sheaths lie outside it. (*Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 5.16; 5.20) — continents catalogued in [Bhāratavarṣa & Purāṇic Geography](../06-sacred-geography/bharatavarsha-and-puranic-geography.md).
  - *Variant (Viṣṇu Purāṇa total):* the *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* gives the **fourteen-fold bhuvana** (7 lokas + 7 pātālas) as extending over **50 crore yojanas** vertically, with **1 yojana ≈ 8 miles** in the traditional reckoning. (*Viṣṇu Purāṇa* 2.7, Wilson)
- **The yojana-length controversy** — the *yojana* has **no fixed value**; conversions swing the universe's physical size by orders of magnitude. [disputed]
  - **8 miles/yojana** — the traditional/Wilson value; gives Bhū-maṇḍala ≈ 4 billion miles across.
  - **~5 miles/yojana** — a common alternative (yojana = 4 *krośa*, krośa ≈ 1.25 mi).
  - **8.5 miles/yojana** — Thompson's adjusted value, chosen so the *Bhāgavata*'s disc-features align with geocentric planetary orbits (a deliberately *astronomical*, not geographic, reading). [scholarly]
  - **Two-clock caution** — these are figures *internal to scripture* (the cosmographic clock), not claims on the academic/astronomical clock; the dvīpas are a *mythic-symbolic* map, not a surveyed one. See note in [Bhāratavarṣa & Purāṇic Geography](../06-sacred-geography/bharatavarsha-and-puranic-geography.md).

## 4. The interior fluid — the Garbhodaka ocean & the reclining Viṣṇu

The egg is not solid inside: its **lower half is flooded** by the *Garbhodaka* ("womb-water") ocean, on which the universe-maintaining form of Viṣṇu lies.

- **Garbhodaka ocean** — fills the bottom half of the Brahmāṇḍa; the seven lower worlds (pātālas) and the disc rest in relation to it, and the manifest cosmos is built upward from its surface. (*Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 3.8; 5)
- **Garbhodaka-śāyī Viṣṇu** — the form of Viṣṇu reclining *within this egg* on the womb-ocean; from his navel-lotus is born **Brahmā**, who then builds the dvīpas and lokas. He is the **per-universe** Viṣṇu — one inside *each* egg. (*Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 3.8; *Brahma-saṁhitā* 5.51) — see [Trimūrti & Tridevī](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/trimurti-and-tridevi.md) and [Viṣṇu's Forms & Vyūhas](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/vishnu-forms-and-vyuhas.md).
- **Distinction of the three Viṣṇus** — *Kāraṇodaka-śāyī (Mahā-Viṣṇu)* lies on the Causal Ocean *outside* the eggs (§5); *Garbhodaka-śāyī* lies *inside each* egg on the womb-ocean; *Kṣīrodaka-śāyī* (the milk-ocean Viṣṇu / Paramātmā) pervades every atom and being. (*Brahma-saṁhitā* 5; ISKCON systematization) [late text]

## 5. Infinite eggs in the Causal Ocean

The single Brahmāṇḍa is **not unique**: Purāṇic and Vaiṣṇava cosmology insists on **innumerable** universes, each a separate egg, each with its own Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva.

- **The Causal (Kāraṇa) Ocean** — beyond all the eggs lies the *Kāraṇārṇava*; on it reclines **Mahā-Viṣṇu (Kāraṇodaka-śāyī)**. (*Brahma-saṁhitā* 5.47–48)
- **Eggs from the pores of Mahā-Viṣṇu** — when Mahā-Viṣṇu **exhales**, millions of Brahmāṇḍas issue from the pores of his body and float on the Causal Ocean; when he **inhales**, they are withdrawn. The full lifetime of any one egg (a *Mahā-kalpa*, 100 Brahmā-years) equals **a single out-breath** of Mahā-Viṣṇu — nesting [Brahmā's Lifetime](./brahma-lifetime.md) inside a still vaster clock. (*Brahma-saṁhitā* 5.48) [late text]
- **"Bubbles in the ocean"** — the eggs are likened to bubbles rising and bursting; "innumerable Brahmās rising and disappearing." (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, citing *Bhāgavata*) [late text]
- **Plurality already in the Mahāpurāṇas** — the *Bhāgavata* and others affirm worlds "beyond counting," each with its own demiurge; the breathing-Mahā-Viṣṇu image is the Gauḍīya-Vaiṣṇava sharpening of this older theme. (*Bhāgavata Purāṇa* 6.16; 10.14) [disputed: degree of multiplicity by school]

## 6. The bhuvana-kośa as a Purāṇic genre

"**Bhuvana-kośa**" ("sheath/treasury of worlds") is the technical name for the cosmographic *chapters* — a recurring, near-formulaic textual block describing dvīpas, oceans, mountains, lokas, pātālas and the egg's shell. Knowing the genre lets one *triangulate* the systematic disagreements catalogued in the geography and lokas files.

- **Viṣṇu Purāṇa — Book 2, ch. 1–7** — the most influential early bhuvana-kośa; dvīpas (ch. 1–4), lower worlds/pātālas and the egg's elemental coverings (ch. 5–7). (Wilson trans.)
- **Bhāgavata Purāṇa — Canto 5, ch. 16–26** — the most detailed; Bhū-maṇḍala dimensions (16–20), planetary/heavenly arrangement (21–24), Śiva/Ananta and the pātālas (25–26); the seven sheaths recur at 3.26 and 6.16.
- **Vāyu Purāṇa — ch. 32–53** (approx.) — one of the oldest cosmographic strata, closely parallel to *Brahmāṇḍa*.
- **Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa** — named for, and structured around, the egg; among the earliest and most extensive cosmographies.
- **Agni Purāṇa — ch. 108** — explicitly titled *Bhuvana-kośa*; compact account of the seven continents, oceans and Meru. (*Agni Purāṇa* 108)
- **Matsya Purāṇa** (ch. ~113–129 region) and **Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa** (the *Bhuvana-kośa* section, ch. ~54–58) — further parallels, often siding with the *Viṣṇu/Vāyu* recension against the *Bhāgavata*. [open-ended: chapter boundaries vary by edition]
- **Mahābhārata — Bhīṣma-parva (Jambūkhaṇḍa-nirmāṇa-parva)** — the epic's own bhuvana-kośa, a pre-Purāṇic witness to the same map.
- **Genre caution** — the texts **disagree systematically** on dvīpa rulers, ocean liquids, varṣa names and presiding deities; the egg-shell scheme (§2) is most fully articulated in the *Bhāgavata*. Treat any single roster as one recension among several. [open-ended roster]

## 7. How the three maps nest (synthesis)

- **Horizontal map** → the *sapta-dvīpa* lotus-disc = the **floor/interior** of the egg → catalogued in [Bhāratavarṣa & Purāṇic Geography](../06-sacred-geography/bharatavarsha-and-puranic-geography.md).
- **Vertical map** → the *14 lokas* stacked on **[Meru](../06-sacred-geography/meru-kailash-and-cosmic-mountains.md)** = the **axis** standing in the interior → catalogued in [Devas, Asuras & the 14 Lokas](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).
- **Shell** → the *seven elemental sheaths* (§2) = the **wall** around both, unique to this node.
- **Fluid** → the *Garbhodaka* ocean (§4) = the **water** the floor rests in, with Viṣṇu reclining.
- **Beyond** → infinite eggs in the *Causal Ocean* (§5) = the **outside**, breathed out by Mahā-Viṣṇu.
- **Time** → the whole egg lasts one **[Brahmā lifetime](./brahma-lifetime.md)** = one **breath** of Mahā-Viṣṇu, then dissolves at the elemental **[pralaya](./kalpa-and-pralaya.md)** back through the sheaths into prakṛti.
