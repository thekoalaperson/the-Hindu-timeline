- **Gaṇeśa-caturthī moon-curse aetiology** — A vrata-kathā frame explains Kṛṣṇa's slander as the fruit of seeing the moon on Gaṇeśa-caturthī, and prescribes reciting the Syamantaka story to avert false accusation _(actors: Gaṇeśa, Candra, Kṛṣṇa)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/syamantaka-jewel.md) — (Gaṇeśa-Purāṇa / vrata-kathā tradition attached to Bhāgavata 10.56–57)
- **Sending the seven clouds and the great flood** — Śiva commands the seven clouds to deluge the land (the Vaikai flood), showing mastery of the elements over the Pāṇḍya realm _(actors: Śiva)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-tiruvilaiyadal-64-lilas.md) — (Tiruviḷaiyāḍal Purāṇam)
- **Āstīka's snakebite-protection blessing** — The grateful serpents grant Āstīka a boon; he decrees that whoever recalls his name shall fear no snakes—the charm tied to Nāga Pañcamī _(actors: Āstīka, the nāgas)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/astika-sarpa-satra.md) — (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva 58; Nāga Pañcamī folk tradition)
- **Grace-sports: curing the king, granting children, restoring devotees** — A cluster of miracle-sports in which Śiva heals the Pāṇḍya, grants heirs, and answers devotees' vows _(actors: Śiva, Pāṇḍya king, devotees)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-tiruvilaiyadal-64-lilas.md) — (Tiruviḷaiyāḍal Purāṇam)
- **Recitation of the Mahābhārata at the sacrifice** — With the snakes spared, Vyāsa's pupil Vaiśampāyana narrates the entire Mahābhārata to Janamejaya at this very session, and Sauti later retells it at Naimiṣāraṇya _(actors: Vaiśampāyana, Janamejaya, Vyāsa, Ugraśravas Sauti)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/astika-sarpa-satra.md) — (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva 1, 4–5, 54–58)
- **Disciplining rival sects / the Jain (Samaṇa) contests** — Sports vindicating Śaivism against Jain (Samaṇa) opponents at Madurai, the sectarian-polemic layer of the cycle _(actors: Śiva, Tamil Śaiva saints, Jain opponents)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-tiruvilaiyadal-64-lilas.md) — (Tiruviḷaiyāḍal Purāṇam; Tamil Śaiva hagiography)
- **The Dharma Daivas Appear at Birmanna Pergade's House** — Four guardian spirits of Dharma test and are pleased by the Jain chieftain Birmanna Pergade's hospitality, then ask him in a dream to dedicate his home to their worship, founding Dharmasthala _(actors: Kalarahu, Kalarkayi, Kumarasvami, Kanyakumari, Birmanna Pergade)_ → [details](../06-sacred-geography/sthala-puranas-major-temples.md) — (Dharmasthala daiva legend)
- **Arjuna Returns Gandiva to the Waters** — After Krishna's death, his powers gone, Arjuna casts Gandiva back into Varuna's waters on the final journey and ascends north toward Svarga _(actors: Arjuna)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/characters/arjuna.md) — (Mahabharata, Mahaprasthanika Parva)
- **Birth of Hariharaputra (Shiva + Mohini)** — After the Samudra Manthana, Vishnu reassumes the Mohini form for Shiva, and from their union the divine child Sasta/Ayyappa is born, deliberately to produce the being able to slay the demoness Mahishi _(actors: Shiva, Vishnu-as-Mohini, Ayyappa)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Brahmanda Purana, Bhutanatha Upakhyana; Bhagavata Purana 8; Keralan tradition)
- **Mahishi's boon of unslayability** — The buffalo-demoness Mahishi, sister of Mahishasura, does penance and Brahma grants that she can be killed only by a being born of both Vishnu and Shiva — the engine of Ayyappa's birth _(actors: Mahishi, Brahma)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Pandalam/Keralan legend)
- **The infant Ayyappa left on the Pampa** — The divine child is left on the banks of the Pampa river with a golden bell at his neck, to be found and adopted as Manikandan by the childless King Rajashekhara of Pandalam _(actors: Ayyappa/Manikandan, King Rajashekhara)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan Pandalam legend)
- **The tiger's-milk plot against Manikandan** — Resentful of the favored foundling, the queen feigns an illness curable only by tigress's milk, sending Manikandan on a seemingly fatal forest errand _(actors: Manikandan, the queen, the Diwan)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan Pandalam legend)
- **Ayyappa slays Mahishi** — In the forest Manikandan confronts and kills the demoness Mahishi (often on the Azhutha river), fulfilling his cosmic mission; her death-dance is commemorated _(actors: Ayyappa/Manikandan, Mahishi)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan legend)
- **Janamejaya's Snake-Sacrifice** — Avenging his father Parikshit, killed by Takshaka, Janamejaya holds the sarpasatra, the occasion at which the Mahabharata is first recited by Vaishampayana _(actors: Janamejaya, Takshaka, Vaishampayana)_ → [details](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md) — (Mahabharata Adi Parva)
- **Ayyappa returns riding a tiger** — Manikandan returns to Pandalam astride a tiger (Indra and the devas in tiger-forms), revealing his divinity and fulfilling the milk-errand _(actors: Ayyappa/Manikandan, Indra, the devas)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan legend)
- **Kali Yuga begins (3102 BCE)** — The Age of Strife begins at Krishna's departure with dharma on a single leg; chanting the divine name becomes the prescribed yuga-dharma _(actors: Krishna, humanity)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md) — (Surya Siddhanta, Bhagavata Purana 12.2-3)
- **Founding of the Sabarimala shrine** — Ayyappa shoots an arrow and ordains a temple be built where it lands — at Sabari, where the Ramayana ascetic Sabari awaited Rama — then merges into the deity-form _(actors: Ayyappa, King Rajashekhara)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan legend)
- **Onset of Kali Yuga, Parikṣit restrains personified Kali** — Kali Yuga formally begins as Kṛṣṇa leaves the earth (traditionally 3102 BCE); Parikṣit confronts and restrains a personified Kali _(actors: Parikṣit, Kali)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 1.16-17)
- **Curse of Leela into demonic form** — The celestial woman Leela is cursed to buffalo/demonic form (becoming Mahishi) after refusing to return to the divine realm with sage Dattatreya, and is freed upon her death at Ayyappa's hands _(actors: Leela, Dattatreya, Ayyappa)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan supplementary tradition)
- **Parikṣit cursed to die in seven days** — Parikṣit is cursed by the boy-brahmana Sṛṅgi to die in seven days by the snake Takṣaka, the dramatic engine for the rest of the Bhagavata _(actors: Parikṣit, Sṛṅgi, Takṣaka)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 1)
- **Malikappurathamma's deferred marriage proposal** — The goddess Malikappurathamma wishes to marry Ayyappa, who replies he will wed her only when no first-time pilgrim comes to Sabarimala — forever deferring the marriage and preserving his celibacy _(actors: Malikappurathamma, Ayyappa)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan legend)
- **Suka teaches Parikṣit on the Ganga** — Sukadeva Gosvami speaks the bulk of the Bhagavata to King Parikṣit on the bank of the Ganga during the seven days before his death _(actors: Sukadeva, Parikṣit)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 2-12)
- **The great departure of the Pāṇḍavas** — The Pāṇḍavas and Draupadī walk toward Mount Meru and fall one by one; only Yudhiṣṭhira and a dog (Dharma in disguise) reach heaven's gate _(actors: Yudhiṣṭhira, Pāṇḍavas, Draupadī, Dharma)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/smriti-itihasa-epics.md) — (Mahābhārata 17 (Mahāprasthānika Parva))
- **Ayyappa defeats the bandit Udayanan** — Aided by his companion Vavar and local forces, Ayyappa defeats the forest robber-chief Udayanan _(actors: Ayyappa, Vavar, Udayanan)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/ayyappa.md) — (Keralan temple/pilgrimage tradition)
- **Yudhiṣṭhira's heaven-test and ascension** — Yudhiṣṭhira is tested by an illusion of his kin in hell/heaven, then reconciled, and the heroes attain their celestial states _(actors: Yudhiṣṭhira)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/smriti-itihasa-epics.md) — (Mahābhārata 18 (Svargārohaṇa Parva))
- **Ugrashravas recites the Mahabharata to the Naimisha sages** — The outermost frame of the Gita is Ugrashravas' recitation of the epic to the sages of the Naimisha forest, nesting Vaishampayana's and Sanjaya's reports within it _(actors: Ugrashravas, Naimisha sages)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/bhagavad-gita/README.md) — (Mahabharata frame narrative)
- **Future-dynasty prophecy** — Suka foretells the post-Mahabharata kings (Magadha, Nanda, Maurya, Sunga, Andhra) down into Kali in the prophetic past tense _(actors: Sukadeva)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12)
- **Death of Parikshit by Takshaka** — King Parikshit fearlessly meets death by the serpent Takshaka's bite, closing the frame, followed by Janamejaya's snake-sacrifice _(actors: Parikshit, Takshaka, Janamejaya)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/bhagavata-tales-catalog.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12.5-6)
- **Manu's law-code narrated to the sages** — The Manava-Dharmasastra (Laws of Manu) is narrated by Manu to the sages, codifying dharma, varna, and ashrama for the current age _(actors: Manu, sages)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/agamas-tantras-dharmashastras.md) — (Manusmṛti)
- **The Pandavas' Great Departure (Mahaprasthana)** — Hearing of Krishna's death, the Pandavas crown Parikshit and walk north toward the Himalayas, all dying on the way except Yudhishthira, who ascends to heaven with a dog who is Dharma in disguise _(actors: the Pandavas, Parikshit, Yudhishthira, Dharma)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/03-dvapara-yuga.md) — (Mahabharata Mahaprasthanika and Svargarohana Parvas)
- **Kalki avatara ends Kali Yuga** — Kali Yuga closes with the Kalki avatara, who destroys adharma and ushers in a new Satya Yuga, restarting the Mahayuga _(actors: Kalki)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12.2; Kalki Purana; Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **Śeṣa incarnates as Patañjali** — Ādiśeṣa takes birth as the sage Patañjali, falling as a tiny serpent into the cupped hands of his mother Goṇikā, to witness Śiva's Tāṇḍava and honour him as first teacher of yoga _(actors: Śeṣa, Patañjali, Goṇikā)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/shesha-ananta.md) — (Vaiṣṇava & Yoga tradition; Patañjali hagiography)
- **Buddha avatar deludes the asuras** — Vishnu descends in ascetic Buddha form, in one reading to delude the asuras away from the Vedic path so the devas can defeat them, and in another to stop Vedic animal sacrifice out of compassion _(actors: Buddha (Vishnu), the asuras)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 1.3.24; Vishnu Purana 3.17-18; Gita Govinda)
- **Jīmūtavāhana offers his body to Garuḍa** — The compassionate Vidyādhara prince Jīmūtavāhana offers his own body to Garuḍa in place of a Nāga victim, halting Garuḍa's serpent-slaughter _(actors: Jīmūtavāhana, Garuḍa)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/vidyadharas-siddhas-and-spirits.md) — (Kathāsaritsāgara; Nāgānanda)
- **Naravāhanadatta consecrated emperor of the Vidyādharas** — The mortal-born prince Naravāhanadatta wins the vidyās and is consecrated Cakravartin emperor of the Vidyādharas across the Kathāsaritsāgara _(actors: Naravāhanadatta)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/vidyadharas-siddhas-and-spirits.md) — (Kathāsaritsāgara)
- **Tulasī Vivāha — annual marriage of Tulsī to Viṣṇu** — The ceremonial marriage of Tulsī to Viṣṇu/Śālagrāma is performed each year from Prabodhinī Ekādaśī to Kārttika Pūrṇimā, ending Cāturmāsya and opening the wedding season _(actors: Tulsī, Viṣṇu)_ → [details](../09-artifacts-symbols-and-arts/sacred-flora-and-fauna.md) — (Tulasi in Hinduism (Wikipedia))
- **Vaishampayana recites the Anugita to Janamejaya** — The Anugita reaches the world as Vaishampayana's recitation to King Janamejaya at the snake-sacrifice, reporting the postwar exchange between Krishna and Arjuna _(actors: Vaishampayana, Janamejaya)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/anugita.md) — (Mahabharata)
- **Caṇḍeśa made lord of the gaṇas' provisions** — In the Nāyanār tradition the boy-saint Caṇḍeśa strikes his own father to defend Śiva-worship and is made steward and lord of the gaṇas' provisions _(actors: Caṇḍeśa, Śiva)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/vidyadharas-siddhas-and-spirits.md) — (Śaiva Siddhānta)
