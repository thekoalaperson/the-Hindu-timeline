*Events of the six manvantaras before ours, and the primordial kings/sages (Dhruva, Pṛthu, Vena, Daksha).*

---

- **Aged Śaibyā bears Vidarbha** — In time the old queen conceives and bears Vidarbha, the miracle son whose birth makes the king's blurted promise true _(actors: Śaibyā, Jyāmagha, Vidarbha)_ → [details](../03-lineages-vamsha/jyamagha.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa IX.23.38; Viṣṇu Purāṇa IV.12; Matsya Purāṇa 44)

- **Aṅga Abandons the Throne over Vena** — King Aṅga weds Sunīthā, daughter of Death, whose evil grandson Vena is born; despairing of his wicked son, Aṅga abandons the throne for the forest _(actors: Aṅga, Sunīthā, Vena)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/prithu-prithvi.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 4.13; Vishnu Purana 1.13)
- **Ajāmila's fall and life of sin** — The virtuous brāhmaṇa Ajāmila is ruined by lust for a dāsī, abandons his wife and dharma, and lives by theft and deceit, naming his youngest son Nārāyaṇa _(actors: Ajāmila)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/ajamila.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.1)
- **Atri and Anasūyā's austerity for a divine son** — The sage Atri and his wife Anasūyā perform severe tapas seeking a son equal to the Supreme, and the Trimūrti grant that portions of all three will be born to them _(actors: Atri, Anasūyā, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/dattatreya.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1; Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa)

- **Avatar Vaikuntha creates the realm of Vaikuntha** — Vishnu's avatar Vaikuntha, born of his own accord, creates his eternal abode Vaikuntha at the request of his consort Rama (Lakshmi) _(actors: Vaikuntha (Vishnu), Rama (Lakshmi))_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-05-raivata.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.5)

- **Betrothal of Ruru and Pramadvarā** — The Bhārgava youth Ruru is betrothed to Pramadvarā, foundling daughter of the apsarā Menakā reared by sage Sthūlakeśa, and a wedding-day is fixed _(actors: Ruru, Pramadvarā, Pramati, Sthūlakeśa, Menakā, Viśvāvasu)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/ruru-pramadvara.md) — (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva, Pauloma sub-parva (~1.8–12))
- **Bhṛgu kicks Viṣṇu's chest, origin of the Śrīvatsa mark** — The sage Bhṛgu kicks Viṣṇu on the chest to test which of the three gods is greatest (the trimūrti-test). - **Birth of Andhaka from Parvati's covering of Shiva's eyes** — Parvati playfully covers Shiva's three eyes, plunging the cosmos into darkness; a drop of her sweat becomes the blind, deformed boy Andhaka _(actors: Parvati, Shiva, Andhaka)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/andhakasura-vadha.md) — (Shiva Purana (Rudra-samhita); Matsya Purana; Kurma Purana)
- **Birth of Cyavana** — The Rākṣasa Puloman abducts the pregnant Pulomā, and the unborn Bhārgava child slips prematurely from the womb (hence 'Cyavana'), his radiance burning the Rākṣasa to ashes _(actors: Bhṛgu, Pulomā, Puloman, Cyavana)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/cyavana-sukanya.md) — (Mahābhārata Ādi Parva 5–6)

- **Birth of Kakshivat from the substituted nurse** — Queen Sudeshna sends her shudra nurse to the blind sage in her place, and on her Dirghatamas begets eleven brahmin sons headed by the future rishi Kakshivat (Aushija) _(actors: Dirghatamas, Sudeshna, the dasi nurse, Kakshivat)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/dirghatamas.md) — (Mahabharata Adi Parva ~104; Rigveda 1.116-126)

- **The Prachetas' quarrel with the trees and birth of the second Daksha** — Emerging to find the Earth overgrown, the Prachetas nearly burn the forests until Soma mediates and gives them the tree-born maiden Marisha, from whom the reborn Daksha is born _(actors: The Prachetas, Soma, Marisha, Daksha)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-01-svayambhuva.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.15; Bhagavata Purana 4.30)
- **The two queens bear five sons** — Yayāti secretly fathers children on Śarmiṣṭhā despite the oath; Devayānī bears Yadu and Turvaśu, Śarmiṣṭhā bears Druhyu, Anu, and Pūru — the breach that later provokes the curse of old age and splits the Lunar dynasty _(actors: Devayānī, Śarmiṣṭhā, Yayāti)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/characters/kacha-devayani.md) — (Mahābhārata Ādi Parva 83-84; Bhāgavata 9.18)
- **Viśvāmitra wars with Vasiṣṭha over the wish-cow** — The kṣatriya king Viśvāmitra covets Vasiṣṭha's wish-cow Śabalā, wars with him and loses, then begins millennia of tapas to become a brahmarṣi _(actors: Viśvāmitra, Vasiṣṭha)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/ramayana/1-bala-kanda.md) — (Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa 51–65)
- **Vīrabhadra destroys Dakṣa's sacrifice** — After Satī self-immolates at her father Dakṣa's yajña, the grief-maddened Śiva creates Vīrabhadra, who storms the sacrifice and beheads Dakṣa; Satī's scattered limbs become the Śakti Pīṭhas _(actors: Śiva, Satī, Dakṣa, Vīrabhadra)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md) — (Śiva Purāṇa; Devī-Bhāgavata Purāṇa; Kālikā Purāṇa; Vāyu Purāṇa)
- **Triśaṅku raised to heaven and the counter-heaven** — Viśvāmitra raises the outcast king Triśaṅku bodily to heaven and, when the gods cast him down, creates a counter-heaven holding him mid-sky _(actors: Viśvāmitra, Triśaṅku)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/ramayana/1-bala-kanda.md) — (Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa 57–60)
- **Birth of Budha and the Tara Scandal** — Candra abducts Tara, wife of Brihaspati, triggering the Tarakamaya war; Tara bears Budha of disputed paternity, and the Moon's waning is linked to a resulting curse _(actors: Candra, Tara, Brihaspati, Budha)_ → [details](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md) — (Vishnu Purana IV.6; Bhagavata IX.14)
- **Śunaḥśepa saved from sacrifice** — The human-sacrifice victim Śunaḥśepa is saved by mantras Viśvāmitra gives him at Ambarīṣa's rite _(actors: Śunaḥśepa, Viśvāmitra, Ambarīṣa)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/ramayana/1-bala-kanda.md) — (Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa 61–62)
- **Dhruva's Penance at Madhuvana** — The boy Dhruva performs tapas at Dhruva-ṭīlā in Madhuvana, one of the twelve forests of Braj _(actors: Dhruva)_ → [details](../06-sacred-geography/mathura-vrindavan-braj.md) — (Vraja-mahatmya)
- **Menakā breaks Viśvāmitra's penance; birth of Śakuntalā** — The apsarā Menakā seduces Viśvāmitra, begetting Śakuntalā; he later conquers desire and anger to win the title Brahmarṣi _(actors: Menakā, Viśvāmitra, Śakuntalā)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/ramayana/1-bala-kanda.md) — (Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa 63–65)
- **Savitri wins back Satyavan from Yama** — The devoted wife out-argues Yama to win back her husband's life _(actors: Savitri, Satyavan, Yama)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/mahabharata-minor-episodes.md) — (Mahabharata Vana Parva, Pativrata-mahatmya Parva)
- **Daksha populates the worlds through his daughters** — Daksha Prajapati's daughters, married to the sages and to Kashyapa, become mothers of the Adityas (gods), Daityas, Danavas, animals, nagas and all living kinds; he later resorts to sexual generation _(actors: Daksha, Kashyapa, Aditi, Diti, Danu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.15, 1.21; Bhagavata Purana 6.6)
- **Indra seduces Ahalyā and is cursed** — Indra, disguised as the sage Gautama, seduces his wife Ahalyā; Gautama curses Indra and condemns Ahalyā to penance to be released only by Rāma _(actors: Indra, Ahalyā, Gautama)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/ramayana/1-bala-kanda.md) — (Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa 48)
- **Galava and Madhavi** — Sage Galava lends out the princess Madhavi, who bears a king to each of four lords while regaining her virginity each time, to gather the guru-dakshina horses _(actors: Galava, Madhavi, Vishvamitra)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/mahabharata-minor-episodes.md) — (Mahabharata Udyoga Parva, Bhagavadyana Parva)
- **Yayati's Exchange of Old Age** — Cursed with premature old age by Shukra for wronging Devayani, Yayati borrows youth from his son Puru, later returns it and crowns Puru over the elder Yadu _(actors: Yayati, Shukra, Devayani, Puru, Yadu)_ → [details](../03-lineages-vamsha/suryavamsha-and-chandravamsha.md) — (Mahabharata Adi Parva; Vishnu Purana IV.10; Bhagavata IX.18-19)
- **Pururavas and Urvasi** — The Lunar-line tale of Pururavas and the apsara Urvasi, a morality story on lust and renunciation _(actors: Pururavas, Urvasi)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 9)
- **Sudyumna/Ila's Monthly Sex-Change** — Vaivasvata Manu's child enters Shiva's forbidden grove and alternates monthly between man (Sudyumna) and woman (Ila), branching the solar and lunar lines _(actors: Sudyumna, Ila)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/bhagavata-tales-catalog.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 9.1)
- **Nahusa kicked from heaven into serpenthood** — The over-proud Nahusa, made temporary Indra, is kicked from heaven by Agastya into serpenthood _(actors: Nahusa, Agastya)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/mahabharata-minor-episodes.md) — (Mahabharata Anushasana/Santi Parva)
- **Reign of Svayambhuva, the first Manu** — Svayambhuva, the self-born spiritual son of Brahma, rules the first manvantara and is the reputed promulgator of the Manu Smriti _(actors: Svayambhuva Manu, Brahma)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md) — (Manu Smriti 1, Vishnu Purana 3.1)
- **Vaivasvata's sons found the royal lineages** — Vaivasvata Manu's sons found the great dynasties — Ikshvaku begins the Solar line and Ila/Sudyumna seeds the Lunar line through Pururavas _(actors: Vaivasvata Manu, Ikshvaku, Ila/Sudyumna)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md) — (Vishnu Purana 3.2, 4.1, Bhagavata Purana 9.1)
- **Bhṛgu kicks Viṣṇu's chest (the Trimūrti test)** — The sage Bhṛgu kicks Viṣṇu's chest in the Bhṛgu-pada test to decide the supreme god, cursing and blessing the Trimūrti accordingly and earning the Śrīvatsa mark _(actors: Bhṛgu, Viṣṇu)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/rishi-lineages-and-gotras.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 10.89; Padma Purāṇa)
- **Soma Abducts Tara and the Birth of Budha** — Soma abducts Brihaspati's wife Tara, Budha is born of the union, and the lunar dynasty begins _(actors: Soma, Tara, Budha)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/bhagavata-tales-catalog.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 9.14)
- **Vamana subdues Bali in the Vaivasvata manvantara** — The dwarf-brahmin Vamana, manvantara-avatara of the present age, reclaims the three worlds from the demon-king Bali in three steps and restores them to Indra Purandara _(actors: Vamana, Bali, Indra Purandara)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.15-23, Vishnu Purana 3.1)
- **Birth of the submarine fire from Aurva's wrath** — The seer Aurva's world-burning wrath against the kṣatriyas produces the submarine fire (Vāḍava), which is restrained into the ocean-mare's-mouth _(actors: Aurva)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/rishi-lineages-and-gotras.md) — (Mahābhārata Ādi Parva)
- **Rantideva's Fourfold Charity** — After a 48-day fast, King Rantideva gives away his last food and water to a brahmana, a shudra, a man with dogs, and a chandala, and the testing Trimurti grant him liberation _(actors: Rantideva, Trimurti)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/bhagavata-tales-catalog.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 9.21)
- **The caru-swap of Ṛcīka and Satyavatī** — The brahmin and kṣatriya rice-cakes are exchanged in the marriage of Ṛcīka and Satyavatī, producing a martial brahmin line and a priestly king _(actors: Ṛcīka, Satyavatī)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/rishi-lineages-and-gotras.md) — (Mahābhārata; Viṣṇu Purāṇa)
- **Birth of Soma, Dattātreya and Durvāsas to Atri** — By the Trimūrti's boon, the sage Atri and his wife Anasūyā father three deities as sons: Soma (Candra), Dattātreya and Durvāsas _(actors: Atri, Anasūyā, Soma, Dattātreya, Durvāsas)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/rishi-lineages-and-gotras.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1; Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa)
- **Birth of Chandra from Atri** — Chandra is born to the sage Atri and his chaste wife Anasuya, welling from Atri's penetrating gaze/tears of austerity (in some versions as an amsa of Brahma), and is placed in the sky by Brahma _(actors: Atri, Anasuya, Chandra, Brahma)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/chandra-soma.md) — (Vishnu, Bhagavata 4, Brahmanda Puranas)
- **Budha weds Ila and Pururavas is born** — Budha marries Ila (originally Sudyumna, Vaivasvata Manu's son cursed to alternate sexes), and their son Pururavas begins the human lunar dynasty (Aila line) _(actors: Budha, Ila/Sudyumna, Pururavas)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/chandra-soma.md) — (Vishnu Purana 4; Bhagavata 9.14)
- **Indra grants the Puṣpaka and Uparicara Vasu's vimāna** — Indra grants King Uparicara Vasu a crystalline aerial car letting him move godlike through the sky _(actors: Indra, Uparicara Vasu)_ → [details](../09-artifacts-symbols-and-arts/vahanas-and-celestial-vehicles.md) — (Vimana (Wikipedia); Mahabharata)
- **Dakṣa-yajña: Satī's self-immolation and Dakṣa's beheading** — Satī self-immolates at her father Dakṣa's sacrifice; in the catastrophe Dakṣa is beheaded by Vīrabhadra and later restored with a goat's head _(actors: Dakṣa, Satī, Vīrabhadra)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1–7; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7)
- **Nārada cursed by Dakṣa to wander ceaselessly** — For persuading Dakṣa's sons to renounce procreation, Nārada is cursed by Dakṣa to wander the worlds eternally _(actors: Nārada, Dakṣa)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.5)
- **Nārada converts the Haryaśvas to renunciation** — Dakṣa's first thousand sons, the Haryaśvas, sent to procreate, are dissuaded by Nārada's riddle and renounce and disperse forever _(actors: Nārada, Haryaśvas, Dakṣa)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.5; Viṣṇu Purāṇa)
- **Nārada converts the Śabalāśvas** — Dakṣa's next thousand sons, the Śabalāśvas, are likewise converted by Nārada, enraging Dakṣa into cursing him, after which Dakṣa begets daughters instead _(actors: Nārada, Śabalāśvas, Dakṣa)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.5; Viṣṇu Purāṇa)
- **Dakṣa's daughters wed Kaśyapa and breed all beings** — Dakṣa's daughters married to Kaśyapa (Aditi, Diti, Danu, Kadrū, Vinatā and others) produce every class of being — devas, daityas, dānavas, nāgas, birds, beasts and more _(actors: Dakṣa, Kaśyapa, Aditi, Diti, Danu, Kadrū, Vinatā)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Mahābhārata Ādi Parva; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21; Bhāgavata 6.6)
- **Dakṣa gives 27 nakṣatra-daughters to Candra; the Moon's curse** — Dakṣa marries 27 daughters (the lunar mansions) to Candra, whose neglect of all but Rohiṇī provokes Dakṣa's curse that the Moon waxes and wanes, partly relieved at Prabhāsa _(actors: Dakṣa, Candra, Rohiṇī)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 9.14; Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Skanda Purāṇa)
- **Birth of Nara-Nārāyaṇa to Mūrti and Dharma** — Dakṣa's daughter Mūrti, married to Dharma, mothers the twin sage-avatāra Nara-Nārāyaṇa of Viṣṇu _(actors: Mūrti, Dharma, Nara-Nārāyaṇa)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1)
- **Satī reborn as Pārvatī** — After self-immolating at the Dakṣa-yajña, Dakṣa's daughter Satī, wife of Śiva, is reborn as Pārvatī _(actors: Satī, Śiva, Pārvatī)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) — (Śiva Purāṇa; Bhāgavata 4.2–7)
- **Agastya's curse of Indradyumna** — King Indradyumna, a Pandya/Dravida devotee, is cursed by sage Agastya to be reborn as an elephant for a lapse of courtesy, becoming Gajendra _(actors: Agastya, Indradyumna)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-04-tamasa.md) — (Bhagavata Purana)
- **Devala's curse of the gandharva Huhu** — The gandharva Huhu is cursed by sage Devala to become a crocodile for seizing his leg in the water, becoming the graha that attacks Gajendra _(actors: Devala, Huhu)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-04-tamasa.md) — (Bhagavata Purana)
