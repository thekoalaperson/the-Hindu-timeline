**55 events.**

*What the texts foretell after our age, up to the Night of Brahmā.*

---

- **Birth-story of Bhautya Manu** — When the sacrificial fire in sage Bhuti's ashram goes out, his disciple Shanti propitiates Agni, who rekindles the fire and grants the boon that the childless Bhuti will have a son — the future 14th Manu, Indra-Savarni/Bhautya _(actors: Bhuti, Shanti, Agni, Angiras)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md) — (Markandeya/Bhagavata Purana Manu-cycle; popular retellings)
- **Descent of Vishnu as Dharmasetu (11th Manvantara)** — In the 11th (Dharma-Savarni) manvantara Vishnu incarnates as Dharmasetu, born of Vaidhrita and Aryaka, to sustain cosmic order, the Manu himself being a prior manifestation of this avatar _(actors: Vishnu; Dharmasetu; Vaidhrita; Aryaka)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-11-dharma-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13)
- **Descent of Vishnu as Rishabha (9th Manvantara)** — In the 9th (Daksha-Savarni) manvantara Vishnu incarnates as Rishabha, born of Ayushman and Ambudhara, to uphold dharma and support Indra Adbhuta and Manu Daksha-Savarni _(actors: Vishnu; Rishabha; Ayushman; Ambudhara)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-09-daksha-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13)
- **Descent of Vishnu as Svadhaman (12th Manvantara)** — In the 12th (Rudra-Savarni) manvantara the Vishnu amsha Svadhaman/Sudhaman is born of Sunrita, wife of Satyasaha, to uphold dharma and Manu Rudra-Savarni's rule _(actors: Vishnu; Svadhaman; Satyasaha; Sunrita)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-12-rudra-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.29)
- **Descent of Vishnu as Vishvaksena (10th Manvantara)** — In the 10th (Brahma-Savarni) manvantara Vishnu manifests as Vishvaksena, born from the womb of Vishuchi in the house of the brahmana Vishvasrishta, to assist Manu Brahma-Savarni and his friend Indra Shambhu in upholding dharma _(actors: Vishnu; Vishvaksena; Vishuchi; Vishvasrishta; Shambhu)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-10-brahma-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.21-23)
- **Kali Yuga reaches its nadir** — Dharma stands on one leg as mleccha and thieving kings dominate, prompting Vishnu's final descent _(actors: mleccha kings, the demon Kali, Vishnu)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Vishnu Purana 4.24; Bhagavata Purana 12.2)
- **Ruci Refuses Marriage and Is Rebuked by the Pitris** — The ascetic Ruci refuses to wed, holding household life breeds only suffering, until his ancestral Pitris appear, teach the value of the five great sacrifices and householder duty, and Ruci composes the Pitri-stotra _(actors: Ruci; the Pitris)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md) — (Markandeya Purana 92-95)
- **Suratha's worship of the Goddess and rebirth as Savarni** — King Suratha, having lost his kingdom, worships Mahamaya/Durga and by her boon is destined to be reborn as Savarni, the eighth Manu _(actors: King Suratha, the merchant Samadhi, Durga (Mahamaya))_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md) — (Markandeya Purana 81-93 (Devi Mahatmya); Devi Bhagavata)
- **Bali ascends to the throne of Indra** — As recompense for his truthfulness, the daitya king Bali becomes Indra in the Savarni manvantara, fulfilling Vishnu's Vamana-boon _(actors: Bali, Vishnu)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md) — (Vishnu Purana 3.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13.15)
- **Birth of Kalki in Shambhala** — Vishnu is born as Kalki, son of the brahmin Vishnuyasha and Sumati, in the village of Shambhala _(actors: Kalki, Vishnuyasha, Sumati)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 1.3.25, 12.2.18; Kalki Purana 1)
- **Brahma Commands Ruci to Marry** — After Ruci performs tapas for a hundred divine years, Brahma appears and commands him to marry and beget a son destined to be a Manu _(actors: Brahma; Ruci)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md) — (Markandeya Purana 92-95)
- **Close of the 14th Manvantara and end of the Kalpa** — When Bhautya's 71 mahayugas complete, the last Manu-period and the entire Day of Brahma end, dissolving into the Night of Brahma _(actors: Bhautya Manu, Brahma)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md) — (Vishnu Purana; Bhagavata Purana)
- **Reign of Bhautya Manu (14th Manvantara)** — Indra-Savarni (Bhautya) presides as the fourteenth and final Manu of the current Kalpa, with Suci as Indra, seven named sages, and the avatar Brhadbhanu sustaining the age _(actors: Indra-Savarni/Bhautya, Suci (Indra), Brhadbhanu)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md) — (Vishnu Purana III.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13.33-35; Matsya Purana 9)
- **Avatar Sarvabhauma enthrones Bali** — Vishnu incarnates as Sarvabhauma, son of Devaguhya and Sarasvati, wrests Svarga from the reigning Indra and bestows it on Bali _(actors: Sarvabhauma (Vishnu), Bali, Indra Purandara)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.15-16)
- **Descent of Brhadbhanu (avatar of the Bhautya age)** — Vishnu's sustaining avatar Brhadbhanu is born of Satrayana and Vitana as Hari's manifestation for the final Manvantara, though no deeds are narrated _(actors: Brhadbhanu, Satrayana, Vitana)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.35; Vishnu Purana III.2)
- **Malini Given to Ruci in Marriage** — The apsaras Pramlocha presents her daughter Malini as Ruci's bride, fulfilling Brahma's command _(actors: Pramlocha; Malini; Ruci)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md) — (Markandeya Purana 92-95)
- **Tutelage under Parashurama and Shiva's gifts** — The chiranjivi Parashurama teaches and arms Kalki, who receives the white horse Devadatta, the sage-parrot Shuka, and a blazing sword from Shiva _(actors: Kalki, Parashurama, Shiva)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Kalki Purana; Bhagavata Purana 12.2.19-20)
- **Bali Ascends as Indra of the Savarni Age** — In the 8th (Savarni) manvantara the chiranjivi Bali ascends to the throne of Indra, king of the devas, fulfilling Vishnu's earlier boon _(actors: Bali)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md) — (Vishnu Purana 3.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13.15)
- **Birth of Manu Raucya** — From Malini's womb is born a son of great heroism and intellect who becomes the Manu Raucya (Deva-Savarni), presiding over the 13th manvantara _(actors: Malini; Ruci; Raucya)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md) — (Markandeya Purana 92-95)
- **Campaign against the adharmic and mleccha kings** — Mounted on Devadatta with his sword, Kalki sweeps the earth slaying thieves and barbarian warlords who abandoned dharma _(actors: Kalki, Devadatta, mleccha kings)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12.2.19-20; Agni Purana)
- **Close of the 14th Manvantara / end of the Kalpa** — When Bhautya's 71 Mahayugas complete, the last Manu-period and the entire Day of Brahma end, closing the present Kalpa _(actors: Bhautya Manu)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md) — (Vishnu Purana; Bhagavata Purana)
- **Advent of Kalki** — When adharma reaches its limit, Vishnu incarnates as Kalki in the village of Shambhala, son of the brahmana Vishnuyasha, mounting the white horse Devadatta and bearing a blazing sword from Shiva _(actors: Kalki/Vishnu, Vishnuyasha, Sumati)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md) — (Kalki Purana; Bhagavata Purana 12.2.18-19; Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **Descent of Vishnu as Sarvabhauma** — In the 8th manvantara Vishnu incarnates as Sarvabhauma, son of Devaguhya and Sarasvati, who wrests Svarga and the office of Indra from the reigning Indra Purandara and bestows it on Bali _(actors: Vishnu; Sarvabhauma; Bali; Purandara)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-08-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.15-16)
- **Descent of Vishnu as Yogeshvara (13th Manvantara)** — In the 13th (Deva-Savarni/Raucya) manvantara Vishnu incarnates as Yogeshvara, born of Devahotra and Brihati, who by his mystic yoga power frees the worlds from misery _(actors: Vishnu; Yogeshvara; Devahotra; Brihati)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.32)
- **Night of Brahma (naimittika pralaya)** — After the Kalpa ends the three worlds (Bhur, Bhuvar, Svar) are burned and flooded, and creation rests for one Night of Brahma before the next Kalpa dawns → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md) — (Vishnu Purana; Bhagavata Purana)
- **Slaying of the demon Kali** — Kalki and his allies wage cosmic war against Kali personified and his progeny, finally vanquishing the age-demon _(actors: Kalki, demon Kali, Krodha, Himsa)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Kalki Purana)
- **Kalki's cleansing of the wicked** — Kalki slaughters the mleccha and dasyu kings and robbers by the millions, exterminating the wicked who have lost all spiritual sense _(actors: Kalki)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12.2.19-20; Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **Pramiti / Kalki prophecy in the Mahabharata** — Markandeya foretells the brahmin destroyer Pramiti (Kalki Vishnuyasha) who slaughters the mleccha kings and re-establishes the Krita age _(actors: Pramiti/Kalki, Markandeya)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Mahabharata 3.188-189 (Vana Parva))
- **Future Collapse of the Badrinath Route and Shift to Bhavishya Badri** — When adharma overwhelms the world, the Nara and Narayana mountains will collapse and seal the route to Badrinath, whereupon Badrinath will manifest at Bhavishya Badri, marked by the withering arm of the Joshimath Narasimha idol _(actors: Vishnu (Narasimha))_ → [details](../06-sacred-geography/pancha-kedar-pancha-badri.md) — (Skanda Purana (Badari-mahatmya) tradition)
- **Marriage to Padma/Padmavati and Rama** — Kalki weds Padma (Padmavati) of Simhala, an incarnation of Lakshmi, and the princess Rama of the Brihadratha line, fathering the first kings of the new age _(actors: Kalki, Padmavati, Rama, Lakshmi)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Kalki Purana)
- **Return of Satya Yuga and the cosmic reset** — With the destroyers gone the survivors' minds become spontaneously pure, becoming the seed-population of the next Satya Yuga, which dawns when Moon, Sun, and Jupiter together enter the asterism Pushya _(actors: Kalki, the survivors)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12.2.23-24; Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **The future Manus (Sāvarṇi and after)** — The Madhya-bhāga details the future manvantaras — the Sāvarṇi group of Manus with their Indras, ṛṣis and devas in the second half of the present kalpa _(actors: Sāvarṇi Manus)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/puranas/brahmanda-purana.md) — (Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa (Madhya-bhāga))
- **Re-emergence of Devapi and Maru** — Two immortals, Devapi of the lunar line and Maru of the solar line, who survived all four yugas in meditation at Kalapa, re-emerge to re-found the kshatriya dynasties and teach proper dharma _(actors: Devapi, Maru)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/04-kali-yuga/README.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12.2.37-38; Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **Turning of Kali Yuga into the next Satya Yuga** — With the wicked destroyed and Kali subdued, the righteous are purified, dharma is restored on all four legs, and a new Satya Yuga dawns marked by the Sun, Moon and Jupiter conjoining in Pushya _(actors: Kalki, the purified righteous)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Vishnu Purana 4.24; Bhagavata Purana 12.2.23-24; Kalki Purana)
- **Future collapse of route and shift to Bhaviṣya Badrī** — At the close of the age the Nara and Nārāyaṇa peaks collapse and seal the route, after which Badrinath manifests permanently at Bhaviṣya Badrī, signalled by the withering arm of the Joshimath Narsingh idol _(actors: Nara/Nārāyaṇa peaks, Viṣṇu as Narasiṃha)_ → [details](../06-sacred-geography/badrinath-badarikashrama-kshetra.md) — (Wikipedia (Sapta Badri) [folk eschatology])
- **Future manvantaras and the Sāvarṇi Manus** — Account of the fourteen Manus including the future Sāvarṇi group with their Indras, ṛṣis and devas, manvantaras 8–14 of the present kalpa _(actors: Sāvarṇi and the future Manus)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/puranas/vayu-purana.md) — (Vāyu Purāṇa; Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa)
- **Kalki's return and continuation of the wheel** — His mission complete, Kalki ascends to his abode while the new royal line he founded carries the cycle into the next Mahayuga _(actors: Kalki, the new kings)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kalki.md) — (Kalki Purana; Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **Bali becomes Indra of the Savarni Manvantara** — Bali, demoted by Vamana in the present age, is foretold to become the Indra of the future 8th (Savarni) manvantara, the classic demotion-to-promotion arc _(actors: Bali)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13, 8.22-23)
- **Parashurama to be Kalki's preceptor** — Parashurama is prophesied to reappear at Kali's end as the preceptor of the Kalki avatara, closing the cosmic loop into the next Satya Yuga _(actors: Parashurama, Kalki)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/the-chiranjivis.md) — (Kalki Purana)
- **The pralaya and final dissolution** — The Vishnu Purana describes the naimittika, prakritika, and atyantika dissolutions, with yoga as the road to moksha at the kalpa- and Brahma-life boundaries _(actors: Vishnu)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/puranas/vishnu-purana.md) — (Vishnu Purana (Amsha 6))
- **Kalki closes the Kali age** — The Kalki avatara destroys the mleccha kings and restores the Krita-yuga, ending the age _(actors: Kalki)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/puranas/vishnu-purana.md) — (Vishnu Purana (Amsha 4))
- **Kalki prophecy** — The Bhāgavata Purāṇa and Kalki Purāṇa prophesy the future Kalki avatāra of Viṣṇu who will end Kali Yuga _(actors: Kalki (Viṣṇu))_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/smriti-puranas.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Book 12; Kalki Purāṇa)
- **Preceptor of Kalki** — As a cirañjīvi surviving into Kali Yuga, Paraśurāma initiates and arms Kalki, the tenth avatāra, closing the Daśāvatāra loop _(actors: Paraśurāma, Kalki)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/parashurama.md) — (Kalki Purāṇa (late text))
- **Prakritika Pralaya — the Great Dissolution** — At the end of Brahma's 100-year life the cosmos collapses in reverse order of emanation, each element reabsorbed into its subtler source until only Prakriti and Brahman remain _(actors: Brahma, Prakriti, Brahman/Mahavishnu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md) — (Vishnu Purana 6.4, Bhagavata Purana 12.4.15-26)
- **Death of Brahma and birth of a new Brahma** — After 311.04 trillion years Brahma is reabsorbed; the cosmos rests in Maha-Vishnu, and after an equal span a new Brahma is born to begin the next Mahakalpa _(actors: Brahma, Maha-Vishnu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 3.11.40-42, Vishnu Purana 6.4)
- **Bali becomes Indra in the future Savarni manvantara** — The demon-king Bali, demoted by Vamana in the present age, is foretold to become Indra in the 8th (Savarni) manvantara — the demotion-to-promotion arc _(actors: Bali)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/manvantara-system.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13, 8.22-23)
- **Kalki ends Kali Yuga and restores Satya** — At Kali's close the Kalki avatara destroys adharma and ushers in a new Satya Yuga, restarting the Mahayuga cycle _(actors: Kalki)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12.2, Kalki Purana, Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **Kalki avatara descends** — At Kali's end the Lord descends as Kalki, son of Viṣṇuyasas in Sambhala, to destroy the wicked and inaugurate a new Satya Yuga _(actors: Kalki/Viṣṇu, Viṣṇuyasas)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/bhagavata-purana-skandha-map.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 12)
- **Eleven Rudras emerge from Saṅkarṣaṇa's brows at pralaya** — At cosmic dissolution, eleven three-eyed trident-bearing Rudras emerge from between Ananta-Saṅkarṣaṇa's wrath-knit eyebrows to annihilate the entire creation _(actors: Saṅkarṣaṇa-Ananta, Rudra)_ → [details](../08-beings-and-bestiary/shesha-ananta.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 5.25.3)
- **Kalki ends Kali Yuga** — At the close of Kali Yuga, Vishnu will be born as Kalki, son of Vishnuyashas in Shambhala, riding the white horse Devadatta, destroying the wicked, defeating the demon Kali, and inaugurating the next Satya Yuga _(actors: Kalki (Vishnu), Vishnuyashas, the demon Kali, Padmavati)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 1.3.25, 12.2; Kalki Purana; Vishnu Purana 4.24)
- **Naimittika Pralaya — the Night of Brahma** — At each kalpa's end seven suns and the Samvartaka fire scorch the three worlds, floods drown them into one ocean, and the universe is withdrawn into Narayana sleeping on Shesha until the next dawn _(actors: Narayana, Shesha)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md) — (Vishnu Purana VI.3; Bhagavata Purana 12.4.6-13)
- **Maha-pralaya at the end of Brahma's life** — When Brahma's 100 years complete, twelve suns and the Samvartaka fire scorch the worlds, a deluge floods the cosmos, and all tattvas including Brahma reabsorb in reverse order into unmanifest Prakriti _(actors: Brahma, Prakriti, Brahman/Maha-Vishnu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md) — (Vishnu Purana 6.4; Bhagavata Purana 12.4.15-26)
- **Prakritika Pralaya — reverse-order elemental collapse** — At the end of Brahma's life each element loses its quality and merges into its cause back to Prakriti, dissolving everything manifest including Brahma into Purusha/Brahman _(actors: Brahma, the elements, Prakriti, Purusha)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md) — (Vishnu Purana VI.4; Bhagavata Purana 12.4.15-22)
- **Birth of a new Brahma after Maha-pralaya** — After a span equal to Brahma's life, a new Brahma is generated from Maha-Vishnu's navel-lotus and the entire Mahakalpa cycle begins anew, time being beginningless and endless _(actors: Maha-Vishnu, new Brahma)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/brahma-lifetime.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 3.11.40-42; Vishnu Purana 6.4)
- **Kalpa-ending night of Brahma after the 14th manvantara** — At the close of the 14th (Indra-savarni/Bhautya) manvantara comes the Kalpa-ending night of Brahma (naimittika pralaya) _(actors: Brahma)_ → [details](../01-manvantaras/README.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.13; Vishnu Purana 3.2)
