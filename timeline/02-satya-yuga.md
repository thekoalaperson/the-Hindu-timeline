- **Markandeya granted a short brilliant life** — The childless sage Mrkandu propitiates Shiva and chooses a virtuous son fated to die at sixteen; the radiant boy Markandeya is born and masters the Vedas _(actors: Mrkandu, Marudvati/Manasvini, Markandeya, Shiva)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/markandeya.md) — (Mahabharata Vana Parva; Shiva Purana; Linga Purana)
- **Matsya Rescues Manu and the Vedas** — The fish-avatara Matsya rescues Manu and the Vedas from the deluge at the pralaya _(actors: Matsya, Manu)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/bhagavata-tales-catalog.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.24)
- **Descent of Ganga (Gangavatarana)** — Ganga is brought to earth by Bhagiratha's austerities, her fall broken by Shiva's matted hair, to liberate the ashes of Sagara's sons _(actors: Ganga, Bhagiratha, Shiva)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/yamuna-and-river-goddesses.md) — (Ramayana Balakanda; Bhagavata; Mahabharata)
- **Shiva as Kalantaka conquers Death for Markandeya** — When Yama casts his noose around the boy clinging to a Shiva-linga, Shiva bursts forth as Kalantaka/Mrtyunjaya, strikes down Death, and blesses Markandeya to remain eternally sixteen and deathless _(actors: Markandeya, Shiva (Kalantaka/Mrtyunjaya), Yama)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/markandeya.md) — (Shiva Purana; Linga Purana)
- **Sudarshana Chakra beheads Rahu** — Vishnu's time-disc the Sudarshana Chakra (Kala-chakra) beheads the demon Rahu who stole the amrita, the weapon-face of Time cutting off every form at its appointed moment _(actors: Vishnu, Rahu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/kalachakra-wheel-of-time.md) — (Mahabharata; Bhagavata Purana; Vishnu Purana)
- **Rishabhadeva avatara born to Nabhi and Merudevi** — Vishnu descends as Rishabha, son of King Nabhi and Merudevi of the Priyavrata line, an avatara of Narayana who fathers a hundred sons, the eldest being Bharata _(actors: Rishabhadeva, Nabhi, Merudevi, Jayanti)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 5.3-5.4; Vishnu Purana 2.1)
- **Rishabha's renunciation and death by forest fire** — After delivering the Rishabha-gita to his sons and crowning Bharata, Rishabha wanders as a naked avadhuta and quits his body, consumed by a wildfire, absorbed in the Self _(actors: Rishabhadeva, Bharata)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 5.5-5.6)
- **Emperor Bharata's reign and renunciation** — Bharata rules Bharata-varsha righteously while inwardly detached, then divides the empire among his sons and retires to Pulaha-ashrama on the Gandaki to meditate _(actors: Bharata, Panchajani)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 5.7)
- **Bharata's attachment to the deer and rebirth as a deer** — The ascetic Bharata becomes attached to an orphaned fawn he rescues; dying with the deer in his mind, he is reborn as a conscious deer remembering his past life _(actors: Bharata, the fawn/deer)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 5.8)
- **Jada Bharata the silent sage and the Rahugana teachings** — Reborn as a brahmana, the soul feigns idiocy as Jada Bharata, is nearly sacrificed to Bhadra Kali (who saves him), then teaches King Rahugana the forest-of-samsara allegory and attains liberation _(actors: Jada Bharata, King Rahugana, Bhadra Kali)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/rishabha-bharata-jada.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 5.9-5.14)
- **Satya Yuga — the Age of Truth** — Dharma stands on all four legs; humans live ~100,000 years in spontaneous righteousness, with meditation as the path to liberation _(actors: humanity)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/yugas-and-mahayuga.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.3, Bhagavata Purana 12.3)
- **Soma emerges from the churning of the ocean** — In the Samudra Manthana the Moon (Soma/Chandra) emerges among the fourteen ratnas and is taken by Shiva onto his head, fusing the soma-as-nectar and soma-as-moon ideas _(actors: Soma/Chandra, Shiva, devas and asuras)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/chandra-soma.md) — (Bhagavata, Vishnu, Matsya Puranas)
- **Kapila born to Kardama and Devahuti** — Kapila, an avatara of Viṣṇu and founder-sage of Sankhya, is born to the Prajapati Kardama and Devahuti to impart self-knowledge to his mother _(actors: Kapila, Kardama, Devahuti)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/darshanas/samkhya-karika.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 3.24-33)
- **Matsya rescues Manu and the Vedas from the deluge** — Vishnu as the fish Matsya warns Manu of the coming pralaya, has him build a boat loaded with the seeds of life and the seven sages, tows it through the flood, and (in variants) slays the demon Hayagriva to restore the stolen Vedas — marking the start of our Manvantara _(actors: Matsya (Vishnu), Manu, the saptarshi, Hayagriva)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) — (Shatapatha Brahmana 1.8.1; Matsya Purana; Bhagavata Purana 8.24)
- **Kurma supports Mount Mandara at the churning** — As devas and asuras churn the ocean of milk for amrita using Mount Mandara and the serpent Vasuki, the sinking mountain is supported on the back of Vishnu as the giant tortoise Kurma _(actors: Kurma (Vishnu), devas, asuras, Vasuki)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8.7-8; Vishnu Purana 1.9; Mahabharata Adi Parva)
- **Dhanvantari and Mohini emerge at the churning** — During the Samudra Manthana the physician-god Dhanvantari emerges bearing the nectar pot, and Vishnu takes the Mohini form to trick the asuras out of the amrita _(actors: Dhanvantari, Mohini (Vishnu), devas, asuras)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8; Vishnu Purana)
- **Narasimha slays Hiranyakashipu and saves Prahlada** — To evade the demon-king Hiranyakashipu's boon, Vishnu bursts from a pillar as the man-lion Narasimha at twilight on a threshold, and disembowels him with his claws while saving his devotee-son Prahlada _(actors: Narasimha (Vishnu), Hiranyakashipu, Prahlada)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/dashavatara.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 7.2-10; Vishnu Purana 1.16-20)
- **Rahu beheaded at the churning** — At the Samudra Manthana the asura Rahu steals the amrita and is beheaded by Vishnu as Mohini, becoming the eclipse-node _(actors: Rahu, Mohini (Vishnu))_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 8; Vishnu Purana)
- **Exile of the Daityas and Danavas** — In Krita/Satya Yuga the Daityas and Danavas are exiled from heaven by the devas and take refuge in the Vindhya mountains and the sea (Patala) _(actors: Daityas, Danavas, the devas)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md) — (Puranas)
