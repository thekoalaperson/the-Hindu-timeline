- **Ash and rudrākṣa lore codified** — Kālāgnirudra, Bhasmajābāla, Bṛhajjābāla, and Rudrākṣajābāla codify the tripuṇḍra ash-marks and the rudrākṣa beads born of Śiva's tears _(actors: Kālāgni-Rudra; Bhusuṇḍa)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/upanishads/vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md) — (Kālāgnirudra, Bhasmajābāla, Bṛhajjābāla, Rudrākṣajābāla Upaniṣads)
- **Brahma seeks the root of the lotus-stalk** — Brahma, born on the lotus and ignorant of his origin, descends the stalk seeking its root, fails, performs tapas, and is granted vision of Vishnu, establishing Vishnu's supremacy _(actors: Brahma, Vishnu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 3.8-9)
- **Devi creates the Trimurti (Shakta account)** — The Supreme Goddess (Adi Parashakti) brings forth Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva, assigns their cosmic functions, and grants each a consort-shakti from her own portions _(actors: Mahadevi (Adi Parashakti), Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Devi Bhagavata Purana 3, Devi Mahatmya)
- **Kaśyapa drains lake Satīsaras and founds Kashmir** — Kaśyapa, with Viṣṇu and the gods, breaches the mountain at Varāhamūla to drain lake Satīsaras; Viṣṇu slays Jalodbhava and the valley Kaśmīra is named for and settled by Kaśyapa _(actors: Kaśyapa, Viṣṇu, Śiva, Jalodbhava, the Nāgas)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kashyapa.md) — (Nīlamata Purāṇa; Rājataraṅgiṇī 1.25–28)
- **Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad as TĀ prapāṭhaka 10** — Prapāṭhaka 10 of the Taittirīya Āraṇyaka is the Mahānārāyaṇa (Yājñikī) Upaniṣad, hymning Nārāyaṇa as Brahman and supplying sandhyā and sannyāsa mantras _(actors: Nārāyaṇa as supreme Brahman/Antaryāmin)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/vedas/aranyakas.md) — (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka 10 (Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad))
- **Martanda cast away and reclaimed as Surya** — In the Rigvedic account Aditi casts away her eighth son Martanda, who is reclaimed as Surya the Sun _(actors: Aditi, Martanda/Surya)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/kamadeva-and-minor-deities.md) — (Rigveda)
- **Agastya Mediates Indra and the Maruts** — As Rigvedic seer Agastya composes hymns mediating a quarrel between Indra and the storm-gods, reconciling the Indra-Maruts dispute _(actors: Agastya, Indra, the Maruts)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/agastya.md) — (Rigveda 1.165, 1.170-1.171)
- **Brahma forms within the cosmic egg and splits it** — The creator's seed becomes a golden egg incubating a divine year; Brahma develops within and splits it into heavens, earth, and the sky between, with fluids becoming mountains and oceans _(actors: Brahma / Svayambhu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Manusmriti 1.5-13; Vishnu Purana 1.2)
- **Embedded suktas and famous mantras housed in the TĀ** — The Taittirīya Āraṇyaka houses Yajurvedic Puruṣa-, Nārāyaṇa-, Bhū-, Durgā-, and Medhā-sūktas plus the Mṛtyuñjaya/Tryambaka mantra _(actors: Puruṣa; Nārāyaṇa; Rudra-Tryambaka; Durgā)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/vedas/aranyakas.md) — (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka (3, 10))
- **Gaṇeśa declared Brahman** — Gaṇapati-Atharvaśīrṣa proclaims Gaṇeśa as Brahman with the bīja 'gaṃ' — the principal Gāṇapatya Śruti text _(actors: Gaṇeśa)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/upanishads/vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md) — (Gaṇapati-Atharvaśīrṣa Upaniṣad)
- **Kaśyapa father of Vāmana** — On Kaśyapa's instruction Aditi performs the Payovrata and Viṣṇu is born to the couple as the dwarf Vāmana to reclaim the worlds from Bali _(actors: Kaśyapa, Aditi, Vāmana/Viṣṇu, Bali)_ → [details](../04-deep-dives/kashyapa.md) — (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.16–18)
- **Primary unfolding of prakriti (Prakrita sarga)** — From Vishnu's will, unmanifest prakriti is disturbed and the gunas lose equilibrium, emitting mahat, ahamkara, the tanmatras, senses, and gross elements into the cosmic egg _(actors: Vishnu, Brahma, Prakriti)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.2, Bhagavata Purana 3.5)
- **Viśvakarmā forges the twin bows Pināka and Śārṅga** — To settle the Śiva–Viṣṇu superiority contest, Viśvakarmā crafts the twin bows Pināka (Śiva) and Śārṅga (Viṣṇu); Viṣṇu 'wins' and Śiva's bow passes to the kings of Mithilā _(actors: Viśvakarmā, Śiva, Viṣṇu)_ → [details](../09-artifacts-symbols-and-arts/divyastras-and-divine-weapons.md) — (Rāmāyaṇa; divine-bows tradition)
- **Bhairava severs Brahmā's fifth head** — From his fury at the arrogant fifth head of Brahmā, Śiva manifests Kāla Bhairava, who shears off the offending head, incurring brahmahatyā that clings as a skull until expiated at Kāśī _(actors: Kāla Bhairava, Brahmā)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-myth-cycle.md) — (Śiva Purāṇa Śatarudra-saṃhitā; Skanda Purāṇa; Matsya Purāṇa)
- **Brahma creates the universe and begins time (Ugadi charter)** — The Gudi Padwa/Ugadi charter marks the day Brahma created the universe and began kala (time) — Yuga-adi, the 'beginning of the age' _(actors: Brahma)_ → [details](../05-concepts-and-dharma/regional-temple-festivals.md) — (traditional)
- **Lingodbhava — the infinite pillar of light** — Brahma and Vishnu dispute supremacy when an endless fiery jyotirlinga appears; neither finds its end and Shiva emerges from the linga as the true source of both _(actors: Shiva, Brahma, Vishnu)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Shiva Purana; Linga Purana)
- **Āraṇyaka–Upaniṣad embedding map** — Each surviving Āraṇyaka embeds a principal Upaniṣad; the Bṛhadāraṇyaka is simultaneously Āraṇyaka and Upaniṣad of the Śukla Yajurveda _(actors: Yājñavalkya; the four śruti strata)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/vedas/aranyakas.md) — (Aitareya, Kauṣītaki, Taittirīya, Bṛhadāraṇyaka traditions)
- **Sadāśiva's five faces and the Hari-Hara harmony** — Pañcabrahma sets out Śiva's five faces (Sadyojāta to Īśāna), while Rudrahṛdaya affirms the non-difference of Śiva and Viṣṇu _(actors: Sadāśiva; Śiva and Viṣṇu)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/upanishads/vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md) — (Pañcabrahma, Rudrahṛdaya Upaniṣads)
- **The nine creations (sargas) of Brahma** — Creation proceeds through nine graded sargas — three elemental (Prakrita) and five derived (Vaikrita) plus the Kaumara — from Mahat down to the Kumaras _(actors: Brahma)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.5, Bhagavata Purana 3.10)
- **Birth of the mind-born sons (Manasaputras)** — Brahma produces from his mind the Brahmarshis/Prajapatis — Bhrigu, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu, Angiras, Marichi, Daksha, Atri, Vasishtha — progenitors of all beings _(actors: Brahma, the nine Prajapatis)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.7, Manusmriti 1.34-35)
- **Śiva as Śarabha subdues Narasiṃha** — Śarabha Upaniṣad asserts Śaiva supremacy: Śiva as the Śarabha overpowers Viṣṇu's Man-Lion avatāra — a sectarian polemic _(actors: Śiva (Śarabha); Narasiṃha)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/upanishads/vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md) — (Śarabha Upaniṣad)
- **Yoganidra withdraws so Vishnu slays Madhu-Kaitabha** — At a cycle's start the Goddess as Yoganidra withdraws from the sleeping Vishnu so he can wake and slay Madhu and Kaitabha, showing the Goddess drives even Vishnu's creative role _(actors: Devi (Yoganidra/Mahamaya), Vishnu, Madhu, Kaitabha)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Devi Mahatmya 1)
- **Devi brings forth the Trimurti** — The Supreme Goddess brings forth Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva, assigns their cosmic functions, and grants each a consort-shakti who is her own portion _(actors: Mahadevi, Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Devi Bhagavata Purana 3)
- **The Goddess answers 'I am Brahman'** — Devī-Atharvaśīrṣa and Bahvṛca have the Goddess declare herself the sole Brahman and primordial existent, with the Devī mantra and Gāyatrī _(actors: Devī / Mahā-tripurasundarī; the gods)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/upanishads/vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md) — (Devī-Atharvaśīrṣa, Bahvṛca Upaniṣads)
- **The Kumaras refuse to procreate** — Brahma's first sons, the four eternal celibate Sanaka-kumaras, reject creation and remain renunciant, frustrating Brahma's plan to populate the world _(actors: Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanatana, Sanatkumara, Brahma)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Bhagavata Purana 3.12, Vishnu Purana 1.7)
- **Lalita Tripurasundari manifests from the sacrificial fire** — Lalita Tripurasundari manifests from the cosmic fire of the gods' sacrifice and creates and orders the worlds _(actors: Lalita Tripurasundari)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Brahmanda Purana (Lalitopakhyana))
- **Lingodbhava establishes Shiva's supremacy** — Shiva manifests as an infinite column of fire whose top (Brahma as swan) and bottom (Vishnu as boar) neither can find, establishing his supremacy and grounding the 12 Jyotirlingas _(actors: Shiva, Brahma, Vishnu)_ → [details](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/shiva-avatars-and-forms.md) — (Shiva Purana Vidyeshvara Samhita)
- **Śrīvidyā / Tripurā mantra and Śrīcakra expounded** — Tripurā, Tripurātāpinī, and Bhāvanā expound Tripurasundarī, the pañcadaśī mantra, and the Śrīcakra interiorised on the body _(actors: Tripurā/Tripurasundarī; Bhāskararāya (commentator))_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/upanishads/vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md) — (Tripurā, Tripurātāpinī, Bhāvanā Upaniṣads)
- **Brahma splits into Svayambhuva Manu and Shatarupa** — Brahma divides his body into male and female halves becoming Svayambhuva Manu (the first Manu) and Shatarupa, whose union begins the human dynastic line _(actors: Brahma, Svayambhuva Manu, Shatarupa)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.7, Manusmriti 1.32)
- **Lakṣmī, Sarasvatī, Sītā, Annapūrṇā as the supreme Śakti** — Saubhāgyalakṣmī, Sarasvatī-rahasya, Sītā, and Annapūrṇā identify these goddesses with Mūla-Prakṛti / non-dual Brahman and give their vidyās _(actors: Lakṣmī; Sarasvatī; Sītā; Annapūrṇā)_ → [details](../90-literature-corpus/upanishads/vaishnava-shaiva-shakta-upanishads-overview.md) — (Saubhāgyalakṣmī, Sarasvatī-rahasya, Sītā, Annapūrṇā Upaniṣads)

- **Brahma emanates the mind-born sons (Manasaputras)** — Brahma produces his Brahmarshi/Prajapati sons from his mind, the progenitors of all beings _(actors: Brahma, Bhrigu, Marichi, Daksha, Atri, Vasishtha and others)_ → [details](../00-time-and-cosmology/cosmogony-creation-accounts.md) — (Vishnu Purana 1.7; Manusmriti 1.34-35)
