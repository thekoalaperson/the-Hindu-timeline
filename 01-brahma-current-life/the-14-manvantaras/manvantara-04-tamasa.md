# Manvantara 4 — Tāmasa (Past)

> Hierarchy: Brahma's current life → Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → the 14 Manvantaras → **4th Manvantara (Tāmasa, past)**. The 4th of 14 Manu-cycles in this Day of Brahma; long elapsed. We are now in the [7th (Vaivasvata)](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). Duration ≈ 306,720,000 human years (71 Mahayugas + 1 sandhya).

A STUB node — concise skeleton; can be expanded later. Each Manvantara has its own Manu, Indra, set of gods, Saptarishi, and a Vishnu avatar. Sources disagree on most names; all notable variants are cataloged below.

## The Presiding Figures

- **Tāmasa Manu** — the 4th Manu, brother of the 3rd Manu [Uttama](./manvantara-03-uttama.md); also called **Tāpasa**. Sometimes said to be Hari (Vishnu) born as Tāmasa Manu (Bhagavata Purana, Vishnu Purana).
- **Sons of Tāmasa** — ten sons; chief among them **Pṛthu, Khyāti, Nara, Ketu** (Vishnu Purana). Bhagavata names heads as **Pṛthu, Khyāti, Nara, Ketu**; other lists give **Khyāti, Keturūpa, Jānujaṅgha** etc. (Puranic Encyclopaedia).
- **Indra of this Manvantara** — **Triśikha** (Vishnu Purana) / **Śibi**, who had performed a hundred yajñas (Bhagavata Purana). [Variant]
- **Classes of Devas** — four groups, each of 27 gods: **Supāras (Satyakas), Haris, Vīras, Sudhīs/Satyas** (Bhagavata / Vishnu Purana); **Sādhyagaṇa** named as prominent deity class in some lists (Matsya tradition). [Variant]

## The Saptarishis (Seven Sages)

- **Variant (Vishnu/Bhagavata Purana):** Jyotirdhāma, Pṛthu, Kāvya, Chaitra, Agni, Vanaka, Pīvara — list led by **Jyotirdhāma**.
- **Variant (alternate Puranic list):** Kavi, Pṛthu, Agni, Akapi, Kapi, Janya, Dhāma.
- *(Saptarishi rosters differ markedly between recensions; both forms recur in the Puranas.)*

## Key Episode

- **Gajendra-mokṣa (the liberation of Gajendra)** — the elephant-king Gajendra, seized by a crocodile in a lake, cries out to Vishnu, who arrives and frees him; the classic story of surrender and divine grace. Traditionally placed in the Tāmasa Manvantara: Harimedha (by his wife Hariṇī) begot **Hari**, the Vishnu avatar of this period, born expressly to liberate the devotee Gajendra (Bhagavata Purana 8.4; Vishnu Purana). See also the full narration in the [literature corpus](../../90-literature-corpus/).
  - *Variant (Bhagavata Purana):* the avatar is simply called **Hari**, son of Harimedha and Hariṇī; in some readings the rescued elephant is the reborn king **Indradyumna** and the crocodile a cursed gandharva.
