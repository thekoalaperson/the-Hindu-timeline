# Manvantara 1 — Svāyambhuva (Past)

> First of the 14 Manvantaras of the current Kalpa (Shveta-Varaha). Ruled by **Svāyambhuva Manu**, the mind-born son of Brahmā. We are now 6 Manvantaras later, in the 7th ([Vaivasvata](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/)). *[STUB — expandable.]*

## Cosmic Address
- **Manvantara 1 of 14** · Manu: **Svāyambhuva** · Indra of the era: **Yajña** · ~306,720,000 yrs (1 Manvantara). See [../../00-time-and-cosmology](../../00-time-and-cosmology) for the clock.

## Key Events & Stories
- **Creation of Svāyambhuva Manu & Shatarūpā** — Brahmā divides his body into male and female halves; the male becomes Svāyambhuva Manu, the female **Shatarūpā** ("hundred-formed"). Treated as the first human couple / progenitors of mankind (*Manusmriti* 1; *Vishnu Purana* 1; *Bhāgavata Purāna* 3–4).
  - *Variant (Matsya/Brahma Purāna):* Shatarūpā is Brahmā's daughter (Sarasvatī/Vāc), and Brahmā's infatuation with her is a noted episode [contested across texts].
- **Children of Manu** — Two sons, **Priyavrata** and **Uttānapāda**; three daughters, **Ākūti**, **Devahūti**, **Prasūti** (*Bhāgavata Purāna* 3.12, 4.1; *Vishnu Purana* 1.7).
  - Ākūti m. ṛṣi Ruci → son **Yajña** (and a daughter, Dakṣiṇā). Devahūti m. Prajāpati Kardama → son **Kapila** (Sāṃkhya founder, Viṣṇu avatāra). Prasūti m. Prajāpati Dakṣa → many daughters (Sati, Aditi, Diti, etc.).
- **Dhruva** — Son of **Uttānapāda**; spurned by his stepmother, performs extreme tapas and is fixed as the immovable **pole star** (Dhruva-loka) by Viṣṇu (*Bhāgavata Purāna* 4.8–4.12; *Vishnu Purana* 1.11–12). See [../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy).
- **Pṛthu & the milking of the Earth** — In Priyavrata/Uttānapāda's line: tyrant Vena is destroyed; from his body the righteous king **Pṛthu** arises (Viṣṇu avatāra). The Earth flees as a cow; Pṛthu chases her and "milks" her, drawing out grains and herbs so beings can be fed. Earth thus named **Pṛthivī** after him (*Bhāgavata Purāna* 4.13–18; *Vishnu Purana* 1.13).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata):* gods, sages, animals, etc. each milk the Earth-cow in turn for their own sustenance, using different calves and vessels.
- **Yajña as Indra** — In the Svāyambhuva era the post of **Indra** (king of the devas) is held by **Yajña**, son of Ākūti and an incarnation of Viṣṇu, with the Yāmas/Tuṣitas as the gods of the age (*Bhāgavata Purāna* 8.1).
