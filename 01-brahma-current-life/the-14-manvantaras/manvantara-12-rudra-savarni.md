# Manvantara 12 — Rudra-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Hierarchy: 01-brahma-current-life → the-14-manvantaras → **12th Manvantara (Rudra-Sāvarṇi)**. A *future* manvantara within the current Shveta-Varaha Kalpa; five manvantaras still lie ahead after the present [7th, Vaivasvata](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). Each spans ~306,720,000 human years (71 mahayugas + 1 sandhya).

This is a **STUB** — skeleton only; expandable later.

## Orientation
- We are presently in the **7th (Vaivasvata)** manvantara. Rudra-Sāvarṇi is the **12th**, so it remains entirely in the future (manvantaras 8–14 are the *future* set detailed mainly in the Bhāgavata Purāṇa). See ../the-14-manvantaras/ for the full sequence.
- The four future Sāvarṇi-line Manus (Dakṣa-, Brahma-, Dharma-, Rudra-, plus Deva-/Indra-Sāvarṇi) are variously described; sources broadly agree on the core officers below.

## Officers of the 12th Manvantara
- **Manu — Rudra-Sāvarṇi.** Presiding Manu of the age; his sons are headed by **Devavān** (Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).
  - *Variant (folk / Mānava-tradition):* Rudra-Sāvarṇi described as a son of Śiva (Rudra) and Pārvatī, hence the name "Rudra-Sāvarṇi."
- **Indra — Ṛtadhāma** (a.k.a. Ṛtudhāma / Ṛtadhāman); king of the devas for this manvantara (Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Bhāgavata Purāṇa).
- **Saptarṣis (Seven Sages):** Tapasvī, Sutapas, Tapomūrti, Taporati, Tapodhṛti, Tapodyuti, Tapodhana (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata Purāṇa).
- **Classes of devas:** the **Haritas** and others (Viṣṇu Purāṇa).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata):* five deva-classes — **Harita, Rohita, Sumanas, Sukarman, Surāpa** (Suparṇa) — each group with ten members.
- **Viṣṇu avatāra — Sudhāman (Svadhāman).** Born of **Sunṛtā**, wife of **Satyasaha**; the form of Viṣṇu who upholds dharma in this manvantara (Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).

## Notes & cautions
- Spellings of the Indra (Ṛtadhāma vs Ṛtudhāma) and the avatāra (Sudhāman vs Svadhāman) differ across recensions/translations; both are attested.
- As a future age, no narrative *events* are recorded — only the roster of officers (Manu, Indra, Saptarṣis, deva-classes, avatāra) is given in the Purāṇas.
