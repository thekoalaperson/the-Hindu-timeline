# Manvantara 5 — Raivata (Past)

> Hierarchy: Shveta-Varaha Kalpa (current Day of Brahma) → 5th of 14 Manvantaras → presided over by **Raivata Manu**. Long elapsed; precedes [Chakshusha (6th)](./manvantara-06-chakshusha.md) and the current [Vaivasvata (7th)](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). Each Manvantara ≈ 306,720,000 human years (71 Mahayugas + 1 sandhya).

## Overview
- **Raivata Manu** — fifth Manu, presiding sovereign of mankind for this era. Said to be the twin brother of the 4th Manu, **Tamasa** (both born of the same line). (*Vishnu Purana* 3.1; *Bhagavata Purana* 8.5)
  - *Variant (Bhagavata Purana 8.5):* Raivata Manu named **Raivata** as son of the sage line, with the era's deva-groups and Indra listed slightly differently from the Vishnu Purana.

## Key Office-Holders
- **Indra of this Manvantara: Vibhu** — the Indra (king of the devas) for the Raivata era. (*Vishnu Purana* 3.1)
  - *Variant (older Puranic recensions):* the Indra is named **Vibhu / Vipashchita** ("the all-knowing"). [variant spelling]
- **Classes of devas (gods):** named as **Amitabhas, Abhutarajas (Bhutarajas), Vaikunthas,** and **Sumedhas** (group names differ across recensions). (*Vishnu Purana* 3.1; *Bhagavata Purana* 8.5) [variant]
- **Saptarishis (seven sages):** **Hiranyaroma, Vedashri, Urddhabahu, Vedabahu, Sudhaman, Parjanya,** and **Mahamuni**. (*Vishnu Purana* 3.1)
  - *Variant (HinduPad / popular lists):* **Devabahu, Subahu, Parjanya, Somapa, Muni, Hiranyaroma,** and **Saptashva**. [variant]
- **Sons of Raivata Manu:** **Balabandhu, Susambhavya, Satyaka** and other kings (*Vishnu Purana*).
  - *Variant (HinduPad):* ten sons — Varuna, Tattvadarshi, Chitiman, Havyapa, Kavi, Mukta, Nirutsuka, Sattva, Vimoha, Prakashaka. [variant]

## Vishnu Avatar of the Era
- **Vaikuntha** — Vishnu's incarnation associated with the Raivata Manvantara; born to Vikuntha (the goddess) and credited with creating the Vaikuntha realm to please her. (*Bhagavata Purana* 8.5; secondary: Wikipedia "Manu") [late/secondary attribution — Puranic detail sparse]

## Notes & Status
- This Manvantara is fully **past** and lightly documented; Puranic accounts give mainly the lists above (Manu, Indra, deva-classes, Saptarishis, sons, avatar) with notable name variants between texts. Stub — expandable with full per-text variant tables.
