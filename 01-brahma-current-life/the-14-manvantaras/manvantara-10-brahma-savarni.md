# Manvantara 10 — Brahma-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Hierarchy: 1st Day of Brahma's 51st year (Shveta-Varaha Kalpa) → **10th of 14 Manvantaras**. A *future* age: three Manvantaras after the current [7th, Vaivasvata](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). One of the "Sāvarṇi" group of future Manus. STUB — expandable.

**Duration:** ~306,720,000 human years (71 Mahayugas + 1 sandhya), like every Manvantara. See [framework constants](../../00-time-and-cosmology/).

- **Brahma-Sāvarṇi Manu** — the presiding Manu of this age; tenth in the sequence of 14. Son of **Upaśloka**, who in turn is described as a son of the Sun-god / of the Sāvarṇi line (Bhagavata Purana VII.13.21-23). His ten sons include **Bhūriṣeṇa** (Bhagavata Purana).
  - *Variant (Vishnu Purana III.2):* the name **Brahma-sāvarṇi** is given for the 10th Manu, but parentage and sons are not detailed; the Purana simply lists him in the sequence after Dakṣa-sāvarṇi and before Dharma-sāvarṇi.

- **Indra of this Manvantara** — **Śambhu** (Bhagavata Purana VII.13).
  - *Variant (Vishnu Purana III.2):* **Śānti** is named as the Indra of the Brahma-sāvarṇi Manvantara.

- **Saptarishis (Seven Sages)** — **Haviṣmān (Haviṣmat), Sukṛti, Satya, Apāṃmūrti, Nābhāga, Apratimaujas, Satyaketu** (Vishnu Purana III.2.25-28).

- **Classes of devas** — two groups, **Sudhāma** and **Viśuddha** (a.k.a. Viśvadeva grouping), each with a set membership (Vishnu Purana). [Variant deva-class names appear across Puranas; cross-check Brahmanda / Vayu / Markandeya when expanding.]

- **Avatar of Viṣṇu** — Vishnu manifests as **Viśvaksena** (Viśvaksena/Śrīmūrti), born to assist the Manu and uphold dharma in this age (Bhagavata Purana VII.13; Purana Index).

**Note:** As an unmanifest future age, accounts are skeletal and Puranas disagree chiefly on the Indra's name (Śambhu vs Śānti) and on the deva-class lists. See sibling future ages: [9 Dakṣa-sāvarṇi](manvantara-09-daksha-savarni.md) · [11 Dharma-sāvarṇi](manvantara-11-dharma-savarni.md).
