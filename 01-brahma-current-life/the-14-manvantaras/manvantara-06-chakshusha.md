# Manvantara 6 — Cākṣuṣa (Past)

> Hierarchy: Brahma's current life → Śveta-Varāha Kalpa → **6th of the 14 Manvantaras** (the one immediately before ours). Presided over by Cākṣuṣa Manu. See [the current 7th](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/) and [overview of all 14](../) (per [framework constants](../../../00-time-and-cosmology/), each manvantara ≈ 71 mahāyugas + 1 sandhyā ≈ 306,720,000 years).

**Status:** STUB — essentials only; expandable. The last completed manvantara before Vaivasvata (ours).

## The Presiding Manu

- **Cākṣuṣa Manu** — the sixth Manu, son of the deity Cakṣus (the personified faculty of sight); his manvantara takes its name from him (Viṣṇu Purāṇa; Bhāgavata Purāṇa).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata / Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa):* born in a prior life as the sage **Ānanda**, who through severe penance was blessed by Brahmā to become a Manu. [account differs by text]
- **Sons of Cākṣuṣa** — listed as headed by **Puru, Pūru(ṣa), Sudyumna** (Bhāgavata Purāṇa); **Ruru** named the most prominent in other tellings (Viṣṇu Purāṇa). [variant lists]

## Cosmic Officers of this Manvantara

- **Indra (king of the devas):** **Manojava** (Bhāgavata Purāṇa).
  - *Variant:* **Mantradruma** (Viṣṇu Purāṇa); also given as Manoja / Camūmān in other recensions. [variant]
- **Class of devas:** the **Āpyas** (and associated groups — Ādya, Pṛthuga, Lekha, etc., by text) (Bhāgavata Purāṇa). [lists differ across Purāṇas]
- **Saptarṣis (Seven Sages):** **Sumedhas, Virajas, Haviṣmat, Uttama, Madhu, Abhināman, Sahiṣṇu** (Viṣṇu Purāṇa).
  - *Variant (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, alt. enumeration):* Hiraṇyaromā, Vedaśrī, Ūrddhabāhu, Vedabāhu, Sudhāman, Parjanya, Mahāmuni. [variant — texts give multiple sage-lists]
- **Avatāra of Viṣṇu:** **Ajita** — manifested to uphold dharma; in this period the sages **Nara and Nārāyaṇa** (sons of Dharma) also appear (Bhāgavata Purāṇa).
  - *Variant:* the avatāra is named **Vaikuṇṭha** (alt. Purāṇic account). [variant]

## Notes

- The avatāra **Ajita** is the form traditionally said to churn the ocean of milk (Kṣīra-sāgara manthana) carrying Mount Mandara as the tortoise **Kūrma** in the Bhāgavata frame; attribution of the churning to this manvantara is text-dependent. [disputed across sources]
- This manvantara ended at the close of its sandhyā; the dissolution-and-renewal led into the **7th, Vaivasvata Manvantara** — our own. See [Manvantara 7 — Vaivasvata](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/).
