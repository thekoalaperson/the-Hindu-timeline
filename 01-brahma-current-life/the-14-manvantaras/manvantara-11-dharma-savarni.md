# Manvantara 11 — Dharma-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Cosmic address: Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → 11th of the 14 Manvantaras (a *future* manvantara; we are currently in the 7th, [Vaivasvata](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/)). One of four "Savarni" Manus prophesied for the latter half of this Kalpa. See [the 14-Manvantara index](README.md) and [the cosmic clock](../../00-time-and-cosmology/).

- A Manvantara = 71 Mahāyugas + 1 sandhyā ≈ **306,720,000 human years**. Each Manvantara installs a fresh Manu, set of seven Ṛṣis, Indra, and classes of devas (Vishnu Purana III). This entry is a STUB; expand as variant lists are collated.

## The Manu

- **Dharma-Sāvarṇi** — the 11th Manu, ruling the 11th Manvantara (Dharma-sāvarṇi Manvantara). One of the "Sāvarṇi" group (sons/offshoots of Sūrya/Sāvarṇa lineage) prophesied for the future (Vishnu Purana III.2; Bhagavata Purana VIII.13).
  - *Variant (Bhagavata Purana VIII.13):* names the Manu's sons/kings (e.g., Satyadharma and others) — son-lists differ by recension and are sparsely attested; mark as [late/uncertain] pending collation.

## Saptarishi (Seven Sages)

- **Nischara, Agnitejas, Vapuṣmān, Viṣṇu, Aruṇi, Haviṣmān, Anagha** — the seven Ṛṣis of this Manvantara (Vishnu Purana III.2).
  - *Variant:* Bhagavata/other Puranas give partly differing sage-lists for future Manvantaras; cross-check before treating any single roster as canonical [disputed].

## Indra & Devas

- **Indra: Vṛṣa (Vṛṣabha)** — the king of the gods for this Manvantara (Vishnu Purana III.2).
- **Classes of devas:** the **Vihaṅgamas, Kāmagamas, and Nirmāṇaratis**, each thirty in number (Vishnu Purana III.2).
  - *Variant:* deva-class names/counts vary across Puranic recensions for the Savarni Manvantaras; [uncertain].
