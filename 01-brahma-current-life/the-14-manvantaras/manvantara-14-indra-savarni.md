# Manvantara 14 — Indra-Sāvarṇi (Bhautya) (Future)

> Where this sits: [01-brahma-current-life](../) → the 14 manvantaras of the current Kalpa (Shveta-Varaha) → the **14th and LAST** Manu-period. It runs after [Manvantara 13 — Deva-Sāvarṇi](manvantara-13-deva-savarni.md), and at its close comes the **Night of Brahma** (naimittika pralaya). All of this is **future / prophetic** relative to us (we are in the 7th, Vaivasvata — see [manvantara-07-vaivasvata](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/)).

This is the **final movement of Brahma's present Day**. After Bhautya Manu's 71 Mahayugas elapse, the seventh and last "Sāvarṇika-block" Manvantara closes, the present Kalpa ends, and the worlds dissolve into the **Night of Brahma** (≈4.32 billion years) — see [../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md](../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md). Duration ≈306,720,000 years (71 Mahayugas + 1 sandhya), per the constants in [../../00-time-and-cosmology](../../00-time-and-cosmology/). The record for this Manvantara is genuinely **sparse and almost wholly schematic** in the primary texts — the Puranas give little more than the roster (Manu, Indra, sages, avatar, deva-classes) plus one short birth-story for the Manu; this file flags where nothing more is attested and does not invent narrative.

## The presiding Manu

- **Manu — Indra-Sāvarṇi, a.k.a. Bhautya** — the fourteenth and final Manu of this Kalpa; son of **Bhūti** (hence the patronymic *Bhautya*) (*Vishnu Purana III.2*; *Bhagavata Purana 8.13.33–35*; *Matsya Purana 9*). His reign is the last Manu-period before the Day of Brahma ends.
  - *Variant (naming):* the same Manu is called **"Indra-Sāvarṇi"** (the fifth and last of the Sāvarṇa/Sāvarṇika group — Sāvarṇi, Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi, Brahma-Sāvarṇi, Dharma-Sāvarṇi, Rudra/Deva-Sāvarṇi, Indra-Sāvarṇi) and **"Bhautya"** (patronymic from Bhūti). Both names denote the 14th Manu. [Lists of the Sāvarṇika ordering vary slightly by recension.]
  - **Birth-story of Bhautya** — his father **Bhūti**, a disciple of sage **Aṅgiras**, was stern-tempered and long childless despite many sacrifices. While Bhūti travelled with his brother Suvarcha, his ashram (and its sacred fire) was left to his disciple **Śānti**; when the **sacrificial fire went out**, Śānti propitiated **Agni**, who rekindled the fire and granted the boon that Bhūti would have a son. So Bhautya, the future Manu, was born ([folk]/Puranic episode; told in popular retellings of the *Mārkaṇḍeya/Bhāgavata* Manu-cycle). (Chronologically: in the deep future, end of this Kalpa.)
  - *Variant (sons of Manu):*
    - *Bhagavata Purana 8.13.34:* names **Uru** and others (*"Uru and others were his sons"*) as the sons/royal founders of this period.
    - *Vishnu Purana III.2:* names **Uru, Gambhīra** (and others) as his sons.
    - *Folk/expanded list:* one popular enumeration gives **ten sons** — Anugraha, Bharata, Brahma, Guha, Gambhīra, Pratīra, Śaṅkaramaṇ, Śrīmaṇ, Subala, and Viṣṇu [folk; not in the core Purana verses].

## Indra (king of the devas)

- **Indra — Śuci (Suchi)** — the Indra who rules the heavens during this Manvantara (*Bhagavata Purana 8.13.34: "During his period Śuci was Indra"*; *Vishnu Purana III.2*; *Matsya Purana*).
  - *Variant:* "Bṛhadbhānu" (the avatar, below) occasionally bleeds into renderings of the Sāvarṇika sequence, but **Śuci** is the consistent name for Bhautya's Indra across Vishnu, Bhagavata, and Matsya Puranas.
  - Note the homonymy: **Śuci** is also the name of one of this Manvantara's **saptarishis** (see below) and a son of Bhṛgu — the Puranas reuse the name; the Indra Śuci and the sage Śuci are distinguished only by role.

## The Saptarishi (seven sages)

- **Agnibāhu, Śuci, Śukra, Māgadha, Gṛdhra, Yukta, and Ajita** — the seven rishis (saptarishi) of the Bhautya Manvantara (*Vishnu Purana III.2*; *Bhagavata Purana 8.13.34* abbreviates as "Agnibāhu and others"; *Matsya Purana 9*).
  - *Variant (transliteration):* "Agnibāhu" appears in some scans as **Agnibshu/Agnibhu**; "Śukra" as **Aukra** (OCR/recension noise). The canonical seven are as listed above.
  - *Note (Śukra):* "Śukra" here is a *saptarishi* name in the Bhautya age — not to be confused with the famous **Śukrācārya**, son of Bhṛgu and guru of the asuras (the planet Venus). The Puranas note that **Bhṛgu's sons** included a Śuci and a Śukra, so the names recur across the Manu-rosters.

## Vishnu's avatar for the age

- **Bṛhadbhānu** — Vishnu's (Hari's) sustaining descent for the Bhautya Manvantara; born of **Sātrāyaṇa** (father) by **Vitānā** (mother) (*Bhagavata Purana 8.13.35: "Bṛhadbhānu was a manifestation of Hari"*; also in *Vishnu Purana III.2* in outline).
  - *Variant (spelling):* rendered **Bṛhadbhānu / Brihadbhanu / Brhadbhanu**; parents appear as **Satrāyaṇa & Vitānā** (sometimes "Satrayana"/"Vitana"). The texts give only the name and parentage — no deeds of this future avatar are narrated (the avatar-list for the Sāvarṇika/Bhautya periods is purely schematic). ## The deva-classes (orders of gods)

- **Cākṣuṣas, Pavitras, Kaniṣṭhas, Bhrājiras, and Vāvṛddhas** — the five classes/orders of gods reigning with Indra Śuci (*Vishnu Purana III.2*).
  - *Variant (Bhagavata):* *Bhagavata Purana 8.13.34* names **the Pavitras and others** as the demigods of this period (compressed list); the Vishnu Purana gives the fuller five-fold roster.
  - *Variant (spelling/recension):* "Bhrājiras" also appears as **Bhrāji/Bhrājika**; "Vāvṛddhas" as **Vāvṛddha**. Counts of god-classes per Manvantara vary by Purana.

## What happens (and the close of the Kalpa)

- **A schematic reign, sparsely narrated** — beyond the roster above and the short birth-story of the Manu, the primary texts attach **no extended narrative cycle** to the Bhautya Manvantara. It functions as the closing bracket of the Kalpa-list. (Honest gap: the record here is thin by design — these future Manvantaras are enumerated, not storied.)
- **Close of the 14th Manvantara → end of the Kalpa** — when Bhautya's 71 Mahayugas (+ sandhya) complete, the **fourteenth and last Manu-period ends**, and with it the entire **Day of Brahma (Kalpa)** (*Vishnu Purana; Bhagavata Purana*). See the full Manu sequence in [./README.md](./README.md) and the start of the day at [manvantara-01-svayambhuva.md](manvantara-01-svayambhuva.md).
- **→ Night of Brahma (naimittika pralaya)** — dissolution follows: the three worlds (Bhūr, Bhuvar, Svar) are burned and flooded, and creation rests for **one Night of Brahma (≈4.32 billion years)** before the next Kalpa dawns and a new cycle of 14 Manus begins (*Vishnu Purana; Bhagavata Purana*). Full account: [../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md](../../00-time-and-cosmology/kalpa-and-pralaya.md).
