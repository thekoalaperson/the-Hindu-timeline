# Manvantara 13 — Deva-Sāvarṇi / Raucya (Future)

> Hierarchy: Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → the [14 Manvantaras](README.md) → **13th Manvantara (future)**. Lies far ahead of our current [7th Vaivasvata Manvantara](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/) — six manvantaras (the [8th](manvantara-08-savarni.md) through [12th](manvantara-12-rudra-savarni.md)) still separate us from it. Each manvantara ≈ 306,720,000 yrs (71 mahayugas + 1 sandhya). Next: [Manvantara 14 — Indra-Sāvarṇi / Bhautya](manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md). Up-link: [the-14-manvantaras/README](README.md).

The 13th of the 14 manvantaras, presided over by Manu **Deva-Sāvarṇi** — known in a parallel scriptural tradition as **Raucya** ("son of Ruci"). Like all post-Vaivasvata manvantaras, the record is **prophetic**: the office-holders are given only as terse lists in the Puranas' rosters of *future* Manus, plus (for the Raucya tradition) a back-story attached to the sage Ruci. There is no narrative event-cycle, no war, no incarnation-deeds — only the cast of the age-to-come. [folk/scriptural future-projection — not historical]

A central caution governs this whole entry: **the two main scriptural streams may be describing the same Manu under two names, or two distinct Manus.** The [Bhāgavata](../../90-sources-and-apparatus/) and Vishnu Puranas call the 13th Manu **Deva-Sāvarṇi** and group him with the five other "Sāvarṇi" Manus (8th–13th). The Mārkaṇḍeya/Harivamsha stream instead calls the 13th Manu **Raucya**, son of the Prajāpati **Ruci**, with an entirely independent biography. Most modern compilers treat the two as aliases of one office; the texts themselves do not reconcile them. [disputed]

## The Manu

- **Deva-Sāvarṇi** — 13th Manu in the Bhāgavata/Vishnu enumeration, described as advanced in spiritual knowledge (*jñāna*); his father is named **Satyasaha** in the Bhāgavata gloss (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.31–32; Vishnu Purāṇa 3.2). Like the other "Sāvarṇi" Manus, the surname *Sāvarṇi* ("of like colour / of the Savarna") marks him as one of a cluster born of a shared solar lineage, distinguishing him from the 8th Manu Sāvarṇi proper (see [Manvantara 8 — Sāvarṇi](manvantara-08-savarni.md)).
- **Raucya** — the same office in the Mārkaṇḍeya line, named as the son of the sage **Ruci** by his wife **Mālinī**. The Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa (chs. 92–95) gives a full origin-story (below) before noting that "the devas, saptarṣis, and all the royal sons" of this manvantara are catalogued there (Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, Raucya section).
  - *Variant (one Manu or two):* the Bhāgavata never uses "Raucya"; the Mārkaṇḍeya never uses "Deva-Sāvarṇi." Compilers equate them by **shared sequence position (13th)** and **shared avatar/parent names (Yogeśvara, Devahotra)**, which the Bhāgavata also attaches to the 13th Manu — strong but not explicit evidence of identity. [disputed]
  - *Variant (Bhautya confusion):* some popular lists swap the names of the 13th and 14th Manus, calling the 14th "Raucya" or the 13th "Bhautya." The standard order keeps **Raucya = 13th**, **Bhautya = 14th** (cf. [Manvantara 14](manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md)). [disputed]

## Origin-story of Raucya (Mārkaṇḍeya tradition)

The Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa is unusual in giving the 13th Manu a real narrative rather than a bare list — it is the richest single source for this otherwise sparse manvantara.

- **Ruci's refusal of marriage** — the sage Ruci, a wandering ascetic committed to total renunciation, holds that marriage and household life only breed suffering and bondage. He refuses to wed (Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa).
- **The Pitṛs' rebuke** — Ruci's ancestral spirits (*Pitṛs*) appear and correct him, teaching that the **five great sacrifices (pañca-mahā-yajña)** performed without selfish desire destroy inauspiciousness, and that one who shirks prescribed householder duty cannot reach the higher worlds; offspring are owed to the ancestors. Ruci composes a hymn of praise to the Pitṛs (the "Pitṛ-stotra," a noted devotional set-piece of the text).
- **Brahmā's command** — after Ruci performs *tapas* for a hundred divine years, **Brahmā** appears and commands him to marry and beget a son destined to be a Manu (Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa).
- **Mālinī given in marriage** — the Apsaras **Pramlocā** presents her daughter **Mālinī** as Ruci's bride.
  - *Variant (Mālinī's parentage):* one strand makes Mālinī the daughter of Pramlocā and **Puṣkara, son of Varuṇa**; the apsaras Pramlocā is the same celestial associated elsewhere with the seduction of the ascetic Kaṇḍu. [folk]
- **Birth of Raucya** — from Mālinī's womb is born a son "of great heroism and intellect," who becomes the Manu **Raucya**, presiding over the 13th manvantara (Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa).
- *Honest gap:* the Mārkaṇḍeya text foregrounds Ruci's spiritual instruction and the Pitṛ-hymn, then names Raucya — but the **detailed roster** (which sages, which Indra, which devas) is where the Mārkaṇḍeya and Bhāgavata diverge, and the Mārkaṇḍeya's own list is less commonly quoted than the Bhāgavata's. The enumerated office-holders below are therefore given primarily on Bhāgavata authority. [scholarly]

## Office-holders of the 13th Manvantara

- **Indra:** **Divaspati** ("Lord of Heaven") holds the post of Indra for this manvantara (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.31; Vishnu Purāṇa 3.2). The name is a stock epithet of Indra reused here as the personal name of the cyclic incumbent — a pattern across the future manvantaras, where the *office* of Indra is filled by a new being each age (see [Indra in the cosmic cycle](../../03-deities/) and [the 14 Manvantaras README](README.md) on rotating office-holders).
- **Saptarishis (Seven Sages):** **Nirmoha, Tattvadarśin, Niṣprakampa, Nirutsuka, Dhṛtimat, Avyaya,** and **Sutapas** (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.32). The names are markedly abstract — "Free-of-Delusion," "Seer-of-Truth," "Unshaken," "Desireless," "Steadfast," "Imperishable," "Of-Good-Austerity" — reading as personified virtues of the coming age rather than the older mythic ṛṣi-names of the [Vaivasvata saptarishis](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/).
  - *Variant (Nirmoka vs Nirmoha):* several lists, including the Vishnu Purāṇa stream and Sanskrit-dictionary glosses, give **Nirmoka** ("Cast-off-skin / Sloughed") in the place of, or alongside, **Nirmoha**. The two spellings are routinely treated as one sage; the Bhāgavata index records both readings. [disputed]
  - *Variant (membership drift):* because future-Manu saptarishi lists were transmitted as memorized name-strings, individual members vary across the Vishnu, Bhāgavata, Mārkaṇḍeya, and Brahma Puranas. No two manuscript traditions are guaranteed identical for this future age. [scholarly]
- **Deva-classes (demigods):** the **Sukarmas** ("Of-good-deeds") are the principal deva-class of this manvantara (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.31).
  - *Variant (Sutramas):* some enumerations pair or replace the Sukarmas with the **Sutramas** ("Well-protecting") class among the devas of the 13th manvantara. [disputed]
- **Sons of the Manu:** **Chitrasena (Citrasena)** and others are named among Deva-Sāvarṇi's progeny / the kings of the age (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.30–31; Sanskrit-dictionary index "Devasāvarṇi"). The full roster of "royal sons" is what the Mārkaṇḍeya promises for the Raucya line but is rarely quoted in detail.

## Avatāra of the period

- **Yogeśvara (Yogeshwara)** — "Lord of Yoga," the manvantara-avatāra of Viṣṇu for the 13th age, born of the father **Devahotra** and the mother **Bṛhatī** (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13.32). The Bhāgavata adds that Yogeśvara will, "by His mystic power (yoga), free the worlds from misery." His appearance through **Devahotra** is one of the shared data-points that lets compilers identify the Bhāgavata's Deva-Sāvarṇi with the Mārkaṇḍeya's Raucya.
  - *Variant (name as office vs personal):* "Yogeśvara" is also a generic title of Kṛṣṇa/Viṣṇu; here it functions as the proper name of the period-specific [manvantara-avatāra](../../03-deities/), one of the recurring "Manvantara avatāras" (each manvantara has its own — cf. Vaivasvata's avatāras). [scholarly]

## Reconciliation notes

- **The six "Sāvarṇi" Manus (8th–13th):** the Bhāgavata groups Manus 8 through 13 under the surname *Sāvarṇi*, said to share a solar/Savarna lineage — [8 Sāvarṇi](manvantara-08-savarni.md), [9 Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi](manvantara-09-daksha-savarni.md), [10 Brahma-Sāvarṇi](manvantara-10-brahma-savarni.md), [11 Dharma-Sāvarṇi](manvantara-11-dharma-savarni.md), [12 Rudra-Sāvarṇi](manvantara-12-rudra-savarni.md), and **13 Deva-Sāvarṇi**. The 13th is the last to bear the name before the final [14th Bhautya](manvantara-14-indra-savarni.md). [late text]
- **Why the record is sparse:** every detail here is a *future* projection internal to the current Shveta-Varaha Kalpa; the Puranas furnish names, not deeds, for ages yet to come. Treat the entire entry as scriptural prophecy, honestly thin on narrative. [folk]
