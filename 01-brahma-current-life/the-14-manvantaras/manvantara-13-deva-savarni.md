# Manvantara 13 — Deva-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Hierarchy: Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → the 14 Manvantaras → **13th Manvantara (future)**. Lies far ahead of our current [7th Vaivasvata Manvantara](manvantara-07-vaivasvata/) — six manvantaras still separate us from it. Each manvantara ≈ 306,720,000 yrs (71 mahayugas + 1 sandhya). **STUB** — skeletal; expand later.

The 13th of the 14 manvantaras presided over by Manu **Deva-Sāvarṇi** (also called **Raucya**, "son of Ruci"). Like all post-Vaivasvata manvantaras, the details are prophetic, given in the Puranas' lists of future Manus. [folk/scriptural future-projection — not historical]

## The Manu
- **Deva-Sāvarṇi (Raucya)** — 13th Manu, described as highly advanced in spiritual knowledge / *jñāna* (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.31–32; Vishnu Purana 3.2).
  - *Variant (naming):* listed as **Deva-sāvarṇi** in Bhagavata/Vishnu Purana enumerations; the alias **Raucya** (patronymic from sage Ruci) appears in the Markandeya/Harivamsha line of Manu lists.

## Office-holders of the 13th Manvantara
- **Indra:** **Divaspati** holds the post of Indra for this manvantara (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.31).
- **Saptarishis (Seven Sages):** Nirmoha, Tattvadarśin, Nishprakampa, Nirutsuka, Dhṛtimat, Avyaya, Sutapas (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.32).
  - *Variant:* some lists name **Nirmoka** among the sages (Vishnu Purana 3.2) in place of / alongside Nirmoha.
- **Devas (demigods):** the **Sukarmas** and associated classes (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.31).
- **Sons of the Manu:** Chitrasena and others named among Deva-Sāvarṇi's progeny (Bhagavata Purana 8.13).

## Avatara of the period
- **Yogeśvara (Yogeshwara)** — the manvantara-avatara of Vishnu, born of **Devahotra** and **Bṛhatī** (Bhagavata Purana 8.13.32).
