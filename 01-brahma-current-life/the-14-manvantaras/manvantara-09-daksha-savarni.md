# Manvantara 9 — Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Hierarchy: Brahma's current life → Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → the 14 Manvantaras → **9th (Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi)**. A *future* manvantara — two cycles after our own [7th Vaivasvata](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). Each manvantara ≈ 306,720,000 human years (71 mahayugas + 1 sandhya).

The ninth of the 14 cosmic ages, ruled by Manu **Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi**. One of three "Sāvarṇi" Manus grouped after [Sāvarṇi (8th)](./manvantara-08-savarni.md). Details below are scriptural projections; this is a STUB and can be expanded.

## Manu & rulers

- **Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi, the 9th Manu** — said to be a son of Varuṇa; presides over the 9th manvantara (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).
- **Indra: Adbhuta** — the king of the devas this cycle, described as mighty and powerful (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).
- **Classes of devas:** three groups — the **Pārṇas (Pāras)**, **Marīcigarbhas**, and **Sudharmans** — each comprising twelve gods (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13):* lists the deva-class **Marīcigarbhas** among the gods of Adbhuta's reign; group names/counts vary slightly between Purāṇas.
- **Saptarṣis (Seven Sages):** Savana, Dyutimān, Bhavya, Vasu, Medhātithi, Jyotiṣmān, and Satya (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2).
- **Sons of the Manu:** Dhṛtaketu, Dīptiketu, Pañcahasta, Nirāmaya, Pṛthuśravā, and others — who become rulers/kings of this manvantara (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).

## Vishnu avatar

- **Ṛṣabha** — the avatāra of Viṣṇu for this manvantara, said to be born of **Āyuṣmān and Ambudhārā** (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13). [Distinct from the earlier Ṛṣabhadeva avatāra.]
  - *Note:* the Viṣṇu Purāṇa account of this manvantara emphasizes Manu, Indra, sages and sons but does not detail a Viṣṇu incarnation here; the avatāra detail is principally from the Bhāgavata.
