# Manvantara 9 — Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Hierarchy: [Brahma's current life](../) → Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → [the 14 Manvantaras](./README.md) → **9th (Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi)**. A *future* manvantara — two cycles after our own [7th, Vaivasvata](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/), and the second of the six "Sāvarṇi" Manus that follow [Sāvarṇi (8th)](./manvantara-08-savarni.md).
>
> **Duration:** ~306,720,000 human years (71 mahāyugas + 1 sandhyā), like every manvantara. See [framework constants](../../00-time-and-cosmology/).

The ninth of the 14 cosmic ages, ruled by Manu **Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi** ("Dakṣa-of-like-complexion"). Because this age is unmanifest and yet to come, the Purāṇas describe it only by its standing roster — Manu, Indra, seven sages, deva-classes, royal sons, and the Viṣṇu avatāra — rather than by narrative events. There is no surviving *story* literature for this manvantara; the record is genuinely a name-list, and the entries below catalog the Purāṇic variants of those names. Next: [10th Brahma-Sāvarṇi](./manvantara-10-brahma-savarni.md).

## The Manu

- **Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi, the 9th Manu** — described as a **son of Varuṇa** (the deva of waters/cosmic order). One of the six "Sāvarṇi"-type Manus (Dakṣa-, Brahma-, Dharma-, Rudra-, Deva-, Indra-sāvarṇi) grouped after the chief [Sāvarṇi (8th)](./manvantara-08-savarni.md) (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).
  - *Note on the name:* "Sāvarṇi" ("of like varṇa/complexion") attaches to a cluster of future Manus; here it is paired with **Dakṣa**, distinguishing him from the patriarch [Dakṣa Prajāpati](../../03-deities-and-beings/) of the present age. The Purāṇas do not narrate how this future Manu relates to that earlier Dakṣa — the epithet is a classificatory tag, not a claimed descent.

## Key figures of the age

- **Indra: Adbhuta** — the king of the devas for this manvantara, described as "mighty and powerful." Each manvantara has its own Indra; Adbhuta succeeds [Bali](./manvantara-08-savarni.md) (the 8th-manvantara Indra) in the sequence (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).

- **Saptarṣis (Seven Sages):** **Savana, Dyutimān (Dyutimat), Bhavya, Vasu, Medhātithi, Jyotiṣmān, and Satya** (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13):* the Bhāgavata's brief account highlights **Dyutimān** among the seven sages of this age but does not enumerate the full set in the same order; the seven-name list is principally from the Viṣṇu Purāṇa.
  - *Note:* names such as **Medhātithi**, **Vasu**, and **Jyotiṣmān** recur across different manvantara saptarṣi-lists in the Purāṇas (the sages are reconstituted each age), so identity-of-name does not imply identity-of-person across cycles.

- **Classes of devas:** three groups, each comprising **twelve gods** — the **Pāras (Pāra / Pārṇas)**, the **Marīcigarbhas**, and the **Sudharmas (Sudharmāna)** (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13):* the Bhāgavata names the **Marīcigarbhas** among the demigods of Adbhuta's reign but does not give all three group-names or fix the count at twelve; group names and member-counts vary slightly between Purāṇas. Cross-check Brahmāṇḍa / Vāyu / Mārkaṇḍeya when expanding.

- **Sons of the Manu (heirs/kings of the age):** **Dhṛtaketu, Dīptiketu, Pañcahasta, Nirāmaya, Pṛthuśravā**, and others — who become the rulers/kings during this manvantara (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).
  - *Variant (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13):* the Bhāgavata gives the Manu's sons as **headed by Bhūtaketu** (sometimes rendered Dīptaketu), differing in the lead name and spellings from the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's "Dhṛtaketu, Dīptiketu …" The two lists overlap but are not identical.

## Avatāra of Viṣṇu

- **Ṛṣabha** — Viṣṇu incarnates as **Ṛṣabha** in this manvantara, born of the parents **Āyuṣmān** (father) and **Ambudhārā** (mother). In this age he upholds dharma and supports the Indra Adbhuta and Manu Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.13).
  - *[Disambiguation]* This Ṛṣabha is **distinct from [Ṛṣabhadeva](../../01-brahma-current-life/)**, the earlier "lila" avatāra (son of Nābhi and Merudevī, father of Bharata) narrated in Bhāgavata Purāṇa 5 within the *present* Vaivasvata manvantara. The two share a name but are different incarnations in different ages.
  - *Note (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2):* the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's account of this manvantara enumerates Manu, Indra, sages, deva-classes and sons but **does not detail a Viṣṇu incarnation** here; the Ṛṣabha-avatāra datum is supplied principally by the Bhāgavata's parallel list of the fourteen Manvantara-avatāras.

## Honest gaps in the record

- No *events* survive — Because Dakṣa-Sāvarṇi's age has not yet come, no battles, sages' deeds, or king-stories are told of it. The Purāṇic "account" is a fixed administrative roster (Manu / Indra / sages / devas / sons / avatāra), not a narrative.
- **Where the texts disagree:** lead son (Dhṛtaketu/Dīptiketu vs Bhūtaketu/Dīptaketu); whether all three deva-classes are named or only the Marīcigarbhas; and whether a Viṣṇu avatāra (Ṛṣabha) belongs to this age at all (present in Bhāgavata, absent in Viṣṇu Purāṇa).
