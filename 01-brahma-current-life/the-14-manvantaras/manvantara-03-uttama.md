# Manvantara 3 — Uttama (Past)

> Hierarchy: Brahma's current life → Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → 3rd of the 14 [Manvantaras](./). Long past; the 6 pre-Vaivasvata manvantaras are sketched only in lists across the Puranas. We are now in the [7th — Vaivasvata](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). See also the prior [2nd — Svarochisha](./manvantara-02-svarochisha.md) and the next [4th — Tamasa](./manvantara-04-tamasa.md). Each manvantara ≈ 306,720,000 human years (71 Mahayugas + 1 sandhya).

The standard "cast list" of any manvantara names a Manu, his Indra, the class of devas, the Saptarishis, the Manu's sons, and a Vishnu avatar; sources mostly agree on Uttama but disagree on his Indra and parentage.

- **Uttama Manu (the 3rd Manu).** Presiding Manu of this period; son of Priyavrata, brother of fellow-Manus Tamasa and Raivata (Bhagavata Purana 8.1; Vishnu Purana 3.1). His sons include Pavana, Srinjaya and Yajnahotra (Bhagavata Purana).
  - *Variant (parentage):* most Puranas make Uttama a son of Priyavrata; some genealogies and the name-overlap with "Auttama" treat him simply as the 3rd in the Manu sequence without the Priyavrata link [minor variant].
- **Indra = Satyajit.** During Uttama's reign Satyajit holds the office of Indra, king of the devas (Bhagavata Purana 8.1).
  - *Variant (Brahmanda Purana):* the Indra of this manvantara is **Sushanti** (not Satyajit). [Catalog both — the two main streams disagree.]
- **Devas of the period.** The classes of gods are the **Satyas, Devashrutas (Vedashrutas) and Bhadras** (Bhagavata Purana; Vishnu Purana).
- **Saptarishis (Seven Sages).** The seven seers are the **sons of Vasishtha** — Kaukundihi, Kurundi, Dalaya, Shankha, Pravahita, Mita and Sammita (Bhagavata Purana 8.1; Vishnu Purana 3.1).
  - *Variant (Vishnu Purana, Wilson tr.):* this set is "headed by Pramada," with the seven sages reckoned among Vasishtha's progeny.
- **Vishnu Avatar — Satyasena.** Vishnu incarnates as **Satyasena**, born of **Sunrita**, wife of **Dharma**; accompanied by the **Satyavratas**, he destroys the Yakshas, Rakshasas and lying/evil spirits troubling the worlds, fighting alongside Indra Satyajit (Bhagavata Purana 8.1).
