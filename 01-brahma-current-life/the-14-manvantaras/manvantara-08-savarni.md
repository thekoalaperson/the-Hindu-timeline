# Manvantara 8 — Sāvarṇi (Future)

> Where this sits: Brahma's current life → Shveta-Varaha Kalpa → the 14 Manvantaras → the **8th**, ruling the age that follows our own [7th, Vaivasvata](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/). [Future — not yet begun.] See ../the-14-manvantaras/ for the full sequence.

The next Manu after Vaivasvata. His manvantara begins after our current 28-mahayuga Vaivasvata cycle completes (~426,873 years remain in this Kali Yuga). [STUB — expandable.]

## The Manu

- **Sāvarṇi Manu (8th Manu)** — son of **Sūrya/Vivasvān** by **Chhāyā** (the "Shadow" wife, born of Saṃjñā/Saranyu), hence "Sāvarṇi" ("of like complexion"). Will rule the 8th manvantara (Vishnu Purana 3.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13).
  - *Note:* he is the chief Sāvarṇi; the next several Manus (9th–14th) are also "Sāvarṇi" types (Dakṣa-, Brahma-, Dharma-, Rudra-, Deva-, Indra-sāvarṇi) — Bhagavata Purana 8.13.

## Key Figures of the Age

- **Indra: Bali** — the dāitya king Bali, sinless son of Virochana, will become Indra (king of the devas) by Vishnu's grace, having been pushed to Patala in our age by Vamana (Vishnu Purana 3.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13).
- **Saptarishis (Seven Sages):** Diptimat (Dīptimān), Gālava, **Paraśurāma**, Kṛpa, Drauṇi/**Aśvatthāmā**, Vyāsa, and Ṛṣyaśṛṅga — several being **chiranjivis** (immortals) carried over from earlier ages (Vishnu Purana 3.2; Bhagavata Purana 8.13).
- **Gods (deva classes):** Sutapās, Amitābhas, and Mukhyas — each a group of twenty (Vishnu Purana 3.2).
- **Sons of Sāvarṇi (heirs/kings):** headed by **Nirmoka**, Virajaska, and others (Bhagavata Purana 8.13).
  - *Variant (Harivamsha 7):* ten sons named — Varīyān, Avarīyān, Sammala, Dhṛtimān, Vasu, Variṣṇa, Ārya, Dhṛṣṇu, Rāja, Sumati.

## Avatar of the Age

- **Sārvabhauma** — Vishnu incarnates as Sārvabhauma, son of **Devaguhya** and **Sarasvatī**; he wrests Svarga (heaven/Indra-hood) from the then-Indra **Purandara** and bestows it on **Bali** (Bhagavata Purana 8.13).
