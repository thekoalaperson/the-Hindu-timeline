# Manvantara 2 — Svārociṣa (Past)

> Hierarchy: this Day of Brahma (Śveta-Vārāha Kalpa) → 2nd of the 14 [Manvantaras](./) → **Svārociṣa Manu** (long past; 5 Manvantaras now separate it from the current 7th, [Vaivasvata](./manvantara-07-vaivasvata/)). A STUB — expandable later.

A manvantara = 71 mahāyugas + 1 sandhyā ≈ 306,720,000 human years. Each is presided over by its own Manu, set of seven sages (Saptarṣi), an Indra, a class of gods, and an avatāra of Viṣṇu. Records for this early manvantara are brief and the source lists disagree.

## Key Figures

- **Svārociṣa Manu** — the 2nd Manu, son of Agni (a.k.a. Svarociṣ). Presides over the manvantara that bears his name (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.1; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.1).
  - *Variant (Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa):* an elaborate origin legend ties Svārociṣa Manu to Svarocis, son of the gandharva-descended hero, born of the celestial maiden Varūthinī — a fuller back-story than the Vaiṣṇava Purāṇas give. [late/elaborated tradition]
- **Sons of the Manu** — headed by **Dyumat, Suṣeṇa and Rociṣmat** (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.1).
- **Indra: Rocana (Vipaścit)** — Rocana held the post of Indra, ruler of svarga, in this age (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.1).
- **Class of gods** — the **Tuṣitas** (a.k.a. Pārāvatas), the prominent deva-gaṇa of this manvantara (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.1; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.1).
- **Avatāra of Viṣṇu: Vibhu** — born to the sage Vedaśiras and his wife Tuṣitā; a lifelong brahmacārin who never married and instructed 88,000 ascetics (dṛḍha-vratas) in self-control and austerity (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.1).

## The Saptarṣi (Seven Sages) — variant lists

- *List A (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.1, Wilson tr.):* **Ūrja, Stambha, Prāṇa, Dattoli (Dattātreya), Ṛṣabha, Niścara, and Arvarīvat** (names vary in transmission, e.g. Cārvarīvat).
- *Variant (popular/HinduPad compilation):* **Dattātreya, Atri, Cyavana, Stambha, Prāṇa, Kaśyapa, Bṛhaspati** — a later, differently composed list. [secondary compilation]

## Notes / Open Items

- The Saptarṣi roster is the chief point of disagreement; the gods (Tuṣitas), Indra (Rocana), and avatāra (Vibhu) are comparatively stable across the Bhāgavata/Viṣṇu accounts.
- The Mārkaṇḍeya origin-myth of Svarocis vs. the bare genealogy of the Vaiṣṇava Purāṇas should be fully cataloged on expansion.
