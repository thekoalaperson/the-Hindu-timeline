## How this section is sorted

The material divides into **four families**, each with its own detail file. The dividing line is function:

- **Weapons** (*āyudha*) — things made to strike. Split into mantra-invoked *astras* and hand-held *śastras*.
- **Relics & treasures** — named objects of power that are *not* primarily weapons (jewels, vessels, trees, vehicles, garments).
- **Vāhanas** — the living mounts; a sub-class of attribute, but large enough for its own roster.
- **Symbols & arts** — the abstract: emblems/marks (*Om*, svastika, tilaka) and the disciplines (the 64 *kalās*, the *vidyās*).

Many objects belong to a deity as a fixed **iconographic attribute** — Viṣṇu's four hands canonically hold **śaṅkha** (conch), **cakra** (discus), **gadā** (mace), **padma** (lotus); Śiva holds **triśūla** + **ḍamaru**; Sarasvatī the **vīṇā**. These attributes cross-link back to [`02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/`](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/). A recurring sub-theme is the **personified attribute**: weapons and emblems depicted as semi-divine beings, the **āyudhapuruṣas** (e.g. Cakra-puruṣa, Gadā-devī Kaumodakī, Sudarśana, Pāñcajanya). (Source: Wikipedia, *List of mythological objects (Hindu mythology)*; *Ayudhapurusha*)

## The files (each holds a one-bullet-per-item exhaustive roster)

### Weapons — the celestial arsenal

- **[`divine-weapons-astras-and-shastras.md`](divyastras-and-divine-weapons.md)** *(planned)* — the full catalogue of **astras** (mantra-invoked super-weapons presided over by a deity) and **śastras** (hand-held arms). Anchors: **Brahmāstra** (Brahmā; once-per-lifetime, scorches the land) and its escalations **Brahmaśira / Brahmāṇḍāstra**; **Pāśupatāstra** (Śiva's mind-released ultimate, held by Arjuna, Indrajit, Viśvāmitra); **Nārāyaṇāstra** (Viṣṇu's auto-multiplying barrage); **Vaiṣṇavāstra**; **Āgneyāstra** (fire, Agni), **Vāruṇāstra** (water, Varuṇa), **Vāyavyāstra** (wind), **Indrāstra/Vajra** (Indra's thunderbolt, forged from sage Dadhīci's bones), **Garuḍāstra**, **Nāgāstra/Nāgapāśa** vs. **Garuḍāstra** counter, **Sūryāstra**, **Mohanāstra** (stupefaction), **Sammohanāstra**, **Parvatāstra**, **Anjalikāstra** (slew Karṇa). Named **named arms**: **Sudarśana Cakra** (Viṣṇu's 108-edged discus, ground from sun-dust by Viśvakarmā), **Triśūla** (Śiva's trident), **Vajra**, **Gadā Kaumodakī** (Viṣṇu's mace), **Śārṅga** (Viṣṇu's bow) & **Gāṇḍīva** (Brahmā-made bow with 108 strings, Varuṇa→Arjuna), **Vijaya** (Karṇa's bow), **Pināka** & **Ājagava** (Śiva's bow; the broken bow of the [Rāmāyaṇa](../04-deep-dives/)), **Paraśu** (Paraśurāma's axe), **Nandaka** (Viṣṇu's sword), **Khaṭvāṅga**. *Variants:* astra-rosters differ sharply by text (Vedic vs. epic vs. Purāṇic); the set is **open-ended** and presiding-deity attributions disagree. (Source: Wikipedia, *Astra (weapon)*; *List of mythological objects*)

### Relics, treasures & vehicles

- **[`divine-objects-and-treasures.md`](divine-objects-and-treasures.md)** *(planned)* — non-weapon objects of power, many surfacing in the **[Samudra Manthana](../04-deep-dives/)** (churning of the milk-ocean). Anchors: **Amṛta** (nectar of immortality) & the **Kumbha** that held it; **Kaustubha** (Viṣṇu's chest-jewel) and **Syamantaka** (the sun-gem of the Kṛṣṇa cycle); **Cintāmaṇi** (wish-granting jewel); **Kāmadhenu/Surabhi** (wish-cow) & calf **Nandinī**; **Kalpavṛkṣa** & **Pārijāta** (wish-trees of Svarga); **Akṣaya-pātra** (Sūrya's inexhaustible food-vessel of the Pāṇḍavas); **Pāñcajanya** (Kṛṣṇa's conch) & **Devadatta** (Arjuna's); **Puṣpaka Vimāna** (Viśvakarmā→Brahmā→Kubera→Rāvaṇa→Rāma's flying chariot); divine armor **Kavaca-Kuṇḍala** (Karṇa's birth-armor & earrings); **Vaijayantī** (Viṣṇu's garland); **Sañjīvanī** (the resurrection-herb of the Rāmāyaṇa). *Open-ended* — every Purāṇa adds local relics. (Source: Wikipedia, *List of mythological objects (Hindu mythology)*; *Samudra Manthana*)
- **[`vahanas-divine-mounts.md`](vahanas-and-celestial-vehicles.md)** *(planned)* — the **vāhanas**, living vehicles that double as a deity's attribute and tamed-force symbol. Roster: **Garuḍa** (eagle-man; Viṣṇu), **Nandi** (bull; Śiva), **Mūṣika** (mouse; Gaṇeśa), **Haṃsa** (goose/swan; Brahmā & Sarasvatī), **Mayūra/Paravāṇi** (peacock; Kārttikeya), **Airāvata** (white elephant; Indra) & **Uccaiḥśravas** (white horse; churning-loot), **Siṃha/Vyāghra** (lion/tiger; Durgā), **Ulūka** (owl; Lakṣmī), **Mahiṣa** (buffalo; Yama), **Makara** (sea-monster; Gaṅgā, Varuṇa), **Mendha/Aja** (ram; Agni), **Gṛdhra/Kāka** (vulture/crow/raven; Śani; also buffalo, iron chariot). *Variant:* several deities have **multiple/regional** vāhanas (Lakṣmī: owl *or* elephants; Śani: many). (Source: Wikipedia, *Vahana*; Dolls of India, *Hindu Vahanas*)

### Symbols & arts

- **[`sacred-symbols-and-emblems.md`](sacred-symbols-and-emblems.md)** *(planned)* — the abstract marks and auspicious emblems. Anchors: **Om/Auṃ/Praṇava** (the primordial sound-symbol of Brahman); **Svastika** (auspiciousness/the sun's turning); **Śrīvatsa** (Viṣṇu's chest-curl) & **Nandyāvarta**; **Tilaka/Bindi** (sectarian forehead-marks — Vaiṣṇava *ūrdhva-puṇḍra*, Śaiva *tripuṇḍra* [tri-ash-lines]) cross-linking [`07-acharyas-and-sampradayas/`](../07-acharyas-and-sampradayas/); **Padma** (lotus, purity); **Śaṅkha** (conch, *Nāda-Brahma*); **Kalaśa/Pūrṇa-kumbha** (the full-pot of abundance); **Śrī Yantra** & the wider **yantra/maṇḍala** geometry; **Rudrākṣa** & **Tulasī** beads; **Dhvaja** (banners — Garuḍa-dhvaja, Vṛṣabha-dhvaja); the **Aṣṭamaṅgala** ("eight auspicious things," whose membership **varies by list**). (Source: Wikipedia, *Category:Hindu symbols*; *Ashtamangala*)
- **[`the-64-kalas-and-vidyas.md`](sixty-four-kalas.md)** *(planned)* — the **Catuḥṣaṣṭi Kalā**, the 64 fine arts a cultured/perfected person masters (Kṛṣṇa learned all 64 + the 14 **vidyās** in 64 days under Sāndīpani), enumerated in Vātsyāyana's *Kāmasūtra*; Śiva is *Kalā*-master and the Devī (Lalitā-sahasranāma) embodies all 64. Full numbered list — *gīta* (song), *vādya* (instruments), *nṛtya* (dance), *citra/ālekhya* (painting), and so on through 64 — plus the **14 vidyās** (4 Vedas + 6 Vedāṅgas + Mīmāṃsā, Nyāya, Purāṇa, Dharmaśāstra). *Variants:* the membership **shifts by century**; Yaśodhara's 13th-c. *Jayamaṅgalā* commentary lists **512** sub-arts; Jain, Buddhist, and South-Indian lists differ. [disputed] (Source: Wikipedia, *Kalā*; Dharmawiki, *64 Kalas*)
