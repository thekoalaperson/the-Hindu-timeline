# Epic Characters — Index

> Where this sits: landing page for **dedicated deep-dive files on individual characters of the two Sanskrit epics** — the Mahābhārata and the Rāmāyaṇa. For the story told book-by-book see the [parva walkthrough](../mahabharata/README.md) and the [kāṇḍa walkthrough](../ramayana/README.md). Up to [Deep Dives](../README.md) · master [index](../../README.md).
> Scope note: a *dedicated* file gets created when a figure carries enough independent biography, variant traditions, and cross-text afterlife to outgrow the parva/kāṇḍa narrative. Most major figures now have their own page in this directory; the handful still pending are flagged **[in-progress]** and link to their best current inline treatment. The roster remains **open-ended**.

## How to use this index
- **name** - one-line role; (primary source). Files that exist link directly; figures pending a dedicated page link to their best current inline treatment.
- The full epics are *itihāsa* (history within this 28th Mahāyuga of the Vaivasvata Manvantara); academic dating differs sharply — see the recension notes in each epic's overview. Two clocks throughout. [scholarly]

## Mahābhārata — the Pāṇḍava household
- **Yudhiṣṭhira** - Dharma's son, the eldest Pāṇḍava; the dice-game, the Rājasūya, the final ascent (*Mahābhārata*). → [Yudhiṣṭhira](./yudhishthira.md)
- **Bhīma** - Vāyu's son, strongest of the five; slayer of Duḥśāsana and the hundred Kauravas (*Mahābhārata*). → [Bhīma](./bhima.md)
- **Arjuna** - Indra's son, the peerless archer; recipient of the [Bhagavad Gītā](../bhagavad-gita.md) (*Mahābhārata*). → [Arjuna](./arjuna.md)
- **Nakula & Sahadeva** - the Aśvin-born twins, sons of Mādrī (*Mahābhārata*). [in-progress] → inline across the [parvas](../mahabharata/README.md).
- **Draupadī (Kṛṣṇā)** - Pāñcāla princess born of fire, common wife of the five Pāṇḍavas; the disrobing and her vow (*Mahābhārata, Sabhā/Vana Parvas*). → [Draupadī](./draupadi.md)
- **Kuntī** - mother of the elder Pāṇḍavas by the mantra of Durvāsā; keeper of Karṇa's secret (*Mahābhārata*). → [Kuntī](./kunti.md)
- **Mādrī** - Madra princess, Pāṇḍu's second wife, mother of the twins (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → [Mādrī](./madri.md)
- **Subhadrā** - Kṛṣṇa's sister, Arjuna's wife, mother of Abhimanyu (*Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata*). → [Subhadrā](./subhadra.md)
- **Abhimanyu** - Arjuna's son, breaker of the Cakravyūha, slain within it (*Mahābhārata, Droṇa Parva*). → [Abhimanyu](./abhimanyu.md)
- **Ghaṭotkaca** - Bhīma's rākṣasa son by Hiḍimbī, the night-battle giant who absorbs Karṇa's Śakti (*Mahābhārata*). → [Ghaṭotkaca](./ghatotkacha.md)
- **Hiḍimba & Hiḍimbī** - the forest rākṣasa slain by Bhīma and his sister who weds him (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → [Hiḍimba & Hiḍimbī](./hidimba-hidimbi.md)
- **Ulūpī** - the Nāga princess, Arjuna's serpent-realm wife, engineer of his death-and-revival (*Mahābhārata*). → [Ulūpī](./ulupi.md)
- **Citrāṅgadā of Maṇipura** - warrior-princess, Arjuna's wife by the matrilineal pact (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → [Citrāṅgadā](./chitrangada.md)
- **Babhruvāhana** - Arjuna and Citrāṅgadā's son, who slays and revives his father at the Aśvamedha (*Mahābhārata, Āśvamedhika Parva*). → [Babhruvāhana](./babhruvahana.md)
- **Arāvāṇ / Irāvān** - Arjuna and Ulūpī's son, the one-night bridegroom of the Kūttāṇṭavar cult (*Mahābhārata*; Tamil tradition). → [Arāvāṇ / Irāvān](./aravan-iravan.md)
- **Barbarīka / Khāṭū Śyām** - Ghaṭotkaca's son, the three-arrow archer who witnesses the whole war (*Skanda Purāṇa*; folk). → [Barbarīka](./barbarika-khatu-shyam.md)
- **Pradyumna** - Kṛṣṇa and Rukmiṇī's son, Kāmadeva reborn, slayer of Śambara (*Bhāgavata; Harivaṃśa*). → [Pradyumna](./pradyumna.md)
- **Kṛṣṇa** - Yādava prince, charioteer-counsellor, the eighth Viṣṇu avatāra; his life has its own pages (*Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata Purāṇa*). → see [Kṛṣṇa-līlā](../krishna-lila.md) and the [Bhagavad Gītā](../bhagavad-gita.md).
- **Balarāma** - Kṛṣṇa's plough-bearing elder brother, neutral in the war (*Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata*). [in-progress] → inline in [Kṛṣṇa-līlā](../krishna-lila.md) and the [Harivaṃśa](../harivamsha.md).

## Mahābhārata — the Kuru court & elders
- **Bhīṣma** - the celibate grand-uncle bound by his terrible vow, fallen on the bed of arrows, instructor of the [Śānti](../mahabharata/12-shanti-parva.md)/[Anuśāsana](../mahabharata/13-anushasana-parva.md) Parvas (*Mahābhārata*). → [Bhīṣma](./bhishma.md)
- **Satyavatī** - the fisher-princess matriarch, mother of Vyāsa and the Kuru line's crisis (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → [Satyavatī](./satyavati.md)
- **Vicitravīrya & Citrāṅgada** - Śantanu and Satyavatī's short-lived sons, whose deaths force the niyoga (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → [Vicitravīrya & Citrāṅgada](./vichitravirya-and-chitrangada.md)
- **Ambikā & Ambālikā** - the Kāśi princesses, mothers by niyoga of Dhṛtarāṣṭra and Pāṇḍu (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → [Ambikā & Ambālikā](./ambika-ambalika.md)
- **Vidura** - the dharma-born half-brother, wisest counsellor; speaker of the [Vidura-nīti](../vidura-niti.md) (*Mahābhārata*). → [Vidura](./vidura.md)
- **Sañjaya** - Dhṛtarāṣṭra's charioteer-narrator, granted divine sight for the war (*Mahābhārata, Bhīṣma Parva*). → [Sañjaya](./sanjaya.md)
- **Droṇa** - brahmin weapons-master of both camps, the Cakravyūha, his death by deceit (*Mahābhārata, Droṇa Parva*). → [Droṇa](./drona.md)
- **Kṛpa** - the Kuru preceptor born of a reed-clump, one of the [Cirañjīvis](../the-chiranjivis.md) (*Mahābhārata*). → [Kṛpa](./kripa.md)
- **Gāndhārī** - the blindfolded queen, mother of the hundred; her curse on Kṛṣṇa (*Mahābhārata*). → [Gāndhārī](./gandhari.md)
- **Dhṛtarāṣṭra** - the blind king whose weakness for his sons drives the war (*Mahābhārata*). [in-progress] → inline across the [parvas](../mahabharata/README.md).
- **Pāṇḍu** - the pale king, cursed by Kindama, father of the five (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). [in-progress] → inline in the [Ādi Parva](../mahabharata/01-adi-parva.md).
- **Vyāsa** - the island-born compiler-grandfather of the whole line and its epic (*Mahābhārata*). → [Vyāsa](../vyasa.md)

## Mahābhārata — the Kaurava side & allies
- **Duryodhana** - eldest Kaurava, the war's prime mover; a Kali-aṃśa incarnation in some readings (*Mahābhārata*). → [Duryodhana](./duryodhana.md)
- **Duḥśāsana** - the disrober of Draupadī, his blood drunk by Bhīma (*Mahābhārata*). → [Duḥśāsana](./dushasana.md)
- **Śakuni** - the Gāndhāra dice-master, architect of the game (*Mahābhārata, Sabhā Parva*). → [Śakuni](./shakuni.md)
- **Karṇa** - the Sun-born eldest Kaunteya raised as a charioteer's son; tragic loyalty to Duryodhana, the kavaca-kuṇḍala, the curses (*Mahābhārata*). → [Karṇa](../karna.md)
- **Jayadratha** - the Sindhu king, Draupadī's abductor, gate-keeper of the Cakravyūha (*Mahābhārata*). → [Jayadratha](./jayadratha.md)
- **Śalya** - the Madra king tricked into the Kaurava camp, Karṇa's demoralizing charioteer (*Mahābhārata, Śalya Parva*). → [Śalya](./shalya.md)
- **Kṛtavarmā** - the Yādava who fought for Duryodhana and joined the night-raid (*Mahābhārata, Sauptika Parva*). → [Kṛtavarmā](./kritavarma.md)
- **Vikarṇa** - the one righteous Kaurava, who protested the disrobing (*Mahābhārata, Sabhā Parva*). → [Vikarṇa](./vikarna.md)
- **Yuyutsu** - Dhṛtarāṣṭra's son by a vaiśya woman, who crossed to the Pāṇḍava side (*Mahābhārata*). → [Yuyutsu](./yuyutsu.md)
- **Aśvatthāman** - Droṇa's son, the night-raid of the [Sauptika Parva](../mahabharata/10-sauptika-parva.md) and the curse of deathless wandering; one of the [Cirañjīvis](../the-chiranjivis.md) (*Mahābhārata*). [in-progress]

## Mahābhārata — Pāñcāla, Matsya & the wider circle
- **Drupada** - the Pāñcāla king, Droṇa's estranged friend-turned-enemy, father by fire-sacrifice (*Mahābhārata*). → [Drupada](./drupada.md)
- **Dhṛṣṭadyumna** - fire-born commander of the Pāṇḍava host, slayer of Droṇa (*Mahābhārata*). → [Dhṛṣṭadyumna](./dhrishtadyumna.md)
- **Ambā → Śikhaṇḍin** - the rejected princess reborn for vengeance on Bhīṣma (*Mahābhārata, Udyoga Parva*). → [Ambā → Śikhaṇḍin](./amba-shikhandi.md)
- **Virāṭa and his children** - the Matsya court of the incognito year — Uttara, Uttarā, and the cattle-raid (*Mahābhārata, Virāṭa Parva*). → [Virāṭa & his children](./virata-and-his-children.md)
- **Ekalavya** - the Niṣāda prince who learned archery before Droṇa's clay image and surrendered his right thumb as guru-dakṣiṇā (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → [Ekalavya](../ekalavya.md)
- **Śiśupāla** - the Cedi king of the hundred offenses, beheaded by Kṛṣṇa's Sudarśana at Yudhiṣṭhira's Rājasūya; a Jaya–Vijaya rebirth (*Mahābhārata, Sabhā Parva*). → [Śiśupāla](../shishupala.md)

## Rāmāyaṇa — Ayodhyā
- **Rāma** - prince of Ayodhyā, seventh Viṣṇu avatāra, the embodiment of dharma whose exile drives the epic (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). [in-progress] → inline across the [kāṇḍas](../ramayana/README.md); devotional reframings in the [recensions](../ramayana/recensions/adhyatma-ramayana.md).
- **Sītā** - daughter of the earth / King Janaka, Rāma's wife, abducted by Rāvaṇa; the agni-parīkṣā and the return to the earth (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Sītā](./sita.md)
- **Lakṣmaṇa** - Rāma's devoted half-brother and exile-companion, slayer of Indrajit (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Lakṣmaṇa](./lakshmana.md)
- **Bharata & Śatrughna (and their wives Māṇḍavī & Śrutakīrti)** - the sandal-regent of Nandigrāma and the slayer of Lavaṇa (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Bharata & Śatrughna](./bharata-shatrughna-and-their-wives.md)
- **Daśaratha** - the king of Ayodhyā, bound by Kaikeyī's boons, dead of grief (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Ayodhyā Kāṇḍa*). → [Daśaratha](./dasharatha.md)
- **Kausalyā** - the senior queen, Rāma's mother (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Kausalyā](./kausalya.md)
- **Kaikeyī** - the warrior-queen whose two boons exile Rāma (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Ayodhyā Kāṇḍa*). → [Kaikeyī](./kaikeyi.md)
- **Sumitrā** - the wise second queen, mother of Lakṣmaṇa and Śatrughna (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Sumitrā](./sumitra.md)
- **Mantharā** - Kaikeyī's hunchbacked nurse, poisoner of the succession (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Ayodhyā Kāṇḍa*). → [Mantharā](./manthara.md)
- **Sumantra** - Daśaratha's charioteer-minister, the exile's witness (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Sumantra](./sumantra.md)
- **Guha** - the Niṣāda king of Śṛṅgaverapura, Rāma's ferryman-friend (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Ayodhyā Kāṇḍa*). → [Guha](./guha.md)

## Rāmāyaṇa — the forest, Kiṣkindhā & Laṅkā
- **Ahalyā** - Gautama's wife, cursed and redeemed by Rāma's coming; a Pañcakanyā (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa*). → [Ahalyā](./ahalya.md)
- **Tāṭakā** - the yakṣī-turned-demoness, Rāma's first slaying (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa*). → [Tāṭakā](./tataka.md)
- **Subāhu** - Mārīca's yajña-wrecking companion, slain at Viśvāmitra's sacrifice (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Bāla Kāṇḍa*). → [Subāhu](./subahu.md)
- **Virādha** - the cursed gandharva-rākṣasa of Daṇḍaka, freed by burial (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Araṇya Kāṇḍa*). → [Virādha](./viradha.md)
- **Kabandha** - the armless barrel-demon who directs Rāma to Sugrīva (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Araṇya Kāṇḍa*). → [Kabandha](./kabandha.md)
- **Śabarī** - the ascetic devotee of the tasted berries (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Araṇya Kāṇḍa*). → [Śabarī](./shabari.md)
- **Jaṭāyu & Sampāti** - the vulture-brothers — the dying witness and the far-seeing guide (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Jaṭāyu & Sampāti](./jatayu-and-sampati.md)
- **Sugrīva & Vālī** - the feuding vānara brothers of Kiṣkindhā (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Kiṣkindhā Kāṇḍa*). → [Sugrīva & Vālī](./sugriva-and-vali.md)
- **Hanumān** - the divine vānara minister, son of Vāyu, who leaps to Laṅkā in the [Sundara Kāṇḍa](../ramayana/5-sundara-kanda.md); one of the [Cirañjīvis](../the-chiranjivis.md) (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → deity deep-dive: [Hanumān](../../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/hanuman.md)
- **Rāvaṇa** - ten-headed rākṣasa king of Laṅkā, the prime antagonist, a Jaya–Vijaya rebirth; abductor of Sītā (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Rāvaṇa](./ravana.md)
- **Kumbhakarṇa** - the sleeping colossus, Rāvaṇa's brother (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Yuddha Kāṇḍa*). → [Kumbhakarṇa](./kumbhakarna.md)
- **Indrajit / Meghanāda** - Rāvaṇa's invincible son, conqueror of Indra (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Yuddha Kāṇḍa*). → [Indrajit](./indrajit-meghnad.md)
- **Vibhīṣaṇa** - the righteous rākṣasa who crossed to Rāma; a [Cirañjīvi](../the-chiranjivis.md) (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). → [Vibhīṣaṇa](./vibhishana.md)
- **Mārīca** - the golden-deer decoy, Tāṭakā's son (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Araṇya Kāṇḍa*). → [Mārīca](./maricha.md)
- **Trijaṭā** - the kind rākṣasī who comforts Sītā in the Aśoka grove (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Sundara Kāṇḍa*). → [Trijaṭā](./trijata.md)
- **Mandodarī & Śūrpaṇakhā** - Rāvaṇa's queen (a Pañcakanyā) and the sister whose mutilation sparks the abduction (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). [in-progress] → inline in the [Araṇya](../ramayana/3-aranya-kanda.md) and [Yuddha](../ramayana/6-yuddha-kanda.md) Kāṇḍas.
