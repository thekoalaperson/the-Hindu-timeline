# Epic Characters — Index

> Where this sits: landing page for **dedicated deep-dive files on individual characters of the two Sanskrit epics** — the Mahābhārata and the Rāmāyaṇa. For the story told book-by-book see the [parva walkthrough](../mahabharata/README.md) and the [kāṇḍa walkthrough](../ramayana/README.md). Up to [Deep Dives](../README.md) · master [index](../../README.md).
> Scope note: a *dedicated* file gets created when a figure carries enough independent biography, variant traditions, and cross-text afterlife to outgrow the parva/kāṇḍa narrative. Many figures below are still covered inline in the parva/kāṇḍa files and in the catalogs; this index marks which have their own page and flags the roster as **open-ended** [in-progress].

## How to use this index
- **name** - one-line role; (primary source). Files that exist link directly; figures pending a dedicated page link to their best current inline treatment.
- The full epics are *itihāsa* (history within this 28th Mahāyuga of the Vaivasvata Manvantara); academic dating differs sharply — see the recension notes in each epic's overview. Two clocks throughout. [scholarly]

## Mahābhārata — characters
- **Karṇa** - the Sun-born eldest Kaunteya raised as a charioteer's son; tragic loyalty to Duryodhana, the kavaca-kuṇḍala, the curses, death in the Karṇa Parva (*Mahābhārata*). → dedicated file: [Karṇa](../karna.md)
- **Ekalavya** - the Niṣāda prince who learned archery before Droṇa's clay image and surrendered his right thumb as guru-dakṣiṇā (*Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva*). → dedicated file: [Ekalavya](../ekalavya.md)
- **Śiśupāla** - the Cedi king of the hundred offenses, beheaded by Kṛṣṇa's Sudarśana at Yudhiṣṭhira's Rājasūya; a Jaya–Vijaya rebirth (*Mahābhārata, Sabhā Parva*). → dedicated file: [Śiśupāla](../shishupala.md)
- **The Pāṇḍavas (Yudhiṣṭhira, Bhīma, Arjuna, Nakula, Sahadeva)** - the five sons of Pāṇḍu by divine fathers (Dharma, Vāyu, Indra, the Aśvins) (*Mahābhārata*). [in-progress] → currently inline across the [parvas](../mahabharata/README.md); Arjuna's teaching is the [Bhagavad Gītā](../bhagavad-gita.md).
- **Draupadī (Kṛṣṇā)** - Pāñcāla princess born of fire, common wife of the five Pāṇḍavas, the disrobing and her vow (*Mahābhārata, Sabhā/Vana Parvas*). [in-progress] → inline in [Sabhā Parva](../mahabharata/02-sabha-parva.md).
- **Kṛṣṇa** - Yādava prince, charioteer-counsellor, the eighth Viṣṇu avatāra; his life beyond the war has its own page (*Mahābhārata; Bhāgavata Purāṇa*). → see [Kṛṣṇa-līlā](../krishna-lila.md) and the [Bhagavad Gītā](../bhagavad-gita.md). [in-progress] as an epic actor.
- **Bhīṣma** - the celibate grand-uncle bound by his terrible vow, fallen on the bed of arrows, instructor of the [Śānti](../mahabharata/12-shanti-parva.md)/[Anuśāsana](../mahabharata/13-anushasana-parva.md) Parvas (*Mahābhārata*). [in-progress] → inline; see also [Bhīṣma Parva](../mahabharata/06-bhishma-parva.md).
- **Droṇa** - brahmin weapons-master of both camps, the Cakravyūha, his death by deceit (*Mahābhārata, Droṇa Parva*). [in-progress] → inline in [Droṇa Parva](../mahabharata/07-drona-parva.md).
- **Duryodhana** - eldest Kaurava, the war's prime mover; a Kali-aṃśa incarnation in some readings (*Mahābhārata*). [in-progress] → inline across the parvas.
- **Aśvatthāman** - Droṇa's son, the night-raid of the [Sauptika Parva](../mahabharata/10-sauptika-parva.md) and the curse of deathless wandering; one of the [Cirañjīvis](../the-chiranjivis.md) (*Mahābhārata*). [in-progress]
- **Other major figures** - Kuntī, Gāndhārī, Dhṛtarāṣṭra, Vidura, Śakuni, Abhimanyu, Ghaṭotkaca, Vyāsa, Balarāma. [in-progress] → catalogued inline in the [parvas](../mahabharata/README.md) and the [minor-episodes catalog](../mahabharata-minor-episodes.md).

## Rāmāyaṇa — characters
- **Rāma** - prince of Ayodhyā, seventh Viṣṇu avatāra, the embodiment of dharma whose exile drives the epic (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). [in-progress] → inline across the [kāṇḍas](../ramayana/README.md); devotional reframings in the [recensions](../ramayana/recensions/adhyatma-ramayana.md).
- **Sītā** - daughter of the earth / King Janaka, Rāma's wife, abducted by Rāvaṇa; the agni-parīkṣā and the return to the earth (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). [in-progress] → inline; variant of the "shadow Sītā" in the [Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa](../ramayana/recensions/adhyatma-ramayana.md).
- **Lakṣmaṇa** - Rāma's devoted half-brother and exile-companion, slayer of Indrajit (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). [in-progress] → inline in the [kāṇḍas](../ramayana/README.md).
- **Hanumān** - the divine vānara minister, son of Vāyu, who leaps to Laṅkā in the [Sundara Kāṇḍa](../ramayana/5-sundara-kanda.md); one of the [Cirañjīvis](../the-chiranjivis.md) (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). [in-progress]
- **Rāvaṇa** - ten-headed rākṣasa king of Laṅkā, the prime antagonist, a Jaya–Vijaya rebirth; abductor of Sītā (*Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa*). [in-progress] → inline in the [Yuddha Kāṇḍa](../ramayana/6-yuddha-kanda.md).
- **Bharata, Daśaratha, Kaikeyī, Sugrīva, Vālī, Vibhīṣaṇa, Jaṭāyu** - the supporting cast of Ayodhyā, Kiṣkindhā, and Laṅkā. [in-progress] → catalogued inline across the [kāṇḍas](../ramayana/README.md).
