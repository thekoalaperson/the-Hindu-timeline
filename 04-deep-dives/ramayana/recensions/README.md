> Landing page for the **dedicated recension/retelling files** — the regional, sectarian, and cross-religious reworkings of the Rāma story. The base **Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa** (7 kāṇḍas) and its critical-edition recensions are covered one level up at [../README.md](../README.md). By tradition there are **"three hundred Rāmāyaṇas"** (A. K. Ramanujan) — too many to file individually; this index links the major dedicated deep-dives and flags the open-ended roster of further regional versions [open-ended roster].

## Dedicated retelling files

- **[Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa](adhyatma-ramayana.md)** - Sanskrit; embedded in the *Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa*, ascribed to Vyāsa; ~4,200 verses, 7 kāṇḍas. Recasts the epic as **Advaita-Vedānta allegory** — Rāma is supreme Brahman; the abducted Sītā is only a *māyā-Sītā* (shadow), the real Sītā hidden with Agni. ~14th–15th c.; primary source for Tulsīdās. [late text]
- **[Kamban — Irāmāvatāram](kamban-iramavataram.md)** - **Tamil**, by **Kamban**, c. 12th c.; ~10,000+ verses, 6 kāṇḍas (omits Uttara). Literary masterwork; heightened bhakti-divinity of Rāma and a more sympathetic Rāvaṇa. [scholarly]
- **[Rāmacaritamānasa (Tulsīdās)](ramcharitmanas-tulsidas.md)** - **Awadhi**, by **Tulsīdās**, 1574 CE; 7 kāṇḍas in *caupāī–dohā*. The single most influential North-Indian *bhakti* retelling; basis of the *Rāmlīlā* pageants; draws on the Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa.
- **[Kṛttivāsī Rāmāyaṇ](krittivasi-ramayan.md)** - **Bengali**, by **Kṛttibās Ojhā**, 15th c. (*Śrīrām Pāñcālī*); most beloved Bengali version; folk + bhakti coloring; introduces Rāma's autumn *akāl-bodhan* worship of Durgā. [folk tradition]
- **[Jain & Buddhist Rāmāyaṇas](jain-and-buddhist-ramayanas.md)** - Cross-religious reworkings: Jain *Paümacariya* (Vimalasūri) and *Padmapurāṇa* (Raviṣeṇa) that **demythologize** the rākṣasas and make Lakṣmaṇa (not Rāma) slay Rāvaṇa; Buddhist **Dasaratha Jātaka** with its truncated, karma-framed plot. [sectarian variant]
- **[Southeast Asian Rāmāyaṇas](southeast-asian-ramayanas.md)** - The Asian diaspora of the story: Thai **Ramakien**, Khmer **Reamker**, Lao **Phra Lak Phra Lam**, Indonesian/Malay **Kakawin Rāmāyaṇa** & **Hikayat Seri Rama**, Burmese **Yama Zatdaw**, Philippine **Maharadia Lawana**. [folk tradition]

## Further regional versions [open-ended roster]

Not yet given dedicated files — flagged for completeness; attributions per *Versions of the Ramayana* survey:

- **Raṅganātha Rāmāyaṇamu** - **Telugu**, Gona Buddhā Reddy, c. 1300–1310 CE; first complete Telugu version, in *dvipada* (~17,290 couplets).
- **Molla Rāmāyaṇamu** - **Telugu**, by the woman poet **Ātukūri Molla**, 16th c.; a rare Rāmakathā authored by a woman.
- **Bhāskara Rāmāyaṇam** & **Rāmāyaṇa Kalpavṛkṣamu** (Viśvanātha Satyanārāyaṇa, 20th c.) - other complete Telugu renderings.
- **Saptakāṇḍa Rāmāyaṇa (Kathā Rāmāyaṇa)** - **Assamese**, Mādhava Kandalī, 14th c.
- **Torave Rāmāyaṇa** (Kumāravālmīki, 16th c.) & **Kumudendu Rāmāyaṇa** (Jain, 13th c.) - **Kannada**.
- **Bhāvārtha Rāmāyaṇa** (Eknāth) - **Marathi**.
- **Giridhar Rāmāyaṇa** - **Gujarati**.

> These are listed for completeness only; promote to dedicated files as the corpus expands.
