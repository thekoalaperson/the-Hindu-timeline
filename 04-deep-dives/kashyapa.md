# Kaśyapa

> *Where this sits: a deep-dive into the genealogical apex of the present creation — Kaśyapa Prajāpati, son of Marīci, grandson of Brahmā, and through Dakṣa's thirteen daughters the common father of devas, daityas, dānavas, nāgas, birds and beasts; the hinge on which the entire bestiary and the deva–asura feud turn. ⬆ Parent index: [04-deep-dives](README.md) · Master index: [README](../README.md)*

**Kaśyapa** (*Kaśyapa*, "tortoise"; also glossed as one who "drinks/sees" — *paśyaka* metathesized) is the single most prolific progenitor (*Prajāpati*) of the present world-age. He is a **mānasaputra-grandson** of Brahmā (son of the mind-born seer **Marīci**), one of the **Saptarṣi** of the current [Vaivasvata Manvantara](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/README.md), and the husband of **thirteen of Dakṣa's daughters**. From those marriages descend the **Ādityas (devas)**, the **Daityas** and **Dānavas** (asuras), the **Nāgas** (serpents), **Garuḍa** and **Aruṇa** (birds), and virtually every other class of living being — making this node the genealogical root of nearly the whole [bestiary](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md) and of the cosmic deva–asura conflict. He is the **default gotra** for those without one (*kāśyapa-gotra*) and, in Kashmiri tradition, the **eponym of Kashmir** itself.

---

## Origin and place in the creation

- **Son of Marīci, grandson of Brahmā** — Kaśyapa is born to **Marīci**, the eldest of Brahmā's mind-born sons (mānasaputras), making him a second-generation Prajāpati of the present *kalpa*. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.10; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.6) — see [The Prajāpatis & Brahmā's Mind-Born Sons](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md).
  - *Mother (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.10):* **Kalā**, daughter of the Prajāpati Kardama, bears Kaśyapa to Marīci.
  - *Variant [disputed]:* Some genealogies make Kaśyapa himself a direct Prajāpati / mind-born son rather than Marīci's son, collapsing the two generations; the rosters of Prajāpatis are notoriously unfixed (7 vs 10 vs 21).
- **Saptarṣi of the present Manvantara** — Kaśyapa is counted among the **Seven Sages** presiding over the current [Vaivasvata Manvantara](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/README.md), alongside Atri, Vasiṣṭha, Gautama, Bharadvāja, Viśvāmitra, and Jamadagni. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.1) — see [Saptarṣi](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md).
- **The thirteen-bride marriage** — **Dakṣa** gives **thirteen** of his daughters to Kaśyapa; their wombs become the matrix of every species. The dominant list names **Aditi, Diti, Danu, Ariṣṭā, Surasā, Surabhi, Vinatā, Tāmrā, Krodhavaśā, Irā (Ilā), Kadru, Khasā,** and **Muni**. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15; Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva 65) — see [Devas, Asuras & the Lokas](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).
  - *Variant (count):* The number of Dakṣa's daughters wed to Kaśyapa ranges from **8 to 17** across Purāṇas; thirteen is the most common figure, but the Bhāgavata and Mahābhārata rosters do not perfectly agree. [disputed]
  - *Variant (sixty daughters):* In the wider Dakṣa-genealogy, Dakṣa fathers **sixty daughters** distributed among Kaśyapa, Dharma, Soma, and other sages; the "thirteen to Kaśyapa" set is a subset. (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva 66)

## The progeny — the genealogical hinge of the bestiary

- **Aditi → the Ādityas (devas)** — From **Aditi** ("the boundless") are born the **twelve Ādityas**, the principal devas, including **Indra**, **Vivasvat/Sūrya** (father of Vaivasvata Manu and so of the present human age), and, in the dwarf-incarnation, **Vāmana/Viṣṇu** as Aditi's son. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15; Bhāgavata 6.6.38–39) — the *deva* pole of the [deva–asura feud](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).
- **Diti → the Daityas** — From **Diti** are born the **Daityas**, foremost among them **Hiraṇyakaśipu** and **Hiraṇyākṣa** (slain by Narasiṃha and Varāha), and later the Maruts after Indra splits Diti's embryo. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15, 1.21; Bhāgavata 6.6) — the senior *asura* line.
- **Danu → the Dānavas** — From **Danu** descend the **Dānavas**, including **Vṛtra**, the **Vipracitti** line, and the titans of the churning of the ocean. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21; Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva) — see [Samudra Manthana](samudra-manthana.md).
- **Kadru → the Nāgas** — From **Kadru** are born the **thousand serpents (Nāgas)** — **Śeṣa/Ananta, Vāsuki, Takṣaka, Karkoṭaka** and the rest — the serpent-race of the netherworld. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21; Bhāgavata 6.6.28–29) — see [Nāgas & Serpents](../08-beings-and-bestiary/nagas-and-serpents.md).
- **Vinatā → Garuḍa and Aruṇa** — From **Vinatā** are born **Aruṇa** (the dawn-charioteer of Sūrya) and **Garuḍa** (the eagle-mount of Viṣṇu and devourer of serpents). The **Kadru–Vinatā rivalry** — Kadru winning their wager by deceit and enslaving Vinatā — is the origin of the **eternal enmity of serpents and birds** and the seed of the [Sarpa-satra](astika-sarpa-satra.md). (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva 16–34) — see [Nāgas & Serpents](../08-beings-and-bestiary/nagas-and-serpents.md).
- **Tāmrā → birds of prey** — From **Tāmrā** descend the **birds** (Śyenī the hawks, Bhāsī, Gṛdhrikā the vultures, Śuci, Grīvā), giving the avian world its Kaśyapa-root. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21)
- **Surabhi → cattle and buffalo** — From **Surabhi** ("the fragrant cow") descend **cows and buffaloes**, and the eleven **Rudras** in some lists. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21)
  - *Variant:* The Rudras are more usually Brahmā's emission; their attribution to Surabhi-via-Kaśyapa is a Purāṇic minority. [disputed]
- **Surasā / Ariṣṭā → serpents and Gandharvas** — Doublet mother-lines: **Surasā** (mother of further serpents, and the rākṣasī who tests Hanumān in the *Rāmāyaṇa*) and **Ariṣṭā** (mother of the **Gandharvas**). (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21; Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva)
- **Krodhavaśā, Irā, Khasā, Muni** — From these descend, respectively, the **fierce fanged beasts / piśācas**, the **trees, creepers and grasses (Irā/Ilā)**, the **Yakṣas and Rākṣasas (Khasā)**, and the **Apsarases (Muni)**. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21)
- **The summary point** — Because devas and asuras are **half-brothers** through Kaśyapa (Aditi's and Diti's sons), the entire **deva–asura conflict is a family feud**; Kaśyapa is the literal common father whose marriages set the cosmic dialectic in motion. (Bhāgavata 6.6; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.15) — see [Devas, Asuras & the Lokas](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/devas-asuras-and-the-lokas.md).

## The default gotra and brahmanical role

- **Kāśyapa-gotra as universal fallback** — In gotra-tradition, anyone **without a known gotra** — converts, foundlings, those of lost lineage — is assigned the **Kāśyapa gotra**, on the logic that Kaśyapa is the progenitor of all beings, so all ultimately descend from him. (gotra tradition; Dharmaśāstra usage) [scholarly]
- **One of the foundational ṛṣi-lineages** — The Kāśyapas are among the principal **gotra-pravara** lines of brahmanical descent, his name invoked in the *pravara* recitation. (gotra-pravara tradition)
- **Vedic seer** — Hymns of the **Ṛgveda** are ascribed to Kaśyapa Mārīca, situating him in the seer-stratum as well as the progenitor-stratum. (Ṛgveda; Anukramaṇī)

## Kaśyapa and Kashmir (Nīlamata Purāṇa)

- **Eponym of Kashmir** — In Kashmiri tradition the valley is **Kaśyapa-mīra / Kaśmīra**, "the lake (or land) of Kaśyapa," named for the sage who reclaimed it. (Nīlamata Purāṇa) [regional]
- **Draining the lake Satīsaras** — The valley was once the vast lake **Satīsaras**, ruled by the water-demon **Jalodbhava**, who could not be killed while in water. Kaśyapa undertakes severe austerity and, with the help of **Viṣṇu, Śiva and the gods**, has the mountain at **Varāhamūla (Baramulla)** breached so the waters drain; **Viṣṇu** then slays Jalodbhava on the now-dry land, and Kaśyapa settles the valley with the **Nāgas** and brahmins. (Nīlamata Purāṇa) [regional]
  - *Variant (Rājataraṅgiṇī):* Kalhaṇa's chronicle opens by accepting the Nīlamata's account of Kaśyapa draining the lake and founding the land before its dynastic history begins. (Rājataraṅgiṇī 1.25–28) [regional]
- **Settlement of the Nāgas** — In the Nīlamata frame Kashmir is given over to the **Nāga king Nīla** (whose teaching the *Nīlamata* purports to record) and the serpent-people — re-tying Kaśyapa's serpent-progeny (via Kadru) to the geography of the valley. (Nīlamata Purāṇa) [regional]

## Other episodes

- **Father of Vivasvat → grandfather of Vaivasvata Manu** — Through Aditi's son **Vivasvat (Sūrya)**, Kaśyapa is the grandfather of **Vaivasvata Manu**, progenitor of present humanity and eponym of the current Manvantara — placing Kaśyapa at the genealogical head of the human age. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.2; Bhāgavata 8.13)
- **Father of Vāmana** — When the gods are dispossessed by **Bali**, Aditi performs the **Payovrata** on Kaśyapa's instruction and Viṣṇu is born to the couple as the dwarf **Vāmana**, reclaiming the three worlds. (Bhāgavata 8.16–18) — see [Vāmana & Bali](vamana-bali.md).
- **Father of Garuḍa's quest** — Garuḍa, Kaśyapa's son by Vinatā, wins the **amṛta** to free his enslaved mother and becomes Viṣṇu's mount; Kaśyapa's household thus stands behind a major churning-adjacent cycle. (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva 20–34)
- **Saṃvatsara / Prajāpati identity** — Some texts gloss **Kaśyapa as Prajāpati and even as the "year" (saṃvatsara)** or as a form of the creator, dissolving him into the cosmic-time and creator principle. (Brāhmaṇa-stratum glosses) [scholarly]
