# The Mind-Born Seers (Marīci, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu)

> *Where this sits: a deep-dive into four of Brahmā's **mānasaputra** Prajāpati-ṛṣis at the genealogical floor of the present creation — Marīci the apex-father of Kaśyapa (and so of devas, asuras and nearly all beings), Pulastya the root of the Rākṣasa line and a transmitter of Purāṇa-lore, and the quieter Pulaha and Kratu. ⬆ Parent index: [04-deep-dives](README.md) · Master index: [README](../README.md)*

The four ṛṣis treated here are **mind-born sons (mānasaputras)** emitted by **Brahmā** at the dawn of the present *kalpa*, simultaneously titled **Prajāpati** ("lord of progeny"), **Brahmarṣi**, and the original **[Saptarṣi](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md)**. They are grouped here as a **genealogical-foundation** node: between them they seed the entire Vaivasvata bestiary (via Marīci → Kaśyapa), the Rākṣasa–Yakṣa dynasties (via Pulastya), and several minor sage-and-creature lines. For the full Prajāpati roster, the 7/10/21 count-variants, and the birth-organ catalogue, see [The Prajāpatis & Brahmā's Mind-Born Sons](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md).

---

## The shared origin — Brahmā's mental creation

- **Mind-born, not begotten** — Charged to populate the worlds, **Brahmā** first emits these seers **from his mind/body without union (mānasika sṛṣṭi)**; they are the first conscious, *sāttvic* beings of the *kalpa*. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12)
- **All four are Saptarṣi of the first manvantara** — Marīci, Pulastya, Pulaha and Kratu are mapped (with Atri, Aṅgiras, Vasiṣṭha) onto the **seven stars of the Great Bear** as the original Seven Sages. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7, 3.1) — see [Saptarṣi](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/saptarishi.md).
  - *Variant (birth-organs):* Purāṇas assign each a faculty of Brahmā — e.g. **Marīci from the mind/eyes, Pulastya from the ear, Pulaha from the navel, Kratu from the hand** — but the assignments differ by text. [disputed] (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12; Matsya Purāṇa)
- **Two clocks** — These events sit on the **scriptural clock** (Brahmā's first day, billions of years before the historical layer); nothing here is datable on the historical clock — the seers function as genealogical *origins*, not as figures of recorded chronology. [scholarly]

## Marīci — the genealogical apex

- **The eldest mind-son** — **Marīci** (मरीचि, "ray of light") is usually named **first** of the mānasaputras, the senior Prajāpati of the present creation. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12)
- **Father of Kaśyapa** — By **Kalā**, a daughter of the Prajāpati Kardama and Devahūti, Marīci begets **Kaśyapa** — making Marīci the **grandfather of the entire Vaivasvata creation**: through Kaśyapa's thirteen brides descend the Ādityas (devas), Daityas and Dānavas (asuras), Nāgas, birds and beasts. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.10; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1) — see [Kaśyapa](kashyapa.md).
  - *Variant [disputed]:* Some genealogies collapse the two generations and make **Kaśyapa himself** a direct mind-born Prajāpati, dropping Marīci as the intervening father.
- **Devas and asuras as his great-grandsons** — Because Kaśyapa is Marīci's son, the **deva–asura feud is a family quarrel two generations below Marīci**; he is the literal patriarch standing behind the cosmic dialectic. (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 6.6)
- **Vedic seer** — Hymns are ascribed to **Kaśyapa Mārīca** (the patronymic preserving Marīci's name), situating the line in the Ṛgvedic seer-stratum. (Ṛgveda; Anukramaṇī) [scholarly]
- **The Mārīca gotra** — Marīci founds one of the foundational brahmana *gotra*-lines, invoked in *pravara* recitation. (gotra-pravara tradition)

## Pulastya — root of the Rākṣasas and transmitter of Purāṇa

- **Mind-son and Prajāpati** — **Pulastya** (पुलस्त्य) is one of the seven seers, a Saptarṣi of the current Manvantara. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12)
- **Father of Viśravas** — Pulastya begets **Viśravas** (Vaiśravaṇa's father), the hinge through which two opposed dynasties branch. (Rāmāyaṇa, Uttara Kāṇḍa; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.8)
- **Grandfather of Kubera** — By Viśravas's brahmin wife **Iḍaviḍā/Devavarṇinī** is born **Kubera**, the Yakṣa wealth-god and first lord of Laṅkā. (Rāmāyaṇa, Uttara Kāṇḍa) — see [Kubera](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/kubera.md).
- **Grandfather of the royal Rākṣasas** — By Viśravas's rākṣasī wife **Kaikasī** are born **Rāvaṇa, Kumbhakarṇa, Vibhīṣaṇa** and **Śūrpaṇakhā** — making the demon-king of the *Rāmāyaṇa* a **great-grandson of Brahmā** and **half-brother of Kubera**. (Rāmāyaṇa, Uttara Kāṇḍa) — see [Rākṣasas](../08-beings-and-bestiary/rakshasas.md).
  - *Variant (descent of rākṣasas):* texts also descend rākṣasas generally from **Khasā** (a daughter of Dakṣa, wife of Kaśyapa) or from **Nirṛti**, reserving the *named, royal* line for Pulastya's house. [disputed] (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.21)
- **Transmitter of the Viṣṇu Purāṇa** — Pulastya grants **Parāśara** (Vyāsa's father) the boon to be a complete narrator of Purāṇa-lore; the Viṣṇu Purāṇa's chain of transmission runs **Pulastya → Parāśara**, marking Pulastya as a key custodian of the genre. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.1) [scholarly]
- **The curse of the rākṣasa-form** — In one account Pulastya, disturbed in his austerities by the daughters of the sages, curses that any maiden seen by him shall conceive; the encounter with **Tṛṇabindu's daughter** yields Viśravas — a Purāṇic etiology tying the seer's irritation to the rākṣasa line. (Rāmāyaṇa, Uttara Kāṇḍa) [regional]

## Pulaha — the progenitor of creature-classes

- **Mind-son and Saptarṣi** — **Pulaha** (पुलह) is among the seven seers, the most lightly attested of the four. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7)
- **Father of beasts and demi-races** — By his wife **Kṣamā** (or **Gati**, a daughter of Kardama) Pulaha is said to father various **animal- and demi-classes** — kimpuruṣas, śarabhas, lions, and certain rākṣasa/piśāca and **Vālakhilya-adjacent** lines in some lists. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.10; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1)
  - *Variant (sons):* the Bhāgavata names his sons **Karmaśreṣṭha, Varīyāṃs and Sahiṣṇu** as the issue of Pulaha and Gati. (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1.41) [disputed]
- **Pulastīpura / Pulahāśrama** — His hermitage (**Pulahāśrama**, near the source of the Gaṇḍakī) is named as a tīrtha in pilgrimage geography. (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 5.7) [regional]

## Kratu — father of the Vālakhilyas

- **Mind-son and Saptarṣi** — **Kratu** (क्रतु, "sacrificial resolve / will") completes the four, named among the seven seers. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7)
- **Father of the Vālakhilyas** — By his wife **Kriyā** (a daughter of Kardama), Kratu is said to father the **sixty thousand thumb-sized ascetic sages, the Vālakhilyas**, who precede and escort the **Sun's chariot** radiating tapas. (Bhāgavata Purāṇa 4.1; Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.10)
  - *Variant (childless):* other accounts make Kratu **issueless**, his line continued only by adoption or absorbed into the Vālakhilya legend. [disputed]
- **The Vālakhilyas and Garuḍa** — In the Mahābhārata the Vālakhilyas, slighted by Indra, generate by their austerity the bird-of-power that becomes **Garuḍa** — tying Kratu's progeny to the Kaśyapa–Vinatā cycle. (Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva) — see [The Prajāpatis](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md).

## Their collective place

- **The genealogical floor** — Between them these four seed the **bestiary** (Marīci → Kaśyapa), the **Rākṣasa and Yakṣa dynasties** (Pulastya → Viśravas), and the **creature- and Vālakhilya-lines** (Pulaha, Kratu) — the foundation on which the rest of the timeline's lineages are built. (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7–10; Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12, 4.1)
- **Founders of gotras** — Each heads a brahmana *gotra* (Mārīca, Paulastya, Paulaha, Krātava) carried in *pravara* recitation. (gotra-pravara tradition) [scholarly]
- **Open membership** — Whether these seven (or ten, or twenty-one) exhaust the Prajāpatis is a matter of variant rosters, catalogued in [The Prajāpatis](../08-beings-and-bestiary/the-prajapatis.md). [disputed]

## Sources

- **Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.7–8, 1.10, 1.21** — the mind-born emission, the Saptarṣi list, the wives and progeny of the seers, Pulastya → Viśravas, and the rākṣasa-descent variants.
- **Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.1** — the Pulastya → Parāśara transmission of the Purāṇa.
- **Bhāgavata Purāṇa 3.12** — the mānasaputra creation and birth-organ catalogue; **4.1** — the wives (Kalā, Kṣamā/Gati, Kriyā) and sons of the seers, and the Vālakhilyas.
- **Rāmāyaṇa, Uttara Kāṇḍa** — the dynastic genealogy of Pulastya: Viśravas, Kubera, and the rākṣasa-children Rāvaṇa, Kumbhakarṇa, Vibhīṣaṇa, Śūrpaṇakhā; the rākṣasa-form curse.
- **Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva** — the Vālakhilyas and the genesis of Garuḍa.
- **Ṛgveda / Anukramaṇī** — hymns ascribed to Kaśyapa Mārīca, placing the line in the seer-stratum.
