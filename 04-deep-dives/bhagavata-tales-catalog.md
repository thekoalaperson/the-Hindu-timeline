# Bhāgavata Purāṇa — Catalog of Tales

> Where this sits: a **skandha-keyed index** of the narrative episodes of the *Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa* (Bhāgavata Purāṇa) — the great Vaiṣṇava Purāṇa of 12 skandhas (cantos) and ~18,000 verses, narrated by Śuka to King Parīkṣit in his last seven days.
> This is an **honest index, not a re-telling**: many of its biggest tales already have dedicated files in [04-deep-dives](README.md); this node enumerates *every* major episode, marks which are **[has file]** vs **[index-only]**, and routes the reader. Back to the [deep-dives index](README.md) · [master index](../README.md).

This file is **deliberately an index.** The Bhāgavata is a vast narrative anthology; it would be redundant to re-prose episodes that are already full nodes. Where a tale **has a dedicated file**, the entry is a one-line pointer. Where it does **not**, the entry is a fuller stub flagged **[index-only — candidate for own file]**. Chapter (adhyāya) numbers follow the Gītā Press / wisdomlib vulgate; recensions vary by a chapter or two, catalogued as nested variants. [scholarly]

---

## Skandha map (the 12 cantos at a glance)

The Bhāgavata's own self-description (the *daśa-lakṣaṇa*, ten themes, 2.10 & 12.7) frames it; narratively the cantos run:

- **1 — Prologue & frame:** Naimiṣa assembly, Vyāsa's despondency & Nārada's counsel, Kṛṣṇa's departure, Parīkṣit cursed, Śuka arrives. (Bhāg. 1)
- **2 — Cosmology:** Śuka on dhāraṇā, the Virāṭ-puruṣa, the avatāra-list, Brahmā's instruction. (Bhāg. 2)
- **3 — Vidura's pilgrimage; Varāha; Kapila→Devahūti.** (Bhāg. 3)
- **4 — Dakṣa, Dhruva, Pṛthu, the Pracetas.** (Bhāg. 4)
- **5 — Priyavrata, Ṛṣabha, Bharata/Jaḍa Bharata, the bhuvana-kośa (geography) & the hells.** (Bhāg. 5)
- **6 — Ajāmila, the Daksa-curse cycle, Vṛtra, the diti/Marut tale.** (Bhāg. 6)
- **7 — Prahlāda & Nṛsiṃha; varṇāśrama-dharma.** (Bhāg. 7)
- **8 — Manvantaras, Gajendra, Samudra-manthana, Vāmana, Matsya.** (Bhāg. 8)
- **9 — The solar & lunar dynasties (the great genealogical canto).** (Bhāg. 9)
- **10 — Kṛṣṇa-līlā (the longest canto, 90 chs.): Vraja & Dvārakā.** (Bhāg. 10)
- **11 — Yadu-destruction; the Uddhava-gītā & the 24 gurus; Kṛṣṇa's departure.** (Bhāg. 11)
- **12 — Kali-yuga prophecy, Parīkṣit's death, pralaya, Mārkaṇḍeya, the Purāṇa-catalogue, colophon.** (Bhāg. 12)

## Skandha 1–2 — Frame & cosmology

- **Vyāsa's despondency & the Bhāgavata's birth** - Nārada finds Vyāsa unsatisfied even after the Veda-division & Mahābhārata, and prescribes the singing of Hari's *līlā*; the Bhāgavata is composed. Aetiology of the text itself. (Bhāg. 1.4–6). **[index-only]**
- **Parīkṣit cursed by Śṛṅgī** - Abhimanyu's son, last of the Kuru line, garlands a meditating sage with a dead snake; the boy-ṛṣi's son curses him to die of Takṣaka's bite in seven days — the frame that occasions the whole recitation. Cross-link: [Uttaṅka](uttanka.md), [Mahābhārata frame](mahabharata/README.md). (Bhāg. 1.18–19). **[index-only]**
- **Kṛṣṇa's departure & the onset of Kali** - the Yadu-end and Kṛṣṇa's passing; Kali personified enters the world. → fuller in [the chiranjīvis / Mausala frame](mahabharata/16-mausala-parva.md). (Bhāg. 1.15). **[partial elsewhere]**
- **Śuka's dhāraṇā & the Virāṭ-puruṣa** - the cosmic-body meditation and the catalogue of avatāras (the locus classicus of the "22 avatāras" list). Cross-link: [avatāras of Viṣṇu](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/README.md). (Bhāg. 2.1, 2.6, 2.7). **[index-only]**

## Skandha 3 — Varāha & the teaching of Kapila

- **Vidura & Maitreya; Varāha-avatāra** - the framing dialogue; Varāha lifts the Earth and slays Hiraṇyākṣa. Cross-link: [Jaya-Vijaya](jaya-vijaya.md) (the doorkeepers cursed by the Four Kumāras, whose three asura-births include Hiraṇyākṣa/Hiraṇyakaśipu). (Bhāg. 3.13–19). **[partial — see jaya-vijaya.md]**
- **Kardama & Devahūti** - the prajāpati Kardama's tapas, his marriage to Svāyambhuva Manu's daughter Devahūti, the aerial palace, and the birth of nine daughters + the son who is Viṣṇu. (Bhāg. 3.21–24). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Kapila's teaching to Devahūti (Kapila-upadeśa / "Devahūti-putra-gītā")** - Viṣṇu incarnate as the sage **Kapila** (founder-figure of Sāṅkhya) instructs his mother in the analysis of *prakṛti/puruṣa*, the 24 (or 25) *tattvas*, devotional *bhakti-yoga* as supreme, *aṣṭāṅga*, embryology (the *garbha-stuti*), and liberation; Devahūti attains mokṣa. One of the two great philosophical dialogues of the Bhāgavata (with the Uddhava-gītā). Cross-link: [Sāṅkhya](../05-concepts-and-dharma/README.md), [Dattātreya's allied avadhūta teaching](dattatreya.md). (Bhāg. 3.25–33). [scholarly]
  - *Variant (chapter-count):* the *upadeśa* proper is most often cited as **3.25–32**, with 3.33 narrating Devahūti's liberation; some indices give **3.25–33** inclusive.
  - *Variant (tattvas):* Bhāgavata Sāṅkhya counts **24 elements + puruṣa (= 25)**, sometimes **+ kāla/time (= 26)** — distinct from classical Īśvarakṛṣṇa's atheistic 25. [disputed]
  **[index-only — candidate for own file]**

## Skandha 4 — Dakṣa, Dhruva, Pṛthu, the Pracetas

- **Dakṣa's sacrifice & Satī** - Dakṣa insults Śiva, Satī self-immolates, Vīrabhadra wrecks the yajña, Dakṣa is restored with a goat's head. Cross-link: [Śiva](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/README.md). (Bhāg. 4.2–7). **[index-only]**
- **Dhruva** - the slighted boy-prince who wins the pole-star by tapas. → **[has file]** [dhruva.md](dhruva.md). (Bhāg. 4.8–12).
- **Pṛthu & the milking of the Earth** - Vena's wickedness, his churning into Pṛthu (first consecrated king), and the milking of Pṛthivī. → **[has file]** [prithu-prithvi.md](prithu-prithvi.md). (Bhāg. 4.13–23).
- **Purañjana (allegory of the city)** - Nārada's allegory to King Prācīnabarhi: **Purañjana** the soul, his "city of nine gates" (the body), the queen (intelligence) and his entanglement in saṃsāra and rebirth as a woman. A rare sustained Purāṇic allegory. (Bhāg. 4.25–29). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **The Pracetas & the Rudra-gītā** - the ten **Pracetas** (sons of Prācīnabarhi), sent to perform tapas, meet **Śiva**, who teaches them the **Rudra-gītā** (a stotra/hymn to Viṣṇu-Saṅkarṣaṇa); they perform 10,000-year underwater austerity, marry the daughter of the trees, and father **Dakṣa** (rebirth). A notable Śaiva→Vaiṣṇava bridge passage. Cross-link: [lineages](../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md). (Bhāg. 4.24, 4.30–31). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**

## Skandha 5 — Ṛṣabha, Bharata, geography & hells

- **Priyavrata** - Svāyambhuva's renunciate son who, recalled to kingship, drives his chariot round Meru seven times, the ruts becoming the seven oceans & continents (a *bhuvana-kośa* aetiology). (Bhāg. 5.1). **[index-only]**
- **Ṛṣabha, Bharata & Jaḍa Bharata** - the Tīrthaṅkara-like avatāra-king Ṛṣabha, his son Bharata (eponym of Bhārata-varṣa), and Bharata's three lives (king → deer → the realised "dull" brāhmaṇa who instructs King Rahūgaṇa). → **[has file]** [rishabha-bharata-jada.md](rishabha-bharata-jada.md). (Bhāg. 5.2–14).
- **The bhuvana-kośa & the 28 hells (narakas)** - the Purāṇa's systematic cosmic geography (Jambūdvīpa, the dvīpas, Bhū-maṇḍala, the planetary śiśumāra) and the catalogue of **28 narakas** with their punishments. Cross-link: [sacred geography](../06-sacred-geography/README.md), [cosmology](../00-time-and-cosmology/README.md). (Bhāg. 5.16–26). **[index-only — geography handled in 06]**

## Skandha 6 — Ajāmila, Vṛtra, Dakṣa-curse cycle

- **Ajāmila** - the fallen brāhmaṇa saved at death by calling his son **"Nārāyaṇa"** — the great *nāma-mahimā* (glory-of-the-Name) parable; frames the Viṣṇudūta–Yamadūta debate. → **[has file]** [ajamila.md](ajamila.md). (Bhāg. 6.1–3).
- **Dakṣa's Haṃsaguhya & the curse of Nārada** - Dakṣa's hymn, and Nārada's cursing of Dakṣa's sons (the Haryaśvas & Savalāśvas) into renunciation, earning Nārada Dakṣa's counter-curse to wander. (Bhāg. 6.5). **[index-only]**
- **Vṛtra-vadha & the Nārāyaṇa-kavaca** - Indra slays **Vṛtra** with Dadhīci's bones; here Vṛtra is paradoxically a great devotee. The episode embeds the **Nārāyaṇa-kavaca** (armor-mantra). Cross-link: [Dadhīci](dadhichi.md), [Indra](../02-deities-and-cosmic-hierarchy/README.md). (Bhāg. 6.7–13). **[partial — see dadhichi.md]**
- **Citraketu** - the grieving king shown the soul's continuity, who becomes a vidyādhara, mocks Śiva-Pārvatī, is cursed by Pārvatī to be born the demon **Vṛtra**. (Bhāg. 6.14–17). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Diti's vow & the birth of the Maruts (the Pumsavana / Marut tale)** - Diti's penance to bear an Indra-killer; Indra enters her womb and cuts the embryo into the **49 Maruts**. (Bhāg. 6.18–19). **[index-only]**

## Skandha 9 — The Solar & Lunar dynasties (the genealogical canto)

The great *vaṃśa* canto (24 chapters). Most of its kings are catalogued in [03-lineages-vamsha](../03-lineages-vamsha/README.md); the **named exemplary episodes** are indexed here. Many already have files.

### Solar dynasty (Sūrya-vaṃśa / Ikṣvāku line)

- **Sudyumna / Ilā** - Vaivasvata Manu's child who, entering Śiva's forbidden grove, alternates between man (Sudyumna) and woman (Ilā) monthly — origin-point branching the solar & lunar lines. Cross-link: [Pururavas-Urvaśī](pururavas-urvashi.md) (Ilā's son). (Bhāg. 9.1). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Ikṣvāku & the eating of the śrāddha-hare; Vikukṣi** - dynastic seed-episodes. (Bhāg. 9.6). **[index-only]**
- **Saubhari** - the sage who, disturbed in his underwater tapas by mating fish, weds **all fifty** daughters of King Māndhātā by multiplying his body, then renounces it all — a *vairāgya* parable on the snares of desire. (Bhāg. 9.6). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Māndhātā** - the cakravartin born from his father, suckled by Indra. Cross-link & fuller stub: [minor-upakhyanas-catalog.md](minor-upakhyanas-catalog.md). (Bhāg. 9.6–7). **[stub elsewhere]**
- **Trishaṅku** - the king who sought heaven in his body, hung between worlds. → **[has file]** [trishanku.md](trishanku.md). (Bhāg. 9.7).
- **Hariścandra** - the truth-king who lost all and was restored. → **[has file]** [hariscandra.md](hariscandra.md); the Bhāgavata version is brief (the Sunaḥśepa link is fuller in [sunahshepa.md](sunahshepa.md)). (Bhāg. 9.7).
- **Sagara, the 60,000 sons & the descent of Gaṅgā (Bhagīratha)** - the aśvamedha-horse, the sons burnt by Kapila's glance, and **Bhagīratha's** tapas bringing Gaṅgā down to liberate their ashes. Cross-link: [Gaṅgā](../06-sacred-geography/README.md). (Bhāg. 9.8–9). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Khaṭvāṅga** - the warrior-king who, granted one *muhūrta* of life on returning from aiding the devas, attains liberation in that single hour by total surrender — a famed *kṣaṇa-mukti* exemplum. (Bhāg. 9.9). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Rāma (Raghu-vaṃśa précis)** - the Bhāgavata's condensed Rāmāyaṇa (Rāma as avatāra, Rāvaṇa's death, the return). → fuller treatment in [Rāmāyaṇa kāṇḍas](ramayana/README.md). (Bhāg. 9.10–11). **[partial — see ramayana/]**
- **Nimi & the Videha (Janaka) line** - Nimi's body-less curse (he becomes the "blink" in the eyes); rise of the bodiless-then-Mithilā Janakas. (Bhāg. 9.13). **[index-only]**

### Lunar dynasty (Candra-vaṃśa / Aila line)

- **Soma–Tārā–Budha & the birth of the Moon-line** - the abduction of Bṛhaspati's wife Tārā by Soma; Budha born; lunar dynasty begins. → **[has file]** [soma-tara-budha.md](soma-tara-budha.md). (Bhāg. 9.14).
- **Purūravas & Urvaśī** - the mortal king and the apsarā. → **[has file]** [pururavas-urvashi.md](pururavas-urvashi.md). (Bhāg. 9.14).
- **Paraśurāma** - the Bhṛgu avatāra who clears the earth of kṣatriyas 21 times; the Kārtavīrya / Jamadagni cycle. (Bhāg. 9.15–16). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Nahuṣa & Yayāti** - Nahuṣa's fall (cross-link [nahusha.md](nahusha.md)); Yayāti's exchange of old age, Devayānī & Śarmiṣṭhā. → **[has file]** [yayati.md](yayati.md); [Soma-Tārā](soma-tara-budha.md). (Bhāg. 9.18–19).
- **Bharata (son of Duṣyanta & Śakuntalā)** - eponym of the line. → **[has file]** [shakuntala-bharata.md](shakuntala-bharata.md). (Bhāg. 9.20).
- **Rantideva** - the supremely charitable Lunar-line king (descendant of Bharata) who, after a **48-day fast**, gives away his last food and water in succession to a brāhmaṇa, a śūdra, a man with dogs, and finally a *caṇḍāla* begging water — "I desire not the eight siddhis... only that I may dwell in all beings and suffer their suffering, so they be freed"; the testing Trimūrti reveal themselves and he attains liberation. A keystone *dāna* / compassion exemplum. Cross-link: [Śibi](shibi.md) (the allied self-giving-king archetype). (Bhāg. 9.21). [the Mahābhārata Droṇa/Śānti-parva tells him too]. **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **The Pūru / Kuru / Yadu branches → toward Kṛṣṇa** - the canto closes by running the Yadu line down to **Vasudeva** and the imminent birth of Kṛṣṇa, hinging the genealogical canto into Skandha 10. (Bhāg. 9.23–24). **[index-only]**

## Skandha 10 — Kṛṣṇa-līlā (the longest canto, 90 chapters)

The narrative heart. The overall arc has a **[has file]** in [krishna-lila.md](krishna-lila.md); the **named song-insets** (the *gītā*s) are indexed here, since each is a discrete devotional set-piece often anthologised on its own.

### Vraja (Vṛndāvana) — the boyhood

- **Birth & the Kaṃsa-demons** - Devakī's eighth child, the river-crossing, Yaśodā's fosterage; Pūtanā, Tṛṇāvarta, Śakaṭāsura, the twin Arjuna-trees (Yamala-Arjuna / Dāmodara-līlā), the universe in Kṛṣṇa's mouth. → **[has file]** [krishna-lila.md](krishna-lila.md). (Bhāg. 10.1–11).
- **Forest demons & Govardhana** - Aghāsura, Bakāsura, Dhenukāsura; the **Kāliya-damana**; the swallowing of the forest-fire; the **lifting of Govardhana** and the snubbing of Indra. → **[has file]** [krishna-lila.md](krishna-lila.md). (Bhāg. 10.12–27).
- **Veṇu-gītā (the Song of the Flute)** - the gopīs' rapturous description of Kṛṣṇa playing his flute and herding cattle home — Vṛndāvana's creatures, rivers and hills enraptured; sung *by the gopīs about* Kṛṣṇa. The lyrical overture to the rāsa cycle. (Bhāg. **10.21**). **[index-only — candidate for own file]**
- **Yugala-gītā, Gopī-gītā, Bhramara-gītā** - the cluster of gopī-songs:
  - *Rāsa-pañcādhyāya frame:* the **rāsa-līlā** proper (the circle-dance, the gopīs' pride and Kṛṣṇa's disappearance & return). (Bhāg. **10.29–33**).
  - **Gopī-gītā** - the 19-verse lament the gopīs sing when Kṛṣṇa vanishes mid-rāsa ("*jayati te'dhikaṃ janmanā vrajaḥ...*") — among the most beloved bhakti hymns. (Bhāg. **10.31**).
  - **Bhramara-gītā (the Song to the Bee)** - Rādhā/the gopīs' mad address to a bumblebee, reproaching the absent Kṛṣṇa, when Uddhava later visits Vraja. (Bhāg. **10.47**). [the locus of *vipralambha-bhakti*]
  **[index-only — candidate for own file(s)]**
- **The mahā-rāsa & the departure for Mathurā** - Akrūra's arrival, the breaking of the bow, Kaṃsa's death. → **[has file]** [krishna-lila.md](krishna-lila.md). (Bhāg. 10.38–44).

### Mathurā & Dvārakā — the kingly career

- **Uddhava's mission to Vraja** - Kṛṣṇa sends Uddhava to console the gopīs; occasion of the Bhramara-gītā above. (Bhāg. 10.46–47). **[index-only]**
- **Jarāsandha, Kālayavana, the founding of Dvārakā; Rukmiṇī-haraṇa; the 16,108 queens; Syamantaka jewel; Naraka-vadha; the burning of Kāśī (Pauṇḍraka); the Śiva–Bāṇāsura war (Uṣā–Aniruddha)** - the Dvārakā cycle. → **[has file, in part]** [krishna-lila.md](krishna-lila.md). (Bhāg. 10.50–90).
- **Śiśupāla** - the rival cousin whose 100 offences fill up at the Rājasūya. → **[has file]** [shishupala.md](shishupala.md). (Bhāg. 10.74; cf. MBh. Sabhā-parva).

## Skandha 11 — The Uddhava-gītā, the 24 gurus & Kṛṣṇa's departure

- **The curse on the Yadus & the Mausala destruction** - the Yādava sages cursed; the iron-club fratricide at Prabhāsa; Kṛṣṇa's withdrawal. Cross-link: [Mausala-parva](mahabharata/16-mausala-parva.md). (Bhāg. 11.1, 11.30–31). **[partial elsewhere]**
- **The Nine Yogendras (Nava-yogendra) to Nimi** - Nārada relates how the nine adept sons of Ṛṣabha taught King Nimi the *bhāgavata-dharma* — a compact theology of devotion. (Bhāg. 11.2–5). **[index-only]**
- **The Uddhava-gītā (Haṃsa-gītā / "the last message of Kṛṣṇa")** - Kṛṣṇa's final, longest discourse, to his friend-minister **Uddhava** on the eve of his departure: jñāna, bhakti, yoga, *varṇāśrama*, renunciation, the nature of the guru, and detachment — 1,077 verses, often published as a "sequel" to the Bhagavad-gītā. The second great philosophical dialogue of the Purāṇa (with Kapila's, S.3). Cross-link: [Bhagavad-gītā](bhagavad-gita.md). (Bhāg. **11.6–29**). [scholarly]
  - *Variant (extent):* commonly cited **11.7–29**; some editions count the framing of 11.6 in, giving **11.6–29**.
- **The 24 gurus of Dattātreya (the avadhūta's teachers)** - embedded *within* the Uddhava-gītā: Kṛṣṇa recounts the **avadhūta Dattātreya's** dialogue with King Yadu, listing the **24 gurus** the avadhūta took from nature — earth, air, sky, water, fire, moon, sun, pigeon, python, sea, moth, bee/honey-gatherer, elephant, honey-thief, deer, fish, the courtesan **Piṅgalā**, kuraṛa-bird, child, maiden (her bangles), arrow-maker, serpent, spider, and the *bhṛṅga-kīṭa* (wasp-and-worm). → **[has file]** [dattatreya.md](dattatreya.md); the courtesan Piṅgalā is also stubbed in [minor-upakhyanas-catalog.md](minor-upakhyanas-catalog.md). (Bhāg. **11.7–9**).
  - *Variant (count):* always **24** in the Bhāgavata; sub-lists of the named gurus vary slightly in ordering/identity across translations (e.g. "honey-gatherer" vs "bee," "hawk/kuraṛa" identifications). [scholarly]
- **Kṛṣṇa's departure (the hunter Jarā)** - the hunter mistakes the resting Kṛṣṇa's foot for a deer; Kṛṣṇa ascends; Dvārakā sinks. Cross-link: [Mausala-parva](mahabharata/16-mausala-parva.md). (Bhāg. 11.30–31). **[partial elsewhere]**

## Skandha 12 — Kali-yuga, the end of Parīkṣit, & the colophon

- **The course of Kali-yuga & future kings** - the prophetic catalogue of post-Mahābhārata dynasties (Nandas, Mauryas, etc.) and the symptoms of Kali. Cross-link: [yuga-time](../00-time-and-cosmology/README.md), [Kali-yuga](../01-manvantaras/manvantara-07-vaivasvata/mahayuga-28-current/README.md). (Bhāg. 12.1–3). **[index-only]**
- **The four pralayas** - the systematic catalogue of dissolution (nitya, naimittika, prākṛtika, ātyantika). Cross-link: [cosmology / pralaya](../00-time-and-cosmology/README.md). (Bhāg. 12.4). **[index-only]**
- **Parīkṣit & Takṣaka** - the king's fearless death by the serpent-bite, closing the frame; Janamejaya's snake-sacrifice. Cross-link: [Uttaṅka](uttanka.md), [Āstīka / snake-sacrifice](mahabharata/01-adi-parva.md). (Bhāg. 12.5–6). **[index-only]**
- **Mārkaṇḍeya** - the deathless sage who beholds the pralaya and the child on the banyan-leaf, and resists Kāmadeva. → **[has file]** [markandeya.md](markandeya.md). (Bhāg. 12.8–10).
- **The Purāṇa-catalogue & colophon** - enumeration of the 18 Mahāpurāṇas, the glory of the Bhāgavata, and the closing benediction. Cross-link: [literature corpus](../90-literature-corpus/README.md). (Bhāg. 12.7, 12.13). **[index-only]**
