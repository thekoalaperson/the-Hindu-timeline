# The Hindu Timeline

An exhaustive, source-cited map of the **entire Hindu chronology**, laid onto the traditional cosmic
time hierarchy of Hinduism — from the units of a single instant up through the 311-trillion-year life of
Brahmā, down to the documented history of our own age.

**Scope:** maximal — scripture *and* recorded history *and* philosophy *and* regional/folk traditions.
**Method:** every recorded event/story gets a dense, cited bullet. Where the texts disagree
(different Purāṇas, or Vaishnava / Shaiva / Shakta / regional traditions), **all variants are cataloged**
and attributed to their source, never silently merged.

> ⚠️ Two clocks run in parallel here. The **scriptural/cosmic clock** (yugas, manvantaras, kalpas) is the
> organizing spine. The **historical/academic clock** (datable events, scholarly text-dating) appears
> mainly in the Kali-Yuga history branch and is always flagged as such. Traditional time is **cyclic**,
> not linear — the whole tree repeats.

## Conventions used in every file
- **H1 title**, then a one-line "where this sits in the hierarchy" breadcrumb.
- Dense **event bullets**: `**Event** — what happens, key actors. (Source text)`.
- Nested `- *Variant (SourceText):* …` bullets catalog disagreements.
- Tags: `[disputed]`, `[folk tradition]`, `[late text]`, `[scholarly]` distinguish certainty levels.
- A `## Sources` section at the foot of every file lists the texts and references used.

## Status & how this was built
- **38 content files**, ~53,000 words, built by a 39-agent research workflow (depth-first on the current
  manvantara and its four yugas; the other 13 manvantaras are concise, expandable stubs).
- This is a **living first pass**. Stubs are marked `[STUB — expandable]`. Natural next steps: deepen the
  past/future manvantaras, add per-event dedicated files for the biggest episodes (Samudra Manthana, the
  full Rāmāyaṇa/Mahābhārata parva-by-parva), and expand the literature corpus into per-text files.

*Sources are scriptural (Vedas, Itihāsa, Purāṇas, etc.) and scholarly; cosmic dating is traditional, historical dating is academic and flagged as such.*
