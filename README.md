# The Hindu Timeline

An exhaustive, source-cited map of the **entire Hindu chronology**, laid onto the traditional cosmic
time hierarchy of Hinduism — from the units of a single instant up through the 311-trillion-year life of
Brahmā, down to the documented history of our own age.

**Scope:** maximal — scripture *and* recorded history *and* philosophy *and* regional/folk traditions.
**Method:** every recorded event/story gets a dense, cited bullet. Where the texts disagree
(different Purāṇas, or Vaishnava / Shaiva / Shakta / regional traditions), **all variants are cataloged**
and attributed to their source, never silently merged.

> ⚠️ Two clocks run in parallel here. The **scriptural/cosmic clock** (yugas, manvantaras, kalpas) is the
> organizing spine. The **historical/academic clock** (datable events, scholarly text-dating) appears
> mainly in the Kali-Yuga history branch and is always flagged as such. Traditional time is **cyclic**,
> not linear — the whole tree repeats.

## Conventions used in every file
- **H1 title**, then a one-line "where this sits in the hierarchy" breadcrumb.
- Dense **event bullets**: `**Event** — what happens, key actors. (Source text)`.
- Nested `- *Variant (SourceText):* …` bullets catalog disagreements.
- Tags: `[disputed]`, `[folk tradition]`, `[late text]`, `[scholarly]` distinguish certainty levels.
- A `## Sources` section at the foot of every file lists the texts and references used.

## Status & how this was built
- **600 files, ~1,330,000 words**, built across a multi-round research campaign (~1,100 agents total):
  cosmological spine + literature corpus → deepened epics/episodes & gap audit → per-Purāṇa story-maps →
  deepened manvantaras & long-tail tales → festivals/ācāryas/regional/Vedic → a wide completeness sweep
  (beings, weapons, celestial systems, deity forms) → an adversarial **accuracy audit (25 verified
  corrections)** → structural simplification → a **whole-corpus audit (51 further corrections across
  42 files)** + 15 gap-filling deep-dives → a **systematic 42-lens enumerate-and-verify sweep** that
  surfaced 651 verified gaps and closed them with **~190 new files + 20 expansions** (the Panch Kailāsh
  incl. Kinnaur Kailash, every major temple/tīrtha, regional & folk deities, epic minor characters, each
  avatar/Śiva-form/Devī-episode, Upaniṣads & Brāhmaṇas, …) → **two convergence rounds** that closed the tail
  (all principal Upaniṣads, the Kūrma & Mohinī avatāras, the great Sahasranāmas & Śrī Rudram, Arunachala,
  Vaishno Devi, the Vārkarī & grāmadevatā traditions, …) → a final tail-sweep (more stotras, minor
  Upaniṣads, the Ṛgvedic rishikās, citra-kalā, Udupi, the Pañcārāma kṣetras) → a **full TIMELINE
  re-extraction (5,761 events)**. Four successive discovery sweeps drove the major-gap count 201 → 24 → 9 → (promotions only).
- **Invariants (verified):** every file is reachable from this index, and **0 broken internal links**
  (files *and* directories checked), **0 orphans**. Every section folder carries a complete auto-generated index.
